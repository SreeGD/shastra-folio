Chapter 10 · Class prep

A printable handout for teaching this chapter. For each verse with Personal Guidance, you'll see Prabhupāda's translation, the essence, two or three practical applications, and a discussion prompt. Press ⌘P / Ctrl P to print as a handout.

BG 10.1

The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: Listen again, O mighty-armed Arjuna. Because you are My dear friend, for your benefit I shall speak to you further, giving knowledge that is better than what I have already explained.

Essence. Hear again My supreme word, which I speak for your benefit, dear friend — knowledge of Kṛṣṇa is repeated for those who can receive it.

Practical applications
  • Welcome repetition as kindness, not redundancy.
  • Receive the same teaching twice with greater openness.
  • Don't dismiss familiar verses; receive them fresh.

BG 10.2

Neither the hosts of demigods nor the great sages know My origin or opulences, for, in every respect, I am the source of the demigods and sages.

Essence. Neither the demigods nor the great sages know My origin — for I am the source of them all.

Practical applications
  • Hold humility about Kṛṣṇa's origin; even great souls don't fathom it.
  • Don't pretend to comprehend what is by nature beyond.
  • Approach Him with awe, not analysis.

BG 10.3

He who knows Me as the unborn, as the beginningless, as the Supreme Lord of all the worlds – he only, undeluded among men, is freed from all sins.

Essence. He who knows Me as unborn, beginningless, the great Lord — among mortals, he is undeluded and freed from all sins.

Practical applications
  • Cultivate this knowledge — Kṛṣṇa as unborn, beginningless, great Lord.
  • Use it as a daily refrain.
  • Trust that this knowledge frees.

BG 10.4-5

Intelligence, knowledge, freedom from doubt and delusion, forgiveness, truthfulness, control of the senses, control of the mind, happiness and distress, birth, death, fear, fearlessness, nonviolence, equanimity, satisfaction, austerity, charity, fame and infamy – all these various qualities of living beings are created by Me alone.

Essence. Intelligence, knowledge, freedom from doubt, forgiveness, truthfulness, sense control, peace, pleasure and pain, birth and death, fear and fearlessness, nonviolence, equanimity, satisfaction, austerity, charity, fame, infamy — all these qualities of beings come from Me alone.

Practical applications
  • Trace your virtues back to their source — Kṛṣṇa.
  • Don't claim authorship of your own qualities.
  • Use this verse as a humbling daily contemplation.

BG 10.6

The seven great sages and before them the four other great sages and the Manus [progenitors of mankind] come from Me, born from My mind, and all the living beings populating the various planets descend from them.

Essence. The seven great sages and four Manus — all originate in Me; all populations come from them.

Practical applications
  • Honor the lineage — even great sages have a source.
  • Recognize traditions as descending, not invented.
  • Don't approach Vedic culture as folklore.

BG 10.7

One who is factually convinced of this opulence and mystic power of Mine engages in unalloyed devotional service; of this there is no doubt.

Essence. He who knows in truth this opulence and yoga of Mine engages in unfaltering devotional service — there is no doubt about it.

Practical applications
  • Cultivate knowledge of Kṛṣṇa's opulence (vibhūti).
  • Trust that the right knowledge produces unfaltering devotion.
  • Don't try to manufacture devotion without knowledge.

BG 10.8

I am the source of all spiritual and material worlds. Everything emanates from Me. The wise who perfectly know this engage in My devotional service and worship Me with all their hearts.

Essence. I am the source of all material and spiritual worlds; everything emanates from Me — the wise know this and worship Me with all their heart.

Practical applications
  • Recognize Kṛṣṇa as the source of even the spiritual world.
  • Worship 'with all heart' as the appropriate response.
  • Don't compartmentalize devotion.

BG 10.9

The thoughts of My pure devotees dwell in Me, their lives are fully devoted to My service, and they derive great satisfaction and bliss from always enlightening one another and conversing about Me.

Essence. The thoughts of My pure devotees dwell in Me; their lives are surrendered to Me — they derive great satisfaction enlightening one another about Me.

Practical applications
  • Cultivate sat-saṅga that dwells on Kṛṣṇa.
  • Take satisfaction in mutually enlightening conversation.
  • Reduce time in conversations that don't dwell on Him.

BG 10.10

To those who are constantly devoted to serving Me with love, I give the understanding by which they can come to Me.

Essence. To those constantly devoted, worshiping Me with love, I give the understanding by which they come to Me.

Practical applications
  • Trust that understanding is given, not constructed.
  • Cultivate constant devotion as the qualifier.
  • Don't grasp at understanding — receive it.

BG 10.11

To show them special mercy, I, dwelling in their hearts, destroy with the shining lamp of knowledge the darkness born of ignorance.

Essence. Out of compassion for them, I, dwelling in their hearts, destroy with the shining lamp of knowledge the darkness born of ignorance.

Practical applications
  • Recognize Kṛṣṇa's active role within your heart.
  • Don't try to dispel darkness alone; invite His lamp.
  • Trust the indwelling Lord.

BG 10.12-13

Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Personality of Godhead, the ultimate abode, the purest, the Absolute Truth. You are the eternal, transcendental, original person, the unborn, the greatest. All the great sages such as Nārada, Asita, Devala and Vyāsa confirm this truth about You, and now You Yourself are declaring it to me.

Essence. Arjuna says: You are the Supreme Brahman, the supreme abode, the supreme purifier, the eternal divine person, the original god, unborn — all the great sages confirm it.

Practical applications
  • Cultivate explicit articulation of Kṛṣṇa's identity.
  • Speak it aloud in prayer — don't keep it implicit.
  • Trust the testimony of great souls who have said the same.

BG 10.14

O Kṛṣṇa, I totally accept as truth all that You have told me. Neither the demigods nor the demons, O Lord, can understand Your personality.

Essence. All You have said, I accept as truth — neither demigods nor demons can understand Your personality.

Practical applications
  • Practice Arjuna's posture: 'all You have said, I accept.'
  • Don't reserve mental veto over what He says.
  • Surrender the cognitive judge.

BG 10.15

Indeed, You alone know Yourself by Your own internal potency, O Supreme Person, origin of all, Lord of all beings, God of gods, Lord of the universe!

Essence. You alone know Yourself by Yourself — O Supreme Person, source and Lord of all beings, God of gods, master of the universe.

Practical applications
  • Acknowledge that He alone fully knows Himself.
  • Approach Him through Himself, not through speculation.
  • Use scripture as His self-revelation.

BG 10.16

Please tell me in detail of Your divine opulences by which You pervade all these worlds.

Essence. Please describe in detail Your divine opulences by which You pervade all worlds — I never tire of hearing of You.

Practical applications
  • Cultivate the same hunger Arjuna shows.
  • Increase your appetite for hearing about Kṛṣṇa.
  • Never tire of His glorification.

BG 10.17

O Kṛṣṇa, O supreme mystic, how shall I constantly think of You, and how shall I know You? In what various forms are You to be remembered, O Supreme Personality of Godhead?

Essence. How shall I know You constantly thinking of You? In what forms should You be remembered?

Practical applications
  • Ask Kṛṣṇa to teach you how to remember Him.
  • Don't assume you know how; the Gītā gives technique.
  • Receive the answer that follows.

BG 10.18

O Janārdana, again please describe in detail the mystic power of Your opulences. I am never satiated in hearing about You, for the more I hear the more I want to taste the nectar of Your words.

Essence. Tell me again in detail of Your yogic mystery and opulences; I never tire of hearing Your nectarean words.

Practical applications
  • Treat hearing Kṛṣṇa's words as nectar, not lecture.
  • Notice your own attention quality during scripture-reading.
  • Cultivate the appetite by feeding it.

BG 10.19

The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: Yes, I will tell you of My splendorous manifestations, but only of those which are prominent, O Arjuna, for My opulence is limitless.

Essence. I shall describe to you the principal of My divine opulences — there is no end to My grandeur.

Practical applications
  • Trust that Kṛṣṇa's grandeur exceeds any list.
  • Use the listed opulences as starting points, not exhaustive.
  • Continue noticing Him in unlisted places.

BG 10.20

I am the Supersoul, O Arjuna, seated in the hearts of all living entities. I am the beginning, the middle and the end of all beings.

Essence. I am the Self situated in the heart of all living beings — I am the beginning, middle, and end.

Practical applications
  • Recognize Kṛṣṇa in the heart of every being you meet.
  • Treat every encounter as opportunity to honor Him.
  • Reduce abstract devotion in favor of concrete recognition.

BG 10.21

Of the Ādityas I am Viṣṇu, of lights I am the radiant sun, of the Maruts I am Marīci, and among the stars I am the moon.

Essence. Of the Ādityas I am Viṣṇu; of luminaries I am the radiant sun; of the winds I am Marīci; of stars I am the moon.

Practical applications
  • See the sun and moon as Kṛṣṇa's specific opulences, not generic phenomena.
  • Step outside daily and recognize Him.
  • Use sky as reminder.

BG 10.22

Of the Vedas I am the Sāma Veda; of the demigods I am Indra, the king of heaven; of the senses I am the mind; and in living beings I am the living force [consciousness].

Essence. Of the Vedas I am the Sāma; of demigods I am Indra; of the senses I am the mind; of beings I am consciousness.

Practical applications
  • Recognize Kṛṣṇa as the consciousness within you.
  • Honor mind as His specific opulence — and yet a tool.
  • Use mental phenomena as remembrance, not just function.

BG 10.23

Of all the Rudras I am Lord Śiva, of the Yakṣas and Rākṣasas I am the Lord of wealth [Kuvera], of the Vasus I am fire [Agni], and of mountains I am Meru.

Essence. Of the Rudras I am Lord Śiva; of the Yakṣas and Rākṣasas I am the lord of wealth; of the Vasus I am fire; of mountains I am Meru.

Practical applications
  • Don't dismiss other deities; Kṛṣṇa says even they manifest Him.
  • Honor the layered theological richness of the Vedas.
  • Avoid sectarian dismissal.

BG 10.24

Of priests, O Arjuna, know Me to be the chief, Bṛhaspati. Of generals I am Kārttikeya, and of bodies of water I am the ocean.

Essence. Of priests I am the chief, Bṛhaspati; of generals I am Skanda; of bodies of water I am the ocean.

Practical applications
  • See excellence in any field as Kṛṣṇa's specific opulence.
  • Honor masters in their craft as expressions of Him.
  • Don't separate 'spiritual' from 'excellent.'

BG 10.25

Of the great sages I am Bhṛgu; of vibrations I am the transcendental oṁ. Of sacrifices I am the chanting of the holy names [japa], and of immovable things I am the Himālayas.

Essence. Of the great sages I am Bhṛgu; of vibrations I am the syllable Om; of sacrifices I am japa; of immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Practical applications
  • Of all sacrifices, japa is supreme — make it central.
  • Don't undervalue the simplest practice.
  • Cultivate it as the fundamental yajña.

BG 10.26

Of all trees I am the banyan tree, and of the sages among the demigods I am Nārada. Of the Gandharvas I am Citraratha, and among perfected beings I am the sage Kapila.

Essence. Of trees I am the banyan; of demigod sages I am Nārada; of Gandharvas I am Citraratha; of perfected beings I am Kapila.

Practical applications
  • Honor Nārada Muni as the model of the wandering devotee.
  • Read Nārada's pastimes regularly.
  • Receive Bhakti-sūtra-style teachings as Kṛṣṇa-direct.

BG 10.27

Of horses know Me to be Uccaiḥśravā, produced during the churning of the ocean for nectar. Of lordly elephants I am Airāvata, and among men I am the monarch.

Essence. Of horses I am Uccaiḥśravas, born of nectar; of elephants Airāvata; of men, the king.

Practical applications
  • See magnificence in beings as His expression.
  • Honor leadership as His specific opulence.
  • Don't be cynical about kingly authority — it can be His.

BG 10.28

Of weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows I am the surabhi. Of causes for procreation I am Kandarpa, the god of love, and of serpents I am Vāsuki.

Essence. Of weapons I am the thunderbolt; of cows the surabhi; of progenitors I am Kandarpa; of serpents I am Vāsuki.

Practical applications
  • Honor the cow as His opulence — protect, don't exploit.
  • Recognize even procreative force as His expression when in dharma.
  • Don't reduce nature to commodity.

BG 10.29

Of the many-hooded Nāgas I am Ananta, and among the aquatics I am the demigod Varuṇa. Of departed ancestors I am Aryamā, and among the dispensers of law I am Yama, the lord of death.

Essence. Of Nāgas I am Ananta; of aquatics I am Varuṇa; of ancestors I am Aryamā; of dispensers of law I am Yama.

Practical applications
  • Honor Yama (Lord of death) — He brings dharma's accountability.
  • Treat dharmic discipline as Kṛṣṇa's specific opulence.
  • Don't fear death; it serves dharma.

BG 10.30

Among the Daitya demons I am the devoted Prahlāda, among subduers I am time, among beasts I am the lion, and among birds I am Garuḍa.

Essence. Of the demoniac I am Prahlāda; of subduers I am time; of beasts I am the lion; of birds Garuḍa.

Practical applications
  • Recognize Prahlāda as a demoniac descendant who is supreme devotee.
  • Trust that lineage doesn't determine devotion.
  • Apply this to any 'wrong family' you may feel born into.

BG 10.31

Of purifiers I am the wind, of the wielders of weapons I am Rāma, of fishes I am the shark, and of flowing rivers I am the Ganges.

Essence. Of purifiers I am the wind; of warriors I am Rāma; of fishes I am the shark; of flowing rivers I am the Ganges.

Practical applications
  • Bathe in or remember the Ganges as a purification practice.
  • Honor Rāma as warrior-Lord, not just gentle prince.
  • Recognize the wind, rivers, and creatures as specific Kṛṣṇa-presences.

BG 10.32

Of all creations I am the beginning and the end and also the middle, O Arjuna. Of all sciences I am the spiritual science of the self, and among logicians I am the conclusive truth.

Essence. Of all creations, I am the beginning, the end, and the middle; of sciences, the science of the self; of debaters, the conclusive truth.

Practical applications
  • Honor self-knowledge (adhyātma-vidyā) as the highest science.
  • Aim for conclusive truth, not endless debate.
  • Use debate to arrive, not to wander.

BG 10.33

Of letters I am the letter A, and among compound words I am the dual compound. I am also inexhaustible time, and of creators I am Brahmā.

Essence. Of letters I am the letter A; of compounds I am the dual; I am inexhaustible time; the creator with faces in all directions.

Practical applications
  • Treat sacred sound as His specific manifestation.
  • Read scripture aloud as participation in His sound-form.
  • Honor language itself.

BG 10.34

I am all-devouring death, and I am the generating principle of all that is yet to be. Among women I am fame, fortune, fine speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and patience.

Essence. I am the all-devouring death; I am the principle of all that is yet to be; among women, I am fame, fortune, fine speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness, patience.

Practical applications
  • Recognize feminine virtues as Kṛṣṇa's specific opulence.
  • Honor the seven feminine qualities listed; cultivate them.
  • Don't separate the feminine from the divine.

BG 10.35

Of the hymns in the Sāma Veda I am the Bṛhat-sāma, and of poetry I am the Gāyatrī. Of months I am Mārgaśīrṣa [November-December], and of seasons I am flower-bearing spring.

Essence. Of hymns I am Bṛhat-sāma; of meters I am Gāyatrī; of months I am Mārgaśīrṣa; of seasons I am flower-bearing spring.

Practical applications
  • Chant the Gāyatrī if initiated and inclined.
  • Honor sacred timing — months, seasons, days.
  • Use seasonal cycles for sādhana renewal.

BG 10.36

I am also the gambling of cheats, and of the splendid I am the splendor. I am victory, I am adventure, and I am the strength of the strong.

Essence. Of cheats I am gambling; of the splendid I am the splendor; I am victory, I am adventure, I am the strength of the strong.

Practical applications
  • Recognize that even ambivalent qualities (gambling) are within Him.
  • Don't simplify Kṛṣṇa to only positive traits.
  • Honor the full range of His opulences.

BG 10.37

Of the descendants of Vṛṣṇi I am Vāsudeva, and of the Pāṇḍavas I am Arjuna. Of the sages I am Vyāsa, and among great thinkers I am Uśanā.

Essence. Of the Vṛṣṇis I am Vāsudeva; of the Pāṇḍavas I am Arjuna; of sages I am Vyāsa; of great thinkers I am Uśanā.

Practical applications
  • Honor Vyāsa, who compiled the Vedas.
  • Read Vyāsa's works (Mahābhārata, Bhāgavatam) regularly.
  • Treat the writers of scripture as Kṛṣṇa's specific opulences.

BG 10.38

Among all means of suppressing lawlessness I am punishment, and of those who seek victory I am morality. Of secret things I am silence, and of the wise I am the wisdom.

Essence. Of all means of subduing the lawless I am punishment; of those who seek victory I am morality; of secret things I am silence; the wisdom of the wise I am.

Practical applications
  • Honor silence as His specific manifestation.
  • Use silence in your spiritual practice deliberately.
  • Don't treat speech as default.

BG 10.39

Furthermore, O Arjuna, I am the generating seed of all existences. There is no being – moving or nonmoving – that can exist without Me.

Essence. I am the seed of all existences — there is no being, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Practical applications
  • Recognize Him as the seed of all being.
  • Look at any being and see the seed.
  • Use this seeing as portable contemplation.

BG 10.40

O mighty conqueror of enemies, there is no end to My divine manifestations. What I have spoken to you is but a mere indication of My infinite opulences.

Essence. There is no end to My divine manifestations — what I have described is only a fragment.

Practical applications
  • Hold the lists as samples, not catalogs.
  • Continue noticing Him beyond the listed.
  • Cultivate ongoing surprise at His expressions.

BG 10.41

Know that all opulent, beautiful and glorious creations spring from but a spark of My splendor.

Essence. Whatever is glorious, beautiful, opulent — know that it springs from but a spark of My splendor.

Practical applications
  • Trace beauty, glory, opulence back to Him.
  • Don't separate aesthetic experience from His presence.
  • Use beauty as remembrance.

BG 10.42

But what need is there, Arjuna, for all this detailed knowledge? With a single fragment of Myself I pervade and support this entire universe.

Essence. But what need is there of all this detailed knowledge? With a single fragment of Myself I sustain the entire universe.

Practical applications
  • Hold Kṛṣṇa's grandeur in mind: a fragment sustains all.
  • Stop measuring Him with your scale.
  • Trust that your worry is small in His sight.
