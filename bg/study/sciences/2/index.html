2. What are The Essential Teachings of the Bhagavad-Gita?

God is one and He is known by different names The living entity is the eternal soul and not the temporary body All souls are 100% equal. All are eternal, full of knowledge, and full of bliss All souls are equally very dear to God Life is an intelligent design, nothing happens by chance (Karma) No violence towards any living being (animals, humans, trees) No intoxications (alcohol, drugs, tobacco), and gambling No relationship outside marriage No hatred, lying, deception, stealing, and cursing No eternal damnation for any soul Perform activities without expectations Give up lust, greed, and anger Live a simple life by being satisfied with what you have and not make demands on others Be grateful to God and others for all you have, even if minimal Appreciate what others have done for you, even if they gave you just one grain of rice
