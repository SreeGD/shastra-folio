Everywhere in India and other countries, people are destroying trees and grass, replacing them with buildings. People have lost their basic common sense. Please stop this non-sense now by saving tees and planting more trees and grass. In Bhagavad-Gita 12.13-14, Lord Krishna very clearly states that one who is friendly and compassionate to all living beings is very dear to Him. Trees and grass are living beings.
