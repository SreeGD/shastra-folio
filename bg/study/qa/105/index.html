Q105. What is leather?

Leather items like sofas, car seats, jackets, handbags, and chair covers, are mainly made from cows. This means cows are killed so you can sit on them or wear them. Please don’t buy leather, buy cloth sofas, cloth car seats, cloth clothing, and clothe bags. Due to mass ignorance, Hindus will not eat cows, but will happily sit on them (leather sofas and leather car seats). It has become a custom for rich Hindus to buy luxury items like leather sofas and cars with leather seats. Please spread the message. Don’t buy leather, go for cloth. You should not buy anything that hurts any living being.
