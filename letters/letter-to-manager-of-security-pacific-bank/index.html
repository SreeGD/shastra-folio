Letter to: Manager of Security Pacific Bank

May 21st 1970 · Los Angeles · To: Manager of Security Pacific Bank

70-05-21

Manager Security Pacific Bank Culver City Branch No. 94 Los Angeles, Cal.

Dear Sir:

Re: My account No. 836-237 with you

Please transfer $3,600 (Three thousand, and six hundred dollars) to the First National Bank of Boston, Brighton Avenue Branch, in favor of account No. 516-5642, ISKCON Press, and charge the same to my account as above mentioned.

Yours sincerely, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
