Letter to: To Whom It May Concern

August 28th 1967 · To: To Whom It May Concern

Shri Radha Damodar Temple, Sebakunja, Vrindaban

Shri Radha Krishna Temple, 2439, Chhipi Wada Kalan, Post Box No. 1846, DELHI-6.

Be it Hereby known that Kirtanananda Brahmachari (Keith Gordon Ham) was initiated by me into the Sannyas Order, Goudiya Sampradaya, as TRIDANDI SWAMI KIRTANANANDA, on this 28th day of August, 1967, at Vrindaban, India.

(signed) His Holiness A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

Witnessed and assisted by: His Holiness B.S. Yachak
