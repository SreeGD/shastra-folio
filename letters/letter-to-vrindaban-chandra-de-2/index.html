Letter to: Vrindaban Chandra De

April 13th 1976 · To: Vrindaban Chandra De

Sriman Vrindaban Chandra De, Vrinda Book Co., 30/1B, College Row, Calcutta-700-009, W. Bengal

Hare Krishna Land, Juhu Road, Juhu, Bombay 400-054

My dear Vrindaban, Please accept my blessings. With further reference to my letter dated 7/4/76, I beg to enclose herewith a "true copy" of the receipt by Punjab National Bank. You can go immediately to see the bank manager and do the needful.

It is signed (signature illegible) by a bank official and it has the official stamp of the Punjab National Bank dated 5/3/71.

I hope that this meets you in good health.

Your ever well-wisher, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
