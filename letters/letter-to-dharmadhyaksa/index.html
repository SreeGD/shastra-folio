Letter to: Dharmadhyaksa

May 29th 1976 · Honolulu · To: Dharmadhyaksa

76-05-29

Los Angeles

My dear Dharmadhyaksa dasa,

Please accept my blessings. I am in due receipt of your "Simple Living and High Thinking" magazine. The article is very well-written and convincing. Thank you very much.

I hope that this meets you in good health.

Your ever well-wisher, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

ACBS/pks
