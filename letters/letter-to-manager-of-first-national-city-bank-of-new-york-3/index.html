Letter to: Manager of First National City Bank of New York

October 31st 1969 · Tittenhurst · To: Manager of First National City Bank of New York

69-10-31

Manager FIRST NATIONAL CITY BANK OF NEW YORK (Great Jones Street Branch) 686 Broadway New York, NY 10012 USA

Dear Sir;

I beg to enclose herewith a copy of a letter sent to your Bowery and Grand Street Branch in New York. Please do the needful and send me my passbook as early as possible. Thanking you in anticipation of your early reply.

Sincerely,

A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
