Letter to: Jagadananda

May 11th 1977 · To: Jagadananda

Sri Chaudhury Jagannath Prasad Dash, Sub-Assistant Engineer, Nuasahi, Nayapalli, Bhubaneswar 12, District Puri, Orissa

Reply: 21A Feroze Gandhi Rd. Lajpatnagar III New Delhi 110024 CAMP: Risikesh

My dear Jagadananda, Please accept my blessings and offer the same to all of your family members. I beg to acknowledge receipt of your letter dated 1st May, 1977.

Regarding your daughter's illness, all of the family members should sit down together and hold Hare Krsna kirtan. Let your daughter hear and she will be alright.

Hoping this meets you well.

Your ever well-wisher, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
