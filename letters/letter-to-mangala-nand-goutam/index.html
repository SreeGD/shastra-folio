Letter to: Mangala Nand Goutam

February 29th 1964 · Delhi · To: Mangala Nand Goutam

64-02-29

Sri Mangala Nand Goutam Editor Rastrapati Jama Masjid Dispensary Delhi-6

Please receive the following books on sales and return basis in terms of you letter of date. Please also receive the order book also herewith. 5 copies of SRIMAD BHAGAVATAM Vol. 1 sales price Rs. 16/- (Returnable if not sold within one week) 1 copy Order book No. 23 to 10C.

A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
