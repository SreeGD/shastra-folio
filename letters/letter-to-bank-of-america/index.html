Letter

Letter to: Bank of America

August 15th 1972 · Los Angeles · To: Bank of America

LT_720815_A1

Manager,

Bank of America,

Pico-La Cienega Branch,

Los Angeles.

Dear sir,

Re:My checking Account No. 3081-61625

Kindly transfer the above account with all balance to your Branch No. 366, 3849 Main Street, Culver City, which is nearer to my residence at the above address.

Thanking you once more.

Yours sincerely,

A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

ACBS/sda
