Letter to: Manager, State Bank of India

September 10th 1975 · To: Manager, State Bank of India

State Bank of India Vrindaban Branch Att: The Manager

Krishna Balaram Mandir Vrindaban, U.P.

Re: Savings A/c No. P2236

Dear Sir: Enclosed herewith please find one check on the State Savings Bank of Victoria, Melbourne, Australia, Check No. L 375362 dated September 5, 1975 for the amount of U.S. Dollars 126.50.

Please credit this amount in the above mentioned account, and bring my passbook up to date, and return.

Thanking you.

Yours sincerely, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

Enc: 1 Check No. L 375362, 1 passbook RECEIVED ON BEHALF OF STATE BANK F INDIA, 1 check for U.S. Dollars 126.50, and 1 passbook No. 1682

Signature: Dated: September 10, 1975
