Letter to: Manager of First National City Bank

November 25th 1969 · London · To: Manager of First National City Bank

69-11-25

Manager First National City Bank 686 Broadway (near Great Jones Street) New York, NY 10012

Dear Sir,

Replying your letter of November 6, 1969 I beg to send you herewith as follows:

1) My Passbook a/c No. 042016131 with your Grand Street branch.

2) Six pieces of paper duly signed by me on the cross spots as marked by you.

3) Two checks:

No. 59-102/1213 for $30.00

No. 137 for 15.00 for credit of my a/c.

Please return the Passbook duly made up registered mail, and send me a few pieces of deposit slips for savings bank a/c.

Yours truly, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
