Letter to: Manager, State Bank of India

October 18th 1975 · To: Manager, State Bank of India

Regd. Post State Bank of India, Vrindaban Branch Attn: The Manager

Hare Krishna Land, Gandhi Gram Road, Juhu, Bomby 400-054, India 18 October, 1975

[Copy]

Re: Savings Account No. P2236

Dear Sir: Enclosed please find the following checks:

1. Bank check on Bank of Hawaii, Kapaa, Hawaii, No. 30-122492 (1-23/210), dated September 29,1975 for the amount of U.S. Dollars 141.29 (One-hundred forty-one dollars and twenty-nine cents).

2. Check of I.S.K.C.O.N., The International Society for Krishna Consciousness, 439 Henry Street, Brooklyn, N.Y. 11231, U.S.A. whose checking account is with First National City Bank, 181 Montague Street, Brooklyn, N.Y. 11201, U.S.A., check no. 6314 (1-8/210 51) for the amount of U.S. Dollars 500.00 (Five-hundred U.S. Dollars), dated 9/22/75.

3. Check of the International Society for Krisna Consciousness, Inc., 2805 S.E. Hawthorne, Portland, Oregon, U.S.A., whose checking account is with First National Bank of Oregon, 26th and E. Burnside Branch, Portland, Oregon, dated Sept. 25, 1975, Check No. 1409 (24-191/1230), for the amount of U.S. Dollars 200.00 (Two hundred U.S. dollars).

You please credit the above amount (totaling U.S. Dollars 841.29), immediately as usual on receipt of this letter at the current rate of exchange and send the duplicate of this letter duly signed by you to my Bombay address as follows:

Hare Krishna Land Gandhi Gram Road, Juhu, Bombay 400-054, India.

Thanking you,

Yours sincerely, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

Encs:
