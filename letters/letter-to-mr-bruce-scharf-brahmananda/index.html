Letter to: Mr. Bruce Scharf, Brahmananda

November 22nd 1967 · Calcutta · To: Mr. Bruce Scharf, Brahmananda

67-11-22

Mr. Bruce Scharf c/o Iskcon Inc. 26 Second avenue New York 10003 NY

Dear Mr. Scharf,

I hereby authorize you to sign the contract with Messrs. MacMillan and Co. in the matter of publishing my Gitopanisad. You are also authorized to receive payment on my behalf.

Yours sincerely, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
