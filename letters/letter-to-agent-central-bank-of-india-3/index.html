Letter to: Agent, Central Bank of India

January 26th 1976 · To: Agent, Central Bank of India

The Agent, Central Bank of India, Camac Street Branch, 10, Camac Street Calcutta 700017

P.O. Sreedham Mayapur Dist. Nadia, West Bengal

Dear Mr. Dutta, Please accept my greetings. When I first met you you suggested that there are some newly built houses in Akhra. If there is a 4-Bedroom house or at least a 3-Bedroom house, please kindly send me the details and papers.

Hoping this meets you well.

Yours faithfully, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami
