Letter to: Karandhara

August 17th 1973 · Bhaktivedanta Manor · To: Karandhara

73-08-17

Los Angeles

My Dear Karandhara,

Please accept my blessings. I beg to acknowledge receipt of your letter date August 11, 1973 and the duplicate deposit slips.

I herewith send you three more checks;

1,111.00

300.00

108.00

1,519.00 US DOLLARS all of them may be deposited in my investment account No. 8-80613 Bank of America 366.

I hope this letter meets you well.

Your ever well wisher, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

ACBS:hda N.B. Syamasundara Prabhu is still unable to pay because the things are not sold yet. Let us wait & see.

In the investment a/c they do not receive less than $100.00 at a time.
