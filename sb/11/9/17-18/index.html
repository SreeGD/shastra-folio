Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.9.17-18

कालेनात्मानुभावेन साम्यं नीतासु शक्तिषु । सत्त्वादिष्वादिपुरुष: प्रधानपुरुषेश्वर: ॥ १७ ॥ परावराणां परम आस्ते कैवल्यसंज्ञित: । केवलानुभवानन्दसन्दोहो निरुपाधिक: ॥ १८ ॥

When the Supreme Personality of Godhead displays His own potency in the form of time and guides His material potencies, such as the mode of goodness, into a neutral condition of equilibrium, He remains as the supreme controller of that neutral state, called pradhāna, as well as of the living entities. He is also the supreme worshipable object for all beings, including liberated souls, demigods and ordinary conditioned souls. The Lord is eternally free from any material designation, and He constitutes the totality of spiritual bliss, which one experiences by seeing the Lord’s spiritual form. The Lord thus exhibits the fullest meaning of the word “liberation.”
Purport

One who fixes his mind in the Absolute Truth, the Personality of Godhead, gets immediate relief from the waves of material anxiety because the Lord’s transcendental form is completely free of any material contamination or designation. Less intelligent persons accept the illogical doctrine that the Lord is transformed into His creation and maintains no separate, individual existence. They falsely imagine that they can merge their individuality into the universal oneness and become exactly equal to the Supreme Personality of Godhead. However, in the opinion of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam the Personality of Godhead is not impersonal but is instead full of all transcendental qualities. The three modes of material nature constitute His inferior energy, and the omnipotent time factor, upon which the modes rest, is the personal expansion of the Lord. Thus, the Lord creates, maintains and annihilates the material manifestation and yet remains completely apart from it. The conditioned souls who desire to exploit the Lord’s inferior creation are impelled by the Personality of Godhead to do so, and thus they become imitation enjoyers in the temporary world of matter. But when one gains practical experience that the gross and subtle material bodies are simply coverings of the eternal soul, one gives up the foolishness of material attachment and becomes attached to the Supreme Personality of Godhead. He realizes that his constitutional position is neither to enjoy matter nor merge into the Lord’s existence. His real nature is that he is a servant of God. Service rendered to the Lord is eternal, full of bliss and knowledge, and by the potency of such service one becomes liberated and his activities become glorious. Such service is eternal and gradually promotes one to the platform of kevalānubhavānanda-sandoha, or merging into the ocean of bliss by seeing the transcendental personal form of the Lord.

— A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
When he brought his energies such as sattva to equilibrium by his powerful energy of time, the Lord, who enjoys in himself, who is the controller of prakṛti and the jīvas, who is worshipped by liberated and conditioned jīvas, remained in the form of complete bliss, without māyā. When his energies like sattva were brought to equilibrium by time, a form of the Lord’s power (ātmā-anubhāvena), the controller of māyā (pradhāna) and the jīvas (puruṣa), the most worthy object of worship for liberated and conditioned jīvas, the Lord remained. He is called kaivalya. He remains only with his form of bliss, since there were no actions such as protecting the universe. He is without māyā (nirupādhikaḥ) which was sleeping at that time. sa vā eṣa tadā draṣṭā nāpaśyad dṛśyam ekarāṭ mene ’santam ivātmānaṁ supta-śaktir asupta-dṛk The one puruṣa, who glances over prakṛti, at the beginning of creation, could not see her. He, possessor of māyā, who was sleeping, and who is the possessor of spiritual consorts who were awake, considered himself non-existent without her. SB 3.5.24

Personal Guidance
Kṛṣṇa is the personal Lord behind time, the modes, and even pradhāna — liberation is not merging into oneness but tasting His form as the totality of bliss.
Practical Applications
  • Today, refuse the inner pull to call the Absolute 'energy' or 'consciousness' — name Kṛṣṇa as a Person whenever you remember Him.
  • When anxiety surges, fix your mind on Kṛṣṇa's transcendental form for one full minute and notice the wave settle, as the purport promises.
  • Catch one moment when you act as an 'imitation enjoyer' of matter and re-route that energy into a small, deliberate act of service to Him.
Reflection Questions
  • Where am I still hoping to merge, dissolve, or disappear — and what does this verse ask me to want instead?
  • If time itself is Kṛṣṇa's personal potency, which event in my day am I treating as random that is actually His arrangement?
For Each Age Group
youth
  • When a professor or friend frames spirituality as 'energy' or 'the universe,' don't argue — just privately substitute Kṛṣṇa's name and notice how the inner texture changes.
  • Before an exam or interview, spend thirty seconds remembering that time itself is His potency, then walk in without bargaining with outcomes.
  • Audit one app or feed today where you go to feel like an enjoyer, and trade ten minutes of it for hearing about Kṛṣṇa's form.
householder
  • When a family decision feels out of your hands — a job loss, a child's choice, a parent's illness — name it as His time-potency at work and act from service rather than control.
  • In conversations with your spouse about money or the future, refuse the role of sole controller; offer the day's work and its results to Kṛṣṇa each night before sleeping.
  • Teach your children, by one concrete sentence at dinner, that God is a Person they can address — not a vague force — so they inherit the personalist line from you.
senior
  • Sit with the bodies you have already passed through and let this verse settle you: the witness of all of them is Kṛṣṇa's eternal servant, not a spark about to dissolve.
  • Loosen one attachment this week — an object, a grievance, a plan for the family — by formally handing it to Kṛṣṇa as the supreme worshipable object.
  • Mentor a younger seeker by correcting, gently, one piece of impersonalist language they use, so the personalist understanding outlives you.
Common Pitfalls
  • Hearing kaivalya and importing the impersonalist meaning — picturing liberation as featureless oneness rather than seeing His form.
  • Treating pradhāna, the modes, and time as autonomous forces to be managed, instead of as His subordinate potencies.
  • Using 'free from material designation' to license spiritual vagueness — refusing personal relationship with Kṛṣṇa under the cover of being beyond form.
Discussion Prompts
  • The verse calls Kṛṣṇa the supreme worshipable object even for liberated souls — what does that do to the common idea that liberation is the goal?
  • Where in your own speech about God do you slip into impersonal language, and what would change if you corrected it for a week?
  • If the modes of nature are His inferior energy, how should that reshape the way you respond to your own moods of sattva, rajas, and tamas tomorrow morning?
Role Play

A classmate says, "I meditate to dissolve my ego into universal consciousness — that's the real liberation, not bowing to a personal deity." Using only this verse and its purport, respond as a fellow student, not as a debater — aim to make the personalist position attractive rather than to win.

Objections & Responses
  • Calling the Absolute a 'Person' sounds like projecting human limitation onto the infinite — surely the formless is higher than the formed.
    The verse explicitly says He is nirupādhika — free of every material designation — and yet kevalānubhavānanda-sandoha, the totality of bliss experienced by seeing His form. His personhood is not a limitation imported from matter; it is what remains when every material covering is removed.
  • If Kṛṣṇa controls pradhāna, the modes, and time itself, then my choices are scripted and moral effort is theatre.
    The purport says conditioned souls who desire to exploit matter are impelled to do so — the desire is ours, the facility is His. The same Lord who supplies the rope for imitation enjoyment also accepts our service the moment we turn; that turning is the one real choice, and this verse is asking you to make it.
