Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.11.43-45

Me.
My dear Uddhava, one should worship Me within the sun by chanting selected Vedic mantras and by performing worship and offering obeisances. One may worship Me within fire by offering oblations of ghee, and one may worship Me among the brāhmaṇas by respectfully receiving them as guests, even when uninvited. I can be worshiped within the cows by offerings of grass and other suitable grains and paraphernalia for the pleasure and health of the cows, and one may worship Me within the Vaiṣṇavas by offering loving friendship to them and honoring them in all respects. Through steady meditation I am worshiped within the inner space of the heart, and within the air I can be worshiped by knowledge that prāṇa, the life air, is the chief among elements. I am worshiped within water by offerings of water itself, along with other elements such as flowers and tulasī leaves, and one may worship Me within the earth by proper application of confidential seed mantras. One may worship Me within the individual living entity by offering food and other enjoyable substances, and one may worship Me within all living entities by seeing the Supersoul within all of them, thus maintaining equal vision.
Purport

Significantly, the Lord emphasizes in these three verses that one should worship the Supreme Personality of Godhead who is expanded within all living beings. It is not recommended that one accept any material or spiritual object other than the Lord as supreme. By maintaining steady consciousness of the Lord in His all-pervading feature, one can remain in a worshipful mood twenty-four hours a day. Thus, one will naturally try to engage all material and spiritual elements in the loving service of Lord Kṛṣṇa. If because of ignorance one forgets the Supreme Personality of Godhead, one may become inclined to worship powerful material phenomena independent of the Supreme Lord, or one may foolishly consider oneself to be supreme. One should remain sane and accept the worshipable presence of the Supreme Lord within everything.

— A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
One worships me in the sun by reciting verses and performing specific Vedic rituals. One worships me in the fire by offering oblations. One worships me in the brāhmana by giving him proper reception. One worships me in the cow by feeding her grass and tickling her. “How does one worship the Lord in these places?” Three verses explain this. One should worship the sun by Vedic verses, approaching with respect (upasthāna), [Note: This is a ritual for worshipping the sun with mantras, performed during sandhyā rites. ] and offering respects. One worships the Lord in the cow by offering grass and tickling the cow.

Personal Guidance
Worship Kṛṣṇa where He stands — in sun, fire, brāhmaṇas, cows, Vaiṣṇavas, heart, air, water, earth, in each being and in all — with equal vision.
Practical Applications
  • Pick one element you meet today — sunlight on your face, the flame on your stove, a cup of water — and consciously offer it back to Kṛṣṇa before using it.
  • When a Vaiṣṇava or a brāhmaṇa enters your day, drop the transactional posture and receive them as guests, with friendship and honor, not utility.
  • Refuse to split your day into 'sacred' and 'secular' — practice seeing the Supersoul in every living entity you speak to between now and bedtime.
Reflection Questions
  • Which element or being have I been treating as a resource to consume, rather than a place where Kṛṣṇa is waiting to be worshiped?
  • Where does my 'equal vision' break down — which person or creature falls outside the circle in which I see the Supersoul?
For Each Age Group
youth
  • Before opening your phone in the morning, take one breath and remember Kṛṣṇa as the chief within that prāṇa — set the day's tone before the feed sets it for you.
  • In a group of friends or classmates today, pick the person you find easiest to dismiss and consciously look for the Supersoul in them before you speak.
  • When you drink water between classes or study sessions, pause for two seconds and offer it inwardly — a small, repeatable sādhana that fits a student's life.
householder
  • Welcome the next unexpected guest — a relative, a neighbor, a colleague — as a place where Kṛṣṇa is to be worshiped, not as an interruption to be managed.
  • Bring the kitchen fire into your sādhana — cook one meal today with the conscious mood of offering, and serve your family from that mood.
  • In a disagreement with your spouse or child this week, practice samatvena for sixty seconds — see the same Supersoul in them before you respond.
senior
  • Spend time with cows, trees, or running water this week if you can — let the elements teach you the worship the body is slowing down enough to receive.
  • Mentor a younger person not by giving them advice but by demonstrating equal vision — let them see how you treat the server, the driver, the stranger.
  • As attachments loosen, deepen the heart-dhyāna of this verse — Kṛṣṇa within the inner space of the heart is the location no aging body can take from you.
Common Pitfalls
  • Sliding into pantheism — worshiping the sun, fire, or water as supreme rather than worshiping Kṛṣṇa within them.
  • Using 'samatvena' as a flat egalitarianism that erases the special honor due to brāhmaṇas, Vaiṣṇavas, cows, and tulasī.
  • Picking the convenient locations (heart-meditation, equal vision) and quietly dropping the inconvenient ones (cows, fire offerings, hospitality to guests).
Discussion Prompts
  • Of the ten locations Kṛṣṇa names here, which one feels furthest from your daily life — and is that distance practical or excuse?
  • How do you distinguish worshiping Kṛṣṇa within the sun from sun-worship as practiced by other traditions? Where exactly is the line?
  • Equal vision toward all living entities — does this dissolve varṇāśrama distinctions, or operate alongside them? Argue your position.
Role Play

A guest arrives at your home unannounced during a busy workday — a traveling Vaiṣṇava you barely know, asking for a meal and a place to rest a few hours. Your spouse is irritated, you have a deadline, and the guest is awkward. Enact the next ten minutes in light of this verse — what do you actually do, and what do you say to your spouse afterward?

Objections & Responses
  • Seeing Kṛṣṇa in everything sounds indistinguishable from pantheism or vague nature-mysticism. Why call this distinctively Gauḍīya?
    Kṛṣṇa is not the sun, the fire, the water — He is worshiped within them as the Supreme Personality who pervades them. The personalist line is preserved: surrender is to Him, not to the elements. The elements become tīrthas of worship, not deities in themselves.
  • Offering grass to cows and ghee to fire is a pre-modern ritual world. What does any of this mean for someone in an apartment with no fire, no cow, and no brāhmaṇa nearby?
    The verse names ten locations, not one. Where the outer form is unavailable, the inner ones remain — the heart through dhyāna, the prāṇa within the air you are breathing right now, water you drink, earth you stand on, and every living being you meet. Begin with the access you have and let the rest grow.
