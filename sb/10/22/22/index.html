Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.22.22

द‍ृढं प्रलब्धास्त्रपया च हापिता: प्रस्तोभिता: क्रीडनवच्च कारिता: । वस्त्राणि चैवापहृतान्यथाप्यमुं ता नाभ्यसूयन् प्रियसङ्गनिर्वृता: ॥ २२ ॥
dṛḍhaṁ pralabdhās trapayā ca hāpitāḥ
[dridham pralabdhaas trapayaa cha haapitaah]

[taa naabhyasooyan priya-sanga-nirvritaah]

Important Words
  • pralabdhāḥ — cheated
    Past participle of pra-√labh (to deceive, dupe). The poet uses the strongest available word — they were swindled. And yet they bear no resentment. The verse measures the depth of their bhāva by the strength of the wrong they have absorbed without anger.
  • trapayā — of their shame
    Instrumental of trapā (modesty). Their modesty was the one possession bigger than their clothes; it too has been taken. And still — na abhyasūyan. Prema is greater than even śīla.
  • krīḍana-vat — just like toy dolls
    Compound: krīḍana (toy) + vat (like). They acknowledge being handled like puppets in His līlā. Gauḍīya tattva: to be a krīḍana in Kṛṣṇa's hand is the highest position — every jīva is meant to be a willing instrument of His pleasure.
  • na abhyasūyan — did not feel inimical
    Negation + imperfect of abhi-√asūy (to envy, resent). The decisive verb. After being cheated, exposed, made puppets — no resentment. This is the verse Caitanya Mahāprabhu's prayer cited above formalizes.
  • priya-saṅga-nirvṛtāḥ — joyful by the association of their beloved
    Compound: priya (beloved) + saṅga (association) + nirvṛti (deep joy). The technical Gauḍīya phrase for the rasa of being near Kṛṣṇa. Nirvṛti — settled, fully-arrived joy — is the saturated state of prema; everything else is forgotten.
Although the gopīs had been thoroughly cheated, deprived of their modesty, ridiculed and made to act just like toy dolls, and although their clothing had been stolen, they did not feel at all inimical toward Śrī Kṛṣṇa. Rather, they were simply joyful to have this opportunity to associate with their beloved.
Purport

Śrīla Prabhupāda comments, “This attitude of the gopīs is described by Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu when He prays, ‘My dear Lord Kṛṣṇa, You may embrace Me or trample Me under Your feet, or You may make Me brokenhearted by never being present before Me. Whatever You like, You can do, because You have complete freedom to act. But in spite of all of Your dealings, You are My Lord eternally, and I have no other worshipable object.’ This is the attitude of the gopīs toward Kṛṣṇa.”

— A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
Although the gopīs had been thoroughly cheated, deprived of their modesty, ridiculed and made to act just like toy dolls, and although their clothing had been stolen, they did not feel at all inimical toward Śrī Kṛṣṇa. Rather, they were simply joyful to have this opportunity to associate with their beloved.
Commentary

Though they criticized him, they had done as he said, which was impossible for any others. More astonishing however, is that they became blissful. They had been deceived to the extreme by his words criticizing their nakedness. They had been made to give up their modesty. He mocked them, insisting that they come and get their clothing. They were made to dance like puppets when he said, "this is true, it is not a joke." They did not find fault in him even when he told them to put their hands about their heads as prayascitta. Though all this was done by their beloved (priya) they became blissful because they could meet him. Because he was their beloved even actions causing suffering became causes of joy for them. They became more attached to him for doing that.

Jīva Gosvāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

How he spoke to them is described. He treated them like dolls. They became blissful by his association. Or, rather, they became blissful even in his association. Ah! See the glory of deep prema!

Śrīdhara Svāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

O king! See the greatness of their prema. Or speaking of Kṛṣṇa’s ability to bewilder, he speaks to the girls as if with some hatred. He made them act like puppets (krīḍana-vat) because they were happy to associate with him. Or they became happy along with him.

Personal Guidance
Analogy

A child who has been teased, embarrassed, and stripped by an elder beloved brother — and who, finding herself somehow in his lap at the end of it, can feel only joy that she was the one chosen for the teasing. Priya-saṅga-nirvṛtāḥ: when prema fills the heart, even loss reads as the cost of having been near.

Stories
Caitanya's verse of total surrender

Prabhupāda's purport to this verse quotes Caitanya Mahāprabhu's own line: 'My dear Lord Kṛṣṇa, You may embrace Me or trample Me under Your feet, or You may make Me brokenhearted by never being present before Me. Whatever You like, You can do, because You have complete freedom to act. But in spite of all of Your dealings, You are My Lord eternally, and I have no other worshipable object.'

Gauḍīyas mark this as the doctrinal high-water of the chapter. The gopīs' non-resentment is not stoicism; it is the same śikṣāṣṭakam mood Mahāprabhu would model five thousand years later. Surrender that does not depend on outcome is prema.
