Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.85.54

अपाययत् स्तनं प्रीता सुतस्पर्शपरिस्‍नुतम् । मोहिता मायया विष्णोर्यया सृष्टि: प्रवर्तते ॥ ५४ ॥

Important Words
  • parisnutam — completely wet (flowing around)
    Past participle of pari + √snu, 'to flow all around'. The same root snu- as in verse 53. The Bhāgavatam doubles down on the somatic detail — repetition is not redundancy but emphasis. The breast does not merely drip; it is parisnuta — flooded.
  • mohitā māyayā viṣṇoḥ — bewildered by the māyā of Viṣṇu
    Phrase: mohitā (bewildered) + māyā + viṣṇu. The technical surprise: Devakī's vātsalya is itself a working of yoga-māyā. Gauḍīyas read this as proof that the highest love-states are not 'jñāna-clear' — they are deliberately rasa-veiled.
  • suta-sparśa — by the touch of her sons
    Compound: suta (son) + sparśa (touch). The milk flows in response to physical contact. The same biology that animates Yaśodā's overflow. Bhakti is somatic.
  • sṛṣṭiḥ pravartate — creation proceeds
    Phrase: sṛṣṭi (creation) + pra + √vṛt (proceeds). The same māyā that bewilders Devakī is the māyā by which the universe arises. The Bhāgavatam quietly links the personal moment to the cosmic process.
Lovingly she let her sons drink from her breast, which became wet with milk just by their touch. She was entranced by the same illusory energy of Lord Viṣṇu that initiates the creation of the universe.
Purport

In the opinion of Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī, the word sṛṣṭi can here also refer to the creative process by which Lord Viṣṇu’s Yoga-māyā arranges the settings and situations of His pastimes. There is indeed no question of Mother Devakī being affected by the material aspect of Māyā.

— A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
Lovingly she let her sons drink from her breast, which became wet with milk just by their touch. She was entranced by the same illusory energy of Lord Viṣṇu that initiates the creation of the universe.
Commentary

Being bewildered by yogamaya of visnu (mayaya) by which the spiritual creation with the lord’s pastimes and associates takes place (not the maya by which the creation of Brahma takes place), she fed them her breast milk.

Jīva Gosvāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

Her breasts were completely flowing with milk which had been touched by Kṛṣṇa and other sons. Another version has pariplūtam. This means her breasts were covered with the affection of her sons. The cause is explained. She was bewildered by yoga-māyā-śakti, for his pastimes. The creation pastime is mentioned in this regard but the statement using ordinary words is only for easily understanding of common people.

Śrīdhara Svāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

Her breast was completely soaked with milk touched by her son Kṛṣṇa, or her sons. She was attracted in her heart (mohitāḥ) by māyā in the form of affection for her sons. She could not avoid that māyā since it belonged to the Lord who pervades the universe (viṣṇoḥ). “Why would the Lord put his mother into illusion?” Without that māyā, the universe would not be created. Or by that mercy she will again become his mother in the next birth.

Concepts
Yoga
Personal Guidance
Devakī nurses her returned sons, entranced. Some mercies are so unexpected they feel like dream; receiving them fully is itself a discipline.
Reflection Questions
  • What unexpected mercy have I had that I struggled to actually receive?
  • Where do I push grace away by disbelief?
Analogy

A mother who knows philosophically that her son is the Lord still gives him her breast — and even is herself sweetly bewildered, because the bewilderment IS the love. Devakī is 'mohitā māyayā viṣṇoḥ' — not as a defect, but as the proper rasa-state of a vātsalya-mother.
