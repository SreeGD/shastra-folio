Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.84.20

तस्माद् ब्रह्मकुलं ब्रह्मन् शास्‍त्रयोनेस्त्वमात्मन: । सभाजयसि सद्धाम तद् ब्रह्मण्याग्रणीर्भवान् ॥ २० ॥

Important Words
  • brahmaṇya — of those who respect brahminical culture
    From brāhmaṇa + -ya (relating to). Kṛṣṇa is brahmaṇya-deva — the God who honors brāhmaṇas (SB 10.84.20, Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma names Him brahmaṇyaḥ). Gauḍīyas insist this title names Him before any of the sovereign epithets — He is first the friend of brāhmaṇas, then king.
  • agranīḥ — the foremost, leader
    agra (front) + nī (leading). Kṛṣṇa is the agranīḥ — the leader — of brāhmaṇa-respecters. Gauḍīyas note that the model He sets becomes the binding standard: a Vaiṣṇava cannot ignore brāhmaṇa-pūjā if Bhagavān Himself enacts it.
  • śāstra-yoneḥ — whose means of realization is śāstra
    śāstra + yoni (source, womb). The brāhmaṇas are the womb through which śāstra becomes realizable. Gauḍīyas read this as the rationale for guru-paramparā: the text alone does not transmit; the brāhmaṇa-vaiṣṇava transmits.
  • sat-dhāma — the perfect abode
    sat (true, existent) + dhāma (abode). The brāhmaṇa-kula is called the abode of sat. Gauḍīyas distinguish this from the brāhmaṇa-by-birth: the verse intends those whose conduct manifests sat — that is, the Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇa.
Therefore, O Supreme Brahman, You honor the members of the brahminical community, for they are the perfect agents by which one can realize You through the evidence of the Vedas. For that very reason You are the foremost worshiper of the brāhmaṇas.
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
Therefore, O Supreme Brahman, You honor the members of the brahminical community, for they are the perfect agents by which one can realize You through the evidence of the Vedas. For that very reason You are the foremost worshiper of the brāhmaṇas.
Commentary

You worship the brahmanas who are the best source of realization (sad dhama) of yourself (atmanah) who is proven by the Veda (sastra yoneh).

Jīva Gosvāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

“If I am like that, why do I worship you sages?” You honor the brāhmaṇas to teach the people. In the manner that they started the discussion with this concept, they finish with the same statement.

Śrīdhara Svāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

You worship the brāhmaṇas, for they are your (ātmanaḥ) excellent (sat) abode (dhāma), since you are the promoter of the Vedas (śāstra-yoneḥ). O direct form of the Vedas (brahman)! The brāhmaṇas are the abode of the Lord because they possess or promote the Vedas. Thus it is proper that the Lord worships them.

Concepts
Jiva
Personal Guidance
Kṛṣṇa honors brāhmaṇas because they hold the purest wisdom of self. The Lord's pattern is to dignify those who carry sacred knowledge — devotion includes honoring living knowledge-bearers.
Reflection Questions
  • Whom in my life carries sacred knowledge that I have honored too casually?
  • How might I become more receptive to learning from those who know more than I do?
Analogy

Kṛṣṇa as brahmaṇya-deva is the king who calls his prime minister 'my elder' in public, not because the minister outranks him but because the office of brāhmaṇa is the channel by which the kingdom recognizes its sovereign. Bhagavān honors the brāhmaṇa because that is how He chooses to be honored.

Stories
Kṛṣṇa serving Sudāmā

Again the Sudāmā episode (SB 10.80–81): the Lord, who owns all opulences, washes the feet of a brāhmaṇa friend whose only wealth is his loyalty to vidyā and his memory of guru-kula.

Gauḍīyas hold that scene as the lived form of this verse — brahmaṇya-deva in action, not merely in epithet.

Caitanya at Advaita Ācārya's house

Mahāprabhu, the same Bhagavān incarnate in Gauḍa-deśa, repeatedly took the lower position in Advaita Ācārya's house. Caitanya-caritāmṛta records Advaita scolding Him for it; He kept doing it.

Gauḍīyas read this as proof that brahmaṇya behavior is not period-specific — Bhagavān honors the brāhmaṇa-Vaiṣṇava in every age.
