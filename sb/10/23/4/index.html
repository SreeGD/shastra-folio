Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.23.4

तत्र गत्वौदनं गोपा याचतास्मद्विसर्जिता: । कीर्तयन्तो भगवत आर्यस्य मम चाभिधाम् ॥ ४ ॥

Important Words
  • yācata — just request
    Imperative of √yāc. Bhagavān — the source of all opulence — has His friends beg. This is the first hint of verse 47's wonder: Lakṣmī's husband puts out His hand. Gauḍīyas treasure this as a sign of His vraja-bhāva — He plays the role of the dependent.
  • kīrtayantaḥ — announcing
    Present participle of √kīrt. The boys are told to do kīrtana of Kṛṣṇa-and-Balarāma's names as their request. Even hunger-relief is to be obtained through nāma-kīrtana — Mahāprabhu's later teaching is prefigured here.
  • bhagavataḥ — of the Supreme Lord (Balarāma)
    Genitive. Balarāma receives bhagavān-status in the same breath as Kṛṣṇa — Gauḍīya tattva of the prakāśa-mūrti, the immediate self-expansion of Svayam Bhagavān.
  • āryasya — of the elder
    From √ṛ; āryasya — 'of the noble elder.' Kṛṣṇa, even as Svayam Bhagavān, names His brother first. The mood of the cowherd brothers governs the syntax.
  • abhidhām — name
    From abhi + √dhā, 'to name.' Specifically the divine name, not a label. The boys carry nāma as their currency — a Gauḍīya emblem.
When you go there, My dear cowherd boys, simply request some food. Declare to them the name of My elder brother, the Supreme Lord Balarāma, and also My name, and explain that you have been sent by Us.
Purport

Lord Kṛṣṇa encouraged His boyfriends to request charity without being embarrassed. In case the boys felt they had no right to personally approach such respectable brāhmaṇas, the Lord told them to mention the names of Balarāma and Kṛṣṇa, the holy names of God.

— A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
When you go there, My dear cowherd boys, simply request some food. Declare to them the name of My elder brother, the Supreme Lord Balarāma, and also My name, and explain that you have been sent by Us.
Commentary

You should ask for rice (yacata), calling out our names. Though they are supposed to be intelligent, they must be woken from deep slumber by calling out our names, calling out Baladeva’s name first. He is more elevated than I, since he is of ksatriya birth. The materialists will give to him out of respect. Even if they give a little, it is acceptable.

Jīva Gosvāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

If you are feeling a little shy about this, say that you have been sent by us, not your parents. You should glorify the name of my brother who has great power, who is related to me.

Śrīdhara Svāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

“As devotees we should not beg for food.” You were sent by us two. Thus you have no fault. “But the followers also are at fault.” By doing kīrtana of our names, the fault will be destroyed. Why? The Lord is full of all powers (bhagavataḥ). He taught a method of begging while chanting the Lord’s names.

Personal Guidance
'Request food. Mention My name and Balarāma's, and say We sent you.' The Lord teaches the boys to use His name as a credential. Mentioning Him is sufficient — they need not earn the request on their own merit.
Reflection Questions
  • Where am I trying to earn merit when 'sent by Kṛṣṇa' is the credential needed?
  • What request have I been making in my name when His name would carry it?
Analogy

A prince sending messengers with his royal seal expects every door to open. Kṛṣṇa hands the boys His name and Balarāma's as a passkey — but the door this key truly opens is not the brāhmaṇas' hall; it is the inner chamber of the wives.
