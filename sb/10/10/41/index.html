Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.10.41

साधूनां समचित्तानां सुतरां मत्कृतात्मनाम् । दर्शनान्नो भवेद् बन्ध: पुंसोऽक्ष्णो: सवितुर्यथा ॥ ४१ ॥

When one is face to face with the sun, there is no longer darkness for one’s eyes. Similarly, when one is face to face with a sādhu, a devotee, who is fully determined and surrendered to the Supreme Personality of Godhead, one will no longer be subject to material bondage.
Purport

As stated by Caitanya Mahāprabhu (Cc. Madhya 22.54): ‘sādhu-saṅga,’ ‘sādhu-saṅga’ — sarva-śāstre kaya lava-mātra sādhu-saṅge sarva-siddhi haya If by chance one meets a sādhu, a devotee, one’s life is immediately successful, and one is freed from material bondage. It may be argued that whereas someone may receive a sādhu with great respect, someone else may not receive a sādhu with such respect. A sādhu, however, is always equipoised toward everyone. Because of being a pure devotee, a sādhu is always ready to deliver Kṛṣṇa consciousness without discrimination. As soon as one sees a sādhu, one naturally becomes free. Nonetheless, persons who are too much offensive, who commit vaiṣṇava-aparādhas, or offenses to a sādhu, will have to take some time before being rectified. This is also indicated herein.

— A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
When one is face to face with the sun, there is no longer darkness for one's eyes. Similarly, when one is face to face with a sādhu, a devotee, who is fully determined and surrendered to the Supreme Personality of Godhead, one will no longer be subject to material bondage.
Commentary

Since, on seeing Narada, you disrespected him, how is it possible that he could give mercy to you? This verse answers the question. From seeing those whose minds are not disturbed by respect or disrespect ( sama cittanam) who have offered their minds completely to me (sutaram matkrt atmanam), one is released from bondage. Instead of darsanat na there is another reading darsan antah. In that case the meaning would be just until one takes darsana of the devotee, bondage will remain. Just as the sun destroys darkness just by its rising, , so just up to seeing the devotee, bondage remains, by seeing him it is destroyed. It should be understood from this example however that though the sun rises, if a person is blind, the darkness is not dissipated. In the same way, if there is nama aparadha, as in the case of the asuras, even seeing Narada will not release them from bondage.

Jīva Gosvāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

Taking the meaning of the first line from Śrīdhara Svāmī, by seeing those who follow proper conduct (sādhūnām) or who realize ātmā (sama-cittānām), bondage is somewhat reduced. More than this (sutarām), by seeing those who are constantly thinking of me, bondage is completely removed, since they have the greatest mercy. Or, the devotees are most merciful (sādhūnām) and they do not take offense seriously (sama-cittānām). Because they are constantly devoted to me (mat-kṛtātmanām), the other two qualities naturally follow. Seeing them, no human being has bondage to saṁsāra. What to speak of the devotees talking to them. And what to speak of you two being great offenders. Sutarām indicates that this effect does not take place by hearing and chanting to the extent that it does by seeing devotees. The eyes are relieved of darkness to a high degree from (sutarām) on seeing the sun. This indicates that even previous to seeing the sun, darkness is removed.

Śrīdhara Svāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

Just by seeing devotees, any person becomes free of material bondage. So what is the need of you two asking for mercy by uttering so many words? Any human (puṁsaḥ) is qualified to see the devotee. Though before seeing the sun, darkness is destroyed and the eyes can see to some degree, by seeing devotees, saṁsāra is completely destroyed.

Concepts
Chit
Personal Guidance
Stand before a sādhu fixed in Kṛṣṇa as you would face the sun — the darkness of material bondage simply cannot remain.
Practical Applications
  • Seek out one sādhu-saṅga today — in person, through Prabhupāda's recorded class, or by reading a pure devotee's words — and approach without reservation.
  • Refuse the calculation of whether the sādhu 'likes you back'; he is sama-citta, so receive his mercy without demanding personal recognition.
  • Notice and immediately correct any tendency to criticize or measure a devotee, guarding strictly against vaiṣṇava-aparādha that would delay your freedom.
