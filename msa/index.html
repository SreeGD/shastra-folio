Madhusūdana Sarasvatī

Advaita Vedāntin · Krishna-bhakta · 16th century

Madhusūdana Sarasvatī (16th c.) — one of the greatest Advaita Vedānta thinkers after Śaṅkara, and an ardent devotee of Kṛṣṇa. His Bhakti-rasāyana attempts to merge non-dualist metaphysics with the ecstatic devotion of the Bhāgavata Purāṇa.
