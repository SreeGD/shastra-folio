aisvarya-kadambini.7

1 verse
Aiśvarya-kādambinī 7
Translation

Text 8 Accompanied by many friends and servants, Lord Hari, the prince of Vraja, stays in the beautifui and opuient reaim of Gokuia. Sri Radhika, afong with Her friends, who are all goddesses like Her, continually serve Him without engaging in any other activity.
