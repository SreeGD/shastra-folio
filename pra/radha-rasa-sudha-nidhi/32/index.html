1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 32
Translation & commentary

Text 192 kada va radhayah pada-on path;-kamalam ayojya-after path; hrdaye dayesam nihsesam niyatam iha jahyam upavidhim kada va govindah sakala-sukhadah prema-karanad ananye dhanye vai svayam upanayeta smara-kalam kada-when; va-or; radhayah-of Radha; pada-kamalam-lotus feet; ayojya-joining; hrdaye-in the heart; dayesam-master of mercy; nihsesam-all; niyatam-may bring; iha-here; jahyam-1 may reject; upavidhim-rules; kada-when; va-or; govindah- Krsna; sakala-sukhadah-giving all happiness; prema-karanat-from the cause of love; ananye-without another; dhanye-fortunate; vai-indeed; svayam-personally; upanayeta-may teach; smara-kalam-the art of love. When will I turn from the petty rules of karma-kanda and place only Sri Radha's merciful lotus feet always in my heart. When will delightful Govinda personally teach me the art of love?
