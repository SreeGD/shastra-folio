radha-rasa-sudha-nidhi.91

1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 91
Translation

Text 251 aho dvaidhi-kartum krtibhir anuragamrta-rasapravahaih su-snigdhaih kutila-rucirah syama ucitah itiyarh srmante nava-rucira-sindura-racita su-rekha nah prakhyapayitum iva radhe vijayate aho-Oh; dvaidhr-kartum-to make two; krtibhih-by deeds; anuraga-of love; amrta-rasa-of nectar; pravahaih-with currents; su-snigdhaih-graceful; kutilarucirah-charmingly curled; syama-balck; ucitah-appropriate; iti-thus; iyarn-this; srmante-on the border; nava-rucira-sindura-racita-made with splendid red sidura; su-rekha-nice line; nah-of us; prakhyapayitum-to describe; iva-like; radhe-O Radha; vijayate-all glories. O Radha, all glories to the sindura-anointed part in Your hair, which seems to say to us, "Because of rue the nectar stream of Radha's glistening curly hair is divided into two parts, just like charming, dark, crooked, two-faced Krsna."
