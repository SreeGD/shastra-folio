radha-rasa-sudha-nidhi.56

1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 56
Translation

Text 216 bhuyo bhuyah kamala-nayane kiiir mudha varyate 'sau van-matre 'pi tvad-anugamanam na tyajyaty eva dhurtah kincid radhe kuru kuca-tati-prantam asya mradiyas caksur-dvara tarn anupatitaiir curnatam etu cetah bhuyo bhuyah-again and agaion; kamala-nayane-lotus eyes; kim-whether?; mudha-uselessly; varyate-is covered; 'sau-thsi; vak-words; matre-only; 'pi-even; tvad-anugamanam-following You; na-not; tyajyati-abandons; eva-iindeed; dhurtahrascal; kincit-something; radhe-O Radha; kuru-do; kuca-tati-prantam-on the edge of Your breasts; asya-of Her; mradiyah-gentle; caksur-dvara-by the eyes; tam-that; anupatitam-fallen; curnatam-to powder; etu-may go; cetah -heart. "O lotus-eyed one, why do you try again and again, and always without success, to stop Him? Mere words will not stop that rascal from following You wherever You go. O Radha, do this much: Arrange so that His eyes will see the edge of Your breast. Then His mind will at once be crushed into a powder."
