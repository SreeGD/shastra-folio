1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 38
Translation

Text 198 asasya dasyam vrsabhanu-jayas tire samadhyasya ca bhanujayah kada nu vrndavana-kunja-vlthisv aham nu radhe hy athitir bhaveyam asa-hope; asya-of hiom; dasyam-service; vrsabhanu-jayah-of Radha; tire-on the shore; samadhyasya-arranging; ca-and; bhanujayah-of the Yamuna; kada-when; nu-indeed; vrndavana-kunja-vithisv-on the patheays of Vrndavana forest; aham-I; nu-indeed; radhe-O Radha; hi-indeed; athitih-a guest; bhaveyam-may become. O Radha, when, yearning to become Your maidservant, will I become Your guest by the Yamuna's shore in Vrndavana forest?
