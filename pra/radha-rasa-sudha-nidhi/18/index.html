1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 18
Translation

Text 18 vaidagdhya-sindhur anur aga-r asaika- sindhur vatsalya-sindhur ati-sandra-krpaika-sindhuh lavanya-sindhur amrta-cchavi-rupa-sindhuh sri-radhika sphurati me hrdi keli-sindhuh vaidagdhya-of expert intelligence; sindhuh-ocean; anuraga-love; rasaika-tasting the nectar; sindhuh-ocean; vatsalya-of affection; sindhuh-ocean; ati-sandra-very intense; krpa-mercy; eka-only; sindhuh-ocean; lavanya-of beauty; sindhuh-the ocean; amrta-nectar; cchavi-splendor; rupa-form; sindhuh-the ocean; sri-radhika- Srl Radha; sphurati-may appear; me-before me; hrdi-in the heart; keli-of pastimes; sindhuh-an ocean. May Sri Radha, who is an ocean of intelligence, a nectar ocean of love, an ocean of kind affection, an ocean of great mercy, an ocean of beauty, an ocean of nectar splendor, and an ocean of transcendental pastimes, appear in my heart.
