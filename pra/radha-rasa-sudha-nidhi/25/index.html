Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 25
Translation

Text 25 sat-prema-rasi-saraso vikasat-sarojam svananda-sidhu-rasa-sindhu-vivardhanendum tac chrl-mukham kutila-kuntala-bhrnga-justam sn-radhike tava kada nu vilokayisye sat-prema-rasi-sarasah-of the lake of spiritual love; vikasat-sarojam-blossoming lotus; svananda-sldhu-rasa-the nectar of bliss; sindhu-the ocean; vivardhanaincreasing; indum-the moon; tat-that; sn-mukham-beautiful face; kutila-curly; kuntala-hairs; bhrnga-black bees; justam-endowed; sri-radhike-O Sri Radha; tavaof You; kada-when?; nu-indeed; vilokayisye-I will see. O Sri Radha, when will 1 gaze on Your beautiful face, which is a blossoming lotus in the lake of pure love, a lotus decorated Swith the black bees of Your curling locks of hair, a face that is a moon bringing great tidal waves to the nectar ocean of bliss?
