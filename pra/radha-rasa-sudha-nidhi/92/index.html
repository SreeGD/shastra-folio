radha-rasa-sudha-nidhi.92

1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 92
Translation

Text 252 cakoras te vaktrarnrta-kirana-birnbe ruadhukaras tava srr-padabje jaghana-puline khanjana-varah sphuran-mrno jatas tvayi rasa-sarasyaru madhu-pateh sukhatavyam radhe tvayi ca harinas tasya nayanaru cakorah-cakora; te-of You; vaktra-face; arurta-nectar; kirana-birube-light; ruadhukarah-bee; tava-ofYou; srr-padabje-beautiful lotus feet; jaghana-puline-on the hips; khanjana-varah-khanjana bird; sphuran-rurnah-glistening fish; jatahborn; tvayi-in You; rasa-sarasyaru-lake of nectar; madhu-pateh-of Krsna; sukhatavyarn-in the forest of happiness; radhe-O Radha; tvayi-in You; ca-and; harinah-deer; tasya-of Him; nayanarn-the eyes. O Radha, Krsna's eyes are cakora birds drinking the nectar moonlight of Your face, bees attracted to the lotus hower of Your beauty, khanjana birds playing on the riverbank of Your hips, glistening fish swimming in the lake of Your sweetness, and two dear playing in the forest of Your happiness.
