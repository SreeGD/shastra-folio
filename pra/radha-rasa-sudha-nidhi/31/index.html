1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 31
Translation

Text 191 kirn re dhurta pravara nikatam yasi nah prana-sakhya nunarh bala-kuca-tata-kara-sparsa-matrad vimuhyet ittham radhe pathi pathi rasan nagaram te 'nulagnam ksiptva bhangya hrdayam ubhayoh karhi sammohayisye kim-whether?; re-O; dhurta-rascal; pravara-best; nikatam-near; yasi-you go; nah-of us; prana-sakhya-of the dear friend; nunam-indeed; bala-kuca-tata-karasparsa-matrat-by touching her breast; vimuhyet-will faith; ittham-thus; radhe-O Radha; pathi pathi-on path after path; rasan-sweetly; nagaram-lover; te-of You; 'nufagnam-foffowing; ksiptva-castingaway; bharigya-with crookedness; hrdayamthe heart; ubhayoh-of both; karhi-when?; sammohayisye-1 will charm. "Rascal, why do You come near our dear friend? If You touch Her breast She will faint!" O Radha, when Your lover follows You wherever You go, I will chase Him away with these words. When will I delight both Your hearts with these words?
