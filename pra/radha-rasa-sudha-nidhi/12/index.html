Translation & commentary

Text 12 ujjrmbhamana-rasa-vari-nidhes tararigair angair iva pranaya-lola-vilocanayah tasyah kada nu bhavita mayi punya-drstir vmdatavi-nava-nikunja-grhadhidevyah ujjrmbhamana-manifesting; rasa-of nectar; vari-nidheh-ocean; tararigaih-with waves; angaih-limbs; iva-like; pranaya-with love; lola-restless; vilocanayah-eyes; tasyah-of Her; kada-when?; nu-indeed; bhavita-will be; mayi-to me; punya-sacred; drstih-glance; vrndatavl-nava-nikunja-grhadhidevyah-of the queen of the blossoming forest groves of Vrndavana. When will Vrndavana's queen Radha, Her eyes restless with love and Her limbs like waves in the flooding ocean of nectar, place Her merciful glance upon me?
