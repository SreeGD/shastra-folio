Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 22
Translation

Text 22 sat-prema-sindhu-makaranda-rasaugha-dharasaran ajasram abhitah sravad-asritesu sri-radhike tava kada caranaravindam govinda-jlvana-dhanam sirasa vahami sat-prema-sindhu-the ocean of love; makaranda-rasaugha-dhara-the flood of nectar; saran-flowing; ajasram-always; abhitah-everywhere; sravat-flowing; asritesu-taken shelter; sri-radhike-O Sri Radha; tava-of You; kada-when?; caranaravindam-lotus feet; govinda-jlvana-dhanam-the wealth of Lord Krsna's life; sirasa-with my head; vahami-I carry. O Sri Radha, when will I place on my head Your lotus feet, which are a flood of the nectar of spiritual love, a flood that always flows among Your devotees, and are the treasure more dear than life for Lord Krsna?
