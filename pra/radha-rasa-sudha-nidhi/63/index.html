radha-rasa-sudha-nidhi.63

1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 63
Translation

Text 223 patralim lalitam kapola-phalake netrambuje kajjalam rarigaiii bimba-phaladhare ca kucayoh kasmiraja-lepanam sri-radhe nava-sarigamaya tarale padariguli-pariktisu nyasyanti pranayad alaktaka-rasaiii purna kada syam aham patralim-pictures; lalitam-charming; kapola-phalake-on the cheeks; netrambuje-on the lotus eyes; kajjalam-mascara; rangam-red ointment; bimbaphaladhare-on the bimba fruit lips; ca-and; kucayoh-on the breasts; kasmirajalepanam-red kunkuma; sri-radhe-O Sri Radha; nava-sangamaya-new meeting; tarale-trembling; padariguli-pariktisu-marked with the footprints; nyasyantiplacing; pranayat-out of love; alaktaka-rasam-red lac; purna-filled; kada-when?; syam-become; aham-I. When, drawing graceful pictures and designs on Your cheeks, decorating Your eyes with black kajjala, Your lips with red ranga, Your breasts with kunkuma, and Your toes with red lac, will I feel happy, O Sri Radha who trembles to meet Your beloved?
