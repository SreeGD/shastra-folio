Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 100
Translation

Text 260 sri-radhe rasikendra-rupa-gunavad-gitani samsravayan gunja-manjula-hara-barha-mukutady avedayams cagratah syama-presita-puga-malya-nava-gandhadyais ca samprinayams tvat-padabja-nakha-cchata-rasa-hrade magnah kada syam aham sri-radhe-O Sri Radha; rasikendra-of the king of rasikas; rupa-gunavad-gitanisongs describing the form and quaiities; samsravayan-causing to hear; gunjamanjula-hara-gunja neckfaces; barha-mukutadi-begionning wirth peacock-feather crowns; avedayamh-informing; ca-and; agratah-in the beginning; syama-presitasent by Krsna; puga-betefnuts; malya-garlands; nava-gandhah-new scents; adyaihbeginning with; ca-and; samprinayan-pleasing; tvat-padabja-nakha-cchata-rasahrade-in the nectar lake of the splendor of the toenails on the lotus flower of the feet; magnah-plunged; kada-when?; syam aham-I will be. O Sri Radha, when, singing songs about the form and qualities of the king of rasikas, placing before Him a graceful gunja necklace and a crown, and pleasing Him with the betelnuts, garlands, and fragrances sent by the beautiful gopls, will I become plunged in the nectar lake of the glory of Your lotus toenails?
