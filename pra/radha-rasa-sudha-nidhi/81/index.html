1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 81
Translation

Text 241 ucchistamrta-bhuk tavaiva caritarn srnvams tavaiva smaran padambhoja-rajas tavaiva vicaran kunjarirs tavaivalayan gayan divya-gunarirs tavaiva rasade pasyarirs tavaivakrtirn sri-radhe tanu-vari-manobhir amalaih so 'ham tavaivasritah ucchista-rernnant; arnrta-nectar; bhuk-tasting; tava-of You; eva-indeed; caritarnpastime; srnvarnh-hearing; tava-of You; eva-indeed; smaran-remembering; padambhoja-rajah-the pollen of the lotus feet; tava-of You; eva-indeed; vicaranthinking; kunjarirh-forest groves; tava-of You; eva-indeed; alayan-abodes; gayansinging; divya-gunarnh-transcendental virtues; tava-of You; eva-indeed; rasadesweet; pasyarnh-seeing; tava-of You; eva-indeed; akrtim-form; sri-radhe-O Sri Radha; tanu-van-manobhih-with body, mind, and words; arnalaih-pure; sah-he; 'harn-I; tava-of You; eva-indeed; asritah-taken shelter. Eating the remnants of Your meal, hearing Your pastimes, meditating on the dust of Your lotus feet, walking to the forest groves that are Your home, singing of Your divine virtues, and gazing on Your form, with a pure body, mind, and words, I take shelter of You, O Sri Radha, O giver of nectar.
