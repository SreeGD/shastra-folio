1 verse
Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi 24
Translation

Text 24 gatva kalinda- tanaya-vij anava tar am udvartayanty amrtam angarn ananga-jivam sn-radhike tava kada nava-nagarendram pasyami magna-nayanam sthitam ucca-nipe gatva-going; kalinda-tanaya-of the Yamuna; vijana-secluded; avataram-shore; udvartayanti-massaging; amrtam-nectar; angam-limbs; anariga-jivam-the life of Kamadeva; sri-radhike-O Sri Radha; tava-of You; kada-when?; nava-nagarendramthe youthful king of lovers; pasyami-I see; magna-plunged; nayanam-eyes; sthitamstaying; ucca-tall; nipe-on a kadamba tree. O Sri Radha, when, as I massage Your nectar limbs, which are Kamadeva's life, will I suddenly see the king of youthful lovers sitting high on a kadamba tree, His eyes plunged in the sight of You?
