Atma Nigraha (Self-control)

आत्मनिग्रहātma-nigraha[AHT-ma NI-gra-ha]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Core Value (depth 1)

Ātma-nigraha is the disciplined mastery over one's mind, senses, and impulses through conscious spiritual practice. As Krishna teaches in Bhagavad Gita 6.5-6, one must elevate oneself by the self and not degrade oneself, for the mind can be both friend and enemy depending on whether it is controlled or uncontrolled. This foundational virtue enables the soul to transcend material conditioning and establish itself in its constitutional position as servant of the Supreme.

What this value is NOT

Tamasika Indulgence
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Complete surrender to laziness, sleep, and sensual gratification without any attempt at regulation

Key distinguisher: Lacks any conscious effort toward self-improvement or spiritual elevation
Rajasika Impulsiveness
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Acting on every desire and emotion without pause for consideration or spiritual guidance

Key distinguisher: Driven by passion and immediate gratification rather than dharmic principles
False Renunciation
Source: Gaudiya Vaishnava teachings

Suppressing natural tendencies through force rather than redirecting them toward Krishna

Key distinguisher: Creates internal conflict rather than genuine transformation through devotional engagement
Mechanical Discipline
Source: Spiritual practice without devotion

Following rules and regulations without understanding their spiritual purpose or developing love for Krishna

Key distinguisher: Lacks the devotional heart that transforms discipline into joyful service
