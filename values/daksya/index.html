Daksya (Resourcefulness / Skill)

दक्ष्यdakṣya[DAHK-shya]
Karma (Action)Branch Value (depth 2)

Daksya is the divine skillfulness that emerges when one performs actions with complete surrender to Krishna, transcending personal attachment to results. It represents the competence that naturally develops when the practitioner aligns their efforts with dharmic principles, allowing Krishna's grace to flow through their work. This skillfulness is characterized not by ego-driven expertise, but by humble proficiency that serves the divine will.

Layers of meaning

  1. Surface: Technical competence and skill in worldly activities
  2. Devotional: Skillfulness that emerges through surrender and service to Krishna
  3. Transcendent: Divine competence that flows when ego dissolves in devotional action

What this value is NOT

Rajasika Ambition
Source: BG 18.27-28 (rajasika worker)

Skill driven by ego, attachment to results, and desire for recognition

Key distinguisher: Motivated by personal gain rather than service to the divine
Tamasika Incompetence
Source: BG 18.28 (tamasika worker)

Laziness, carelessness, and deliberate avoidance of developing proper skills

Key distinguisher: Refuses to cultivate competence due to inertia or false renunciation
Mechanical Expertise
Source: Traditional Vedantic teaching

Technical proficiency without spiritual consciousness or dharmic foundation

Key distinguisher: Lacks the devotional surrender that transforms skill into divine service
Prideful Mastery
Source: Bhagavata Purana examples

Competence that inflates the ego and creates separation from Krishna

Key distinguisher: Uses skill to assert superiority rather than serve humbly
