Shanti (Peace / Tranquility)

शान्तिśānti[SHAHN-tee]
Jnana (Wisdom)Core Value (depth 1)

Shanti is the profound inner tranquility that arises when the mind is established in Krishna consciousness, free from the agitations of material desires and mental disturbances. It represents the natural state of the soul when aligned with divine will, characterized by equanimity in all circumstances and freedom from the dualities of pleasure and pain. This peace is not mere absence of conflict but the positive presence of spiritual harmony that enables clear thinking, compassionate action, and steady devotion.

Layers of meaning

  1. Inner tranquility arising from Krishna consciousness
  2. Freedom from mental agitations and material desires
  3. Equanimity in all circumstances and situations
  4. Natural state of the soul aligned with divine will
  5. Foundation for clear thinking and compassionate action

Key distinguisher: Driven by passion and attachment rather than spiritual equanimity
