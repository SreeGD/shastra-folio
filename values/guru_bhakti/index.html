Guru Bhakti (Reverence for Teacher)

गुरुभक्तिguru-bhakti[GOO-roo BHUK-tee]
Prema (Love)Core Value (depth 1)

Guru-bhakti is the sacred devotional relationship between student and spiritual teacher, characterized by humble inquiry, respectful service, and complete trust in the guru's guidance toward Krishna consciousness. This relationship forms the foundation of authentic spiritual learning, as knowledge is transmitted not merely through words but through the living example and mercy of a self-realized soul. In the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, guru-bhakti is essential because the guru serves as Krishna's representative, making the transcendental accessible to the conditioned soul.

What this value is NOT

Intellectual Arrogance
Source: Tamasika mode (BG 14.8)

Approaching spiritual knowledge with pride, challenging the guru's authority, or believing one can understand transcendental truths through mental speculation alone

Key distinguisher: Lacks the humility essential for receiving transcendental knowledge
Guru Shopping
Source: Rajasika attachment (BG 14.7)

Constantly seeking different teachers based on personal preferences or convenience, without commitment to the disciplic succession

Key distinguisher: Treats spiritual guidance as a commodity rather than a sacred relationship
Sentimental Attachment
Source: Material consciousness

Focusing on the guru's personality or material qualities rather than their spiritual realization and connection to Krishna

Key distinguisher: Confuses material appreciation with spiritual devotion
Independence Complex
Source: False ego (ahamkara)

Believing one can progress spiritually without guidance, rejecting the need for a spiritual teacher

Key distinguisher: Denies the fundamental principle that spiritual knowledge descends through disciplic succession
