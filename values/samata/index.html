Samata (Equanimity / Even-mindedness)

समताsamatā[sa-ma-TAA]
Jnana (Wisdom)Core Value (depth 1)

Samatā is the divine quality of maintaining inner equilibrium and equal vision in all circumstances, whether pleasant or unpleasant. It represents the transcendence of dualistic reactions through steady absorption in Krishna consciousness, enabling one to perform duty without attachment to results. This equanimity arises from understanding the temporary nature of material experiences and the eternal nature of the soul's relationship with the Supreme.

Layers of meaning

  1. Emotional steadiness in success and failure
  2. Equal vision toward all beings as souls
  3. Detachment from material dualities
  4. Absorption in Krishna consciousness
  5. Foundation for dharmic action

What this value is NOT

Rajasika Reactivity
Source: Bhagavad Gita mode analysis

Excessive emotional swings between elation and depression based on external circumstances

Key distinguisher: Attachment-driven responses versus steady Krishna consciousness
Tamasika Indifference
Source: Bhagavad Gita mode analysis

Dull apathy or callous disregard masquerading as detachment

Key distinguisher: Ignorant withdrawal versus conscious transcendence through devotion
False Stoicism
Source: Vaishnava philosophical distinction

Suppressing emotions through mental force rather than spiritual elevation

Key distinguisher: Artificial control versus natural equipoise through bhakti
Favoritism
Source: BG 6.9 commentary

Showing partiality based on material designations rather than seeing all as Krishna's parts

Key distinguisher: Material vision versus spiritual equal vision
