Vairagya (Detachment / Dispassion)

वैराग्यvairāgya[vai-RAH-gya]
Jnana (Wisdom)Core Value (depth 1)

Vairagya is the spiritual quality of detachment from material outcomes while remaining fully engaged in one's prescribed duties. It represents the balanced state where one performs actions with dedication but offers all results to Krishna, maintaining equanimity in success and failure. This detachment is not indifference but rather freedom from the anxiety and bondage that comes from attachment to temporary material gains.

Layers of meaning

  1. Detachment from results while performing duty
  2. Freedom from material anxiety through surrender to Krishna
  3. Balanced engagement without emotional bondage to outcomes
  4. Transcendence of success-failure duality through devotional service

What this value is NOT

Rajasika Attachment
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Passionate attachment to results, driven by desire for recognition and material gain

Key distinguisher: Performs actions primarily for personal benefit and becomes disturbed by unfavorable outcomes
Tamasika Indifference
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Lazy disengagement from duty, mistaking inaction for detachment

Key distinguisher: Avoids responsibility and action altogether, lacking both engagement and proper understanding
Mayavadi Renunciation
Source: Gaudiya Vaishnava philosophy

Complete rejection of the material world as illusion, leading to artificial detachment

Key distinguisher: Considers material energy as separate from Krishna, leading to impersonal understanding
Emotional Suppression
Source: Practical spiritual life

Forcing oneself to not care about anything, creating internal conflict and artificiality

Key distinguisher: Attempts to eliminate natural feelings rather than purifying them through devotional service
