Discriminative Wisdom

विवेकviveka[vi-VAY-ka]
Jnana branch (Knowledge & Wisdom)Core Value (depth 1)

Viveka is the discriminative faculty that enables one to distinguish between the eternal and temporary, the real and illusory, dharma and adharma. It is the spiritual intelligence that sees beyond surface appearances to understand the true nature of reality, guiding one toward Krishna consciousness through proper discernment of what leads to spiritual advancement versus material entanglement.

Layers of meaning

  1. Basic discrimination between right and wrong actions
  2. Understanding the difference between temporary pleasure and lasting happiness
  3. Recognizing the eternal soul versus the temporary body
  4. Discerning Krishna's will versus personal desires
  5. Seeing the divine presence in all beings while understanding individual spiritual positions

What this value is NOT

Moha (Delusion)
Source: Bhagavad Gita - tamasic mode

Complete inability to distinguish between right and wrong, being bewildered by material appearances

Key distinguisher: Viveka sees clearly through maya while moha is completely covered by illusion
Rajasic Ambition
Source: Bhagavad Gita - rajasic mode

Making decisions based purely on material gain, passion, or ego-driven desires

Key distinguisher: Viveka considers eternal consequences while rajasic thinking focuses only on immediate material results
Intellectual Pride
Source: Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition

Using knowledge to inflate the ego rather than serve Krishna, becoming proud of one's discrimination

Key distinguisher: True viveka leads to humility and service while intellectual pride leads to spiritual stagnation
Emotional Reactivity
Source: Bhagavad Gita teachings on equanimity

Making decisions based on temporary emotional states rather than spiritual principles

Key distinguisher: Viveka maintains steady wisdom in all circumstances while emotional reactivity fluctuates with external conditions
