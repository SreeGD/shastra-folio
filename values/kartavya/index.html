Kartavya (Duty / Responsibility)

कर्तव्यkartavya[kar-ta-vya]
Karma (Action)Core Value (depth 1)

Kartavya is the sacred duty or prescribed action that one must perform according to one's position, capacity, and circumstances, as ordained by dharmic principles. It represents the understanding that certain actions are obligatory regardless of personal preference, arising from one's relationship with the Divine, society, and one's own spiritual development. True kartavya is performed with dedication and surrender to Krishna, transforming mundane obligations into acts of devotion.

Layers of meaning

  1. Prescribed actions according to one's position and capacity
  2. Obligations that transcend personal preference or convenience
  3. Duties performed as offerings to Krishna with proper consciousness
  4. Actions that maintain cosmic and social order through individual responsibility

What this value is NOT

Tamasika Negligence
Source: Bhagavad Gita (tamas guna)

Avoiding or neglecting duties due to laziness, ignorance, or delusion

Key distinguisher: Kartavya involves active engagement with prescribed duties, while tamasika negligence involves avoidance and inaction
Rajasika Ambition
Source: Bhagavad Gita (rajas guna)

Abandoning one's prescribed duties to pursue more glamorous or profitable activities

Key distinguisher: Kartavya honors one's given position and duties, while rajasika ambition seeks to escape them for personal gain
Selfish Convenience
Source: Modern materialistic mindset

Performing duties only when convenient or personally beneficial

Key distinguisher: Kartavya is performed regardless of personal convenience, while selfish convenience makes duty conditional on personal benefit
Duty Without Devotion
Source: Mechanical ritualism

Performing prescribed actions without proper consciousness or connection to Krishna

Key distinguisher: True kartavya involves conscious offering to Krishna, while mechanical duty lacks spiritual awareness
