Sthitaprajna (Steady Wisdom)

स्थितप्रज्ञsthitaprajña[STHEE-ta-prag-nya]
Jnana (Wisdom)Core Value (depth 1)

Sthitaprajna represents the state of unwavering spiritual consciousness where one remains equipoised in all circumstances, neither elated by gain nor disturbed by loss. This steady wisdom manifests as complete absorption in Krishna consciousness, where the devotee's intelligence remains fixed on the Supreme Lord regardless of external conditions. Like Madhavendra Puri who maintained perfect devotion whether receiving Krishna's special mercy or facing apparent rejection, the sthitaprajna devotee demonstrates unshakeable faith and equanimity.

यस्य स्थिरा बुद्धिर्न विचलत्येषा वर्तमानेऽपि वैषम्ये । निर्द्वन्द्वो निष्कामो निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः स शान्तिमाप्नोति ॥
When one's intelligence, applied through the freewill, does not turn away from the Supreme Personality of Godhead even in the greatest calamities, distresses, difficulties and dangers, then it is to be understood that he is situated in Krsna consciousness. In such a position he becomes free from all attachment, all desires, all sense of proprietorship and false ego, and thus he achieves the peace which is without any material designation.
