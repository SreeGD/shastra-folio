Tejas (Inner Radiance / Vigour)

तेजस्tejas[TEH-jas]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Core Value (depth 1)

Tejas is the divine radiance and spiritual fire that emanates from a soul aligned with dharma and devotion to Krishna. It manifests as an inner luminosity that illuminates truth, burns away ignorance, and inspires others through the power of spiritual conviction and moral courage.

Layers of meaning

  1. Inner spiritual fire that burns away ignorance and impurities
  2. Divine radiance that emanates from dharmic living and devotional practice
  3. Luminous power that inspires and transforms others through spiritual example
  4. Sacred energy that illuminates truth and dispels darkness of maya

What this value is NOT

Tamasika Dullness
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Spiritual lethargy, ignorance, and lack of inner fire or inspiration

Key distinguisher: Absence of spiritual enthusiasm and divine connection
Rajasika Ego-Fire
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Passionate intensity driven by material desires and ego rather than spiritual purpose

Key distinguisher: Self-centered ambition versus dharma-centered radiance
False Spiritual Pride
Source: Devotional literature warnings

Artificial display of spiritual power or knowledge without genuine realization

Key distinguisher: External show versus authentic inner transformation
Material Brilliance
Source: Vedic discrimination between spiritual and material

Intellectual or worldly success without spiritual foundation or divine connection

Key distinguisher: Temporary material achievement versus eternal spiritual radiance
