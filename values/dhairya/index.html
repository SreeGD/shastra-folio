Dhairya (Composure / Courage)

धैर्यdhairya[DHIGH-rya]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Core Value (depth 1)

Dhairya is the divine quality of unwavering steadiness and patient endurance in the face of challenges, rooted in trust in Krishna's plan. It manifests as the ability to remain calm and determined during difficulties, maintaining one's spiritual practice and dharmic duties without being disturbed by temporary setbacks or provocations.

What this value is NOT

Tamasika Inertia
Source: Gita modes of nature

Passive resignation or laziness disguised as patience, lacking spiritual purpose

Key distinguisher: True dhairya involves active spiritual engagement, not passive withdrawal
Rajasika Impatience
Source: Gita modes of nature

Restless agitation and demand for immediate results in spiritual or material pursuits

Key distinguisher: Dhairya accepts divine timing while maintaining steady effort
Vengeful Persistence
Source: Dhruva's initial motivation

Stubborn determination driven by anger, pride, or desire for revenge

Key distinguisher: True dhairya is motivated by love for Krishna, not material desires
Spiritual Bypassing
Source: Modern misconception

Using patience as an excuse to avoid necessary action or responsibility

Key distinguisher: Dhairya includes appropriate action aligned with dharma
