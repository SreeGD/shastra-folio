Bhakti (Devotion)

भक्तिbhakti[BHUK-tee]
Prema (Love)Root Value (depth 0)

Bhakti is the topmost yoga system characterized by pure loving devotional service to Krishna, performed without material desires or expectations. It transforms all activities into spiritual service through surrender and love, providing the most direct path to spiritual realization and eternal happiness.

Also called: devotionloving servicedivine lovesurrender in love

Layers of meaning

  1. Pure loving service to the Supreme Lord Krishna
  2. The topmost yoga system that includes benefits of all other paths
  3. Transformation of mundane activities into spiritual service
  4. Direct path to spiritual world through divine grace

What this value is NOT

Karma-misra (Material Desire Tainted Devotion)
Source: Gita tradition

Devotional service performed with material desires for heavenly pleasures or worldly benefits

Key distinguisher: Seeks personal gain rather than pure love
Tamasika Attachment
Source: BG modes of nature

Ignorant attachment to material objects, people, or outcomes without spiritual understanding

Key distinguisher: Rooted in illusion and darkness rather than divine love
Dry Intellectual Speculation
Source: Jnana-misra tradition

Attempting to understand the divine through mental speculation alone without devotional surrender

Key distinguisher: Relies on limited intellect rather than grace through love
Mechanical Ritualism
Source: Vedic tradition

Performing religious activities without love, devotion, or understanding of their spiritual purpose

Key distinguisher: External form without internal transformation
