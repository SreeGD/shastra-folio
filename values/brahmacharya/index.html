Brahmacharya (Self-discipline / Restraint)

ब्रह्मचर्यbrahmacarya[BRAHM-ah-char-ya]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Core Value (depth 1)

Brahmacharya is the disciplined regulation of one's vital energies and senses in service of spiritual realization, traditionally practiced through celibacy and devoted study. In the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, it represents channeling all physical, mental and emotional energies toward Krishna consciousness rather than material gratification. This sacred discipline creates the inner purity and focus necessary for authentic spiritual progress and devotional service.

Layers of meaning

  1. Physical regulation of sexual energy and sensory indulgence
  2. Mental discipline in thoughts, speech and study habits
  3. Spiritual channeling of life force toward divine realization
  4. Devotional offering of regulated life to Krishna's service
