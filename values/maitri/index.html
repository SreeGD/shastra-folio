Maitri (Friendship / Goodwill)

मैत्रीmaitrī[MY-tree]
Prema (Love)Branch Value (depth 2)

Maitri is the divine quality of unconditional friendship and loving-kindness that flows naturally from seeing Krishna in all beings. It manifests as genuine care, warmth, and goodwill toward all creatures, transcending personal preferences and extending even to those who may oppose us. This friendship is grounded in spiritual vision rather than material attraction, making it stable and transformative.

What this value is NOT

Rajasika Attachment-Based Friendship
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Friendship based on personal gain, pleasure, or mutual benefit that changes when circumstances change

Key distinguisher: Conditional and self-serving versus unconditional and selfless
Sectarian Favoritism
Source: Gaudiya Vaishnava teachings on universal vision

Showing kindness only to those who share our beliefs or background while being indifferent to others

Key distinguisher: Exclusive versus inclusive spiritual vision
Sentimental Attachment
Source: Vedic psychology

Emotional dependency disguised as friendship that creates bondage rather than liberation

Key distinguisher: Creates spiritual bondage versus spiritual freedom
Competitive Jealousy
Source: Indra's reaction to Govardhan worship in corpus

Feeling threatened by others' success or recognition, like Indra's anger when worship was redirected

Key distinguisher: Envious competition versus celebrating others' spiritual progress
