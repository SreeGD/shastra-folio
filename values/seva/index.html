Seva (Service / Selfless Action)

सेवाsevā[say-VAH]
Karma (Action)Core Value (depth 1)

Seva is selfless service performed with devotion to the Divine, where one acts without attachment to results while considering oneself an instrument of Krishna's will. It transforms ordinary activities into spiritual practice by infusing them with love, humility, and the desire to please the Supreme rather than oneself. True seva dissolves the ego and connects the practitioner directly with Krishna through loving action.

Layers of meaning

  1. Physical service to devotees, deities, and creation
  2. Mental service through remembrance and meditation
  3. Emotional service through love and devotion
  4. Spiritual service through complete surrender of results

What this value is NOT

Rajasika Service
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Service performed with attachment to results, recognition, or personal gain

Key distinguisher: Motivated by ego and desire for appreciation rather than love for Krishna
Tamasika Service
Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Service performed reluctantly, carelessly, or with harmful intent

Key distinguisher: Lacks consciousness, care, or positive intention
Mechanical Service
Source: Gaudiya Vaishnava teachings

Service performed as mere ritual or duty without devotional feeling

Key distinguisher: Absence of love, consciousness, and connection to Krishna
Self-Serving Service
Source: Practical observation

Actions disguised as service but primarily benefiting oneself

Key distinguisher: Hidden agenda of personal benefit rather than genuine selflessness
