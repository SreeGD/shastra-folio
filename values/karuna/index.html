Karuna (Kindness / Compassion)

करुणाkaruṇā[ka-ru-NAA]
Prema (Love)Core Value (depth 1)

Karuṇā is the divine quality of compassion that naturally flows from understanding the suffering of all living beings and responding with genuine care and action to alleviate that suffering. In the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, true compassion arises from recognizing the spiritual nature of all souls and their inherent relationship with Krishna, motivating devotees to serve others' highest spiritual welfare. This compassion is not mere sentiment but an active expression of divine love that seeks to guide all beings toward their eternal spiritual happiness.

What this value is NOT

Krūratā (Cruelty)
Source: Gita 16.4 - āsurī sampat

Deliberate infliction of suffering on others, characteristic of demonic nature

Key distinguisher: Enjoys causing pain vs. seeks to alleviate suffering
Udāsīnatā (Indifference)
Source: Tamasic mode

Complete lack of concern for others' welfare or suffering

Key distinguisher: Apathy toward suffering vs. active response to suffering
Kāruṇya-abhāsa (False Compassion)
Source: Material attachment

Sentimental attachment disguised as compassion, often enabling harmful behavior

Key distinguisher: Enables spiritual degradation vs. promotes spiritual elevation
Ahaṅkāra-dayā (Ego-driven Pity)
Source: False ego

Condescending attitude that maintains superiority while appearing compassionate

Key distinguisher: Reinforces separation vs. recognizes spiritual equality
