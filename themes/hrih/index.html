Hrih (Modesty / Sense of Shame)

ह्रीःhrīḥ[hree]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Root Value (depth 0) modestyhumilitybashfulnessshamepropriety

Hrīḥ is the divine quality of modesty that manifests as natural humility, appropriate shame when falling short of dharmic standards, and graceful restraint in speech and behavior. It represents the soul's innate recognition of its position before the Supreme and others, creating space for genuine spiritual growth. This quality protects one from pride and creates the foundation for receiving transcendental knowledge.

Definition — Layers
Natural humility arising from understanding one's true position
Appropriate shame that guides moral behavior
Graceful restraint in self-expression
Protection from false ego and pride
Foundation for spiritual receptivity

Key distinguisher: Seeks external validation vs. internal authenticity
Shamelessness
Source: Tamasika tendency
Complete absence of moral sensitivity or appropriate restraint
Key distinguisher: No recognition of dharmic boundaries vs. natural propriety
Aggressive Self-Promotion
Source: Rajasika mode

Key distinguisher: Pushes self forward vs. allows natural recognition

Source: False renunciation
Using spiritual practices to feel superior to others
Key distinguisher: Spirituality as ego-enhancement vs. ego-dissolution
