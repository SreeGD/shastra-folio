Achapalya (Steadiness / Non-fickleness)

अचापल्यacāpalya[ah-CHAH-pal-ya]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Core Value (depth 1) steadinessunwavering resolvemental stabilityfreedom from fickleness

Achapalya is the quality of mental steadiness and unwavering resolve that enables one to remain focused on spiritual goals without being distracted by temporary pleasures or difficulties. It represents freedom from the restless, fickle nature of the mind that constantly seeks new stimulations and changes direction. This steadiness is essential for sustained spiritual practice and the cultivation of deeper devotional consciousness.

Definition — Layers
Mental stability and freedom from restlessness
Unwavering commitment to spiritual practices and principles
Resistance to temporary distractions and material allurements
Sustained focus on long-term spiritual goals over immediate gratification

Anti-patterns — What this value is NOT
Chapalya (Fickleness)
Source: Direct Sanskrit opposite
Restless, fickle nature that constantly seeks new stimulations and changes direction
Key distinguisher: Lacks sustained commitment to any practice or principle
Rajasika Restlessness
Source: Bhagavad Gita mode analysis
Passionate, agitated state of mind driven by desires for sense gratification
Key distinguisher: Motivated by material desires rather than spiritual conviction
Tamasika Inertia
Source: Bhagavad Gita mode analysis
Lazy, procrastinating tendency that avoids sustained effort
Key distinguisher: Lacks the energy and determination needed for consistent practice
Spiritual Materialism
Source: Contemporary spiritual pitfall
Constantly seeking new spiritual experiences or teachers without depth
Key distinguisher: Treats spirituality as entertainment rather than transformation
