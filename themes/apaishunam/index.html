Apaishunam (Non-slander)

अपैशुनम्apaiśunam[a-pai-SHU-nam]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Primary Value (depth 1) non-slanderabsence of malicious speechnon-backbitingrefraining from gossip

Apaiśunam is the conscious restraint from speaking about others in ways that damage their reputation, cause division, or inflict emotional harm. It encompasses abstaining from gossip, backbiting, tale-bearing, and all forms of verbal cruelty including cyberbullying. Rooted in the understanding that speech carries immense karmic weight, this value calls for using one's words to heal, unite, and uplift rather than to wound, divide, and destroy.

Definition — Layers
Restraint from gossip, backbiting, and speaking ill of others behind their backs
Refusal to participate in or encourage tale-bearing that creates division among people
Active protection of others' dignity and reputation, especially when they are absent
Channeling the power of speech toward truth, kindness, and reconciliation

Anti-patterns — What this value is NOT
Paiśunam (Slander/Tale-bearing)
Source: Bhagavad Gita 16.2 — opposite quality
Deliberately spreading damaging information about others to harm their reputation or create discord
Key distinguisher: Intentional destruction of another's good name for personal satisfaction or advantage
Gossip Culture
Source: Social behavioral analysis
Habitual sharing of rumors and personal information about others as entertainment or social bonding
Key distinguisher: Treats others' private lives as entertainment rather than respecting their dignity
Cyberbullying and Online Shaming
Source: Modern cultural analysis
Using digital platforms to publicly humiliate, spread rumors about, or organize harassment against individuals
Key distinguisher: Amplifies harm through technology, making slander permanent and far-reaching
Righteous Condemnation
Source: Vedic ethical analysis
Disguising gossip and character assassination as moral duty or concern for community standards
Key distinguisher: Uses dharmic language to justify what is essentially malicious speech
