Shraddha (Faith / Reverence)

श्रद्धाśraddhā[SHRAH-dhaa]
Jnana (Wisdom)Core Value (depth 1) faithtrustconvictiondevotional confidence

Shraddha is unwavering faith and trust in Krishna, His teachings, and the spiritual path, arising from both scriptural understanding and personal experience. It manifests as confident devotion that persists through challenges, enabling one to surrender completely to divine guidance. This faith becomes the foundation for all spiritual progress, as Krishna declares that one who has faith (śraddhāvān) obtains knowledge and quickly attains supreme peace.

Definition — Layers
Trust in Krishna's words and promises
Confidence in the efficacy of devotional practices
Faith in the guru-disciple relationship
Conviction in the ultimate goal of prema-bhakti

Anti-patterns — What this value is NOT
Tamasik Doubt
Source: BG 17.2 (tamasik faith category)
Persistent skepticism that rejects spiritual truths without proper inquiry, leading to spiritual stagnation
Key distinguisher: Closes the heart to divine possibility rather than opening it through sincere questioning
Blind Fanaticism
Source: Gaudiya tradition
Unthinking acceptance without understanding, leading to rigid dogmatism rather than loving surrender
Key distinguisher: Lacks the discrimination (viveka) that true shraddha requires
Material Confidence
Source: Bhagavatam tradition
Placing ultimate trust in temporary things like wealth, relationships, or abilities rather than eternal principles
Key distinguisher: Seeks security in the changeable rather than the unchanging
Intellectual Pride
Source: Gita tradition
Relying solely on mental speculation while dismissing the need for faithful surrender to realized souls
Key distinguisher: Elevates reasoning above revelation and personal realization
