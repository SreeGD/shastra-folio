Prema (Divine Love)

प्रेमprema[PRAY-ma]
Prema (Love)Root Value (depth 0) divine lovepure lovespiritual loveunconditional love

Prema is the highest form of divine love characterized by complete selflessness and unconditional devotion to Krishna and all living beings. It transcends material attachment and manifests as spontaneous, causeless affection that seeks only the beloved's happiness without any expectation of return. This pure love represents the soul's natural constitutional position and the ultimate goal of spiritual development.

Definition — Layers
Emotional purification through selfless affection
Transcendence of material desires and expectations
Natural constitutional love for Krishna awakened
Universal compassion flowing from divine connection

Anti-patterns — What this value is NOT
Kama (Lust)
Source: Bhagavad Gita 3.37
Selfish desire that seeks personal gratification and possession of the beloved
Key distinguisher: Prema gives unconditionally while kama takes possessively
Moha (Attachment)
Source: Bhagavad Gita 14.8
Tamasic delusion that binds through ignorant emotional dependency
Key distinguisher: Prema liberates through knowledge while moha binds through ignorance
Ahamkara (False Ego)
Source: Bhagavad Gita 18.58
Self-centered love that ultimately serves one's own identity and pride
Key distinguisher: Prema dissolves the false ego while ahamkara strengthens it
