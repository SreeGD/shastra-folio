Nishkama Karma (Selfless Action)

निष्काम कर्मniṣkāma karma[nish-KAH-ma KAR-ma]
Karma (Action)Core Value (depth 1) desireless actionselfless workduty without attachmentaction without expectation

Niṣkāma karma is the performance of one's prescribed duties without attachment to results, offering all actions to Krishna. This spiritual practice transforms ordinary work into a means of purification and liberation, freeing the soul from karmic bondage while maintaining engagement with the world.

Definition — Layers
Action performed without desire for personal gain or recognition
Duty executed with full dedication but without attachment to outcomes
Work offered as service to the Supreme Lord Krishna
The path of liberation through engaged detachment in action

Anti-patterns — What this value is NOT
Sakāma Karma (Desire-motivated Action)
Source: Bhagavad Gita - rajasika mode
Actions performed with strong attachment to results and personal gain
Key distinguisher: Driven by 'what will I get?' rather than 'what is my duty?'
Akarma (Inaction/Laziness)
Source: Bhagavad Gita - tamasika mode
Avoiding action altogether under the guise of detachment
Key distinguisher: Abandoning duty rather than performing it without attachment
Vikarma (Prohibited Action)
Source: Vedic injunctions
Actions that violate dharmic principles, regardless of motivation
Key distinguisher: Wrong action cannot become right through detachment
Ego-driven Service
Source: Spiritual pride
Performing seemingly selfless acts for recognition or spiritual superiority
Key distinguisher: Hidden desire for appreciation or status corrupts the action
