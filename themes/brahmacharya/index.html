Brahmacharya (Self-discipline / Restraint)

ब्रह्मचर्यbrahmacarya[BRAHM-ah-char-ya]
Vinaya (Inner Character)Core Value (depth 1) celibacyspiritual studentshipwalking with Brahmandivine conduct

Brahmacharya is the disciplined regulation of one's vital energies and senses in service of spiritual realization, traditionally practiced through celibacy and devoted study. In the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, it represents channeling all physical, mental and emotional energies toward Krishna consciousness rather than material gratification. This sacred discipline creates the inner purity and focus necessary for authentic spiritual progress and devotional service.

Definition — Layers
Physical regulation of sexual energy and sensory indulgence
Mental discipline in thoughts, speech and study habits
Spiritual channeling of life force toward divine realization
Devotional offering of regulated life to Krishna's service

Source: Bhagavad Gita modes of nature

Key distinguisher: Lacks the energy and commitment for any meaningful discipline
False Renunciation
Source: Gaudiya Vaishnava teachings
Mechanical external celibacy without inner purification or devotional purpose
Key distinguisher: Focuses on rules without relationship to Krishna
Materialistic Studentship
Source: Traditional ashrama system
Study and discipline aimed only at material success and social status
Key distinguisher: Uses discipline as means for material advancement rather than spiritual growth
