Caitanya-caritāmṛta 10.66

sāta-dike sāta-sampradāya gāya, bājāya madhye mahā-premāveśe nāce gaura-rāya
[saata-dike saata-sampradaaya gaaya, baajaaya madhye mahaa-premaaveshe naache gaura-raaya]
Blended reading (Sanskrit + English inline)
sāta-dike sāta-sampradāya gāya, bājāya madhye mahā-premāveśe nāce gaura-rāya
The seven groups began chanting and beating their drums in seven directions, and Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu began dancing in the center in great ecstatic love.
