His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Śrīla Prabhupāda

1896 – 1977

Founder-Ācārya of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), of which Govardhan Eco Village is a project. Translator of the Śrīmad Bhāgavatam, Bhagavad-gītā As It Is, and the Caitanya-caritāmṛta — the textual root of Vidyā Karaṇa.

🧑‍🏫 For teachers

Ready-to-use values lessons.

Each chapter is a 40-minute or 2-hour lesson, with story, discussion, and worksheet.

📋 Lesson plans

Two timing options per chapter (40 min and 2 hr). Discussion notes built in.

📝 Worksheets

Activities with crafts, games, and reflection. Printable and adaptable.

🎯 Rubric

Three-point assessment per value: emerging, developing, exemplary.
