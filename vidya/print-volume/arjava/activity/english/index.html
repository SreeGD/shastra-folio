About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Arjava Worksheet — A Straight Little Heart

Activity 1: Straight or Tangled? (Circle-the-Heart)

Look at each picture your teacher shows. If the child is being simple and straight, circle the clean heart 💚. If the child is hiding or pretending, circle the tangled heart 💔.

1. A boy telling Amma, 'I broke the cup by mistake.' 💚 💔

2. A girl smiling at a friend and whispering mean things behind her. 💚 💔

3. A child saying, 'I don't know this answer' instead of copying. 💚 💔

4. A boy hiding his bad mark inside his textbook. 💚 💔

5. A girl giving back the pencil she borrowed without being asked. 💚 💔

Activity 2: One Heart, One Face (Drawing)

In the big heart below, draw YOUR face. Above the heart, draw the same face — smiling or sad, whichever is true today. Write one word inside the heart that tells how you really feel.

[Drawing space — big heart shape]

*Arjava is when the face above the heart and the feeling inside the heart are friends, not strangers.*

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times with your teacher, palms together at your heart:

Āār-ja-vam! Āār-ja-vam! Āār-ja-vam! *One heart. One mouth. One clean step.*

Activity 4: The Twisty Sentence Game (Paired)

With a partner, turn a TWISTY sentence into a STRAIGHT one:

  • Twisty: 'Um, I sort of maybe didn't really… lose your pencil.' → Straight: 'I lost your pencil. I am sorry.'
  • Twisty: 'Your drawing is… well… interesting.' → Straight: 'I like the colours, but the tree looks a little tired.'
  • Twisty: 'I'm not hungry.' *(said while staring at the biscuit)* → Straight: 'I would like a biscuit, please.'

Take turns. Use a kind, clear voice.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Clean Heart Diary

For one week, every night before bed, ask yourself ONE question: *'Did I say and do today only what I really felt inside?'*

Draw a clean heart 💚 for yes. Draw a tangled heart 💔 and one word for no. No one else needs to see this page. It is between you and Krishna.

Structured Activities

  • Straight or Tangled (whole_class, 7 min)

    Look at each picture and circle the clean heart if the child is simple and straight, or the tangled heart if they are hiding or pretending.

    Materials: Worksheet, Crayons

  • One Heart, One Face (solo, 8 min)

    Draw your face above a big heart. Write one true word inside the heart. Make the face and the word match.

    Materials: Worksheet, Crayons

  • Say It With Me (whole_class, 3 min)

    Chant arjavam three times with palms at heart. One heart, one mouth, one clean step.

  • The Twisty Sentence Game (paired, 8 min)

    With a partner, turn a twisty sentence into a straight, kind one.

  • The Clean Heart Diary (solo, 5 min)

    For one week, draw a clean or tangled heart in your diary each night and answer one honest question about your day.

    Materials: Diary, Crayons

Home Practice

For one week, each night before bed, sit for one minute with Amma or Appa. Place your hand on your heart. Ask yourself one question: 'Did I say and do today only what I really felt inside?' If yes, draw a clean heart in your diary. If there is one thing you hid or pretended, gently say it out loud to Amma or Appa — OR just whisper it to Krishna and let it go. Also: any time you feel 'stones in your tummy' during the day, stop and ask, 'Is something hidden here?'

Duration: Every night for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can name at least two moments they chose to speak straight instead of tangle, and can describe the difference between the 'heavy tummy' feeling of hiding and the 'light chest' feeling of simplicity.
