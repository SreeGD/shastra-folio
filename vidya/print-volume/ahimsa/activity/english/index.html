About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Ahimsa

Ahimsa means not hurting anyone — not with hands, not with words, not even with angry thoughts.

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times with your teacher, palms together at your heart:

A-him-sā! A-him-sā! A-him-sā! *Do not hurt! Do not hurt! Do not hurt!*

Activity 4: Gentle Voice Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, practice turning an ANGRY sentence into a GENTLE one:

  • Angry: 'Give me that toy NOW!' → Gentle: 'Can I have a turn, please?'
  • Angry: 'You are stupid!' → Gentle: 'Let me help you.'
  • Angry: 'Go away!' → Gentle: 'I need some quiet time.'

Take turns. Use a soft voice.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Ant Walk

For one week, every time you walk outside, look down. Look out for ants, worms, or any small creatures on the path. Step AROUND them, never ON them. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa how many creatures you saved today.

    Materials: Worksheet, Crayons

  • Say It With Me (whole_class, 3 min)

    Chant ahimsa three times with palms at heart.

  • Gentle Voice Role-Play (paired, 8 min)

    With a partner, practice turning an angry sentence into a gentle one.

  • The Ant Walk (solo, 5 min)

    For one week, look down when you walk and step around every small creature. Count how many you save each day.

Home Practice

For one week, do the Ant Walk. Every time you walk outside, look down. Step AROUND ants, worms, and small creatures. Tell Amma or Appa each night how many creatures you saved. Also: each time you feel angry, breathe three times before you speak or touch anyone.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can describe at least 3 times they chose gentle action over rough, and can demonstrate the three-breath pause when upset.
