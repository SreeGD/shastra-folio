About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

दया
Daya

Daya means caring when you see someone hurt or sad.

Worksheet

Daya Worksheet - Being Kind and Caring

Section 1: Story Pictures

Look at these pictures and circle the ones that show daya (caring):
  • A child helping another child who fell down
  • A child sharing food with a hungry animal
  • A child being mean to a smaller child
  • A child comforting someone who is crying

Draw a picture of yourself showing daya to someone: [Drawing space]

Section 2: Kindness Garden Craft

Materials: Paper, crayons, cotton balls, glue

Steps:

1. Draw a big flower on your paper

2. In the center, draw yourself being kind

3. On each petal, draw or write one way to show daya

4. Glue cotton balls around your flower like clouds

5. Color everything with bright, happy colors

While you make it, think: What makes you feel happy when you help others?

Section 3: Animal Friends Game

How to play:

1. Everyone sits in a circle

2. One person acts like a hurt animal (limping, looking sad)

3. Others take turns showing how to help that animal

4. The "animal" says "thank you" with animal sounds

5. Take turns being different animals

After playing, talk about: How did it feel to help? How did it feel to be helped?

Section 4: Color the Caring Heart

Color this big heart with your favorite colors. Inside the heart, draw three things you can do to show daya:

1. At home: ___________

2. At school: ___________

3. With animals: ___________"

Structured Activities

  • Story Pictures (solo, 10 min)

    Look at pictures showing different actions and circle the ones that show daya (caring). Then draw yourself showing daya to someone.

    Materials: worksheet, crayons

  • Kindness Garden Craft (solo, 15 min)

    Create a flower garden where each petal shows a different way to be kind. Use cotton balls for clouds and bright colors.

    Materials: paper, crayons, cotton balls, glue

  • Animal Friends Game (whole_class, 10 min)

    Act like hurt animals and take turns showing how to help each other. Make animal sounds to say thank you.

  • Caring Songs (whole_class, 8 min)

    Sing simple songs about being kind while doing hand motions that show helping actions.

  • Color the Caring Heart (solo, 12 min)

    Color a big heart and draw three ways you can show daya at home, school, and with animals.

    Materials: worksheet, crayons

Home Practice

Each day, look for one person or animal that needs help. Do one small kind thing for them - give a hug, share food, or say kind words.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will notice that helping others makes you feel happy and warm in your heart, and others will smile more around you.
