About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Shraddha

Shraddha means faith — trusting Krishna, trusting our teachers, trusting the sacred stories with our whole heart.

Worksheet

Shraddha Worksheet — A Heart That Trusts

Activity 1: Seed or No Seed? (Circle the Right Picture)

Look at each little story. If the child is showing shraddha (trust), circle the 🌱 seed. If the child is NOT trusting yet, circle the ❓ question mark.

1. Aarav chants 'Krishna' every night, even when nothing feels special. 🌱 ❓

2. Meera says, 'I will only pray if Krishna gives me a new bicycle first.' 🌱 ❓

3. Pihu waters her tulasi every day for two weeks with a smile. 🌱 ❓

4. Rohan stops reading the Gita stories after two days because 'it's boring'. 🌱 ❓

5. Kavya does what her guru-ji asked, even though she does not fully understand yet. 🌱 ❓

Activity 2: Draw Your Faith-Seed

In the box below, draw a pot with soil. Inside the soil, draw a little seed. On top of the pot, write ONE practice you will do every day for ten days — even if you do not see any change yet. Examples: chant Krishna three times, help Amma with one chore, say 'thank You, Krishna' before food.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times with palms pressed at your heart:

Shrad-dhā! Shrad-dhā! Shrad-dhā! *I trust! I try! I wait with love!*

Activity 4: The Trust Walk (Paired)

With a partner:

1. Close your eyes. Let your partner gently lead you three steps forward by the hand.

2. Feel how it feels to trust.

3. Now switch. You lead. Your partner closes their eyes.

4. Be very gentle. A trusting heart is a precious thing.

After: 'What did your heart feel when you trusted? What did you feel when someone trusted you?'

Activity 5: Take Home — The Faith Pot

Ask Amma or Appa for a small cup of soil and a few seeds (methi, moong, or tulasi work well). Plant the seeds. Every morning for 10 days, water them. While watering, chant 'Krishna, Krishna, Krishna' three times. Do NOT dig up the seeds to peek. Trust that Krishna is working under the soil. At the end of 10 days, draw a picture of what you see — or what you still believe is coming.

Structured Activities

  • Seed or No Seed (solo, 6 min)

    Read each little story and circle the seed if the child is showing shraddha, or the question mark if not.

    Materials: Worksheet, Pencil

  • Draw Your Faith-Seed (solo, 8 min)

    Draw a pot with a seed inside. Write on top ONE practice you will do every day for ten days.

    Materials: Paper, Crayons

  • Say It With Me (whole_class, 3 min)

    Chant shraddha three times with palms pressed at the heart.

  • The Trust Walk (paired, 8 min)

    With a partner, take turns leading each other three steps with eyes closed. Feel the trust on both sides.

  • The Faith Pot (solo, 5 min)

    Plant a few seeds. Water and chant Krishna three times every morning for 10 days. Do not dig up to peek. Draw what grows.

    Materials: Small clay pot or paper cup, Soil, Seeds (methi/moong/tulasi), Small water lota

Home Practice

The Faith Pot: plant a few methi, moong, or tulasi seeds with Amma or Appa. Every morning for 10 days in a row, water them. While watering, chant 'Krishna, Krishna, Krishna' three times. Do NOT dig up the seeds to check. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa what you did today. On the day the first green leaf comes up, clap your hands and say, 'Krishna, I trusted — and You kept Your promise.' Also: if a teacher or grown-up you love asks you to try a practice you do not fully understand, try it for three full days before deciding anything.

Duration: Every day for two weeks
Look for: By the end of two weeks, the child can show a sprouted Faith Pot (or describe the waiting if no sprout yet), and can say one specific moment when they chose to 'keep watering' instead of giving up.
