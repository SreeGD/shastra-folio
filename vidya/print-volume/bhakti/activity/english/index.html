About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Section 2: Love Garden Craft

What you need:
  • Paper plate
  • Colored paper
  • Crayons
  • Glue
  • Scissors (ask an adult to help)

How to make it:

1. Color your paper plate green like grass

2. Cut out flower shapes from colored paper

3. On each flower, draw or write one way you can show love

4. Glue the flowers onto your green plate

5. Write "My Love Garden" at the top

While you make it, think: What are some ways you can show love to your family?

Section 3: The Sharing Circle Game

How to play:
  • Sit in a circle with your friends
  • One person starts by saying: "I can share my _____ with _____"
  • Pass a flower (real or paper) to the next person
  • That person says how they can share something too
  • Keep going until everyone has a turn
  • End by all saying together: "Sharing makes love grow!"

Talk about it after:

  • How did it feel to think about sharing?
  • What's the easiest thing to share? The hardest?

Structured Activities

  • Love Garden Craft (solo, 20 min)

    Create a paper plate garden with flowers showing different ways to express love and kindness

    Materials: paper plates, colored paper, crayons, glue, child-safe scissors

  • Sharing Circle Game (whole_class, 15 min)

    Sit in a circle and take turns sharing how you can give to others, passing a flower token

    Materials: paper flower or real flower, circle seating

  • Krishna's Name Song (whole_class, 10 min)

    Learn and sing a simple song with Krishna's names, clapping to the rhythm

    Materials: none

  • Nature's Gifts Hunt (small_group, 15 min)

    Find natural items (leaves, flowers, stones) that you could offer as gifts, discussing how nature gives freely

    Materials: collection baskets, outdoor space

Home Practice

Each day, find one small thing you can give or share with someone in your family - a drawing, a flower, help with a job, or a kind word. Before you give it, think 'This is for Krishna' in your heart.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will notice that giving makes you feel warm and happy inside, and your family will smile more.
