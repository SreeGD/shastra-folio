About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Section 2: Make a Sunshine Heart (Craft Activity)

What you need: Yellow paper, orange paper, glue, crayons

Steps:

1. Cut out a big heart from yellow paper

2. Cut out sun rays from orange paper

3. Glue the rays around your heart

4. Draw Krishna's name in the middle: क्ष्ण (Krishna)

5. Color it bright and beautiful

6. Hold it up and say "My heart shines with Krishna's love!"

Think while you make: What makes your heart feel bright and happy?

Section 3: Tejas Dance Game (Movement Activity)

How to play:
  • Stand in a circle with friends
  • When music plays, dance like you have bright light inside
  • Move your arms like sun rays
  • When music stops, freeze and shine like the sun
  • Take turns being in the middle and showing your brightest pose

Talk about: How did it feel to dance with bright energy?

Section 4: Color the Brave Hearts

Color these pictures of children showing tejas:
  • A child helping someone who fell down
  • A child singing Krishna's name happily
  • A child sharing their lunch with a friend
  • A child standing up to someone being mean

Use bright, happy colors!

Structured Activities

  • Story Pictures (solo, 8 min)

    Look at pictures from our stories and circle the ones that show tejas (bright light from loving Krishna)

    Materials: worksheet with pictures, crayons

  • Make a Sunshine Heart (solo, 15 min)

    Create a heart-shaped sun with Krishna's name in the center using yellow and orange paper

    Materials: yellow paper, orange paper, glue, crayons, scissors

  • Tejas Dance Game (whole_class, 10 min)

    Dance in a circle showing bright energy, freeze in sun poses when music stops

    Materials: music player, open space

  • Nature's Bright Things (small_group, 12 min)

    Go outside and find three things in nature that shine or glow (flowers, leaves, stones) and tell how they remind you of tejas

    Materials: outdoor space, collection bags

  • Color the Brave Hearts (solo, 10 min)

    Color pictures of children showing tejas by helping others and being brave

    Materials: coloring pages, crayons

Home Practice

Each morning when you wake up, stand in front of a mirror. Put your hands on your heart and say 'Krishna, please make my heart bright today.' Then smile at yourself and imagine golden light filling your heart.

Duration: Once every morning for one week
Look for: You will feel happier and braver during the day. Your family will notice you being more helpful and cheerful.
