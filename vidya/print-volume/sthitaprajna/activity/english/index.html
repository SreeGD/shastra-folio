About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Sthitaprajna

Sthitaprajna means steady wisdom.

Worksheet

Sthitaprajna Worksheet — My Steady Heart

Activity 1: My Heart Weather (Draw-and-Tell)

Draw four small circles. In each one, draw YOUR heart as weather.

1. When I WIN a game — is my heart sunny, stormy, exploding fireworks?

2. When I LOSE a game — is my heart rainy, cloudy, a tiny earthquake?

3. When I am PRAISED — what weather is inside?

4. When I am SCOLDED — what weather is inside?

Under each circle, draw a tiny sea underneath. That sea is YOU — the steady part — no matter the weather on top.

Activity 2: The Steady Breath

Sit with legs crossed. Place one hand on your tummy. Chant with your teacher three times, slowly:

Sthi-ta-prag-nya! Sthi-ta-prag-nya! Sthi-ta-prag-nya! *Steady mind! Steady mind! Steady mind!*

With each chant, breathe in slowly (4 counts), breathe out slowly (4 counts). Feel the tummy rise and fall like a gentle wave.

Activity 3: Win-Lose Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, take turns:

  • Partner A: pretend to win a game — do a BIG celebration! Then a SMALL steady smile.
  • Partner B: pretend to lose a game — do a BIG sad cry! Then a SMALL steady smile.

Notice: which one feels better inside? Big reactions, or a small steady smile?

Activity 4: The Praise-Scold Mirror (Solo)

Look in a small mirror. Whisper these sentences:

  • 'You are the BEST student!' Keep your face steady. Smile small.
  • 'You are NOT good enough!' Keep your face steady. Smile small.
  • 'Good job.' Stay steady. 'Bad job.' Stay steady.

At the end, say: 'Krishna is inside me. He is never worried. I stay steady.'

Activity 5: Take Home — The Calm Jar

At home, fill a clear jar with water. Add one teaspoon of silver glitter. Shake it hard.

Look — the glitter is everywhere, like your feelings when big things happen! Now set the jar down. Watch. In 2-3 minutes the glitter settles to the bottom. The water becomes clear again.

That is your heart. Big feelings come — and if we sit still for a moment, they settle. Then we see clearly again.

Use the Calm Jar every time you feel stormy. Shake it. Sit with it. Wait until clear. Then decide what to say or do.

Structured Activities

  • My Heart Weather (solo, 10 min)

    Draw your heart as weather in four situations: winning, losing, being praised, being scolded. Draw a steady sea under each.

    Materials: Paper, Crayons

  • The Steady Breath (whole_class, 5 min)

    Chant sthita-prajna three times with palm on tummy, breathing in and out slowly.

  • Win-Lose Role-Play (paired, 8 min)

    With a partner, practice a BIG reaction and a SMALL steady smile for winning and losing. Notice which feels better.

  • The Praise-Scold Mirror (solo, 7 min)

    Look in a mirror. Whisper praise and scolding sentences while keeping your face steady. End with 'Krishna is inside me. I stay steady.'

    Materials: Small hand mirror

  • The Calm Jar (solo, 10 min)

    At home, make a Calm Jar with water and glitter. Use it whenever you feel a big feeling — shake, sit, watch, breathe, decide.

    Materials: Small clear bottle, Water, Silver glitter, Tight-fitting lid

Home Practice

Keep your Calm Jar on your bedside table. Every time you feel a BIG feeling — very happy, very sad, very angry, very scared — pick up the Calm Jar. Shake it. Sit cross-legged. Watch the glitter settle. Breathe slowly in and out. When the water is clear, your heart is clear too. Then decide what to say or do. Also: before bed, share ONE steady-heart moment with Amma or Appa — 'Today something happened and I stayed steady.'

Duration: Every day for two weeks
Look for: By the end of two weeks, the child can describe at least 3 moments where they felt a big emotion, used the Calm Jar or steady breath, and chose a kind response. Parents notice shorter tantrums and quicker recovery from upsets.
