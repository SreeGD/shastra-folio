About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Shaucha

Shaucha means keeping clean — our body, our clothes, our room, AND our heart.

Worksheet

Shaucha Worksheet — Clean Body, Clean Heart

Activity 1: My Daily Shaucha Checklist (Circle Yes or No)

Did you do these today?

1. Brush my teeth in the morning. YES NO

2. Wash my hands before eating. YES NO

3. Wear clean clothes. YES NO

4. Put my shoes in their place. YES NO

5. Say kind words to my family. YES NO

6. Tidy my bed or books. YES NO

Activity 2: Inside and Outside — Sort the Pictures

Your teacher will show pictures. Sort them into TWO buckets:

  • OUTSIDE SHAUCHA (bath, tidy room, clean hands, fresh clothes)
  • INSIDE SHAUCHA (kind thought, truthful word, calm breath, loving prayer)

Which bucket has more? Can one thing be in BOTH? Talk with your partner.

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, hands first at your head, then at your heart:

Shau-cha! Shau-cha! Shau-cha! *Clean body — clean heart — Krishna is near!*

Activity 4: The Temple-of-Me Drawing

Draw a small temple. Inside the temple, draw YOURSELF, clean and bright. Around the temple, draw 4 little pictures:

  • a toothbrush
  • a bar of soap
  • a broom
  • a folded cloth

At the top write: My body is Krishna's little temple.

[Drawing space]

Activity 5: Take Home — The Morning Three

For one week, each morning do these THREE things BEFORE breakfast:

1. Bath (or wash face + hands + feet).

2. Brush teeth.

3. Fold your blanket / tidy your bed.

After the third thing, say softly, 'Krishna, Your little temple is ready.' Tell Amma or Appa each night how it felt.

Some clean inside homework too: each night, say one TRUE thing and one KIND thing you spoke that day.

Structured Activities

  • My Daily Shaucha Checklist (solo, 5 min)

    Circle Yes or No for six daily shaucha actions — three outside (brush, wash, clean clothes) and three inside (tidy space, kind words, folded bed).

    Materials: Worksheet, Pencil

  • Inside and Outside Sort (paired, 8 min)

    Sort picture cards into two buckets — outside shaucha and inside shaucha. Discuss which cards could belong to both.

    Materials: Picture cards, Two labeled baskets

  • Shaucha Chant (whole_class, 3 min)

    Chant shaucha three times with hands moving from head to heart, singing 'clean body, clean heart, Krishna is near'.

  • The Temple-of-Me Drawing (solo, 10 min)

    Draw a small temple with yourself inside. Around the temple draw a toothbrush, soap, broom, and folded cloth. Title the page 'My body is Krishna's little temple.'

    Materials: Paper, Crayons, Pencils

  • The Morning Three (solo, 5 min)

    For one week, do three things every morning before breakfast — bath, brush, tidy bed. Each night also share one kind word and one true word you spoke.

    Materials: Take-home tracker

Home Practice

Each morning this week, do the Morning Three BEFORE breakfast: (1) bath or wash face-hands-feet, (2) brush teeth, (3) tidy your bed. After the third thing, touch your heart and say softly, 'Krishna, Your little temple is ready.' Then each night at bedtime, tell Amma or Appa ONE kind word you spoke today and ONE true word you said. This is your inside shaucha.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can do the Morning Three independently (without being reminded) and can name at least 3 kind-and-true words they spoke during the week.
