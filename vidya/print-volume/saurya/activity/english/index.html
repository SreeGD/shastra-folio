About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Worksheet

Saurya Worksheet — The Little Golden Shield

Activity 1: Stand Up or Walk Past? (Circle the Right Answer)

1. A smaller child is being teased in the playground. STAND UP WALK PAST

2. My friend wants me to jump off a high wall to look cool. STAND UP (say 'No, not safe') JUMP

3. I see a puppy being poked with a stick. STAND UP (say 'Stop') WALK PAST

4. Someone is copying in a test and telling me to copy too. STAND UP (say 'No') COPY

5. A classmate's lunch-box is being taken every day. STAND UP (tell teacher / speak gently) WALK PAST

Activity 2: Draw Your Little Golden Shield

In the box below, draw YOURSELF with a GOLDEN SHIELD shining on your chest. On the shield, draw three pictures: (1) a person smaller than you whom you want to protect, (2) a time you want to be brave, (3) Krishna's peacock feather — because He lends you His strength. Colour the shield bright gold and orange.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me (The Brave-Heart Chant)

Stand up tall. Press one hand on your chest. Plant both feet firmly.

Chant three times, stamping one foot on each beat:

Sau-rya! Sau-rya! Sau-rya! *Brave heart! Plant feet! Krishna stand with me!*

Activity 4: The Plant-Your-Feet Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, take turns being the 'Brave-Heart' and the 'Wobbly-Knee.' The Brave-Heart stands firm and practises saying one of these lines in a kind, clear voice (not loud, not mean):

  • 'Please do not do that. It is not kind.'
  • 'This is not your lunch-box. Please give it back.'
  • 'I will not copy. I will try my own.'
  • 'Let us ask teacher together.'
  • 'You can share. We have enough.'

The Wobbly-Knee pretends to be afraid, then watches the Brave-Heart plant their feet. Swap. Notice: does your chest feel WARMER when you plant your feet and speak kindly than when you shout or run?

Activity 5: Take Home — The Golden Shield Journal

Keep a small notebook by your bed. Each evening, draw a tiny golden shield. Under it, write or draw ONE moment today when you were BRAVE — you stood up for a smaller one, spoke the truth, tried a scary thing, or said 'no' to something wrong. At bedtime, press your hand on your chest and whisper: 'Krishna, thank You for lending me Your bravery today.'

At the end of the week, count your little shields. Each one is a place where Krishna stood beside you.

Structured Activities

  • Stand Up or Walk Past (solo, 6 min)

    Circle STAND UP or WALK PAST for each scenario. Discuss why the brave-kind choice is almost always 'stand up' — but with a gentle voice and thoughtful actions.

    Materials: Worksheet, Pencil

  • Draw Your Little Golden Shield (solo, 10 min)

    Draw yourself with a golden shield shining on your chest. On the shield, draw a smaller friend you want to protect, a brave thing you want to try, and Krishna's peacock feather — because He lends you His strength.

    Materials: Paper, Crayons, Gold and orange markers

  • The Brave-Heart Chant (whole_class, 3 min)

    Stand tall, press one hand on the chest, plant both feet, and stamp-chant 'Sau-rya! Sau-rya! Sau-rya!' three times. End with a whispered 'Krishna, stand with me.'

  • The Plant-Your-Feet Role-Play (paired, 10 min)

    With a partner, take turns being the Brave-Heart and the Wobbly-Knee. The Brave-Heart plants their feet and speaks a kind, clear, not-loud line: 'Please do not do that', 'Please give it back', 'I will not copy', 'Let us share'. Notice how the chest feels when feet are firmly planted.

    Materials: Open floor space

  • The Golden Shield Journal (solo, 5 min)

    Keep a daily journal. Each evening, press your hand on your chest, whisper 'Krishna, lend me Your bravery', draw a tiny golden shield, and name one brave-kind thing you did today. Observe how small protectors in nature (mother birds, elephants, tigresses) also guard their small ones.

    Materials: Small notebook, Pencil, Crayons

Home Practice

For one week, keep your Golden Shield Journal by your bed. Each evening, before sleep, press your small hand on your chest and whisper three times: 'Krishna, lend me Your bravery. I will stand up kindly for what is right.' Then draw a tiny golden shield in your book. Under it, write or draw ONE brave thing you did today — spoke the truth, stood up for a smaller friend, tried a scary new thing, said 'no' to something wrong, or stood up for a puppy or plant. Small shields count just as much as big shields. Tick it before you sleep.

Duration: Every evening for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can describe at least 3 specific moments when they chose to 'stand up kindly' (protect a smaller child, speak the truth when hard, say no to something wrong, or try a scary new thing). They can demonstrate the plant-feet-hand-on-chest gesture while chanting 'saurya' with a smile. The journal has at least 5 little golden shields drawn. Parents report fewer moments of the child freezing or going along silently when something was wrong.
