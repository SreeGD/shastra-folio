About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Worksheet

Satya Worksheet — My Words Match the Truth

Activity 1: True or Not True? (Circle the Face)

Look at each picture your teacher shows. If the child is telling the truth, circle the happy face 🙂. If the child is telling a lie, circle the sad face 😢.

1. A boy saying 'Yes Amma, I brushed my teeth' — after he really brushed them. 🙂 😢

2. A girl saying 'It wasn't me!' — but her hand is in the cookie jar. 🙂 😢

3. A boy saying 'I don't know' — when he really does not know. 🙂 😢

4. A girl blaming the dog for her spilled milk. 🙂 😢

5. A boy telling his teacher 'I forgot my homework' — when he really did forget. 🙂 😢

Activity 2: Draw a Light Heart / Heavy Heart

In the first box, draw a HEART that feels LIGHT — like when you tell the truth. In the second box, draw a HEART with a STONE on it — like when you hide the truth.

[Box 1: Light Heart] [Box 2: Heavy Heart]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, palms together at your heart:

Sat-yam! Sat-yam! Sat-yam! *I speak the truth! I speak the truth! I speak the truth!*

Activity 4: The Brave Truth Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, take turns. One of you is the CHILD, one is AMMA or APPA. Practice saying the brave truth:

  • 'Amma, I spilled the milk.'
  • 'Appa, I did not finish my homework.'
  • 'Teacher, I copied my friend's answer. I am sorry.'
  • 'Amma, I ate two laddus, not one.'

Use a soft voice. Look in the other person's eyes.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Truth Diary

For one week, keep a tiny 'Truth Diary' with Amma or Appa. Each night, tell them:

1. One true thing I said today.

2. One promise I kept today.

3. One time I said 'I don't know' instead of guessing.

Draw a little smile next to each one.

    For one week, each night share one true thing you said, one promise you kept, and one time you said 'I don't know'.

    Materials: Small notebook, Pencil

Home Practice

For one week, keep a Truth Diary with Amma or Appa. Each night at bedtime, share three things: (1) one true thing you said today, (2) one promise you kept, and (3) one time you said 'I don't know' instead of guessing. Draw a small smile beside each one. Also: before sleeping, place your hand on your heart and ask quietly, 'Is there anything I am hiding today?' If yes, tell someone kind tomorrow morning.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can describe at least 3 times they told a hard truth, and can demonstrate the 'stone on heart / light heart' gesture. Parents report the child volunteering truths without being asked.
