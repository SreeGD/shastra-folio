About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This Parent Book helps you reinforce at home what your child is learning at school. You do not need to read the full stories — your child will tell you! Instead, this book gives you conversation starters, signs to look for, and simple practices to do together as a family.

For each value, you will find:

  • Value at a Glance — What the value means, in plain language
  • The Verse — The scriptural foundation, in your language
  • Stories Your Child Heard — Brief summaries so you can ask about them
  • The Sanskrit Word — Ask your child to teach it to you!
  • Home Practice — The same action your child was given at school
  • Dinner Table Questions — 3–4 conversation starters for family discussion
  • Signs of Growth — Observable behaviours showing your child is internalizing the value

The most powerful teaching happens not in the classroom but at the dinner table.

Atma Nigraha

Self-control means being the boss of your own body and mind. It means you can choose to do good things even when you want to do something else.

The Verse

Bhagavad-gita 6.5: We must help ourselves with our mind. We must not let our mind make us do bad things. Our mind can be our best friend or our worst enemy.

What Atma Nigraha is NOT

Self-control is not being mean to yourself or never having fun.

  • Throwing tantrums when you don't get what you want
  • Eating too much candy and getting sick
  • Hitting others when you feel angry

Stories Your Child Heard

Prahlada and the Demons' Temptations — Little Prahlada lived in a palace full of demons. His father was the demon king. But Prahlada loved Lord Vishnu with all his heart. The demon teachers did not like this. They wanted Prahlada to be mean like them. So they tried many tricks.

Maya's Morning Choice — Maya lived in a cozy house in Mumbai with her mother and little brother. Every morning was the same problem. "Maya, time to wake up!" her mother would call. But Maya loved her warm bed. She would pull the blanket over her head and say, "No! Go away!"

The Sanskrit Word

ātma-nigraha (आत्मनिग्रह) — AHT-ma NI-gra-ha

Meaning: controlling yourself

Ask your child to teach you this word!

Home Practice

Each morning when you wake up, before getting out of bed, count to ten and take three deep breaths. Then say 'I am strong and I can control myself today.'

Duration: Every morning for one week
Look for: You will notice it becomes easier to stay calm when things don't go your way, and your family will see you being more peaceful in the mornings.

Signs of Growth

You will notice it becomes easier to stay calm when things don't go your way, and your family will see you being more peaceful in the mornings.
