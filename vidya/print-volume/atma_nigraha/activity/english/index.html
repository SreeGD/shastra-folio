About This Book

This book is part of the Vidyā Karaṇa (विद्या करण) Value Education Series, designed for the Primary (Ages 6-9) age group.

Each chapter explores one of the values from the daivī sampad (divine qualities) described in the Bhagavad Gītā verses 16.1–3. The values are presented in a carefully sequenced order, where foundational values are taught before those that build upon them.

This is the doing volume. Use it together with the Core Values reading book — read about a value first, then come here to live it.

Each chapter offers:

  • Worksheet — Drawing, writing, and reflection prompts
  • Visual Activities — Colouring pages, comic strips
  • Cultural Arts — Bhajans and art projects
  • Devanagari Practice — Hands-on Sanskrit script writing
  • Home Practice — Daily actions with observable results
  • 30-Day Challenge — A month-long journey
  • Abhyasa & Yoga — Embodied practice
  • Quiz — Self-check questions with a compact answer key

Section 2: Make a Self-Control Shield

Materials: Cardboard, crayons, stickers, glue

Steps:

1. Cut out a shield shape from cardboard

2. Write "I AM STRONG" at the top

3. Draw or stick pictures of things that help you stay calm

4. Color it with your favorite colors

5. Hold it up when you need to remember you are in control

Think while making: What makes you feel strong and calm inside?

Section 3: The Stop and Breathe Game

How to play:
  • Everyone walks around the room
  • When teacher says "STOP!" everyone freezes
  • Count to five together: "1-2-3-4-5"
  • Take three deep breaths together
  • Teacher asks: "How do you feel now?"
  • Start walking again

Play for 5 minutes

Section 4: Color the Self-Control Tree

Color this tree. Each leaf shows something you can control:
  • Your voice (loud or soft)
  • Your hands (gentle or rough)
  • Your words (kind or mean)
  • Your body (still or moving)

Structured Activities

  • Story Pictures (solo, 10 min)

    Look at pictures from the stories and circle the correct answers about how the characters showed self-control

    Materials: worksheet, crayons

  • Make a Self-Control Shield (solo, 20 min)

    Create a cardboard shield decorated with pictures and words that remind you of your inner strength and ability to stay calm

    Materials: cardboard, crayons, stickers, glue

  • The Stop and Breathe Game (whole_class, 10 min)

    Practice controlling your body by freezing when you hear 'STOP', then counting and breathing together as a class

  • Color the Self-Control Tree (solo, 15 min)

    Color a tree where each leaf represents something you can control about yourself (voice, hands, words, body)

    Materials: worksheet, crayons

Home Practice

Each morning when you wake up, before getting out of bed, count to ten and take three deep breaths. Then say 'I am strong and I can control myself today.'

Duration: Every morning for one week
Look for: You will notice it becomes easier to stay calm when things don't go your way, and your family will see you being more peaceful in the mornings.
