Value at a Glance

Shaucha means keeping clean — our body, our clothes, our room, AND our heart. Outside shaucha is washing our hands, brushing our teeth, and tidying our space. Inside shaucha is keeping our thoughts kind and our words true. Krishna loves a clean body and a clean heart — both.

The Verse

Bhagavad-gita 16.3: Shine bright. Forgive. Be patient. Stay clean — inside and outside. Do not be jealous. Do not be too proud. These are the divine treasures, Arjuna.

What it is NOT

Shaucha is not just a show — clean hands outside with mean words inside is not shaucha. It is not looking down on anyone because our clothes are new. It is not being clean only when Amma is watching.

  • Eating lunch with muddy hands because 'no one will notice'
  • Wearing a fresh clean shirt while calling a friend mean names
  • Throwing clothes and books on the floor and saying 'someone else will pick up'

Stories Your Child Heard

Prahlada's Clean Heart in the Asura Palace — Long ago, a little prince named Prahlada lived in a huge palace. His father was a mighty demon-king. The palace was big and shiny, but inside, many people had angry faces and shouting voices. Prahlada was only five years old. But he loved Krishna with his whole heart.

Meera's Morning Temple — Meera was seven. She hated two things — bathing and tidying up. Her socks lived on the fan. Her books slept on the floor. Her hair was a bird's nest. One day Amma opened her school bag and a squashed banana fell out.

The Sanskrit Word

śaucha (शौच) — SHOW-cha

Meaning: Cleanliness and purity. Keeping the body, clothes, room, and heart bright and clear. Outside clean and inside clean — both together.

Ask your child to teach you this word!

Home Practice

Each morning this week, do the Morning Three BEFORE breakfast: (1) bath or wash face-hands-feet, (2) brush teeth, (3) tidy your bed. After the third thing, touch your heart and say softly, 'Krishna, Your little temple is ready.' Then each night at bedtime, tell Amma or Appa ONE kind word you spoke today and ONE true word you said. This is your inside shaucha.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can do the Morning Three independently (without being reminded) and can name at least 3 kind-and-true words they spoke during the week.

Signs of Growth

By the end of the week, the child can do the Morning Three independently (without being reminded) and can name at least 3 kind-and-true words they spoke during the week.

When our body and heart are both clean, we naturally have the strength to say 'no' to unkind things — and that inner strength has its own beautiful name, atma-nigraha.
