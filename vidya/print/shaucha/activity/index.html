Shaucha Worksheet — Clean Body, Clean Heart

Activity 1: My Daily Shaucha Checklist (Circle Yes or No)

Did you do these today?

1. Brush my teeth in the morning. YES NO

2. Wash my hands before eating. YES NO

3. Wear clean clothes. YES NO

4. Put my shoes in their place. YES NO

5. Say kind words to my family. YES NO

6. Tidy my bed or books. YES NO

Activity 2: Inside and Outside — Sort the Pictures

Your teacher will show pictures. Sort them into TWO buckets:

  • OUTSIDE SHAUCHA (bath, tidy room, clean hands, fresh clothes)
  • INSIDE SHAUCHA (kind thought, truthful word, calm breath, loving prayer)

Which bucket has more? Can one thing be in BOTH? Talk with your partner.

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, hands first at your head, then at your heart:

Shau-cha! Shau-cha! Shau-cha! *Clean body — clean heart — Krishna is near!*

Activity 4: The Temple-of-Me Drawing

Draw a small temple. Inside the temple, draw YOURSELF, clean and bright. Around the temple, draw 4 little pictures:

  • a toothbrush
  • a bar of soap
  • a broom
  • a folded cloth

At the top write: My body is Krishna's little temple.

[Drawing space]

Activity 5: Take Home — The Morning Three

For one week, each morning do these THREE things BEFORE breakfast:

1. Bath (or wash face + hands + feet).

2. Brush teeth.

3. Fold your blanket / tidy your bed.

After the third thing, say softly, 'Krishna, Your little temple is ready.' Tell Amma or Appa each night how it felt.

Some clean inside homework too: each night, say one TRUE thing and one KIND thing you spoke that day.

🛁 🪥 🧺 🪔

Home Practice

Each morning this week, do the Morning Three BEFORE breakfast: (1) bath or wash face-hands-feet, (2) brush teeth, (3) tidy your bed. After the third thing, touch your heart and say softly, 'Krishna, Your little temple is ready.' Then each night at bedtime, tell Amma or Appa ONE kind word you spoke today and ONE true word you said. This is your inside shaucha.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can do the Morning Three independently (without being reminded) and can name at least 3 kind-and-true words they spoke during the week.
