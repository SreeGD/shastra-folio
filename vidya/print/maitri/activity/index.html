Worksheet

Maitri Worksheet — Everyone in the Circle

Activity 1: Who Would I Include? (Circle YES)

1. A new child standing alone at recess. Would I include them? YES / no

2. A child who speaks a different language at home. Would I greet them? YES / no

3. A quiet child who sits alone at lunch. Would I sit with them? YES / no

4. A child who wears different clothes. Would I smile at them? YES / no

5. A child from another village who came to our school today. Would I say hi? YES / no

Activity 2: Draw the Lotus-Circle

Draw a lotus flower with Krishna in the middle. Around the lotus, draw many children — different colours, different clothes, different hairstyles — all holding hands. Every child is a petal of Krishna's lotus.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Stand in a circle with your classmates. Hold hands. Chant three times:

Mai-trī! Mai-trī! Mai-trī! — *Every child is my friend!*

Activity 4: The Welcome Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, practice WELCOMING a new child:

  • 'Hi! I'm (your name). What's your name?'
  • 'Do you want to sit with me?'
  • 'Let me show you where we play.'
  • 'What do you like to eat?'

Swap roles. Practice until it feels natural.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Friendship Circle

This week, at school, find ONE child you don't usually play with. Say hi. Sit with them at lunch once. Share something. Draw them into your friendship circle.

At home, tell Amma or Appa their name and one thing you learned about them.

🌸 🤝 🫂

Home Practice

This week, every day at school, find ONE child you don't usually play with. Say hi. Include them in a game. Sit with them at lunch once. Keep a small notebook called My Friendship Circle. Each day, write (or draw) the name of ONE new child you included. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa one thing you learned about them. Whisper: 'Krishna, another petal in Your lotus.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By week's end, the child can name at least 5 classmates they now know beyond their original group, and can describe at least one new friendship moment with specificity.
