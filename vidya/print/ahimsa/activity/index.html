Ahimsa Worksheet — Gentle Hands, Gentle Heart

Activity 1: Kind or Unkind? (Circle-the-Face)

Look at each picture your teacher shows. If the action is gentle and kind, circle the smiling face 🙂. If it hurts someone, circle the sad face 😢.

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times with your teacher, palms together at your heart:

A-him-sā! A-him-sā! A-him-sā! *Do not hurt! Do not hurt! Do not hurt!*

Activity 4: Gentle Voice Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, practice turning an ANGRY sentence into a GENTLE one:

  • Angry: 'Give me that toy NOW!' → Gentle: 'Can I have a turn, please?'
  • Angry: 'You are stupid!' → Gentle: 'Let me help you.'
  • Angry: 'Go away!' → Gentle: 'I need some quiet time.'

Take turns. Use a soft voice.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Ant Walk

For one week, every time you walk outside, look down. Look out for ants, worms, or any small creatures on the path. Step AROUND them, never ON them. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa how many creatures you saved today.

🐜 🪱 🐛 🦋 🐞

Home Practice

For one week, do the Ant Walk. Every time you walk outside, look down. Step AROUND ants, worms, and small creatures. Tell Amma or Appa each night how many creatures you saved. Also: each time you feel angry, breathe three times before you speak or touch anyone.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can describe at least 3 times they chose gentle action over rough, and can demonstrate the three-breath pause when upset.
