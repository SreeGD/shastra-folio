Value at a Glance

Dhriti is the inner strength that helps you keep going when things get difficult. It means staying steady with your good choices and not giving up on what is right, even when you face problems or feel like quitting.

The Verse

Bhagavad-gita 16.3: Power, forgiveness, determination, cleanliness, freedom from envy and pride — these are the divine qualities, O son of Bharata, of those born with a divine nature.

What it is NOT

Dhriti is not stubbornness, where you refuse to listen to good advice or change when you are clearly wrong. It is also not pretending you do not feel scared or sad when you actually do.

  • Stubbornness - refusing to change a failing study method
  • Emotional suppression - pretending you are not worried about exams
  • Reckless endurance - continuing harmful activities without thinking

Stories Your Child Heard

Arjuna's Dawn Practice — In the royal school of Dronacharya, all the princes learned archery together. Arjuna was not the strongest or the tallest among them. His cousin Duryodhana was bigger, and his brother Bhima was more powerful. But Arjuna had something different in his heart. Every morning, while the other students slept in their warm beds, Arjuna would wake up before the sun rose. The air was cold and the ground was wet with dew. His body felt heavy with sleep, and his mind whispered, 'Just sleep a little more. You can practice later.' But Arjuna would get up anyway.

Priya's Science Project — Priya had chosen the most ambitious project for the school science fair - a working volcano model that would demonstrate how eruptions happen. She had big dreams of amazing her classmates and teachers. But when she first tested her volcano, everything went wrong. Instead of a controlled eruption, the baking soda and vinegar mixture overflowed messily all over her desk. The papier-mâché mountain collapsed, and red food coloring stained her clothes and the floor.

The Sanskrit Word

dhṛti (धृति) — DHRI-ti

Meaning: Determination, steadfastness, inner strength that sustains right action

Ask your child to teach you this word!

Home Practice

Choose one activity you have been avoiding or wanting to quit (homework, chores, practice, exercise). Do it for 10 minutes each day, even if you do not feel like it. Before you start, notice how you feel. After you finish, notice how you feel.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will notice that the feeling of not wanting to do it becomes less strong, and you will feel proud that you kept your commitment to yourself.

Signs of Growth

You will notice that the feeling of not wanting to do it becomes less strong, and you will feel proud that you kept your commitment to yourself.
