Worksheet

Dhriti Worksheet

Section 1: Story Understanding

Answer these questions about the stories you read:

1. Why did Arjuna wake up before dawn to practice archery?

2. What made Priya want to give up on her volcano project?

3. How did both Arjuna and Priya show dhriti differently from stubbornness?

4. What was the most difficult part of each character's journey?

5. How did their steady effort change them as people, not just their skills?

Section 2: What Would You Do?

Read each situation and write what choice shows dhriti:

Situation 1: You have been learning to play guitar for three months, but you still cannot play your favorite song smoothly. Your fingers hurt, and you feel frustrated. Your friend says, 'Maybe you are just not musical.' What would you do?

Situation 2: You are working on a group project, but two of your teammates are not doing their share of the work. You could complain to the teacher or do all the work yourself or give up trying. What would dhriti look like here?

Situation 3: You have been studying hard for math, but you failed the last test. You feel like you are not smart enough and want to stop trying. What would someone with dhriti do?

Section 3: Personal Reflection

Write one paragraph (4-5 sentences) about this prompt:

Describe a time when you wanted to give up on something but kept going. What made you want to quit? What helped you continue? How did you feel afterward? If you did give up, what do you wish you had done differently?

Section 4: Discussion Prep

Complete this sentence: 'One question I want to ask the class about dhriti is...'

Write your question here: ________________________________

Home Practice

Choose one activity you have been avoiding or wanting to quit (homework, chores, practice, exercise). Do it for 10 minutes each day, even if you do not feel like it. Before you start, notice how you feel. After you finish, notice how you feel.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will notice that the feeling of not wanting to do it becomes less strong, and you will feel proud that you kept your commitment to yourself.
