Worksheet

Viveka Worksheet — The Thinking Swan

Activity 1: Shreyas or Preyas? (Circle the Wise Choice)

Look at each picture. One choice is SHREYAS (good for my heart). One is PREYAS (fun right now but not wise). Circle the SHREYAS.

1. Homework first OR cartoons first? SHREYAS: ______

2. Eating one sweet OR eating the whole box? SHREYAS: ______

3. Telling Amma the truth OR hiding the broken cup? SHREYAS: ______

4. Helping a friend who fell OR laughing with other kids? SHREYAS: ______

5. Brushing teeth OR skipping because I am tired? SHREYAS: ______

Activity 2: Draw Your Thinking Swan

Draw a big white swan. Inside the swan, draw two circles — MILK (good choices) and WATER (not-wise choices). Around the swan, draw or write:

  • Two MILK choices you made this week (things that were good for your heart)
  • Two WATER things the swan decided to leave behind

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, one hand on heart, one hand at forehead:

Vi-VE-ka! Vi-VE-ka! Vi-VE-ka! *Stop. Think. Choose the true.*

Activity 4: The Two Questions Game (Paired)

With a partner, one child whispers a SITUATION. The other child puts hand on heart, takes ONE breath, and asks the two questions out loud:

1. 'Is this TRUE?'

2. 'Is this GOOD for my heart?'

Then they choose: YES-go or NO-wait.

Practice situations:

  • 'Your friend wants you to tease a classmate.'
  • 'You see a ten-rupee note on the floor, no one around.'
  • 'Amma is resting. You want to watch TV very loudly.'
  • 'Someone shares a secret that will hurt another friend.'

Switch roles after each.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Pause Pebble

Amma or Appa will help you pick a small smooth pebble. Carry it in your pocket all week. Every time you feel the fast-feeling say 'GO!' — TOUCH the pebble. Take one breath. Ask your two questions. Then decide.

At the end of the week, count how many times the pebble helped you stop and think.

🦢 🥛 💧 ✋ 🪨

Home Practice

Carry your Pause Pebble in your pocket every day for one week. Whenever you feel the fast-feeling (wanting to grab, shout, copy, or hurry), touch the pebble, take one slow breath, and ask: 'Is this TRUE? Is this GOOD for my heart?' Then choose. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa about ONE moment today when the pebble helped you pause. Whisper to your swan inside, 'Thank you for helping me see.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can name at least 3 specific moments when they paused before acting, and can demonstrate the two-question routine (hand on heart, one breath, two questions) without prompting.
