Mardava Worksheet — Soft Hands, Soft Voice, Soft Heart

Activity 1: Soft or Rough? (Circle the Right Answer)

1. My baby brother is sleeping. I want to wake him to play. SOFT (wait, or touch lightly) ROUGH (shake him hard)

2. The kitten is eating. I want to pet her. SOFT (one finger, slow) ROUGH (two hands, squeeze)

3. Amma is on a phone call. I need her attention. SOFT (wait, touch her arm lightly) ROUGH (shout 'Amma Amma Amma!')

4. My friend dropped her crayons. SOFT (help pick up gently) ROUGH (step over them)

5. The library book has a torn page. SOFT (turn pages like butterfly wings) ROUGH (flip them fast)

Activity 2: Draw Your Soft Hand

Trace your own hand on paper. Inside the hand, draw THREE things you will hold softly this week: a flower, a puppy, a baby's cheek, a grandmother's finger, a book, a butterfly. Colour them in soft warm colours — pink, pale yellow, sky blue.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me (The Soft-Voice Whisper)

Stand in a circle. Everyone lowers their voice to the QUIETEST whisper.

Whisper three times, each time softer than the last, placing one hand on the chest:

Mār-da-va... Mār-da-va... Mār-da-va... *Soft hands... soft voice... soft heart...*

Notice how the room feels after the whispers. Soft? Calm? Warm?

Activity 4: The Butterfly Touch Game (Paired)

With a partner, sit facing each other.

  • Partner A holds out one open palm.
  • Partner B places ONE finger on Partner A's palm — as softly as a butterfly landing.
  • Partner A whispers: 'Soft!' or 'Too hard.'
  • Switch roles. Practise until both partners can touch like a true butterfly.

Then try: a soft pat on the shoulder. A soft hair-stroke. A soft hand-on-back. Each time, the receiver tells the toucher: 'Soft' or 'Too hard.'

Goal: by the end, every touch is a butterfly.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Gentleness Chart

Keep a small chart on your wall. Draw 7 small boxes — one for each day.

Each day, do ONE gentle act and tick the box:

  • Monday: Pat a pet softly (or water a plant softly).
  • Tuesday: Help baby brother/sister with soft hands.
  • Wednesday: Speak in a whisper during Amma's rest.
  • Thursday: Turn a book's pages like butterfly wings.
  • Friday: Hug a grandparent gently.
  • Saturday: Hold a flower without crushing it.
  • Sunday: Tell Amma one soft thing you did this week.

At week's end, count your soft acts. Each tick is a little piece of Krishna's own mārdava inside you.

Home Practice

For one week, keep a small Gentleness Chart on the wall near your bed. Each day, do ONE gentle act with full mārdava — soft hands, soft voice, soft heart. For example: pat a pet with one soft finger; turn the pages of your storybook like butterfly wings; whisper 'good morning' to Amma when she is sleepy; water a plant without splashing; hug a grandparent slowly; hold a flower without crushing; help a younger sibling with very soft hands. Tick a box each day. Before sleep, whisper to Krishna: 'Dear Krishna, today my hands were soft. My voice was soft. My heart was soft. Like You.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can show the 'butterfly touch' gesture, can whisper 'mārdava' without prompting, and can describe at least 3 specific moments when they used soft hands, soft voice, or soft heart. The Gentleness Chart has at least 5 ticked boxes.
