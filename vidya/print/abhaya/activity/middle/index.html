Worksheet

Abhaya (Fearlessness) - Worksheet

Section 1: Story Understanding

1. Why was Abhimanyu afraid at first when he saw the enemy formation?

2. What helped Abhimanyu overcome his fear and find courage?

3. How did Kenji feel when he first saw his friend being bullied?

4. What gave Kenji the strength to stand up to the bullies?

5. What is the difference between the courage shown by Abhimanyu and Kenji versus reckless behavior?

Section 2: What Would You Do?

Read each situation and choose what shows true abhaya (fearlessness):

Situation 1: Your friend asks you to cheat on a test. You know it's wrong but worry they'll stop being your friend.

  • A) Cheat to keep your friend happy
  • B) Politely refuse and explain why cheating is wrong
  • C) Tell the teacher immediately

Situation 2: You see someone being made fun of for their appearance. You want to help but fear becoming a target.

  • A) Walk away and pretend you didn't see
  • B) Calmly stand with the person and show kindness
  • C) Fight the bullies physically

Situation 3: You made a mistake that broke something valuable. You're scared to tell the truth.

  • A) Blame someone else to avoid trouble
  • B) Honestly admit what happened and apologize
  • C) Hide the broken item and hope no one notices

Section 3: Craft Activity - Courage Shield

Materials: Cardboard, colored paper, markers, glue, scissors

Instructions:

1. Cut a shield shape from cardboard (about 8 inches tall)

2. Cover with colored paper of your choice

3. In the center, write "ABHAYA" in bold letters

4. Around the edges, draw or write 4 things that give you courage:

- A spiritual practice (like prayer or chanting) - A person who supports you - A quality you have (like kindness or honesty) - A reminder of Krishna's protection

5. Decorate with symbols that represent strength to you

6. On the back, write one fear you want to overcome

Reflection while making: As you create each section, think about how these sources of strength can help you face challenges with courage.

Section 4: Game Activity - Trust Walk Challenge

Players: Pairs (whole class) Time: 15 minutes Setup: Create a simple obstacle course using chairs, books, or tape lines

Rules:

1. One partner closes their eyes or wears a blindfold

2. The other partner guides them through the obstacle course using only their voice

3. The guide must help their partner feel safe while navigating challenges

4. Switch roles after each person completes the course

5. No physical contact allowed - only verbal guidance

Debrief Questions:

1. How did it feel to trust your partner completely?

2. What did your guide do that made you feel safe or afraid?

3. How is this like having faith in Krishna's guidance in life?

Section 5: Personal Reflection

Write one paragraph (4-5 sentences) about a time when you felt afraid but did the right thing anyway. What helped you find courage? How did you feel afterward? What would you tell a younger student who is facing a similar fear?

Section 6: Discussion Prep

Complete this sentence: "One question I want to ask the class about fearlessness is..."

Examples:

  • How can we tell the difference between brave and foolish actions?
  • What should we do when we feel scared but know something is right?
  • How does remembering Krishna help us be more courageous?

Home Practice

Each evening before bed, think of one small thing you were afraid to do that day. It could be raising your hand in class, talking to someone new, or admitting a mistake. Say a short prayer asking Krishna for courage, then plan to try that one thing the next day. Keep a simple courage journal - write down what you tried and how it felt.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will notice that small acts of courage become easier with practice, and you will feel more confident about doing what is right even when it feels scary.
