Worksheet

Abhaya (Fearlessness) - Philosophical Inquiry Worksheet

Section 1: Textual Analysis

Essay Question 1 (300-400 words): Analyze the philosophical foundation of abhaya as presented in Bhagavad-gita 16.1. How does Krishna's placement of fearlessness as the first divine quality illuminate its relationship to spiritual development? Consider the connection between fearlessness and the purification of existence (sattva-samsuddhi) mentioned in the same verse.

Essay Question 2 (300-400 words): Compare and contrast Vidura's fearlessness with Priya's courage in the modern story. What common philosophical principles underlie both characters' ability to act dharmaically despite material consequences? How does their understanding of spiritual identity inform their choices?

Section 2: Philosophical Application

Case Study Analysis: You are a final-year student who has discovered that your research supervisor has been falsifying data in studies that could affect public health policy. Speaking up could destroy your academic career and research opportunities, but remaining silent compromises scientific integrity and potentially harms society.

1. Philosophical Framework (200 words): Apply the concept of abhaya to analyze this dilemma. How would understanding your spiritual identity versus material identity influence your decision-making process?

2. Dharmic Analysis (200 words): Examine this situation through the lens of dharma versus adharma. What are the competing duties at play, and how does fearlessness help resolve apparent conflicts between personal welfare and universal principles?

3. Practical Resolution (200 words): Propose a specific course of action that demonstrates abhaya while considering practical consequences. How would you prepare yourself spiritually and practically for the potential outcomes?

Section 3: Personal Integration

Reflective Essay (400-500 words): Identify a current situation in your life where fear of material consequences (social disapproval, financial loss, academic/career setbacks) is preventing you from acting according to your deeper principles.

Address the following:

  • What specific fears are operating in this situation?
  • How might these fears be rooted in false identification with temporary roles rather than your eternal spiritual nature?
  • What would abhaya look like in practical application to this situation?
  • Design a specific practice (study, meditation, service) to cultivate the fearlessness needed to act dharmaically in this context.

Section 4: Philosophical Synthesis

Advanced Analysis (300-400 words): Examine the relationship between abhaya and surrender (saranagati). How does complete dependence on Krishna paradoxically create the ultimate independence from material circumstances? Use examples from the stories and your own understanding to explore this apparent contradiction.

Discussion Preparation: Formulate three philosophical questions about fearlessness that you would like to explore in class discussion. Each question should demonstrate sophisticated thinking about the relationship between spiritual understanding and practical courage.

Home Practice

Each morning for the next week, before checking your phone or engaging in daily activities, spend 10 minutes reading and reflecting on Bhagavad-gita 16.1. Focus specifically on the word 'abhayam' and contemplate one area of your life where fear is preventing dharmic action. Then identify one small, concrete step you can take that day to act courageously in alignment with your spiritual principles, even if it feels uncomfortable.

Duration: Daily for one week, 10 minutes each morning
Look for: You will notice increased clarity about the difference between ego-driven fears and legitimate caution, and you will have taken at least seven specific actions that demonstrate growing spiritual courage in practical situations.
