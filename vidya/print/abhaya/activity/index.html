Worksheet

Abhaya (Being Brave) - Worksheet

Section 1: Story Pictures

Look at these pictures and circle the right answer:

1. Why was Dhruva scared in the forest?

- Circle: Wild animals / Hot sun / Too much food

2. What made Dhruva feel brave?

- Circle: Chanting God's names / Eating food / Sleeping

3. How did Yuki help the kitten?

- Circle: She asked her teacher / She hid it / She ran away

Section 2: Make a Courage Shield

Materials: Cardboard, crayons, stickers, string

Steps:

1. Cut a shield shape from cardboard

2. Color it your favorite color

3. Draw or stick pictures of things that make you brave:

- Krishna's picture - Your family - Your friends - A heart for love

4. Write "Krishna protects me" at the top

5. Make two holes and tie string to wear it

While making, think: What makes you feel brave and safe?

Section 3: Brave Heart Game

Players: Whole class Time: 10 minutes

How to play:

1. Everyone sits in a circle

2. Teacher shows cards with different situations

3. If it shows being brave in a good way, stand up and say "Krishna helps me!"

4. If it shows being mean or showing off, stay sitting and shake your head

Situations:

  • Helping a friend who fell down
  • Telling the truth when you broke something
  • Being mean to smaller children
  • Asking teacher for help
  • Showing off your toys
  • Standing up for someone being teased

After the game, discuss:

  • When did you want to stand up?
  • How does it feel to do brave, good things?
  • What makes you feel safe to be brave?

Section 4: Color the Brave Devotees

Color the picture of little Dhruva praying in the forest. As you color, think about how Krishna helped him be brave.

Draw yourself doing something brave and good in the box below.

Home Practice

Each night before bed, hold your courage shield and say 'Krishna protects me' three times. Then think of one brave, good thing you want to do tomorrow.

Duration: Every night for one week
Look for: You will feel less scared about doing good things and more excited to help others.
