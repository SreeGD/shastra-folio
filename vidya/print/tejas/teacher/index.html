Chapter Overview

Tejas is a special bright light that shines from inside us when we love Krishna. It makes us brave and happy. When we have tejas, we feel strong and can help others feel good too.

The Verse: Bhagavad-gita 16.3 — Power, forgiveness, determination, cleanliness, freedom from envy and pride — these are the divine qualities, O son of Bharata, of those born with a divine nature.

Core Teaching

  1. Tejas is the bright light that shines from our heart when we love Krishna.
  2. This special light makes us brave and helps us do good things.
  3. When we have tejas, we can help others and make them happy too.

Five-Minute Introduction

Hold up a flashlight in a dark room. Turn it on and ask: 'What happens when I turn on this light?' (It brightens the room, chases away darkness) 'Krishna gives us a special light inside our hearts called tejas. When we love Krishna, this light makes us brave and bright. Today we'll learn about two children who found their inner light - Prince Prahlada and little Mia from Brazil. This light helped them be brave and help others.'

40-Minute Lesson Plan

40-Minute Lesson Plan

Opening circle & verse (5 min) → Epic story with actions (10 min) → Modern story discussion (8 min) → Sunshine heart craft (12 min) → Tejas dance game (5 min)

Two-Hour Lesson Plan

Opening & verse (10 min) → Epic story with role-play (25 min) → Modern story & discussion (20 min) → Sunshine heart craft (30 min) → Tejas dance game (15 min) → Coloring activity (15 min) → Closing circle & Sanskrit chant (5 min)

Discussion Guide

1. Question: What made Prahlada's heart bright even when his father was mean? Sample response: His love for Krishna and singing Krishna's name made him feel happy and brave inside. Guide discussion toward how spiritual practices create inner strength. 2. Question: How did Mia find courage to help Sam? Sample response: She remembered her prayer and felt Krishna's light in her heart. Help children connect daily spiritual practices to finding courage. 3. Question: When do you feel bright and brave inside? Sample response: When I pray, when I help someone, when I sing bhajans. Encourage sharing personal experiences of feeling spiritually uplifted.

Common Misconceptions

  • Misconception: Tejas means being loud or showing off. Response: Show the difference - tejas is quiet inner strength that helps others, not loud behavior that bothers others.
  • Misconception: Only special people can have tejas. Response: Every child has this light inside when they love Krishna. It's not about being the strongest or smartest - it's about having a loving heart.
