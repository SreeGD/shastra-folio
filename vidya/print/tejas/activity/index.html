Tejas Worksheet - The Bright Light Inside

Section 2: Make a Sunshine Heart (Craft Activity)

What you need: Yellow paper, orange paper, glue, crayons

Steps:

1. Cut out a big heart from yellow paper

2. Cut out sun rays from orange paper

3. Glue the rays around your heart

4. Draw Krishna's name in the middle: क्ष्ण (Krishna)

5. Color it bright and beautiful

6. Hold it up and say "My heart shines with Krishna's love!"

Think while you make: What makes your heart feel bright and happy?

Section 3: Tejas Dance Game (Movement Activity)

How to play:
  • Stand in a circle with friends
  • When music plays, dance like you have bright light inside
  • Move your arms like sun rays
  • When music stops, freeze and shine like the sun
  • Take turns being in the middle and showing your brightest pose

Talk about: How did it feel to dance with bright energy?

Section 4: Color the Brave Hearts

Color these pictures of children showing tejas:
  • A child helping someone who fell down
  • A child singing Krishna's name happily
  • A child sharing their lunch with a friend
  • A child standing up to someone being mean

Use bright, happy colors!

Home Practice

Each morning when you wake up, stand in front of a mirror. Put your hands on your heart and say 'Krishna, please make my heart bright today.' Then smile at yourself and imagine golden light filling your heart.

Duration: Once every morning for one week
Look for: You will feel happier and braver during the day. Your family will notice you being more helpful and cheerful.
