Value at a Glance

Tejas is a special bright light that shines from inside us when we love Krishna. It makes us brave and happy. When we have tejas, we feel strong and can help others feel good too.

The Verse

Bhagavad-gita 16.3: Power, forgiveness, determination, cleanliness, freedom from envy and pride — these are the divine qualities, O son of Bharata, of those born with a divine nature.

What it is NOT

Tejas is not being mean or bossy to show off. It is not being loud or rude to get attention.

  • Being a show-off
  • Being mean to look strong
  • Being lazy and dull

Stories Your Child Heard

The Bright Prince Prahlada — Long ago, there was a little prince named Prahlada. He was only seven years old. Prahlada loved Krishna very much. He would sing Krishna's name every day. He felt so happy when he sang! But Prahlada's father was a mean demon king. The demon king did not like Krishna. He got very angry when he heard Prahlada singing. 'Stop singing that name!' he shouted. But Prahlada could not stop. Because Krishna's name made his heart shine like the sun.

Mia's Brave Light — Mia was playing at the park in her town in Brazil. She loved the big trees and colorful flowers. She was having fun with her friend Sam. Then some big kids came over. They were being mean to Sam. They took his toy car. Sam started to cry. Mia felt scared. The big kids looked so strong!

The Sanskrit Word

tejas (तेजस्) — TEH-jas

Meaning: bright light, special power, inner glow

Ask your child to teach you this word!

Home Practice

Each morning when you wake up, stand in front of a mirror. Put your hands on your heart and say 'Krishna, please make my heart bright today.' Then smile at yourself and imagine golden light filling your heart.

Duration: Once every morning for one week
Look for: You will feel happier and braver during the day. Your family will notice you being more helpful and cheerful.

Signs of Growth

You will feel happier and braver during the day. Your family will notice you being more helpful and cheerful.
