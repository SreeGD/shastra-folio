Worksheet

Daya Worksheet - Being Kind and Caring

Section 1: Story Pictures

Look at these pictures and circle the ones that show daya (caring):
  • A child helping another child who fell down
  • A child sharing food with a hungry animal
  • A child being mean to a smaller child
  • A child comforting someone who is crying

Draw a picture of yourself showing daya to someone: [Drawing space]

Section 2: Kindness Garden Craft

Materials: Paper, crayons, cotton balls, glue

Steps:

1. Draw a big flower on your paper

2. In the center, draw yourself being kind

3. On each petal, draw or write one way to show daya

4. Glue cotton balls around your flower like clouds

5. Color everything with bright, happy colors

While you make it, think: What makes you feel happy when you help others?

Section 3: Animal Friends Game

How to play:

1. Everyone sits in a circle

2. One person acts like a hurt animal (limping, looking sad)

3. Others take turns showing how to help that animal

4. The "animal" says "thank you" with animal sounds

5. Take turns being different animals

After playing, talk about: How did it feel to help? How did it feel to be helped?

Section 4: Color the Caring Heart

Color this big heart with your favorite colors. Inside the heart, draw three things you can do to show daya:

1. At home: ___________

2. At school: ___________

3. With animals: ___________"

Home Practice

Each day, look for one person or animal that needs help. Do one small kind thing for them - give a hug, share food, or say kind words.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will notice that helping others makes you feel happy and warm in your heart, and others will smile more around you.
