Svadhyaya Worksheet — My Little Library Grows

Activity 1: Real Study or Pretend Study? (Circle)

1. I flip through the Gita as fast as I can to look busy. REAL PRETEND

2. I read ONE page of a Krishna story slowly and think about it. REAL PRETEND

3. I memorise a shloka only so I can win a prize at school. REAL PRETEND

4. I sit quietly at bedtime and ask my heart what I did well today. REAL PRETEND

5. I ask Dadaji a gentle question about Krishna, then listen with soft eyes. REAL PRETEND

6. I pretend I had a perfect day when I actually spoke rudely to my sister. REAL PRETEND

Activity 2: My Little Library (Draw)

In the box below, draw YOURSELF holding a little book. Inside the book, write or draw ONE new thing you learned this week — a word, a name of Krishna, a story, a verse, or a kind habit. Next to you, draw a tiny plant with ONE leaf for each day you studied. Colour it bright green.

Activity 3: Say It With Me (The Bow-Ask-Serve Chant)

Stand up tall. Place your palms together at your heart.

Chant three times — BOW on the first word, OPEN HANDS on the second, CUP HANDS on the third:

Pra-ni-PAAT! Pari-PRASHNA! Se-VAA! *I bow. I ask. I serve. Then wisdom comes!*

Activity 4: The Inside-Look Circle (Paired)

Sit with a partner on a mat. Close your eyes for ONE quiet minute. Then open and take turns:

  • 'ONE thing I did well today was...'
  • 'ONE thing I will try to do better tomorrow is...'
  • 'ONE new thing I learned this week was...'

Listen fully. Do not laugh. Do not compare. Just listen with a soft heart. Swap three times.

Activity 5: Take Home — The One-Leaf-A-Day Book

Keep a small notebook by your bed. Every evening, draw a tiny LEAF. On the leaf, write or draw:

  • ONE new thing you learned today (from Gita, from teacher, from Amma, from a story)
  • ONE thing your heart did well today
  • ONE thing you will do better tomorrow

Before sleeping, whisper: 'Krishna, my library grew one leaf today. Thank You for teaching me.'

At the end of the week, count your leaves. Each leaf is a day of svadhyaya.

Home Practice

For one week, keep your One-Leaf-A-Day Book by your bed. Every morning, AFTER brushing your teeth, open a small book — a children's Gita, a Krishna story, or even a page Amma reads to you. Read or listen to ONE little thing. Every evening, before sleep, draw a tiny leaf in your book. On the leaf, write or draw: (1) ONE new thing I learned today, (2) ONE thing my heart did well, (3) ONE thing I will do better tomorrow. Sit quietly for two minutes. Whisper: 'Krishna, my library grew one leaf today. Thank You for teaching me inside and outside.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child has drawn at least 5 leaves in their One-Leaf-A-Day Book, can name at least 3 new things they learned that week (a Krishna name, a story, a verse, a fact), and can share at least 2 honest inside-look reflections without shame or boasting.
