Worksheet

Kartavya Worksheet — My Job, Done With Joy

Activity 1: Whose Job Is It? (Match)

Draw a line from each job to the person whose kartavya it usually is.

1. Cooking dinner → Me / Amma / Papa

2. Putting my plate in the sink → Me / Amma / Papa

3. Earning money for the family → Me / Amma / Papa

4. Brushing my own teeth → Me / Amma / Papa

5. Finishing my homework → Me / Amma / Papa

6. Feeding the baby at night → Me / Amma / Papa

7. Keeping my toys tidy → Me / Amma / Papa

Activity 2: My Duty Wheel (Drawing)

Draw a big circle. Divide it into six slices like a pizza. In each slice, draw or write ONE small job that is YOUR kartavya — at home, at school, with friends, with your body, with Krishna. Colour each slice a different colour. Put this wheel on your wall.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, patting your own chest gently like a drum:

Kar-ta-vya! Kar-ta-vya! Kar-ta-vya! *My job! My job! My job — done with joy!*

Activity 4: The Boring Job Game (Paired)

With a partner, make a list of TWO 'boring' jobs you usually run away from. (Examples: tying laces, brushing teeth, putting books away.) Now take turns pretending to do each boring job like it is the BIGGEST FUN EVER — dance, sing, make funny sounds. Switch roles. Talk after: did the job really feel boring, or was it just our face?

Activity 5: Take Home — The Daily Duty Chart

Make a small chart with 7 squares, one for each day of the week. Pick THREE tiny duties that are yours: for example, making your bed, putting away your shoes, and feeding the birds or a pet. Each day you do all three without being asked, colour in that day's square. At the end of the week, count your coloured squares. Show Amma or Appa. Whisper to Krishna: 'My little jobs were for You.'

🎒 🪥 🍽️ 📚 🌼

Home Practice

Each day this week, pick THREE small duties that are yours — for example, making your bed, putting shoes on the rack, and clearing your plate. Do all three WITHOUT being asked. Colour a square on your Daily Duty Chart each night. Also: when Amma or Appa asks you to help with something, say 'Yes, coming!' and come right away — not after three reminders. At bedtime, whisper: 'Krishna, my little jobs today were for You.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child has coloured at least 5 of the 7 squares, can name their three daily duties without looking at the chart, and the parent reports at least one moment of unreminded helping.
