Tyaga Worksheet — The Open Hand

Activity 1: Let Go or Grab? (Circle the Right Answer)

1. My sister wants the last ladoo. I have already eaten two. LET GO GRAB

2. I am playing. Amma asks me to help. I want to keep playing. LET GO (and help) GRAB (the toy)

3. My friend is sad. I was about to win our game. LET GO (so friend smiles) GRAB (the win)

4. I did a good deed. Everyone is clapping. I want more claps. LET GO (of the claps) GRAB (more praise)

5. My cousin borrowed my crayon and lost it. Amma says 'forgive.' LET GO (of the anger) GRAB (and stay upset)

Activity 2: Draw Your Open Hand

In the box below, draw YOUR hand with the palm OPEN facing up. Inside the palm, draw or write ONE thing you can let go of this week — a turn, a piece of sweet, a chance to be first, a grumpy feeling. Colour the hand warm and bright.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times with hands opening from fist to open palm:

Tyā-ga! Tyā-ga! Tyā-ga! *I let go with joy!*

Activity 4: The Last Ladoo Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, practice the 'Last Ladoo Moment'. One child holds a pretend sweet. The other child approaches. Partner 1 must choose:

  • Grab face: 'MINE!' (frown)
  • Tyaga face: 'You take it' (smile + open palm)

Swap. Try it with a turn on the swing. Try it with the best crayon. Notice how your heart feels each way.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Letting-Go Stone

Find a small smooth stone. Keep it in your pocket or on your desk. Every time you let go of something happily this week — a turn, a sweet, a need to win, a need to be right — hold the stone and whisper 'Tyaga'. At the end of the week, tell Amma or Appa three times the stone made you happy.

🪨 🤲 🌸 🪷

Home Practice

For one week, carry your Letting-Go Stone. Every day, find ONE moment to let go with a smile — a turn, the biggest piece, a toy, your need to be first, your need to be right. Hold the stone and whisper 'Tyaga'. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa what you let go of today. Whisper, 'Krishna, I let this go for You.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can describe at least 3 specific moments of happy letting-go, and can demonstrate the fist-to-open-palm gesture while saying 'tyaga' with a smile.
