Worksheet

Pratijna Worksheet — My Word is My Strength

Activity 1: Promise Kept or Promise Broken? (Circle the Right One)

1. I said I would share my tiffin with Meera. At lunch, I shared it gladly. KEPT BROKEN

2. I promised Amma I would finish my homework before TV. I watched TV first. KEPT BROKEN

3. I told my friend I would return his eraser. Next morning I brought it. KEPT BROKEN

4. I said 'I promise!' to feed the puppy. I forgot for two days. KEPT BROKEN

5. I promised Appa I would water the tulsi plant every evening. I watered it all week. KEPT BROKEN

Activity 2: Draw Your Promise Wall

In the box below, draw a wall made of BRICKS. On each brick, write or draw ONE small promise you will keep this week — water a plant, brush your teeth, greet Amma nicely, finish homework. Colour the wall strong and beautiful. Remember: each kept promise is one brick. The more you keep, the taller your wall grows.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Stand tall. Place one hand over your heart. Chant three times with a strong, kind voice:

Pra-tij-ñā! Pra-tij-ñā! Pra-tij-ñā! *My word is my strength!*

Activity 4: The Two Voices Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, practice a morning like Arjun's. One child is the SLEEPY VOICE: 'It's cold — forget the promise!' The other is the TRUE VOICE: 'I gave my word — I will keep it.' Take turns. After each round, share: which voice felt LIGHTER in the chest?

Activity 5: Take Home — The Promise Ribbon

Ask Amma or Appa for a small ribbon. Tie it loosely around your wrist. This is your Promise Ribbon. Every time you make a small promise this week, touch the ribbon and say 'pratijna'. Every time you KEEP that promise, tell Amma at bedtime. If you forget one — do not throw the ribbon away! A real vow-keeper tries again tomorrow.

🏔️ 🪷 🤝

Home Practice

For one week, wear your Promise Ribbon. Every morning, make ONE small promise to yourself or your family — 'Today I will water the tulsi.' 'Today I will greet Dadi kindly.' 'Today I will finish my homework before play.' Touch the ribbon and whisper 'pratijna'. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa which promise you kept today. If you forgot, say 'Tomorrow I will try again.' On Sunday, count how many promises you kept — and whisper to Krishna, 'My word is Yours.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can name at least 3 specific small promises they kept. They begin to pause before promising — saying 'Let me think first' — and can demonstrate the hand-on-heart pratijna gesture with quiet strength.
