Shanti Worksheet — Calm Hands, Calm Voice, Calm Heart

Activity 1: Loud or Calm? (Sort)

Your teacher will show you pictures. Put a 🔔 next to the LOUD ones and a 🌸 next to the CALM ones.

1. A child shouting at a sibling. 🔔 🌸

2. A child sitting quietly in prayer. 🔔 🌸

3. A child stamping feet on the ground. 🔔 🌸

4. A child breathing slowly under a tree. 🔔 🌸

5. A child closing eyes and chanting softly. 🔔 🌸

Activity 2: The Shanti Breath (Whole Class)

Sit with your back straight. Close your eyes. Place one hand on your heart.

  • Breathe IN slowly while we count: 1... 2... 3...
  • Breathe OUT slowly while we count: 1... 2... 3...
  • Whisper: "Shān-ti, shān-ti, shān-ti."

Repeat three times. Open your eyes. How does your heart feel inside?

Activity 3: Draw Your Quiet Place

Everyone has a quiet place — a mango tree, a corner of a room, a spot next to Amma. Draw YOUR quiet place. Put yourself in it with a calm face.

[Drawing space]

Activity 4: The Noisy Wave (Paired)

With a partner, take turns:

1. One child pretends to be "the loud world" — clapping, tapping the desk, making funny noises (gently).

2. The other child sits still with eyes closed, hands on heart, whispering "Krishna, Krishna."

3. After one minute, swap places.

Talk together afterward: What felt different inside when you were the quiet one?

Activity 5: Take Home — The Shanti Corner

With Amma or Appa, pick one small corner in your home. Put a cushion there. Maybe a picture of Krishna, a flower, or a small bell. Every evening, sit there for TWO QUIET MINUTES. Breathe slowly. Whisper "shanti." Let your heart become calm.

At the end of the week, tell your teacher how your Shanti Corner feels.

🌸 🕉️ 🪷

Home Practice

Each evening, sit in your Shanti Corner for TWO MINUTES with Amma or Appa. Place your hand on your heart. Breathe slowly — in for 3 counts, out for 3 counts. Whisper 'Krishna, Krishna, Krishna.' Notice how your heart feels after. Also: whenever you feel a big feeling (angry, sad, too-excited), remember the three calms — calm hands, calm voice, calm heart — and take three slow breaths before you do anything else.

Duration: Every evening for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can sit still for two full minutes, describes their Shanti Corner with affection, and can demonstrate the three-breath pause when asked — and at least once initiates it on their own during a loud moment.
