Udyama Worksheet — The Little Sun Inside

Activity 1: Begin or Wait? (Circle the Right Answer)

1. Amma says 'Please help set the table.' I am reading a book. BEGIN WAIT

2. My homework is on the table. I have just come home from school. BEGIN (little bits) WAIT

3. Teacher says 'Let's all clean up the classroom.' Everyone stands still. BEGIN (first!) WAIT (for others)

4. My puzzle has 20 pieces. I have only fit 2. I want to quit. BEGIN (again, one piece) WAIT (give up)

5. My little sister has spilled water. No one is helping yet. BEGIN (fetch a cloth) WAIT

Activity 2: Draw Your Little Sun

In the box below, draw YOURSELF with a GOLDEN SUN shining inside your chest. Around the sun, draw three things you will BEGIN this week — reading a book, helping Amma, drawing a picture, trying a cartwheel, planting a seed. Colour the sun bright yellow and orange.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me (The Bright-Eyes Chant)

Stand up tall. Open your eyes WIDE. Put one hand on your chest.

Chant three times, hopping on each word:

Ud-ya-MA! Ud-ya-MA! Ud-ya-MA! *Bright eyes! Happy start! I BEGIN!*

Activity 4: The One-More-Try Game (Paired)

With a partner, take turns:

  • Partner A tries to balance a small book on their head. It falls. Partner B says: 'One more try!' Partner A tries again.
  • Partner A tries to clap exactly 10 times with eyes closed. They lose count. Partner B says: 'One more try!'
  • Partner A tries to hop on one foot to the door. They wobble. Partner B says: 'One more try!'

Switch. Notice: does your heart feel LIGHTER when a friend says 'One more try' than when someone says 'Give up'?

Activity 5: Take Home — The Sunrise Journal

Keep a small notebook by your bed. Each morning, BEFORE anyone else wakes you — try to wake yourself. Sit up. Draw a tiny sun in your book. Under the sun, write or draw ONE thing you will BEGIN today — brush teeth, make your bed, help Papa, start homework. At night, tick it if you did it.

At the end of the week, count your tiny suns. Each one is a little udyama flag planted on your mountain.

☀️ 🌱 🏹 🙏

Home Practice

For one week, keep your Sunrise Journal by your bed. Each morning, try to wake yourself before Amma calls — the little sun should wake you. Sit up, draw a tiny sun in your book, and write or draw ONE thing you will BEGIN today — brush teeth by yourself, help Papa with breakfast, start homework without being asked, water a plant, tidy your toys. At bedtime, tick it if you did it. Before sleeping, whisper: 'Krishna, my little sun shone today. Thank You for lighting me up.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can describe at least 3 mornings when they woke themselves or began a task without being told, and can demonstrate the bright-eyes-hop gesture while chanting 'udyama' with a smile. The journal has at least 5 little suns drawn.
