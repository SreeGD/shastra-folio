Vairagya Worksheet — The Soft Hand, The Full Heart

Activity 1: Clinging or Soft Hand? (Circle)

Look at each picture your teacher shows. If the child is CLINGING (squeezing too tight, crying for days), circle the tight fist ✊. If the child is holding with a SOFT HAND (enjoying, then letting go), circle the open palm 🤲.

1. A child hugs her new doll, then shares her with a little sister. ✊ 🤲

2. A boy screams for an hour because his balloon popped. ✊ 🤲

3. A girl laughs when the kite flies away — 'Bye, kite! Go to Krishna.' ✊ 🤲

4. A boy will not go to school because his friend moved to another city. ✊ 🤲

5. A child says 'Thank you, Krishna' when she finishes her favourite ice cream. ✊ 🤲

Activity 2: Draw Your Soft Hand

Draw YOUR hand holding something you love — a toy, a puppy, a friend. Draw it OPEN, like you are gently showing Krishna. Under the drawing, write: "I love this — and it belongs to Krishna."

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, opening your palms from your heart outward like a flower:

Vai-RAG-ya! Vai-RAG-ya! Vai-RAG-ya! *My hand is soft. My heart is full. Krishna holds everything.*

Activity 4: The Soft-Hand Swap (Paired)

With a partner, sit facing each other. Each of you holds ONE small thing you like — a crayon, a sticker, a pencil.

  • Round 1: Squeeze it VERY tight. Frown. Think, 'MINE, MINE, MINE.' How does your hand feel? How does your face feel?
  • Round 2: Open your hand slowly. Smile softly. Whisper, 'For Krishna.' Pass the thing to your partner. How do you feel now?
  • Round 3: Your partner passes it back. You say, 'Thank you, Krishna.'

Which felt lighter — the tight way, or the soft way?

Activity 5: Take Home — The Letting-Go Shelf

With Amma or Appa, pick ONE shelf or one box at home. Call it the 'Krishna Shelf.' Each day this week, put ONE thing on it that you are ready to let go of — a toy you have outgrown, a drawing you want to give, a sweet you want to share.

At the end of the week, find someone who needs each thing. Give them away with a smile. Whisper: 'Krishna, these were Yours. I return them.'

🤲 🪷 🐘 🌿

Home Practice

Set up a Letting-Go Shelf at home — a small corner or box called 'Krishna's Shelf.' Each day this week, place ONE thing on it that you are ready to release gently — a toy you have outgrown, a drawing to gift, a sweet to share, a kind word for someone. At the end of the week, give each thing away with a smile. Also: whenever something you love breaks, gets lost, or has to go, take three slow breaths, put your hand on your heart, and whisper: 'Krishna, this was Yours. I return it with love.' Notice the soft warmth that stays inside.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the Letting-Go Shelf has at least 5 items ready to give, and the child can demonstrate the soft-hand gesture and the three-breath whisper when something small does not go their way — at least once without a reminder.
