Value at a Glance

Bhakti is pure love for Krishna shown through service. It means doing everything with love for God in your heart. When you offer your actions to Krishna, even simple things become spiritual.

The Verse

Bhagavad-gita 9.34: Always think of Me, become My devotee, worship Me and bow to Me. When you do this with love, you will surely come to Me.

What it is NOT

Bhakti is not doing religious things just to get rewards or look good. It is not fake devotion done only when others are watching.

  • Praying only when you want something
  • Acting religious to impress others
  • Following rules without love in your heart

Stories Your Child Heard

Arjuna's Choice on the Battlefield — Prince Arjuna stood on the great battlefield of Kurukshetra. His chariot driver was Krishna himself. As Arjuna looked across the field, his heart sank. He saw his own cousins, teachers, and uncles ready to fight against him. 'Krishna, I cannot do this,' Arjuna said. His hands shook as he dropped his famous bow. 'How can I fight people I love? Even if I win this war, what joy will I have?'

Priya's Music Offering — Priya sat in her room in Mumbai, holding her harmonium. The school talent show was next week, and she wanted to sing a beautiful bhajan about Krishna. But every time she thought about performing on stage, her stomach felt tight with worry. 'What if people laugh at my devotional song?' she thought. 'What if they think it's too religious? What if I forget the words?'

The Sanskrit Word

bhakti (भक्ति) — BHAK-ti

Meaning: Devotion, loving service, divine love

Ask your child to teach you this word!

Home Practice

Each evening before dinner, spend two minutes thinking about your day. Choose one thing you did and imagine offering it to Krishna with love. Say quietly: 'Krishna, I offer this to You.' Notice how this changes how you feel about that activity.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will start to feel more peaceful and happy about your daily activities, and you may find yourself naturally thinking of Krishna during the day.

Signs of Growth

You will start to feel more peaceful and happy about your daily activities, and you may find yourself naturally thinking of Krishna during the day.
