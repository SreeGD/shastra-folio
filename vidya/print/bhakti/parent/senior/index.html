Value at a Glance

Bhakti is pure loving service to Krishna performed without any desire for personal gain. It is the highest spiritual path that transforms all activities into offerings of love, connecting the soul directly with the Supreme Lord through devotion and surrender.

The Verse

Bhagavad-gita 18.55: One can understand Me as I am, as the Supreme Personality of Godhead, only by devotional service. And when one is in full consciousness of Me by such devotion, he can enter into the kingdom of God.

What it is NOT

Bhakti is not mere ritual worship, emotional attachment to material things, or spiritual practice done for personal benefits like health, success, or recognition.

  • Karma-misra bhakti - devotional service mixed with desires for material rewards
  • Mechanical ritualism - performing religious activities without love or understanding
  • Spiritual pride - using devotional knowledge to feel superior to others

Stories Your Child Heard

Prahlada's Unshakable Devotion — Prahlada was no ordinary child. Born as the son of Hiranyakashipu, the asura emperor who had conquered the three worlds and outlawed the very name of Vishnu, the young prince was an embodied paradox. From his earliest years, while still in his mother's womb listening to the sage Narada's teachings, his heart had turned irrevocably toward Krishna. By the age of five, he was already a paramahaṁsa — a swan among devotees — though dressed in the ornaments of a prince of demons. The ethical tension in Prahlada's life was immense. His father was not merely an antagonist; he was the dharmic figure to whom a son owes obedience and reverence. Yet Hiranyakashipu's commands were, at their root, an assault on truth itself: he demanded that his son worship him as God and renounce the Supreme. Prahlada faced the deepest possible conflict between two dharmas — duty to father and duty to the Absolute. He resolved it the only way bhakti can: by recognizing that genuine love for one's father includes refusing to follow him into illusion. He never grew bitter, never spoke harshly. He simply could not stop chanting the names of Hari.

Arjun's Choice at the Music Competition — Arjun had been preparing for the state music competition for two years. His violin skills were exceptional, and everyone expected him to win. But as the competition approached, he felt increasingly anxious and empty despite his talent. The night before the competition, Arjun visited his grandmother, who was staying at a local care home. He brought his violin, planning to practice one final time. But when he arrived, he found the elderly residents looking sad and lonely. Without thinking, Arjun began playing soft, beautiful melodies.

The Sanskrit Word

bhakti (भक्ति) — BHAK-ti

Meaning: Pure loving devotional service to the Supreme Lord, performed without material desires

Ask your child to teach you this word!

Home Practice

Each morning before breakfast, spend 5 minutes offering your day's activities to Krishna. Mentally go through your planned activities (school, meals, interactions, study) and consciously dedicate each one as devotional service. End by expressing gratitude for the opportunity to serve.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will notice increased mindfulness and purpose in daily activities, less anxiety about results, and a growing sense that ordinary moments can be sacred.

Signs of Growth

You will notice increased mindfulness and purpose in daily activities, less anxiety about results, and a growing sense that ordinary moments can be sacred.
