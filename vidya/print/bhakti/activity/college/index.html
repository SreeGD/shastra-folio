Worksheet

Bhakti (Devotion) - College Level Worksheet

Section 1: Textual Analysis and Synthesis

1. Comparative Analysis: Compare the devotional approaches demonstrated by Sudama and Dr. Elena. How do their different starting points (poverty vs. privilege, traditional vs. academic background) lead to similar realizations about the nature of pure devotion?

2. Philosophical Integration: Analyze how Bhagavad Gita 18.55 establishes bhakti as both the means and the goal of spiritual realization. How does this verse resolve the apparent paradox that one needs devotion to know Krishna, but knowledge of Krishna to develop devotion?

3. Anti-Pattern Recognition: Identify three specific ways that contemporary spiritual seeking might fall into the anti-patterns of karma-misra devotion or dry intellectualism. Provide concrete examples and suggest how each could be transformed into pure bhakti.

Section 2: Devotional Design Challenge (Team Activity - 30 minutes)

Materials: Large paper sheets, colored markers, sticky notes, timer

Challenge: Working in teams of 3-4, design a "Bhakti Integration Framework" for a specific professional field (medicine, education, business, arts, technology, etc.). Your framework should show how the nine processes of devotional service (śravaṇaṁ, kīrtanaṁ, smaraṇaṁ, pāda-sevanam, arcanaṁ, vandanaṁ, dāsyaṁ, sakhyaṁ, ātma-nivedanam) can be authentically integrated into that profession without compromising professional excellence.

Process:

1. Choose your professional field and research its core values and challenges

2. Map each of the nine devotional processes to specific professional activities

3. Design visual representations showing the integration

4. Identify potential obstacles and solutions

5. Present your framework to the class for feedback

Reflection Questions:

  • How does devotional consciousness enhance rather than conflict with professional competence?
  • What would change in your chosen field if practitioners approached their work as devotional service?

Section 3: Philosophical Dialogue Game - "The Bhakti Symposium"

Setup: Class divides into four philosophical schools: Advaita Vedanta, Sāṅkhya-Yoga, Karma-Yoga, and Bhakti-Yoga. Each group researches their position on the ultimate goal of spiritual life and the most effective means to achieve it.

Rules:

  • Round 1: Each school presents their core thesis (5 minutes each)
  • Round 2: Cross-examination - schools question each other's positions
  • Round 3: Bhakti-Yoga school demonstrates how their path includes and transcends the others
  • Round 4: Open dialogue on synthesis and integration

Debrief Questions:

  • How does bhakti relate to and fulfill the goals of other yoga systems?
  • What unique contribution does the devotional path make to human spiritual development?
  • How can understanding other paths deepen rather than diminish devotional practice?

Section 4: Contemplative Research Essay

Prompt: "The Paradox of Surrender: How Devotional Self-Offering Leads to Ultimate Self-Realization"

Drawing from the Bhagavad Gita, Srimad Bhagavatam, and contemporary examples, explore how the apparent contradiction between self-surrender and self-realization is resolved in bhakti-yoga. Your essay should address:

  • The philosophical distinction between false ego and authentic self
  • How devotional surrender differs from mere submission or dependency
  • The role of divine grace in spiritual transformation
  • Contemporary applications of devotional principles in personal and professional life

Length: 1000-1200 words Sources: Minimum 3 primary texts and 2 contemporary applications Format: Academic essay with proper citations

Section 5: Personal Integration Project

Choose one of the following:

Option A - Devotional Practice Design: Create a personalized daily practice that integrates the nine processes of devotional service into your current life circumstances. Document your practice for one week, noting challenges, insights, and transformations. Submit a reflective analysis of how devotional consciousness affects your relationships, work, and inner state.

Option B - Service Learning Project: Identify a community need and design a service project that embodies devotional principles. Your project should demonstrate how selfless service can be both practically effective and spiritually transformative. Include planning documents, implementation timeline, and reflection on the devotional dimensions of service.

Option C - Comparative Study: Research how devotional traditions in other religious contexts (Christian mysticism, Sufi practice, Buddhist devotion, etc.) parallel or differ from Vaishnava bhakti. Create a presentation that honors the unique contributions of each tradition while identifying universal principles of devotional transformation.

Section 6: Synthesis and Application

Final Reflection: Write a one-page response to this question: "How has studying bhakti changed your understanding of the relationship between love, knowledge, and spiritual realization? What specific practices or perspectives will you carry forward into your life and work?"

Class Discussion Preparation: Come prepared to share one insight from your study of bhakti that challenged your previous assumptions about spirituality, success, or the nature of fulfillment.

Home Practice

Each morning upon waking, spend 5 minutes offering your day to Krishna before checking any devices or beginning daily activities. Sit quietly, express gratitude for the gift of life, and mentally offer all your planned activities as service. Throughout the day, pause before significant activities (meals, work tasks, conversations) and briefly remember this offering intention.

Duration: Daily for two weeks
Look for: You will notice increased mindfulness, reduced anxiety about outcomes, and a growing sense that your life has spiritual purpose beyond material achievement. Daily activities will begin to feel more meaningful and connected to something greater than personal ambition.
