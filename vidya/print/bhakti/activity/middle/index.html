Worksheet

Bhakti Worksheet

Section 1: Story Understanding

1. Why was Arjuna confused on the battlefield?

2. What did Krishna teach Arjuna about doing his duty?

3. How did Priya's feelings change when she decided to sing for Krishna?

4. What happened when Priya sang with bhakti at the talent show?

5. How are Arjuna and Priya's situations similar?

Section 2: What Would You Do?

Read each situation and write what a person with bhakti would do:

Situation 1: Your friend asks you to help with homework, but you want to watch TV. *Bhakti response:* _______________

Situation 2: You're nervous about giving a presentation at school. *Bhakti response:* _______________

Situation 3: Your little brother breaks your favorite toy by accident. *Bhakti response:* _______________

Section 3: Craft Activity - Devotion Flower Garland

Materials: Colored paper, scissors, glue, string, markers

Instructions:

1. Cut out 8 flower shapes from different colored paper.

2. On each flower, write one way you can show bhakti in daily life.

3. Decorate each flower with beautiful patterns and colors.

4. Make a small hole at the top of each flower.

5. Thread string through the holes to make a garland.

6. Hang your garland in your room as a reminder of devotional service.

7. Each morning, pick one flower and try to practice that form of bhakti.

Reflection while making: As you create each flower, think about how that action can be an offering to Krishna.

Section 4: Game Activity - Bhakti Circle

Players: 6-20 students Time: 10 minutes Setup: Stand in a circle

Rules:

1. One person starts by saying "I offer my [action] to Krishna" (example: "I offer my studying to Krishna").

2. The next person repeats the first offering and adds their own.

3. Continue around the circle, with each person repeating all previous offerings and adding one new one.

4. If someone forgets an offering, the whole group helps them remember.

5. End when everyone has shared twice.

Debrief Questions:

  • Which offerings were easy to remember? Why?
  • How did it feel to hear everyone's devotional offerings?
  • What new ideas did you get for your own bhakti practice?

Section 5: Personal Reflection

Write one paragraph about a time when you did something with love and care (like helping family, being kind to animals, or sharing with friends). How could you do similar actions as offerings to Krishna? What would change in your heart?

Section 6: Discussion Prep

Complete this sentence: "One question I want to ask the class about bhakti is..."

Write your question here: _______________

Home Practice

Each evening before dinner, spend two minutes thinking about your day. Choose one thing you did and imagine offering it to Krishna with love. Say quietly: 'Krishna, I offer this to You.' Notice how this changes how you feel about that activity.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: You will start to feel more peaceful and happy about your daily activities, and you may find yourself naturally thinking of Krishna during the day.
