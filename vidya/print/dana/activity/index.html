Worksheet

Dana Worksheet — The Happy Heart of Giving

Activity 1: Share or Keep? (Circle the Right Answer)

1. My friend has no pencil. I have three pencils. SHARE KEEP

2. A hungry puppy is by the gate. I have a biscuit. SHARE KEEP

3. My last candy. It is the one I love most. SHARE KEEP (if someone asks)

4. My old shirt that still fits a younger child. SHARE KEEP

5. My toy that I am still playing with. My sister is crying for a turn. SHARE for a turn KEEP

Activity 2: Draw Your Giving Tree

Draw a big tree. On each leaf, write or draw ONE thing you can give this week. Examples: a crayon, a kind word, help with dishes, a smile, a prayer. Colour each leaf a different colour.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, palms cupping outward as if offering:

Dā-na! Dā-na! Dā-na! *I give with joy!*

Activity 4: The Giving Circle (Whole Class)

Sit in a big circle. Each child places ONE thing in the centre — a pencil, a sticker, a drawing, anything small. When everyone has given, take turns picking up ONE gift (not your own). Say 'Thank you, Krishna' as you receive.

Feel how it feels to give AND to receive.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Giving Jar

At home, keep a small jar. Each time you give something — food, a kind word, a chore, a prayer — drop a small pebble into the jar. At the end of the week, count your pebbles. That is your gift to Krishna.

🪷 🤲 🌳 🍬

Home Practice

Each day this week, do ONE act of dana — big or small. Give a crayon to a friend. Help Amma with a chore without being asked. Share your snack. Say a kind word. Drop a pebble in your Giving Jar for each act. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa what you gave today. Whisper, 'Krishna, I gave this for You.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the Giving Jar has at least 7 pebbles, and the child can tell one specific story of giving that made someone else happy.
