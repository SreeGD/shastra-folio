Worksheet

Kritajnata (Gratitude) Worksheet

Section 1: Story Understanding

1. Why did Vidura speak against King Dhritarashtra even though the king had been kind to him?

2. What made Kenji choose to learn cooking instead of playing soccer with his friends?

3. How did both Vidura and Kenji show real gratitude through their actions?

4. What happened because Vidura and Kenji chose to act on their gratitude?

5. Name one person in your life who has helped you grow. What could you do to show them real appreciation?

Section 2: What Would You Do?

Read each situation and write what you would do:

Situation 1: Your teacher has been staying after school to help you with math for free. Now she asks if you can help clean the classroom. Your friends are waiting outside to play. *What would you do?*

Situation 2: Your parents work extra hours to pay for your music lessons. You want to quit because practice is boring, but you know they sacrifice for this. *What would you do?*

Situation 3: An older student has been walking you safely to school every day. Now some classmates are making fun of you for needing help. *What would you do?*

Section 3: Gratitude Garden Craft

Materials needed: Green paper, colored markers, scissors, glue, small pot or container

Instructions:

1. Cut leaf shapes from green paper (at least 10 leaves)

2. On each leaf, write the name of someone who helps you and one specific thing they do

3. Draw small flowers or fruits on some leaves to show the "fruits" of their kindness

4. Arrange the leaves in your container like a growing plant

5. Add a small sign that says "My Gratitude Garden"

6. Place it somewhere you'll see it every day

While making, think about: How does it feel to list all the people who help you? What surprises you about your list?

Section 4: The Thank You Chain Game

Players: 4-15 people Time: 10-15 minutes

How to play:

1. Sit in a circle

2. First player says: "I'm grateful for [someone] because they [specific action]"

3. Next player must connect their gratitude to the previous person's: "That reminds me to be grateful for [someone] because they [specific action]"

4. Continue around the circle, making connections

5. If someone can't think of a connection, the group helps them find one

Debrief questions:

  • Which gratitude surprised you most?
  • How did hearing others' gratitude change how you think about your own life?
  • What connections between different types of help did you notice?

Section 5: Personal Reflection

Write one paragraph (5-7 sentences) about this prompt:

Think of something you use every day that you usually don't think about - like clean water, electricity, or your bed. Trace it back to all the people who made it possible for you to have this. How does this change how you feel about this everyday thing?

Start your paragraph with: "I never really thought about..."

Section 6: Discussion Prep

Complete this sentence to prepare for class discussion:

"One question I want to ask the class about gratitude is: ________________________________"

Examples: "How can we show gratitude when we're angry at someone?" or "Is it possible to be too grateful?"

Home Practice

Each evening before dinner, tell one family member about someone who helped you that day and how it made you feel. Then ask them to share about someone who helped them. Keep a simple list of all the helpers you both mention.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, you will have a list of at least 14 different people who helped your family, and you'll notice helpers you used to overlook.
