Worksheet

Nishkama Karma Worksheet — The Quiet Lamp

Activity 1: For the Prize or For the Right? (Circle)

For each sentence, circle the little star ⭐ if the child is doing the right thing for a REWARD, and the little lamp 🪔 if they are doing it because it is RIGHT.

1. Rohan picks up a pencil from the floor before the teacher sees him. 🪔 ⭐

2. Deepa shares her lunch only after Amma says 'You will get a new doll.' ⭐ 🪔

3. Anu finishes homework carefully even though there are no stars this week. 🪔 ⭐

4. Raj helps Dadaji across the room and then runs to Amma, 'Did you SEE me?' ⭐ 🪔

5. Meera cleans the art class in silence and never tells anyone. 🪔 ⭐

Activity 2: My Quiet Lamp

Draw a beautiful little lamp (diya). Inside the flame, write or draw ONE kind thing you can do this week WITHOUT telling anyone. Around the lamp, write: karmaṇy evādhikāras te — my work is mine, but the fruit is Krishna's.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Stand up. Put both hands on your heart. Chant three times, like a steady bell:

Nish-kaa-ma Kar-ma! Nish-kaa-ma Kar-ma! Nish-kaa-ma Kar-ma! *I do my work with love. I do not grab the reward.*

Activity 4: The Secret Kindness Role-Play (Paired)

With a partner, act out TWO ways of doing a kind thing.

  • Way 1 (Loud): Partner A picks up a dropped book. They shout, 'LOOK! I picked it up! Somebody clap!'
  • Way 2 (Quiet): Partner A picks up the book, smiles softly, puts it on the desk, and walks on.

Swap. Both of you try both ways. Which way feels bigger inside? Which way feels smaller?

Activity 5: Take Home — The Quiet Lamp Jar

At home, find a clean glass jar. With Amma or Appa, stick a paper label that says My Quiet Lamp. Every day this week, do ONE kind thing that NOBODY knows about. No telling, no hinting, no 'did you see?' Put ONE small pebble or button into the jar for every secret kindness. At the end of the week, look at your jar. Count the pebbles only in your heart. Whisper: 'Krishna, these are Yours.'

🪔 ✋ 🌾 🕯️

Home Practice

For one week, keep your Quiet Lamp Jar on your desk or shelf. Every day, do ONE kind thing that NOBODY knows about — tidy a shoe, fold a napkin, refill the water jug, help a sibling silently, put away a toy that is not yours. For each secret kindness, drop ONE pebble or button into the jar. Do not count in front of others. Do not tell. At bedtime, close your eyes and whisper: 'Krishna, these pebbles are Yours. The work was for You.' At the end of the week, empty the jar by pouring the pebbles back into the garden — they belonged to Krishna, not to you.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can describe at least 3 specific quiet kindnesses performed without announcing them, demonstrates the hands-on-heart gesture while saying 'nishkama karma', and has not asked for praise or acknowledgment for the jar's contents.
