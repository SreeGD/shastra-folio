Yajna Worksheet — Every Little Thing is an Offering

Activity 1: Yajna or Just Me? (Circle the Offering)

Read each sentence. If the child is turning the action into yajna, circle the 🪔 (offering lamp). If they forgot to offer, circle the 🙃 (upside-down face).

1. Before eating, Rohan whispers 'Krishna, for You' and takes one bite. 🪔 🙃

2. Deepa sings a bhajan loudly so everyone claps for HER. 🙃 🪔

3. Before school, Anu places her bag on the altar and bows. 🪔 🙃

4. Raj sweeps the yard grumpily and complains the whole time. 🙃 🪔

5. Meera holds her Maths notebook on her heart before doing homework. 🪔 🙃

Activity 2: My Yajna Plate

Draw a big round plate. Inside the plate, draw FOUR things you can offer to Krishna this week — a flower you find, a kind word, a chore well done, a song. Around the edge, write the word यज्ञ — yajña with your best effort.

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me

Chant three times, palms joined and lifted up like an offering:

Yaj-ña! Yaj-ña! Yaj-ña! *Krishna, this is for You!*

Activity 4: The Offering Bell (Whole Class)

Sit in a circle with a small bell in the centre. One by one, each child stands, picks up ANY small thing (a pencil, a crayon, a leaf), holds it to their heart, rings the bell gently once, and says, 'Krishna, for You.' Then they place it back and sit down. When every child has offered, ring the bell three times together.

Activity 5: Take Home — The Morning Offering

Every morning this week, before you brush your teeth, place both palms together on your heart. Close your eyes. Whisper: 'Krishna, today is Yours. My hands, my words, my thoughts — all for You.' Then go about your day. At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa ONE thing from the day that you remembered to offer.

Home Practice

Each morning this week, before brushing your teeth, place both palms together on your heart. Close your eyes. Whisper: 'Krishna, today is Yours. My hands, my words, my thoughts — all for You.' Then through the day, whenever you begin a new thing — a meal, a chore, homework, a game — take ONE quiet second, place your hand on your heart, and whisper 'Krishna, for You.' At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa ONE specific thing you offered today.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child demonstrates the palms-to-heart gesture spontaneously before at least one daily activity, and can describe two or three specific actions they consciously offered to Krishna.
