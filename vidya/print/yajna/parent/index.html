Yajna

यज्ञ · YAG-nyah

Parent Guide

Primary 6–9 · English

A chapter from Vidyā Karaṇa · Values for Life

Govardhan Eco Village

Value at a Glance

Yajna means offering. When we do something — a song, a meal, a drawing, even a chore — and we first offer it to Krishna, that action becomes yajna. It is not only fire and flowers in a temple. Every little thing we do can become sacred when we say, 'Krishna, this is for You.' Our whole life can slowly turn into one beautiful offering.

The Verse

Bhagavad-gita 3.9: Do your work as an offering to Krishna. Then the work will not tie you down. Do everything for Him, with a free and happy heart.

What it is NOT

Yajna is not only for priests or only in the temple. It is not doing something just to show off — 'Look how much I gave!' It is not being grumpy while we serve. It is not keeping the best for ourselves and offering the leftover. Yajna is quiet, joyful, and done with love — even when nobody is watching.

  • Eating your snack without remembering to share even a tiny bit with Krishna or someone else
  • Doing a chore with an angry face — 'I HAVE to do this!'
  • Singing a song loudly so everyone will clap for YOU, forgetting it was meant to be for Krishna

Stories Your Child Heard

King Ambarisha's Quiet Offering — Long ago, there was a gentle king named Ambarisha. He loved Krishna more than his crown. More than his palace. More than his food. Every morning, King Ambarisha woke up and whispered, 'Krishna, today is Yours. My hands, my words, my thoughts — all for You.'

Meera and the Homework Offering — Meera was seven years old. She loved singing. She loved stories. But Maths — Maths made her cheeks go red. It was Tuesday evening. Her Maths notebook lay open on the table. Two times seven. Three times eight. The numbers swam around like angry fish.

The Sanskrit Word

yajña (यज्ञ) — YAG-nyah

Meaning: Offering. Any action — big or small — that we do for Krishna first. When we offer it, the action becomes sacred.

Ask your child to teach you this word!

Home Practice

Each morning this week, before brushing your teeth, place both palms together on your heart. Close your eyes. Whisper: 'Krishna, today is Yours. My hands, my words, my thoughts — all for You.' Then through the day, whenever you begin a new thing — a meal, a chore, homework, a game — take ONE quiet second, place your hand on your heart, and whisper 'Krishna, for You.' At bedtime, tell Amma or Appa ONE specific thing you offered today.

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child demonstrates the palms-to-heart gesture spontaneously before at least one daily activity, and can describe two or three specific actions they consciously offered to Krishna.

Signs of Growth

By the end of the week, the child demonstrates the palms-to-heart gesture spontaneously before at least one daily activity, and can describe two or three specific actions they consciously offered to Krishna.

When every action becomes an offering, our whole life softens into love — and that loving offering has another name, bhakti.
