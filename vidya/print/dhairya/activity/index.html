Worksheet

Dhairya Worksheet - Learning to Wait and Not Give Up

Section 1: Story Pictures

Look at these pictures and circle the right answer:

1. Picture of child waiting in line calmly vs child pushing

Who is showing dhairya? Circle: The calm child / The pushing child

2. Picture of child still trying puzzle vs child throwing pieces

Who has dhairya? Circle: The trying child / The throwing child

3. Picture of Prahlada praying vs getting angry

What did Prahlada do when things were hard? Circle: Kept praying / Got angry

Section 2: Make a Patience Garden (Craft Activity)

Materials needed: Paper plate, cotton balls, crayons, small seeds (drawn or real)

Steps:

1. Color your paper plate brown like soil

2. Glue cotton balls around the edge like clouds

3. Draw or glue seeds in the middle

4. Draw roots going down (patience growing inside)

5. Draw a small green shoot coming up

6. Write "I can wait" on your cloud

While making, think: What helps you stay calm when you have to wait?

Section 3: The Waiting Game (Movement Activity)

Players: Whole class Time: 10 minutes

How to play:

1. Everyone sits in a circle

2. Teacher plays soft music

3. When music stops, freeze like a statue

4. Stay very still and count to 10 slowly

5. If you move, sit down but keep smiling

6. Last person standing wins!

After the game, talk about:

  • How did it feel to stay still?
  • What helped you wait without moving?
  • When do we need to wait in real life?

Section 4: Color the Dhairya Tree

Instructions: Color this special tree. Each part shows dhairya:
  • Roots: Color brown - these are like staying strong inside
  • Trunk: Color dark brown - this is like not giving up
  • Leaves: Color green - these are like happy thoughts while waiting
  • Flowers: Color your favorite colors - these are the good things that come when we wait

Draw yourself sitting under the tree, waiting patiently for the flowers to bloom.

Home Practice

Each morning when you wake up, water a plant or put water in a small cup by your window. While you pour the water, say 'I have dhairya like Kenji' three times. If you don't have a plant, you can pretend to water an imaginary seed.

Duration: Every morning for one week
Look for: You will notice that waiting feels easier, and you might feel more calm when things don't happen right away.
