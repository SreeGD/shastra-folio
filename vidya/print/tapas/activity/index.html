Tapas Worksheet — The Little Fire Inside

Activity 1: Easy Wrong or Hard Right? (Circle the Right Answer)

1. Amma says 'Only one chocolate.' I have eaten one. My hand wants another. EASY WRONG (take it) HARD RIGHT (stop)

2. It is 6 o'clock. Time to wake up for morning prayer. My blanket is warm. EASY WRONG (sleep more) HARD RIGHT (get up)

3. My little brother broke my crayon. I want to SHOUT at him. EASY WRONG (shout) HARD RIGHT (soft words)

4. Nani is old and slow. I want to grab food before her. EASY WRONG (grab) HARD RIGHT (wait — serve Nani first)

5. My mind is jumping to cartoons during bhajan. I want to stop singing. EASY WRONG (stop) HARD RIGHT (bring mind back — sing softly)

Each HARD RIGHT feeds your little fire. Count your fires this week.

Activity 2: Draw Your Three Little Fires

Draw YOUR body, with THREE little flames: one in your tummy (body-fire), one on your lips (speech-fire), one on your forehead (mind-fire). Colour the flames ORANGE and GOLD. Under the picture, write one thing each fire will do tomorrow — e.g., 'My body-fire will wake me up early' — 'My speech-fire will say only kind words' — 'My mind-fire will come back to Krishna when it jumps.'

[Drawing space]

Activity 3: Say It With Me (The Three-Fire Chant)

Stand tall. Put ONE hand on your tummy. Put ONE hand on your mouth. Put BOTH hands folded on your forehead.

Chant three times:

Śā-rī-ram! (body-fire) *pat tummy* Vāṅ-ma-yam! (speech-fire) *touch lips* Mā-na-sam! (mind-fire) *fold hands on forehead* TA-PAS! TA-PAS! TA-PAS!

Activity 4: The Monkey-Mind Game (Paired)

With a partner, take turns being the Monkey and the Shepherd.

  • The MONKEY shouts silly things: 'Mango! Balloon! Game! Sweet! TV!'
  • The SHEPHERD (quietly, gently) says after each one: 'Come back. Sit with Krishna.'
  • The MONKEY calms down, sits cross-legged, whispers: 'Krishna.'

Swap roles. Notice: does the monkey feel HAPPIER when the shepherd is gentle — or when the shepherd is angry?

(The little fire is fed by GENTLENESS, not by scolding.)

Activity 5: Take Home — The Little-Fire Diary

Keep a small notebook beside your pillow. Each night, before sleep:

1. Draw one tiny flame in your diary for EACH hard-right choice you made today. (Said no to a second sweet? One flame. Woke up on time? One flame. Kept kind words when you wanted to shout? One flame.)

2. Whisper — 'Krishna, my little fire is for You. Please keep it burning.'

3. At the end of the week, count your flames. Each one is Krishna's gift back to you.

🔥 🙏 🪔

Home Practice

For one week, keep your Little-Fire Diary beside your pillow. Each evening, before sleep, think about the day. For EACH time you chose the hard-right over the easy-wrong — say no to a second sweet, woke up on time, kept kind words when you wanted to shout, brought your mind back to Krishna during bhajan — draw one tiny orange flame in your diary. Then put your hand on your chest and whisper: 'Krishna, my little fire is for You. Please keep it burning.' In the morning, before rising, touch your chest and whisper: 'Body-fire. Speech-fire. Mind-fire. Three tapas for Krishna today.'

Duration: Every day for one week
Look for: By the end of the week, the child can name at least 3 specific 'hard-right' choices they made, can demonstrate the three-fire gesture (hand on tummy, touch lips, fold on forehead), and the diary contains at least 7 tiny flames. The child can use the phrase 'my little fire' spontaneously to describe a moment of discipline.
