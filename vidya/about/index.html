Vidyā Karaṇa

विद्या knowledge · करण instrument

The LLM is the karaṇa — the instrument. The wisdom comes from the tradition.

What this is

Vidyā Karaṇa is a free, open library of 50 timeless values drawn from the Vedic tradition — the daivī sampad described in Bhagavad-gītā 16.1–3, expanded across the wider canon. For each value, we publish a chapter built for children: a verse, a story from tradition, a story from today, a teaching, a Sanskrit word study, a home practice, and an audio narration. We aim for four age brackets (Primary 6–9, Middle 9–14, Senior 14–18, College 17+) across five languages (English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada).

Why a "map"

Values are not a checklist. They are a network — bhakti rests on shraddha, dāna grows from kṛpā, satya guards ahimsā. The homepage shows the full map seven different ways (mandala, force-graph, tree, sunburst, rings, honeycomb, cards) so that children, teachers, and parents can each find an entry point that suits their mind.

Govardhan Eco Village

This project is an initiative of Govardhan Eco Village — a community of monks, families, and volunteers in the Sahyadri hills near Mumbai. GEV is a project of ISKCON (the International Society for Krishna Consciousness), founded by His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda in 1966. We follow the spiritual lineage of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and the four Vaiṣṇava sampradāyas.

Founder Ācārya

Śrīla Prabhupāda translated and commented on the Śrīmad Bhāgavatam, the Bhagavad-gītā As It Is, and the Caitanya-caritāmṛta — the textual root of every value chapter on this site. At the age of 70 he travelled from India to America with seven dollars to seed Vedic culture worldwide. The 50 values we teach here are the values he embodied, transmitted, and asked his students to live. Every story in this library traces — directly or indirectly — to his books and his lineage.

How content is made

  • Ontology first. Each value has an approved ontology node — definition, anchor verse, anti-patterns, developmental specs per age — before any chapter is written.
  • Verse-grounded. Every Sanskrit verse is verified against the Vedabase before publication.
  • AI as instrument, tradition as source. An LLM helps draft language, but the rubric (fidelity to ontology, age-appropriateness, pedagogy) is human-defined.
  • Audio is free. Narrated by Microsoft Edge TTS with a Tamil-male voice for Sanskrit verses (Valluvar Neural). No subscription, no token.
  • Multilingual is native, not translated. Each language regenerates the modern story with its own protagonists and saints — not a word-for-word translation.

Free, open, no tracking

All content is free. No advertising. No tracking pixels. No login. Audio works offline once cached. We will never sell or monetize children's attention.
