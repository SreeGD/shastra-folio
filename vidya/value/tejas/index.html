Vinaya

Tejas

तेजस् · TEH-jas

EN हिन्दी

What it is

Tejas is a special bright light that shines from inside us when we love Krishna. It makes us brave and happy. When we have tejas, we feel strong and can help others feel good too.

What it is not

Tejas is not being mean or bossy to show off. It is not being loud or rude to get attention.

Word study

Word
तेजस् (tejas)
Say it
TEH-jas
Meaning
bright light, special power, inner glow
Example
When Prahlada sang Krishna's name, his tejas made him shine like the sun.

Anchor verse

Bhagavad-gita 16.3

तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता ।
भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत ॥

tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucam adroho nāti-mānitā bhavanti sampadaṁ daivīm abhijātasya bhārata

Power, forgiveness, determination, cleanliness, freedom from envy and pride — these are the divine qualities, O son of Bharata, of those born with a divine nature.

The Bright Prince Prahlada

Srimad Bhagavatam 7.5-9

Long ago, there was a little prince named Prahlada. He was only seven years old. Prahlada loved Krishna very much. He would sing Krishna's name every day. He felt so happy when he sang!

But Prahlada's father was a mean demon king. The demon king did not like Krishna. He got very angry when he heard Prahlada singing. 'Stop singing that name!' he shouted. But Prahlada could not stop. Because Krishna's name made his heart shine like the sun.

The demon king tried to scare Prahlada. He put him near big elephants. He threw him in hot fire. But something amazing happened! Prahlada's love for Krishna made a bright light around him. The elephants became gentle. The fire felt cool.

Prahlada was never afraid. His heart was full of Krishna's light. This bright light is called tejas. Because Prahlada loved Krishna so much, Krishna kept him safe. Even the demon king saw how bright and brave his son was. In the end, the demon king's mean heart became soft too.

Verse

BG 18.43

शौर्यं तेजो धृतिर्दाक्ष्यं युद्धे चाप्यपलायनम् ।
दानमीश्वरभावश्च क्षात्रं कर्म स्वभावजम् ॥ ४३ ॥

śauryaṁ tejo dhṛtir dākṣyaṁ yuddhe cāpy apalāyanam dānam īśvara-bhāvaś ca kṣātraṁ karma svabhāva-jam

Heroism, power, determination, resourcefulness, courage in battle, generosity and leadership are the natural qualities of work for the kṣatriyas.

Mia's Brave Light

Mia was playing at the park in her town in Brazil. She loved the big trees and colorful flowers. She was having fun with her friend Sam.

Then some big kids came over. They were being mean to Sam. They took his toy car. Sam started to cry. Mia felt scared. The big kids looked so strong!

Mia's heart felt heavy. She wanted to help Sam. But she was just a little girl. What could she do? She closed her eyes. She remembered her morning prayer to Krishna.

Suddenly, Mia felt warm inside. It was like a bright light was growing in her heart. This light made her feel brave. She walked up to the big kids. 'Please give back the car,' she said in a clear voice. 'It makes Sam sad when you take it.'

The big kids looked surprised. Mia was small, but her voice was strong. Her eyes were bright and kind. One big kid smiled. 'Here,' he said, giving back the car. 'We were just playing.'

Sam hugged Mia. 'Thank you!' he said. Mia felt the warm light still glowing in her heart. She had found her tejas - her brave light that comes from loving Krishna.

Core teaching

  • Tejas is the bright light that shines from our heart when we love Krishna.
  • This special light makes us brave and helps us do good things.
  • When we have tejas, we can help others and make them happy too.

Try this at home

Each morning when you wake up, stand in front of a mirror. Put your hands on your heart and say 'Krishna, please make my heart bright today.' Then smile at yourself and imagine golden light filling your heart.

Duration: Once every morning for one week
Watch for: You will feel happier and braver during the day. Your family will notice you being more helpful and cheerful.

Activities

  • Story Pictures · 8 min

    Look at pictures from our stories and circle the ones that show tejas (bright light from loving Krishna)

  • Make a Sunshine Heart · 15 min

    Create a heart-shaped sun with Krishna's name in the center using yellow and orange paper

  • Tejas Dance Game · 10 min

    Dance in a circle showing bright energy, freeze in sun poses when music stops

  • Nature's Bright Things · 12 min

    Go outside and find three things in nature that shine or glow (flowers, leaves, stones) and tell how they remind you of tejas

  • Color the Brave Hearts · 10 min

    Color pictures of children showing tejas by helping others and being brave
