Vinaya

Dhairya

धैर्य · DHIGH-rya

Primary 6–9 Middle 9–14 Senior 14–18 College 17+
EN हिन्दी தமிழ் తెలుగు

What it is

Dhairya means staying calm and not giving up when things are hard. It means waiting with a happy heart and trying again when something doesn't work.

What it is not

Dhairya is not crying and getting angry when we have to wait. It is not giving up right away when something is hard.

Word study

Word
धैर्य (dhairya)
Say it
DHIGH-rya
Meaning
staying calm and not giving up when things are hard
Example
When Kenji's seeds didn't grow right away, he showed dhairya by watering them every day.

युद्धे चाप्यपलायनम्।
दानमीश्वरभावश्च
क्षात्रं कर्म स्वभावजम्॥

śauryaṁ tejo dhṛtir dākṣyaṁ yuddhe cāpy apalāyanam dānam īśvara-bhāvaś ca kṣātraṁ karma svabhāva-jam

Being brave, strong, never giving up, being smart, not running away when things are hard, sharing with others, and being a good leader are special gifts that some people have.

Little Prahlada's Strong Heart

Srimad Bhagavatam 7.5

Once there was a little boy named Prahlada. He was only five years old. Prahlada loved Lord Vishnu very much. Every day he would pray and sing songs for the Lord.

But Prahlada's father was a mean king. He did not like Lord Vishnu. He got very angry when he saw Prahlada praying. 'Stop praying!' his father shouted. 'I am the only one you should love!'

But little Prahlada had dhairya in his heart. He stayed calm and said softly, 'Father, I love you, but I also love Lord Vishnu. He takes care of all of us.'

This made his father even more angry. He tried many ways to make Prahlada stop praying. He put Prahlada in a dark room. He sent scary demons to frighten him. He even threw him off a tall mountain!

But because Prahlada had dhairya, he never got scared. He never stopped praying. Even when bad things happened, he would close his eyes and sing, 'Lord Vishnu, please help me. I will never stop loving you.'

The other children at school asked him, 'Prahlada, why don't you just do what your father says? Then he won't be angry anymore.'

Prahlada smiled and said, 'Sometimes we have to wait for good things. Lord Vishnu knows what is best for me. I will keep praying no matter what happens.'

Finally, Lord Vishnu saw how strong Prahlada's heart was. Because Prahlada never gave up, the Lord came to help him. He came as a special lion-man called Nrisimha. He saved Prahlada from his mean father.

From that day on, Prahlada was safe and happy. He could pray to Lord Vishnu every day. All the people learned that when we have dhairya like little Prahlada, good things happen.

Kenji's Garden Dream

Kenji lived in a small town in Japan. His teacher, Tanaka-sensei, gave the class a special project. 'Everyone will plant seeds and grow a garden,' she said. 'The best garden will be shown to the whole school!'

Kenji was so excited! He picked sunflower seeds because they were his favorite. At home, he planted them in a small pot by his window. 'I can't wait to see my big yellow flowers!' he told his mother.

The next morning, Kenji ran to check his pot. But there were no flowers. Just dirt. His face fell. 'Where are my sunflowers?' he asked sadly.

'Plants take time to grow, Kenji,' his mother said gently. 'You must water them and wait.'

But Kenji felt upset. He saw his friend Yuki's beans already had tiny green shoots. 'My seeds are broken!' he cried. 'I want to throw them away and get new ones!'

That night, Kenji felt like giving up. His tummy felt heavy with sadness. But then he remembered something his grandmother always said: 'Good things come to those who wait with a happy heart.'

The next day, even though he felt disappointed, Kenji watered his seeds. 'Maybe grandmother is right,' he whispered to the pot. 'I will try to be patient.'

Every morning, Kenji watered his seeds. Some days he felt sad because nothing was happening. But he took deep breaths and kept watering. 'I won't give up,' he told himself.

After two weeks, something amazing happened! Tiny green shoots poked through the dirt. Kenji's eyes grew wide with joy. 'They're growing!' he shouted.

Because Kenji kept watering and waiting, his sunflowers grew tall and strong. When the garden contest came, his flowers were the biggest and brightest of all. The whole school clapped when they saw his beautiful sunflowers.

'How did you grow such wonderful flowers?' asked his teacher.

Kenji smiled. 'I learned to wait with a happy heart. Good things really do come when we don't give up!'

Core teaching

  • When things are hard, we can stay calm and keep trying instead of getting upset.
  • Good things take time to happen, so we learn to wait with happy hearts.
  • Krishna helps us when we don't give up and keep doing our best.

Try this at home

Each morning when you wake up, water a plant or put water in a small cup by your window. While you pour the water, say 'I have dhairya like Kenji' three times. If you don't have a plant, you can pretend to water an imaginary seed.

Duration: Every morning for one week
Watch for: You will notice that waiting feels easier, and you might feel more calm when things don't happen right away.

Activities

  • Story Pictures Recognition · 8 min

    Look at pictures showing patient vs impatient behavior and circle the ones that show dhairya

  • Make a Patience Garden · 15 min

    Create a paper plate garden with cotton clouds and seeds to represent how patience helps good things grow

  • The Waiting Game · 10 min

    Practice staying still like statues when music stops, learning to wait calmly without moving

  • Dhairya Tree Coloring · 12 min

    Color a special tree where each part represents different aspects of patience and waiting

  • Dhairya Chant with Gestures · 5 min

    Learn to say 'dhairya' with hand on heart gesture while taking deep breaths
