Prema

Bhakti

भक्ति · BHAK-ti

Primary 6–9 Middle 9–14 Senior 14–18 College 17+
বাংলা EN ગુજરાતી हिन्दी ಕನ್ನಡ मराठी ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ES தமிழ் తెలుగు

What it is

Bhakti is pure love for Krishna shown through service. It means doing everything with love for God in your heart. When you offer your actions to Krishna, even simple things become spiritual.

What it is not

Bhakti is not doing religious things just to get rewards or look good. It is not fake devotion done only when others are watching.

Word study

Word
भक्ति (bhakti)
Say it
BHAK-ti
Meaning
Devotion, loving service, divine love
Example
When we cook prasadam with bhakti, even simple food becomes sacred.

Anchor verse

Bhagavad-gita 9.34

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥

man-manā bhava mad-bhakto mad-yājī māṁ namaskuru mām evaiṣyasi yuktvā evam ātmānaṁ mat-parāyaṇaḥ

Always think of Me, become My devotee, worship Me and bow to Me. When you do this with love, you will surely come to Me.

Arjuna's Choice on the Battlefield

Bhagavad-gita

Prince Arjuna stood on the great battlefield of Kurukshetra. His chariot driver was Krishna himself. As Arjuna looked across the field, his heart sank. He saw his own cousins, teachers, and uncles ready to fight against him.

'Krishna, I cannot do this,' Arjuna said. His hands shook as he dropped his famous bow. 'How can I fight people I love? Even if I win this war, what joy will I have?'

Arjuna sat down in his chariot, confused and sad. He had always been the best archer in the world. He had trained since childhood to be a great warrior. But now, when his biggest test came, he felt lost.

Krishna looked at his dear friend with kind eyes. 'Arjuna, you are thinking like an ordinary person. But you are a devotee. Let me teach you about bhakti.'

For hours, Krishna explained the secrets of devotional service. 'Whatever you do, do it for Me,' Krishna said. 'Offer all your actions to Me with love. When you fight as My devotee, you are not really fighting - you are serving dharma.'

Slowly, Arjuna began to understand. Because Krishna was teaching him with such love, Arjuna's heart opened. He realized that he could do his duty as a warrior while keeping his mind fixed on Krishna.

'I understand now,' Arjuna said. 'You are my dearest friend and my Lord. I will fight, but I will fight for You. Every arrow I shoot will be an offering of love to You.'

Arjuna picked up his bow again. This time, his hands were steady. Because he had surrendered to Krishna with pure bhakti, he found peace even in the middle of war. He fought the great battle, but his heart remained full of devotion and love for Krishna.

Verse

SB 11.14.21

भक्त्याहमेकया ग्राह्य: श्रद्धयात्मा प्रिय: सताम् ।
भक्ति: पुनाति मन्निष्ठा श्वपाकानपि सम्भवात् ॥ २१ ॥

bhaktyāham ekayā grāhyaḥ śraddhayātmā pṛyaḥ satām bhaktiḥ punāti man-niṣṭhā śva-pākān api sambhavāt

The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: My dear Uddhava, only by devotional service can one understand Me as I am, standing before you. By such bhakti, or devotional service, which is unmixed with fruitive activities and knowledge, one can understand that I, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, in My original spiritual body, can be understood only by devotional service. That bhakti, or devotional service, purifies even dog-eaters, what to speak of brāhmaṇas.

Priya's Music Offering

Priya sat in her room in Mumbai, holding her harmonium. The school talent show was next week, and she wanted to sing a beautiful bhajan about Krishna. But every time she thought about performing on stage, her stomach felt tight with worry.

'What if people laugh at my devotional song?' she thought. 'What if they think it's too religious? What if I forget the words?'

Priya had been learning classical music for three years. Her teacher always said she had a sweet voice. But singing alone in her room felt very different from singing in front of the whole school.

That evening, Priya's grandmother found her looking sad. 'What troubles you, beta?' Nani asked gently.

Priya explained her fears. 'I want to sing for Krishna, but I'm scared everyone will judge me.'

Nani smiled and sat beside her. 'Priya, when you sing your bhajans at home, who are you singing for?'

'For Krishna,' Priya replied.

'And does Krishna judge your voice or worry about what others think?'

Priya shook her head. 'No, Nani. Krishna just loves when we offer Him something from our heart.'

'Then sing for Him at the talent show too,' Nani said. 'Let your song be your offering of bhakti. When you sing with love for Krishna, that love will reach everyone who listens.'

The next morning, Priya made a decision. She would sing her bhajan not to win the competition, but as an offering to Krishna. Because she changed her intention from wanting praise to giving love, her fear began to melt away.

At the talent show, Priya walked onto the stage. She closed her eyes for a moment and thought of Krishna's smiling face. Then she began to sing. Her voice was clear and full of devotion.

As Priya sang about Krishna's love, something beautiful happened. The whole auditorium became quiet and peaceful. Even students who had never heard a bhajan before felt touched by the pure love in her voice.

When Priya finished, the applause was warm and long. But the best part was the feeling in her heart - she had offered her gift to Krishna with pure bhakti, and that made everything perfect.

Core teaching

  • True bhakti means doing everything as an offering to Krishna with love in your heart.
  • When we act with devotion instead of selfish desires, we find peace and strength even in difficult times.
  • Bhakti transforms ordinary activities into spiritual service when done with the right intention.

Try this at home

Each evening before dinner, spend two minutes thinking about your day. Choose one thing you did and imagine offering it to Krishna with love. Say quietly: 'Krishna, I offer this to You.' Notice how this changes how you feel about that activity.

Duration: Once daily for one week
Watch for: You will start to feel more peaceful and happy about your daily activities, and you may find yourself naturally thinking of Krishna during the day.

Activities

  • Story Comprehension Questions · 8 min

    Answer questions about how Arjuna and Priya practiced bhakti in their challenging situations

  • Bhakti Response Scenarios · 10 min

    Read three daily-life situations and write how someone with bhakti would respond

  • Devotion Flower Garland Craft · 20 min

    Create a paper flower garland where each flower represents one way to practice bhakti in daily life

  • Bhakti Circle Memory Game · 10 min

    Stand in a circle and build a chain of devotional offerings, helping each other remember all the ways to serve Krishna

  • Personal Bhakti Reflection · 12 min

    Write about a time you acted with love and care, and explore how to transform similar actions into offerings to Krishna
