Bhakti: the essence of devotion

Verses where Kṛṣṇa describes the heart of pure devotional service and its highest expression.

Start with BG 9.22 →

  1. 1 BG 9.22 But those who always worship Me with exclusive devotion, meditating on My transcendental form – to them I carry what they lack, and I preserve what they have.
  2. 2 BG 9.26 If one offers Me with love and devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit or water, I will accept it.
  3. 3 BG 9.27 Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer or give away, and whatever austerities you perform – do that, O son of Kuntī, as an offering to Me.
  4. 4 BG 9.34 Engage your mind always in thinking of Me, become My devotee, offer obeisances to Me and worship Me. Being completely absorbed in Me, surely you will come to Me.
  5. 5 BG 10.10 To those who are constantly devoted to serving Me with love, I give the understanding by which they can come to Me.
  6. 6 BG 11.55 My dear Arjuna, he who engages in My pure devotional service, free from the contaminations of fruitive activities and mental speculation, he who works for Me, who makes Me the supreme goal of his life, and who is…
  7. 7 BG 18.55 One can understand Me as I am, as the Supreme Personality of Godhead, only by devotional service. And when one is in full consciousness of Me by such devotion, he can enter into the kingdom of God.
  8. 8 BG 18.65 Always think of Me, become My devotee, worship Me and offer your homage unto Me. Thus you will come to Me without fail. I promise you this because you are My very dear friend.
  9. 9 BG 18.66 Abandon all varieties of religion and just surrender unto Me. I shall deliver you from all sinful reactions. Do not fear.
