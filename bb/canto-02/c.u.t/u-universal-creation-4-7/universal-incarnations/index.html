Universal Incarnations

U - Universal Creation (4-7) Universal Incarnations Chapter 7 Brahma then described various incarnations of the Lord and outlined the background, purpose and activities of each one. He established Krishna as the supreme source of these manifestations and narrated some highlights from the Lord’s earthly pastimes. He then listed the surrendered personalities who have grasped the Lord’s greatness. By hearing from such pure devotees and embracing the process of surrender, one can attain the same perfection. Having imparted this knowledge, Brahma encouraged Narada to share it throughout the universe. Narada accepted this task as his life and soul - his name literally means ‘one who gives Narayana to others.’
