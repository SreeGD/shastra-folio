A – Astonishing Rule (52-70)

Canto 10 (Part 2)

A – Astonishing Rule (52-70) In Dwaraka, Krishna exhibited His strength by fighting and winning many battles, but in one unique instance, He and Balarama fled from Jarasandha Krishna’s foremost queens were Rukmini and Satyabhama, but He also married 16,100 queens after freeing them from Narakasura. Narada visited Krishna’s 16,108 palaces and was astonished by His daily activities in Dwaraka. Meanwhile, Balarama visited Gokula and enjoyed rasa-lila , and later threatened the Kurus by dragging Hastinapura towards the Yamuna.
