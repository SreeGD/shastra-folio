E - Expanding Entities (20-24)

E - Expanding Entities (20-24) Prajapatis, or progenitors of mankind, are lent the task of procreating and expanding the number of living entities within the universe. Kardama Muni was one such prajapati. He married Devahuti, the daughter of Svayambhuva Manu, and together they had nine daughters and one son (Lord Kapiladeva). The nine daughters were married to various sages and their subsequent union caused further expansion of entities within the universe. The son, Lord Kapiladeva, presented profound philosophical truths to remind the entities what they are meant to achieve in the universe.
