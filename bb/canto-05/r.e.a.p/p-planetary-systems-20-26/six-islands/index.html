Six Islands

P - Planetary Systems (20-26) Six Islands Chapter 20 Having described Jambudvipa, Sukadeva Goswami next described the other six islands that make up Bhu-mandala. The first five of these islands have seven varsas , while the final island (Puskaradvipa) has two varsas . Jambudvipa, described previously, has nine varsas . The original rulers of these dvipas were the sons of Priyavrata, and the islands were named after them. At the periphery of Bhu-mandala are the famous Lokaloka mountain ranges. Sukadeva Goswami thus described the entire structure of Bhu-Mandala.
