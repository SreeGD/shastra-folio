Book Bhagavata

The Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Summarised — by S.B. Keshava Swami

A succinct overview of all 12 cantos of the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, organised around mnemonic keys per canto (S.L.A.P, C.U.T, etc.). Not a commentary — a structured map of the text that lets you locate any narrative or teaching by where it sits in Vyāsadeva's design.

Freely made available by the author at bhagavata.keshavaswami.com under a Creative Commons licence. Print and Kindle editions available; see the copyright page.
