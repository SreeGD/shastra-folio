Manus 7-14

L - Leaders of Society (13-15) Manus 7-14 Chapter 13 Having named the first six Manus, Sukadeva Goswami next listed the subsequent eight Manus: (7) Sraddhadeva (Vaivasvata) (8) Savarni (9) Daksa-savarni (10) Brahma-savarni (11) Dharma-savarni (12) Rudra-savarni (13) Deva-savarni (14) Indra-savarni. Within each Manvantara, Sukadeva Goswami explained who would be the prominent descendants of Manu, primary demigods overseeing universal affairs, seven sages ( saptarsi ), incarnations of the Lord, and finally, who would take the position of Indra.
