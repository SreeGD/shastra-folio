Stavāvalī (with Ananta Das Babaji commentary) 63
Translation & commentary

VERSE 54: MUKUNDA MATTA MATANGA VIHARAPARA DIRGHIKA KRSNA PRANA MAHA MINA KHELANANANDA VARIDHIH mukunda - Mukunda; matta - mad; mdtanga - elephant; vihdra - pastimes; apdra - endless; dirghika - lake; Krsna prana - Krsna's life-airs; mahd - great; mina - fish; khelana - playing; @nanda - bliss; vdridhiH - ocean. She is the endless lake of enjoyment for the mad elephant Mukunda, and She is the ocean of bliss where the great fish of Krsna's life-airs plays. Stavamrta Kana Vyakhya:: Just as a mad elephant is able to sport freely and blissfully with His female elephants in a large lake, the greatly playful Krsna (mahd vilasi) can freely and blissfully sport in the shoreless lake of Srimati, who is compared to a blissful ocean where the big fish of Krsna's life-airs can freely enjoy. The extraordinary mddana rasa of Radhika is the source of all bhdvas, and it is like a bottomless and shoreless ocean where the great fish of the mind of the transcendental Cupid Krsna can freely and blissfully enjoy His play.
