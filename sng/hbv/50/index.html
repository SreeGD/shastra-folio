hbv.50

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 295-300
Translation

saàsäre 'smin mahä-ghore janma-måtyu-bhayäkule püjanaà väsudevasya tärakaà vädibhiù småtam tatra-there; eva-indeed; çré-brahma-närada-samväde-in a conversation of Brahmä and Närada; saàsäre-material world; asmin-in this; mahä-ghore-very horrible; janma-måtyu-bhayäkule-filled with birth, death, and fears; püjanam-worship; väsudevasya-of Lord Kåñëa; tärakam-the deliverer; vädibhiù-by the philosophers; småtam-considered. In the Skanda Puräëa, in a conversation of Brahmä and Närada, it is said: "In the material world, which is full of darkness and dangers, combined with birth and death and full of different anxieties, the only way to get out of the great entanglement is to accept loving transcendental devotional service to Lord Väsudeva. This accepted by all classes of philosophers."* Text 202 sa näma sukåté loke kulaà tena hy alaìkåtam ädhäraù sarva-bhütänäà yena viñëuù prasäditaù sa-he; näma-indeed; sukåté-pious; loke-in the world; kulam-in the family; tena-by him; hi-indeed; alaìkåtam-decorated; ädhäraù-the resting place; sarva-bhütänäm-of everything; yena-by whom; viñëuù-Lord Viñëu; prasäditaù-is pleased. "A person who pleases Lord Viñëu, in whom everything rests, is the most pious person. He is a splendid ornament decorating his entire family. Text 203 yajïänäà tapasäà caiva çubhänäm api karmaëäm tad visiñöa-phalaà nåëäà sadaivärädhanaà hareù yajïänäm-of yajnas; tapasäm-of austerities; ca-and; eva-certainly; çubhänäm-auspicious; api-also; karmaëäm-of deeds; tat-that; visiñöa-phalam-best result; nåëäm-of men; sadä-aslways; eva-indeed; ärädhanam-the worship; hareù-of Lord Kåñëa. "The worship of Lord Kåñëa brings a result far better than anything obtained by performing many yajïas, austerities or pious deeds. Text 204 kalau kali-maläkrantä na jänanti harià param ye 'rcayanti tam éçänaà kåta-kåtyäs ta eva hi nästi çreyottamaà nåëäà viñëor ärädhanät param kalau-in Kali-yuga; kali-mäläkrantä-filled with quarrel; na-not; jänanti-know; harim-Lord Kåñëa; param-the Supreme Personality of Godhead; ye-who; arcayanti-worship; tam-Him; éçänam-the Lord; kåta-kåtyäù-successful; ta-they; eva- indeed; hi-indeed; na-not; asti-is; çreyottamam-better; nåëäm-of men; viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; ärädhanät-than the worship; param-better.

"In Kali-yuga the people generally are impure and quarrelsome, and they do know that Lord Viñëu is the Supreme. However, those fortunate people that do worship Lord Viñëu become successful and perfect in every way. Nothing is better for the people than the worship of Lord Viñëu. Texts 205 and 206 yuge 'smin tämase tasmät satataà harim arcayet arcite deva-deveçe çaìkha-cakra-gadä-dhare arcitäù sarva-deväù syur yataù sarva-gato hariù arcite sarva-lokeçe suräsura-namaskåte keçave keçi-kaàsa-ghne na yäti narakaà naraù yuge-yuga; asmin-in this; tämase-dark; tasmät-from that; satatam-a;ways; harim-Lord Kåñëa; arcayet-shouild worship; arcite-worshiped; deva-deveçe-the master of the demigods; çaìkha-cakra-gadä-dhare-who holds a conch, disc, and club; arcitäù-worshiped; sarva-deväù-all the demigods; syuù-are; yataù-because; sarva-gataù-all-pervading; hariù-Lord Viñëu; arcite-worshiped; sarva-lokeçe-the master of all the worlds; suräsura-namaskåte-to whom the demigods and demons boy down; keçave-Lord Keçava; keçi-kaàsa-ghne-the killer of Kaàsa and Kesi; na-not; yäti-goes; narakam-to hell; naraù-a person. "In this dark Kali-yuga one should always worship Lord Viñëu. When Lord Viñëu, who holds a conchshell, disc, and club, and who is the master of all the demigods, is worshiped, then all the demigods are also worshiped automatically, because Lord Viñëu is all-pervading. A person who worships Lord Keçava, who is the master of the worlds, who is worshiped by the demigods and demons, and who killed Kaàsa and Keçi, will not go to the hellish worlds. Text 207 sakåd abhyarcito yena helayäpi namaskåtaù sa yäti paramaà sthänaà yat surair api püjitam sakåt-once; abhyarcitaù-worshiped; yena-by whom; helayä-with mockery; api-even; namaskåtaù-bowed down; sa-he; yäti-goes; paramam-to the supreme; sthänam-abdoe; yat-that; suraiù-by the demigods; api-even; püjitam-worshiped. "A person who once mocks the Lord by pretending to worship Him or bow down before Him still goes to the supreme abode, the abode worshiped by the demigods. Text 208 samasta-loka-näthasya deva-devasya çärìginaù säkñäd bhagavato nityaà püjanaà janmanaù phalam samasta-loka-näthasya-the master of all the worlds; deva-devasya-the master of the demigods; çärìginaù-who holds the Sarnga bow; säkñät-directly; bhagavataù-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; nityam-always; püjanam-worship; janmanaù-of birth; phalam-success. "Worship of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who holds the Çärìga bow and is the master of the demigods and all

the worlds, makes one's life a complete success." Text 209 tatraivägre asäre khalu saàsäre säram etan nirüpitam samasta-loka-näthasya çraddhayärädhanaà hareù tatra-there; eva-indeed; agre-in the beginning; asäre-useless; khalu-indeed; saàsäre-in the material world; saräm-vaule; etan-this; nirüpitam-is described; samasta-loka-näthasya-of the master of all the worlds; çraddhayä-with faith; ärädhanam- the worship; hareù-of Lord Kåñëa. In the beginning of the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "In this worthless material world the only valuable things is faithful worship of Lord Viñëu, who is the master of all the worlds." Text 210 kià ca yatra viñëu-kathä nityaà yatra tiñöhanti vaiñëaväù kali-bähyä naräs te vai ye 'rcayanti sadä harim kià ca-furthermore; yatra-where; viñëu-kathä-topics of Lord Viñëu; nityam-always; yatra-where; tiñöhanti-are; vaiñëaväù-devotees of Lord Viñëu; kali-bähyäù-untouched by kali-yuga; naräù-people; te-they; vai-indeed; ye-who; arcayanti-worship; sadä-always; harim-Lord Viñëu. It is further said: "The age of Kali cannot enter any place where the devotees of Lord Viñëu stay, where they talk of Lord Viñëu, where they always worship Him." Text 211 käçé-khaëòe harer ärädhanaà puàsäà kià kià na kurute bata putra-mitra-kalaträrthaà räjya-svargäpavargadam käçé-khaëòe-in the Käçé-khaëòa; hareù-of Lord Kåñëa; ärädhanam-worship; puàsäm-of the people; kim-why?; kim- why?; na-not; kurute-do; bata-indeed; putra-mitra-kalaträrtham-children, freinds, wife, and wealth; räjya- svargäpavargadam-which guve kingdom, Svargaloka, and liberation. In the Käçé-khaëòa it is said: "Why, why do the people not worship Lord Viñëu, whose worship brings a beautiful wife, good children, good friends, wealth, kingdom, residence in Svarga, and final liberation?

Text 212 haraty aghaà dhvaàçayati vyädhén ädhén niräsyati dharmaà vivardhayet kñipraà prayacchanti mano-ratham harati-removes; agham-sin; dhvaàçayati-destroys; vyädhén-disease; ädhén-distress; niräsyati-destroys; dharmam-piety; vivardhayet-increases; kñipram-quickly; prayacchanti-gives; mano-ratham-desire. "The worship of Lord Viñëu removes sins, destroys diseases and anguish, increases piety, and quickly brings the fulfillment of desires." Text 213 ata eva skände dhruvaà prati märkaëòeyasya vacanaà sakåd abhyarcito yena deva-devo janärdanaù sa präpnoti paraà sthänaà satyam etan mayoditam ata eva-therefore; skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; dhruvam-Dhruva Mahäräja; prati-to; märkaëòeyasya-of Markandeya; vacanam-the words; sakåt-once; abhyarcitaù-worshiped; yena-by whom; deva-devaù-the master of the demigods; janärdanaù-Lord Viñëu; sa-he; präpnoti-attains; param-supreme; sthänam-place; satyam-truth; etan-this; mayä-by me; uditam-spoken. In the Skanda Puräëa, Märkaëòeya Muni tells King Dhruva: "A person who once worships Lord Viñëu, the master of the demigods, attains the supreme abode in the spiritual world. I tell you the truth." Text 214 tathäìgirasaù yasyäntaù sarvam evedaà yasya nänto mahätmanaù tam ärädhaya govindaà sthänam ägryaà yadécchasi tathä-so; äìgirasaù-of Angira Muni; yasya-of whom; antaù-within; sarvam-all; eva-indeed; idam-this; yasya-of whom; na-not; antaù-the end; mahätmanaù-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; tam-Him; ärädhaya-worship; govindam- Lord Kåñëa; sthänam-the place; ägryam-best; yadi-if; icchasi-you wish. Aìgirä Muni explains: "If you wish to enter the spiritual world, then please worship Lord Govinda, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who has no end, and in whom everything rests." Text 215 pulastasya paraà brahma paraà dhäma yo 'sau çäçvata-puruñaù tam ärädhya harià yäti

muktim apy ati-durlabhäm pulastasya-of Pulastya Muni; param-Supreme; brahma-Brahman; param-supreme; dhäma-abode; yaù-who; asau-He; çäçvata-eternal; puruñaù-person; tam-Him; ärädhya-worshiping; harim-Lord Kåñëa; yäti-goes; muktim-to liberation; api- also; ati-durlabhäm-very rare. Pulastya Muni explains: "One who worships Lord Kåñëa, who is the eternal Supreme Person, the Supreme Brahman, and the Supreme Abode, attains final liberation, which is otherwise very difficult to attain." Text 216 pulahasya aindram indraù paraà sthänaà yam ärädhya jagat-patim präpa yajïa-patià viñëuà tam ärädhaya su-vrata pulahasya-of Pulaha Muni; aindram-the supreme king; indraù-Indra; param-the supreme; sthänam-abode; yam-whom; ärädhya-worshiping; jagat-of the universe; patim-the master; präpa-attained; yajïa-patim-the master of all yajnas; viñëum- Lord Viñëu; tam-Him; ärädhaya-please worship; su-vrata-O saintly one. Pulaha Muni explains: "O saintly one, please worship Lord Viñëu, the master of all yajïas and all the worlds. By worshiping Him, King Indra attained his royal post." Text 217 vaçiñöhasya präpnoty ärädhite viñëau manasä yad yad icchati trailokyäntar-gataà sthänaà kim u sarvottamottamam vaçiñöhasya-of Vasistha Muni; präpnoti-attains; ärädhite-worshiped; viñëau-Lord Viñëu; manasä-with the mind; yad yat-whatever; icchati-desires; trailokyäntar-gatam-in th4e three worlds; sthänam-place; kim u-indeed; sarvottamottamam- the best of all. Vaçiñöha Muni explains: "One who worships Lord Viñëu can attain anything his heart desires in the three worlds, what to speak of in that world above all others. Text 218 yän yän kämayate kämän näré vä puruño 'pi vä tän samäpnoti vipulän samärädhya janärdanam yän yän-whatever; kämayate-desires; kämän-desires; näré-a woman; vä-or; puruñaù-a man; api-also; vä-or; tän-that; samäpnoti-attains; vipulän-many; samärädhya-worshiping; janärdanam-Lord Kåñëa.

"By worshiping Lord Viñëu a man or a woman will attain whatever they desire." Text 219 agastya-saàhitäyäm ärädhyaiva naro viñëuà manasä yad yad icchati phalaà präpnoty avihataà bhüri svalpam athäpi vä agastya-saàhitäyäm-in the Agastya-saàhitä; ärädhya-worshiping; eva-certainly; naraù-a man; viñëum-Lord Viñëu; manasä-with the mind; yad yat-whatever; icchati-desires; phalam-result; präpnoti-attains; avihatam-complete; bhüri-great; svalpam-small; athäpi-or; vä-or. In the Agastya-saàhitä it is said: "A person who worships Lord Viñëu will attain whatever thing, great or small, his heart desires." Text 220 édåçaà viñëu-puräëe 'pi kiïcid adhikaà cedaà çré-maréceù anärädhita-govindair narair sthänaà nåpätmaja na hi sampräpyate çreñöhaà tasmäd ärädhayäcyutam édåçam-like this; viñëu-puräëe-in the Viñëu Puräëa; api-also; kiïcit-something; adhikam-more; ca-and; idam-this; çré- maréceù-of Marici Muni; anärädhita-govindaiù-by they who do not worship Lord Govinda; naraiù-by people; sthänam-the place; nåpätmaja-O prince; na-not; hi-indeed; sampräpyate-is attained; çreñöham-the best; tasmät-from that; ärädhaya- please worship; acyutam--the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead. In the Viñëu Puräëa, Maréci Muni explains: "They who do not worship Lord Govinda cannot attain the Supreme Abode. Therefore, O prince, please worship Him, the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead." Text 221 kià ca tatraiva bhaumän manorathän svargaà svarga-vandyaà tathäspadam präpnoty ärädhite viñëau nirväëam api cottamam kià ca-furthermore; tatra-there; eva-indeed; bhauman-a kingdom; manorathän-desires; svargam-Svarga; svarga- vandyam-worshiped in Svarga; tathä-so; aspadam-the abode; präpnoti-attains; ärädhite-worshiped; viñëau-Lord Viñëu; nirväëam-liberation; api-also; ca-and; uttamam-the highest. In the Viñëu Puräëa it is also said: "By worshiping Lord Viñëu one attains all his desires. He attains a great kingdom, residence in Svargaloka, and residence in the transcendental abode worshiped by the people of Svarga. He attains the highest liberation."
