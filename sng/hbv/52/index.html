hbv.52

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 307-312
Translation

ye yajanti spåhä-çünyä harià vä haram eva vä ta eva bhuvanaà sarvaà punanti vibudharñabhäù ye-who; yajanti-worship; spåhä-çünyä-without material desires; harim-Lord Viñëu; vä-or; haram-Lord Çiva; eva-indeed; vä-or; ta-they; eva-indeed; bhuvanam-world; sarvam-all; punanti-purify; vibudharñabhäù-O best of the sages. "O wise sages, they who without material desires worship Lord Viñëu or Lord Çiva purify all the world." Text 244 pädme çré-näräyaëa-närada-samväde püjä-vidhi-prasaìge mad-bhakto yo mad-arcäà ca karoti vidhi-dåñöaye tasyäntaräyaù svapne 'pi na bhavaty abhayo hi saù pädme-in the Pamda Puräëa; çré-näräyaëa-närada-samväde-in a conversation of Çré Näräyaëa and Çré Närada Åñi; püjä- vidhi-prasaìge-on the topic of the rules of worship; mad-bhaktaù-My devotee; yaù-who; mad-arcäm-My worship; ca-and; karoti-does; vidhi-dåñöaye-properly; tasya-of him; antaräyaù-an obstacle; svapne-in dream; api-even; na-not; bhavati-is; abhayaù-fearless; hi-indeed; saù-he. In the Padma Puräëa, in a conversation about the rules of worship, Lord Näräyaëa tells Närada Muni: "For one who becomes My devotee and properly worships Me, no obstacle will ever appear, even in a dream. Such a devotee becomes fearless." Text 245 tatraiva vaiçäkha-mähätmye näradämbaréña-samväde puträn kalaträn dérghäyü räjyaà svargäpavaragakam sa dadyäd épsitaà sarvaà bhaktyä sampüjito 'jitaù tatra-there; eva-indeed; vaiçäkha-mähätmye-in the Vaiçäkha-mähätmya; näradämbaréña-samväde-in a conversation of Närada Muni and King Ambaréña; puträn-sons; kalaträn-wives; dérghäyü-long life; räjyam-kingdom; svarga-residence in Svargaloka; apavaragakam-and liberation; sa-He; dadyät-gives; épsitam-desire; sarvam-all; bhaktyä-with devotion; sampüjitaù-worshiped; ajitaù-the invicible Supreme Personality of Godhead. In the Padma Puräëa, in the Vaiçäkha-mähätmya, in a conversation of Närada Muni and King Ambaréña, it is said: "When He is worshiped with devotion, the Supreme Personality of Godhead responds by fulfilling all desires. He gives beautiful wife, good children, long life, kingdom, residence in Svargaloka, and final liberation. Text 246 narake 'pi ciraà magnäù pürvajä ye kula-dvaye tatraiväyänti te svargaà yad arceta suto harim narake-in hell; api-even; ciram-for a long time; magnäù-drowning; pürvajä-ancestors; ye-who; kula-dvaye-in both families; tatra-there; eva-indeed; äyänti-go; te-they; svargam-to Svargaloka; yat-because; arceta-worships; sutaù-

descendent; harim-Lord Viñëu. "When one of their descendents worships Lord Viñëu, many ancestors from both sides of the family become released from hell and go to Svargaloka." Text 247 tatraiva çré-yama-brähmaëa-samväde ca anärädhya harià bhaktyä ko lokän präpnuyäd budhaù ärädhite harau kämaù sarve kara-tala-sthitäù tatra-there; eva-indeed; çré-yama-brähmaëa-samväde-in a conversation of Yamaräja and a brähmaëa; ca-and; anärädhya- without worshiping; harim-Lord Viñëu; bhaktyä-with devotion; kaù-what?; lokän-planets; präpnuyät-attain; budhaù- demigod; ärädhite-when worshiped; harau-Lord Viñëu; kämaù-desires; sarve-all; kara-tala-sthitäù-sitting in the palm of one's hand. In the Padma Puräëa, in a conversation of Yamaräja and a brähmaëa, it is said: "What demigod attained the celestial world without devotedly worshiping Lord Viñëu? One who worships Lord Viñëu finds that whatever he wishes appears in the palm of his hand." Text 248 viñëu-dharmottare çré-kåñëämåta-stotre so 'pi dhanyatamo loke yo 'rcayed acyutaà sakåt kià punaù çraddhayä yuktaù su-puñpaiù prati-väsaram viñëu-dharmottare-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khanda; çré-kåñëämåta-stotre-in the Kåñëaamrta-stotra; saù-he; api-also; dhanyatamaù-most fortunate; loke-in this world; yaù-who; arcayet-worships; acyutam-the infallibel Supreme Personality of Godhead; sakåt-onde; kim-what?; punaù-more; çraddhayä-with faith; yuktaù-engaged; su-puñpaiù-with nice flowers; prati-väsaram-every day. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, in the Çré Kåñëämåta-stotra, it is said: "In this world a person who once worships infallible Lord Viñëu is very fortunate. What, then, may be said of a person who faithfully worships the Lord every day with many nice flowers? Text 249 vaiñëavän api ye nityaà prapaçyanty arcayanti ca te 'pi viñëu-padaà yänti kià punar viñëu-sevakäù vaiñëavän-the devotees of Lord Viñëu; api-also; ye-they who; nityam-regularly; prapaçyanti-see; arcayanti-worship; ca- and; te-they; api-also; viñëu-padam-the abode of Lord Viñëu; yänti-attain; kim-what?; punaù-more; viñëu-sevakäù-the servants of Lord Viñëu. "They who regularly see and worship Lord Viñëu's devotees go to Lord Viñëu's transcendental abode. What, then, may be said of Lord Viñëu's devotees themselves?"

Text 250 sa yogé sa viçuddhätmä sa çäntaù sa mahä-matiù sa çuddhaù sa ca sampürëaù kåñëaà seveta yo naraù sa-he; yogé-a yogi; sa-he; viçuddhätmä-pure at heart; sa-he; çäntaù-peaceful; sa-he; mahä-matiù-very intelligent; sa-he; çuddhaù-pure; sa-he; ca-and; sampürëaù-perfect; kåñëam-Lord Kåñëa; seveta-serves; yaù-who; naraù-a person. "A person who serves Lord Kåñëa is a great yogé. He is pure in heart. He is peaceful. He is wise. He is pure. He is perfect. Text 251 agastya-saàhitäyäm ananya-manasaù çaçvad gaëayanto 'kñamälayä japanto räma rämeti sukhämåta-nidhau manaù pravilapyämåté-bhüya mukhaà tiñöhanti kecana agastya-saàhitäyäm-in the Agastya-samhita; ananya-manasaù-whose heart does not go to anyone else; çaçvat-always; gaëayantaù-counting; akña-mälayä-with jkapa beads; japantaù-chanting; räma-Räma; räma-Räma; iti-thus; sukha-of bliss; amåta--of nectar; nidhau-an ocean; manaù-the heart; praviläpya-saying; amåté-bhüya-becoming eternal; mukham-mouth; tiñöhanti-stays; kecana-some. In the Agastya-saàhitä it is said: "Some devotees, again and again intently chanting Räma! Räma! on japa-beads, dive into a nectar ocean of bliss. Attaining eternal liberation, they become filled with bliss. Text 252 paricaryä-paräù kecit präsädeñv eva çerate manuñyam iva taà drañöuà vyavahartuà ca bandhuvat paricaryä-paräù-devoted to worship; kecit-some; präsädeñv-in temples; eva-indeed; çerate-stay; manuñyam-human; iva- as if; tam-Him; drañöum-to see; vyavahartum-to act; ca-and; bandhuvat-like a friend. "Other devotees, attracted to Deity-worship, stay in temples to see Lord Räma, who appeared in a humanlike form to become the friend of His devotees." Text 253 kià ca yathä vidhi-niñedhau tu muktaà naivopasarpataù tathä na spåçato rämo- päsakaà vidhi-pürvakam

kià ca-furthermore; yathä-as; vidhi-niñedhau-injunctions and prohibitions; tu-indeed; muktam-free; na-not; eva- inded; upasarpataù-by approaching; tathä-so; na-not; spåçataù-touched; räma--of Lord Räma; upäsakam-a worshiped; vidhi-pürvakam-according to the rules. It is further said: "As a liberated soul need not follow the great myriad of Vedic orders and prohibitions, so a worshiper of Lord Räma is also free." Commentary by Çréla Sanätana Gosvämé The devotees of the Lord are also glorified in Bhagavad-gétä 11.55, 9.31, 7.17, and 4.36. Text 254 çré-bhagavad-gétäsu evaà satata-yuktä ye bhaktäs tväà paryupäsate ye cäpy akñaram avyaktaà teñäà ke yoga-vittamäù çré-bhagavad-gétäsu-in Çré Bhagavad-gétä; evam-thus; satata-yuktä-always engaged; ye-those who; bhaktäù-devotees; tväm-You; paryupäsate-properly worship; ye-those who; ca-also; api-again; akñaram-beyond the senses; avyaktam-the unmanifested; teñäm-of them; ke-who?; yoga-vittamäù-the most perfect in knowledge of yoga. In Çré Bhagavad-gétä (12.1) Arjuna asked: "Which are considered to be more perfect, those who are always properly engaged in Your devotional service, or those who worship the impersonal Brahman, the unmanifested?"* Text 255 ity arjunena påñöaù çré-bhagavän uväca mayy aveçya mano ye mäà nitya-yuktä upäsate çraddhayä parayopetäs te me yuktatamä matäù iti-thus; arjunena-by Arjuna; påñöaù-asked; çré-bhagavän-the Supreme Personality of Godhead; uväca-said; mayi-upon Me; aveçya-fixing; manaù-the mind; ye-those who; mäm-me; nitya-always; yuktä-engaged; upäsate-worship; çraddhayä- with faith; parayä-transcendental; upetäù-endowed; te-they; me-by Me; yuktatamä-most perfect; matäù-are considered. Asked in this way, the Supreme Personality of Godhead said (Bhagavad-gétä 12.2): "Those who fix their minds on My personal form and are always engaged in worshiping Me with great and transcendental faith are considered by me to be most perfect."* Text 256 caturtha-skandhe çré-påthüktau yat-päda-seväbhirucis tapasvinäm açeña-janmopacitaà malaà dhiyaù

sadyaù kñinoty anv-aham edhaté saté yathä padäìguñöha-viniùsåtä sarit caturtha-skandhe-in the Fourht Canto; çré-påthüktau-in the words of Çré Påthu; yat-päda-whose lotus feet; sevä- service; abhiruciù-inclination; tapasvinäm-persons undergoing severe penances; açeña-innumerable; janma-births; upacitam-acquire; malam-dirtiness; dhiyaù-mind; sadyaù-immediately; kñinoti-destroys; anv-aham-day after day; edhaté- increasing; saté-being; yathä-as; padäìguñöha-the toes of His lotus feet; viniùsåtä-emanating from; sarit-water. In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (4.21.31), King Påthu explains: "By the inclination to serve the lotus feet of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, suffering humanity can immediately cleanse the dirt which has accumulated in their minds during innumerable births. Like the Ganges water, which emanates from the toes of the lotus feet of the Lord, such a process immediately cleanses the mind, and thus spiritual or Kåñëa consciousness gradually increases."* Text 257 kià ca näradoktau yathä taror müla-niñecanena tåpyanti tat-skandha-bhujopaçäkhäù präëopahäräc ca yathendriyäëäà tathaiva sarvärhaëam acyutejyä kià ca-furthermore; näradoktau-in the words of Närada Muni; yathä-as; taroù-of a tree; müla-the root; niñecanena-by watering; tåpyanti-are satisfied; tat-its; skandha-trunk; bhuja-branches; upaçäkhäù-and twigs; präëa-the life air; upahärät- by feeding; ca-and; yathä-as; indriyäëäm-of the senses; tathä eva-similarly; sarva-of all demigods; arhaëam-worship; acyuta-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; ijyä-worship. Çréla Närada Muni explains (Çrémad-Bhägavatam 4.31.14): "As pouring water on the root of a tree energizes the trunk, branches, twigs, and everything, and as supplying food to the stomach enlivens the senses and limbs of the body, simply worshiping the Supreme Personality of Godhead through devotional service automatically satisfies the demigods, who are parts of that Supreme Personality."* Text 258 ekädaça-skandhe ca kavi-yogeçvarasya väkyaà manye 'kutaçcid bhayam acyutasya padämbujopäsanam atra nityam udvigna-buddher asad-ätma-bhäväd viçvätmanä yatra nivartate bhéù ekädaça-skandhe-in the Eleventh Canto; ca-and; kavi-yogeçvarasya-of the Yogic manster Kavi; väkyam-the statement; manye-I think; akutaçcit-not anywhere; bhayam-fear; acyutasya-of the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead; padämbujopäsanam-worship of the lotus feet; atra-here; nityam-alwasy; udvigna-agitated; buddheù-intelligence; asad- ätma-bhävät-my thinking that the self is temporary; viçvätmanä-thoroughly; yatra-where; nivartate-ceases; bhéù-fear. In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (11.2.3) Kavi Muni, a great master of yoga, explains: "I think that service to the lotus feet of the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead is the creator of fearlessness. They whose minds are agitated because they identify with the temporary material world will find that engagement in the Lord's service makes their fears flee far away." Text 259 çré-bhagavataç ca

evaà kriyä-yoga-pathaiù pumän vaidika-tantrikaiù arcann ubhayataù siddhià matto vindaty abhépsitam çré-bhagavataù-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; ca-and; evam-thus; kriyä-yoga-pathaiù-by the opath of kriya- yioga; pumän-a person; vaidika-Vedic; tantrikaiù-an Tantric; arcann-worshiping; ubhayataù-both; siddhim-perfection; mattaù-from Me; vindati-finds; abhépsitam-desired. The Supreme Personality of Godhead explained (Çrémad-Bhägavatam 11.27.49): "By serving Me according to the rules of the Vedas and Tantras, a person will gain from Me his desired perfection in both this life and the next." Text 260 kià ca mäm eva nairapkñyeëa bhakti-yogena vindati bhakti-yogaà sa labhate evaà yaù püjayeta mäm kià ca-furethermore; mäm-Me; eva-indeed; nairapkñyeëa-without material desire; bhakti-yogena-by devotional service; vindati-finds; bhakti-devotional service; yogam-yoga; sa-he; labhate-attains; evam-thus; yaù-who; püjayeta-worships; mäm-Me. The Supreme Personality of Godhead further says (Çrémad-Bhägavatam 11.27.53): "One who serves Me without material desire attains Me. One who worships Me attains pure devotional service unto Me." Text 261 gautaméya-tantre çré-näradasya tulasé-dala-mätreëa jalasya culukena ca vikréëéte svam ätmänaà bhaktebhyo bhakta-vatsalaù gautaméya-tantre-in the Gautamiya Tantra; çré-näradasya-of Çré narada; tulasé-dala-mätreëa-with simply a tulasé leaf; jalasya-of water; culukena-for a sip; ca-and; vikréëéte-sells; svam-His own; ätmänam-Self; bhaktebhyaù-to the devotees; bhakta-vatsalaù-He who loves His devotees. In the Gautaméya Tantra, Närada Muni explains: "The Supreme Personality of Godhead, who dearly loves His devotees, sells Himself to them for the price of a tulasé leaf and a sip of water."
