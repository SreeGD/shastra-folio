hbv.57

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 337-342
Translation

Text 348 kértana-kartå-kula-saìgy-ädi-pävanatvaà tatraiva mahä-pätaka-yukto 'pi kértayenn aniçaà harim çuddhäntaùkaraëo bhütvä jäyate paìkti-pävanaù kértana-chanting; kartå-performers; kula-multitude; saìgi-associating; ädi-beginning with; pävanatvam-purification; tatra-there; eva-indeed; mahä-pätaka-yuktaù-a great sinner; api-even; kértayenn-glorifying; aniçam-day and night; harim- Lord Kåñëa; çuddhäntaùkaraëaù-pure at heart; bhütvä-becoming; jäyate-is born; paìkti-pävanaù-purifying a host sins. Becoming Purified by Associating With They Who Chant the Lord's Holy Name In the Brahmäëòa Puräëa it is said: "By chanting Lord Kåñëa's holy name day and night, even a great sinner becomes pure at heart. He purifies everyone he meets." Text 349 laghu-bhägavate govindeti muòa yuktaù kértayed yas tv ananya-dhéù pävanena ca dhanyena teneyaà påthivé dhåtä laghu-bhägavate-in the Laghu-Bhägavata; govinda-O Govinda; iti-thus; muòa-happily; yuktaù-engaged; kértayet-may chant; yaù-one who; tv-indeed; ananya-dhéù-thinking of nothing else; pävanena-with purity; ca-and; dhanyena-with good fortune; tena-by him; iyam-this; påthivé-earth; dhåtä-is maintained. In the Laghu-Bhägavata it is said: "A person who places his thoughts on Lord Govinda alone and happily chants, `O Govinda!' protects the earth with his purity and his good fortune." Text 350 hari-bhakti-sudhodaye na caivam ekaà vaktäraà jihvä rakñati vaiñëavé äçrävya bhagavat-khyätià jagat kåtsnaà punäti hi hari-bhakti-sudhodaye-in the Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya; na-not; ca-and; evam-thus; ekam-one; vaktäram-speaker; jihvä- the tongue; rakñati-protects ; vaiñëavé-of a devotee of Lord Viñëu; äçrävya-hearing; bhagavat-khyätim-the glories of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; jagat-the universe; kåtsnam-entire; punäti-purifies; hi-indeed. In the Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya it is said: "A tongue busy in chanting the holy names of Lord Viñëu does not purify only the speaker. It purifies everyone who hears it."

Text 351 daçama-skandhe yan-näma gåhëann akhilän çrotåëätmänam eva ca sadyaù punäti kià bhüyas tasya spåñöaù padä hi te daçama-skandhe-in the Tenth Canto; yan-näma-whose name; gåhëann-chanting; akhilän-all; çrotåëätmänam-hearers; eva-indeed; ca-and; sadyaù-at once; punäti-purifies; kim-what?; bhüyaù-more; tasya-of that; spåñöaù-touched; padä-by the foor; hi-inded; te-of You. In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (10.4.17) it is said: "O Lord, a person who chants Your holy name purifies both himself and all who hear him. How much more purified, then, is a person touched by Your foot?" Text 352 ata evoktaà prahlädena narasiàhe te santaù sarva-bhütänäà nirupädhika-bändhavaù ye nåsiàha bhavan-näma gäyanty uccair mudänvitäù ata eva-therefore; uktam-said; prahlädena-by Prahläda; narasiàhe-in the Nåsiàha Puräëa; te-they; santaù-siants; sarva- bhütänäm-of all living entities; nirupädhika-bändhavaù-the unconditional freinds; ye-who; nåsiàha-O Lord Nåsiàha; bhavan-näma-Your holy name; gäyanti-chant; uccaiù-loudly; mudänvitäù-happy. In the Nåsiàha Puräëa, Prahläda Mahäräja says: "O Lord Nåsiàha, Your devotees are the unconditional friends of all living entities. That is why they happily and loudly chant Your holy names." Text 353 sarva-vyädhi-näçitvaà båhan-näradéye bhagavat-toña-prasaìge acytutänanda-govinda- nämoccäraëa-bhéñitaù naçyanti sakalä rogäù satyaà satyaà vadämy aham sarva-vyädhi-näçitvam-destroying all diseases; båhan-näradéye-in the Närada Puräëa; bhagavat-toña-prasaìge-in the matter of pleasing the Supreme Personality of Godhead; acytuta-Acyuta; änanda-Ananda; govinda-Govinda; näma-names; uccäraëa-the loud chanting; bhéñitaù-afraid; naçyanti-perish; sakalä-all; rogäù-diseases; satyam-in truth; satyam-in truth; vadämi-speak; aham-I. Chanting the Holy Name Cures All Diseases In the Närada Puräëa, in the description of pleasing the Lord, it is said: "When they hear the loud chanting of the holy names Acyuta, Ananada, and Govinda, all diseases at once die of fright. I speak to you the truth. It is true."

Text 354 paräçara-saàhitäyäà sämbaà prati vyäsoktau na sämba vyädhijaà duùkhaà heyaà nänyauñadhair api hari-nämauñadhaà pétvä vyädhis tyäjyo na saàsayaù paräçara-saàhitäyäm-In the Paräçara-saàhitä; sämbam-Samba; prati-to; vyäsoktau-in the words of Çré Vyäsa; na-not; sämba-O Sämba; vyädhijam-born of disease; duùkham-suffering; heyam-to be cast away; na-not; anya-by any other; auñadhaiù-medicines; api-even; hari-nämauñadham-the medicine of Lord Kåñëa's holy name; pétvä-drinking; vyädhiù- disease; tyäjyaù-to be abandoned; na-no; saàsayaù-doubt. In the Paräçara-saàhitä, Vyäsa tells Sämba: "By drinking the medicine of the holy name of Lord Kåñëa, one is cured of all disease. Of this there is no doubt. O Sämba, no other medicine is needed." Text 355 skände ädhayo vyädhayo yasya smaraëän näma-kértanät tadaiva vilayaà yänti tam anantaà nämamy aham skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; ädhayaù-anguishes; vyädhayaù-diseases; yasya-of whom; smaraëän-by remembering; näma-kértanät-by chanting the holy name; tadä-then; eva-indeed; vilayam-to destruction; yänti-go; tam-to Him; anantam- limitless; nämami-bow down; aham-I. In the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "A person who chants and remembers Lord Kåñëa's holy names becomes cured of all anxieties and all diseases. That is why I bow down before Lord Kåñëa, the limitless Supreme Personality of Godhead." Text 356 vahni-puräëe mahä-vyädhi-samäcchanno räja-vadhopäpéòitaù näräyaëeti saìkértyä nirätaìko bhaven naraù vahni-puräëe-in the Vahni Puräëa; mahä-vyädhi-samäcchannaù-aflicted with a terrible disease; räja-of a king; bädha- obstructions; upäpéòitaù-torubled; näräyaëa-Näräyaëa; iti-thus; saìkértyä-chanting; nirätaìkaù-free of trouble; bhaven- becomes; naraù-a person. In the Agni Puräëa it is said: "A person afflicted with a terrible disease or harassed by a king becomes free of his troubles when he chants the holy name of Lord Näräyaëa."

Text 357 sarva-duùkhopaçamanatvaà båhad-viñëu-puräëe sarva-rogopaçamanaà sarvopadrava-näçanam çäntidaà sarväriñöänäà harer nämänukértanam sarva-duùkhopaçamanatvam-the end of all sufferings; båhad-viñëu-puräëe-in the Viñëu Puräëa; sarva-all; roga- diseases; upaçamanam-curing; sarva-all; upadrava-harm; näçanam-destruction; çäntidam-bringing peace; sarväriñöänäm-of all calamities; hareù-of Lord Kåñëa; nämänukértanam-chanting of the holy name. Chanting the Holy Name Ends All Sufferings In the Viñëu Puräëa it is said: "the chanting of Lord Kåñëa's holy name cures all diseases, ends all dangers, and stops all calamities." Text 358 brahma-vaivarte sarva-päpa-praçamanaà sarvopadrava-näçnam sarva-duùkha-kñaya-karaà hari-nämänukértanam brahma-vaivarte-in the Brahma-vaivarta Puräëa; sarva-päpa-praçamanam-destroying all sins; sarvopadrava-näçnam- destroying all dangers; sarva-duùkha-kñaya-karam-destroying all sufferings; hari-nämänukértanam-the chanting of Lord Kåñëa's holy name. In the Brahma-vaivarta Puräëa it is said: "The chanting of Lord Kåñëa's holy name destroys all sins, ends all dangers, and removes all sufferings." Text 359 dvädaça-skandhe saìkértyamäno bhagavän anantaù çrutänubhävo vyasanaà hi puàsäm praviçya cittaà vidhunoty açeñaà yathä tamo 'rko 'bhram iväti-vätaù dvädaça-skandhe-in the Twelfth Canto; saìkértyamänaù-being glorified; bhagavän-the Supreme Personality of Godhead; anantaù-limitless; çrutänubhävaù-hearing; vyasanam-troubles; hi-indeed; puàsäm-of the people; pravisya- enbtering; cittam-the heart; vidhunoti-shakes away; açeñam-all; yathä-as; tamaù-darkness; arkaù-the sun; abhram-a cloud; iva-like; ati-a great; vätaù-wind. In the Çrémad-Bhägavatam (12.12.48) it is said: "When the people chant and hear His glories, the limitless Supreme Personality of Godhead enters their hearts. As a rising sun chases away the darkness or a great wind chases away a host of clouds, He chases away all the people's sufferings."

Text 360 viñëu-dharmottare artä viñaëëäù çithilaç ca bhétä ghoreñu ca vyädhiñu vartamänäù saìkértya näräyaëa-çabdam ekaà vimukta-duùkhäù sukhino bhavanti viñëu-dharmottare-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa; artä-distressed; viñaëëäù-dejected; çithiläù-stunned; ca-and; bhétä-afraid; ghoreñu-in terruible; ca-AND; vyädhiñu-diseases; vartamänäù-being; saìkértya-chanting; näräyaëa- çabdam-the name Näräyaëa; ekam-one; vimukta-duùkhäù-free of sufferings; sukhinaù-happy; bhavanti-become. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, it is said: "A person suffering great pain, dejected, stunned, afraid, or tormented by terrible diseases, who once says the name Näräyaëa loses his sufferings and becomes happy." Texts 361 and 362 kértanäd deva-devasya viñëor amita-tejasaù yakña-räkñasa-vetäla- bhüta-pretä-vinäyakäù òäkinyo vidravanti sma ye tathänye ca siàhakäù sarvänartha-haraà tasya näma-saìkértanaà småtam kértanät-by chanting the glories; deva-devasya-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; viñëoù-Lord Viñëu; amita- tejasaù-whose power has no limit; yakña-räkñasa-vetäla-bhüta-pretä-vinäyakäù-the yakñas, räkñasas, vetälas, bhütas, pretäs, and vinäyakas; òäkinyaù-dakinis; vidravanti-fled; sma-indeed; ye-who; tathä-so; anye-others; ca-and; siàhakäù- lions; sarvänartha-haram-removing all that is not good; tasya-of him; näma-saìkértanam-chanting the holy name; småtam-remembered. "When a person chants the holy names of Lord Viñëu, the all-powerful Supreme Personality of Godhead, he finds that the demon yakñas, räkñasas, vetälas, bhütas, pretäs, vinäyakas, and òäkinés, as well as the lions and other ferocious beasts, all flee from him. Chanting and remembering Lord Kåñëa's holy name drives away all that is not good." Text 363 kià ca näma-saìkértanaà kåtvä kñut-tåt-praskhalitädiñu viyogaà çéghram äpnoti sarvänarthair na saàsayaù kià ca-furthermore; näma-saìkértanam-chanting the holy name; kåtvä-doing; kñut-tåt-praskhalitädiñu-beginning with hunger, thirst, and stumbling; viyogam-separation; çéghram-long; äpnoti-attains; sarvänarthaiù-with all that is not good; na-no; saàsayaù-doubt. It is further said: "A person who chants the holy names of the Lord is protected from hunger, thirst, stumbling, and all other troubles."

Text 364 pädme devahüti-stutau mohänalollasaj-jväla- jvälal-lokeñu sarvadä yan-nämämbhodhara-cchäyäà praviñöo naiva dahyate pädme-in the Padma Puräëa; devahüti-stutau-in the prayers of Devahüti; moha-of illusions; anala-by the flames; ullasat-rising; jväla-flames; jvälal-burning; lokeñu-in the worlds; sarvadä-always; yan-näma-of whose holy name; ambhodhara-of the cloud; cchäyäm-the shade; praviñöaù-entered; na-not; eva-indeed; dahyate-is burned. In the Padma Puräëa, Devahüti prays: "In this world always ablaze with flames of illusion, a person who stays under the cloud of the Supreme Lord's holy names is never burned." Text 365 kali-bädhäpahäritvaà skände kali-käla-ku-sarpasya tékñna-daàstrasya mä bhayam govinda-näma-dävena dagdho yäsyati bhasmatäm kali-of kali-yuga; bädha-the obtsacles; apahäritvam-removing; skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; kali-käla-ku-sarpasya-of the black poison snake of Kali-yuga; tékñna-daàstrasya-with sharp fangs; mä-don't; bhayam-fear; govinda-näma-dävena- with the forest fire of Lord Govinda's holy names; dagdhaù-burned; yäsyati-will go; bhasmatäm-to ashes. Chanting the Holy Name Removes All the Obstacles Presented By the Kali-yuga In the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "Don't be afraid of the sharp-fanged, poisonous, black snake of Kali-yuga. The forest-fire of Lord Govinda's holy name will burn it to ashes." Text 366 båhan-näradéye kali-dharma-prasaìge hari-näma-parä ye ca ghore kali-yuge naräù ta eva kåta-kåtyäç ca na kalir bädhate hi tän båhan-näradéye-in the Narada Puräëa; kali-dharma-prasaìge-in the discussion of religion in the age of Kali; hari-näma- parä-devoted to the holy name of Lord Kåñëa; ye-who; ca-and; ghore-horrible; kali-yuge-in Kali-yuga; naräù-people; ta- they; eva-indeed; kåta-kåtyäù-successful; ca-and; na-not; kaliù-Kali; bädhate-stops; hi-indeed; tän-them. In the Närada Puräëa, in the discussion of religion in the age of Kali, it is said: "In the horrible Kali-yuga they who devotedly chant the Lord's holy names are all-successful and all-perfect. Kali
