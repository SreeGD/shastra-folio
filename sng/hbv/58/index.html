Chanting the Holy Names Destroys the Results of Past Sins In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (6.2.46) it is said: "Therefore one who desires freedom from material bondage should adopt the process of chanting and glorifying the name, fame, form, and pastimes of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, at whose feet all the holy places stand. One cannot derive the proper benefit from other methods, such as pious atonement, speculative knowledge, and meditation in mystic yoga, because even after following such methods one takes to fruitive activities again, unable to control his mind, which is contaminated by the base qualities of nature, namely passion and ignorance."* Text 372 dvädaçe ca yan-nämadheyaà mriyamäna äturaù patan skhalan vä vivaço gåëan pumän vimukta-karmärgala uttamäà gatià präpnoti yakñyanti na taà kalau janäù dvädaçe-in the Twelfth Canto; ca-and; yan-nämadheyam-whose holy name; mriyamäna-dying; äturaù-distressed; patan-falling; skhalan-stumbling; vä-or; vivaçaù-agitated; gåëan-chanting; pumän-a pwerson; vimukta-karmärgala-free from the chains of karma; uttamäm-supreme; gatim-destination; präpnoti-attains; yakñyanti-will worship; na-not; tam- Him; kalau-in Kali-yuga; janäù-the people. Chanting the Holy Name Makes Everything Perfect In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (8.23.16) Çré Çukräcärya tells the Supreme Personality of Godhead: "There may be discrepancies in pronouncing the mantras and observing the regulative principles, and, moreover, there may be discrepancies in regard to time, place, person, and paraphernalia.
