hbv.42

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 247-252
Translation

In the Nārada Purāṇa, at the end of Yajñadhvaja's story, it is said:

"A person who worships Tulasī-devī in his home finds that his good fortune increases day by day."

Text 113

ata eva-therefore; pādme devadūta-vikuṇḥala-samvāde-in the Padma Purāṇa, in a conversation of Devadūta and Vikuṇḥala; pakṣe pakṣe-fortnight after fortnight; tu-indeed; samprāpte-attained; dvādaśyām-on dvadasi; vaiśya-sattama-O best of vaisyas; brahmādayaḥ-headed by Brahmā; api-even; kurvanti-do; tulasī-vana-pūjanam-worship of the Tulasī forest.

In the Padma Purāṇa, in a conversation of Devadūta and Vikuṇḥala, it is said:

"O best of vaiśyas, dvādaśī after dvādaśī, the demigods headed by Brahmā worship the Tulasī forest."

Text 114

ata eva-therefore; śrī-tulasī-stuti-mahimā-the glory of offering prayers to Tulasī; ananya-manasā-with unwavering mind; nityam-regularly; tulasīm-Tulasī; stauti-praises; yaḥ-who; naraḥ-a person; pitṛ-deva-manuṣyānām-of the pitas, devas, and human beings; priyaḥ-dear; bhavati-is; sarvadā-in all respects.

The Glories of Offering Prayers Praising Tulasī

A person who with an unwavering heart glorifies Tulasī with prayers becomes very dear to the devas, pitās, and human beings.

The Glory of the Tulasī Forest

In the Skanda Purāṇa it is said:

"In the age of Kali, Lord Hari, the master of the universes and demigods, is especially pleased by a Tulasī forest and by nothing else.

Text 116

hitvā-leaving aside; tīrtha-sahasrāṇi-thousands of holy places; sarvān-all; api-even; śiloccayān-mountains; tulasī- kānane-in a Tulasī forest; nityam-always; kalau-in kali-yuga; tiṣṭhati-stays; keśavaḥ-Lord Kṛṣṇa.

"Avoiding a thousand holy places and the chains of sacred mountains, in Kali-yuga Lord Kṛṣṇa stays always in a Tulasī forest.

Text 118

"Any place where there is no Deity of Lord Viṣṇu, no fruit-bearing dhātrī tree, no Tulasī forest, or no Vaiṣṇavas is like a cremation-ground.

Text 119

"Even if they are displeased with him, what can Yamarāja and his servants do to a person who in this world has

planted a Tulasī forest for Lord Kṛṣṇa's sake.

"One should plant Tulasī at the time of the star Śravaṇa. The Supreme Personality of Godhead forgives a thousand offenses for one who plants Tulasī in holy places and temples. Wherever Tulasī is planted, that place becomes sacred to Lord Viṣṇu.
