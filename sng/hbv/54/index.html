hbv.54

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 319-324
Translation

"O descendent of King Yadu, at the time of the nakñatras, beginning with Kåttikä, one should remmber the Lord by chanting His names of Viçva, Viñëu, Vañaö-kära, Bhüta-bhavya-bhavat-prabhu, Bhüta-bhåt, Bhüta-kåt, Bhäva, Bhütätmä, Bhüta-bhävana, Avyakta, Puëòarékäkña, Viçvakarmä, Çuciçravä, Sad-bhäva, Bhävana, Bhartä, Prabhava, Prabhu, Içvara, Aprameya, Håñékeça, Padmanäbha, Amara-prabhu, Agrähya, Çäçvata, Dhätä, Kåñëa. Texts 284 and 285 brahmäëaà çré-patià viñëuà kapilaà çrédharaà prabhum dämodaraà håñékeçaà govindaà madhusüdanam bhüdharaà gadinaà devaà çaìkhinaà padminaà tathä cakriëaà ca mahä-räja prathamädiñu saàsmaret brahmäëaà çré-patià viñëuà kapilaà çrédharaà prabhum dämodaraà håñékeçaà govindaà madhusüdanam bhüdharaà gadinaà devaà çaìkhinaà padminaà tathä cakriëam-these names; ca-and; mahä-räja-O great king; prathamädiñu-on the tithis beginning with pratipat; saàsmaret-should remember. "O great king, at the time of Pratipat and the other tithis, one should remember the Lord by chanting His names of Brahmä, Çrépati, Viñëu, Kapila, Çrédhara, Prabhu, Dämodara, Håñékeça, Govinda, Madhusüdana, Bhüdhara, Gadé, Deva, Çaìkhé, Padmé, and Cakré. Text 286 sarvaà vä sarvadä näma deva-devasya yädava sarvam-all; vä-or; sarvadä-in all times; näma-names; deva-devasya-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; yädava-O descendent of King Yadu. "O descendent of King Yadu, one may always chant all the names of the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Text 287 nämäni sarväëi janärdanasya kälaç ca sarvaù puruña-pravéraù tasmät sadä sarva-gatasya näma grähyaà yatheñöaà varadasya räjan nämäni-names; sarväëi-all; janärdanasya-of Lord Kåñëa; kälaù-time; ca-and; sarvaù-all; puruña-pravéraù-the most powerful person; tasmät-therefore; sadä-always; sarva-gatasya-everywhere; näma-name; grähyam-to be taken; yatheñöam- as desired; varadasya-of the giver of boons; räjan-O king. "An intelligent person will always chant all the names of Lord Kåñëa. O king, please chant the names of the all- pervading Supreme Personality of Godhead whenever you wish." Text 288 vividha-käma-siddhaye ca pulastyoktau

kämaù käma-pradaù käntaù käma-pälas tathä hariù änando mädhavaç caiva käma-saàsiddhaye japet vividha-käma-siddhaye-to attain various desires; ca-and; pulastyoktau-in the statement of Pulastya Muni; kämaù käma-pradaù käntaù käma-pälas tathä hariù änando mädhavaù-these names; ca-and; eva-indeed; käma-saàsiddhaye-to attain desires; japet-should chant. Chanting the Lord's Holy Name to Attain the Fulfillment of Various Desires Pulastya Muni explains: "To attain one's desire one should chant the names of Käma, Käma-prada, Känta, Käma-päla, Hari, Ananda, and Mädhava. Text 289 rämaù paraçurämaç ca nåsiàho viñëur eva ca vikramaç caivam ädéni japyäny ari-jigéñubhiù rämaù paraçurämaç ca nåsiàho viñëur eva ca vikramaù-these names; ca-and; evam-thus; ädéni-beginning; japyäni- should be chanted; ari-jigéñubhiù-by one who desires victory over his enemies. "One who desires victory over his enemies should chant names like Räma, Paraçuräma, Nåsiàha, Viñëu, and Vikrama. Text 290 vidyäm abhyastatä nityaà japtavyaù puruñottamaù dämodaraà bandha-gato nityam eva japen naraù vidyäm abhyastatäby one who studies to attain knowledge; nityam-always; japtavyaù-should be chanted; puruñottamaù-Puruñottama; dämodaram-Dämodara; bandha-gataù-by one in bonds; nityam-always; eva-indeed; japen- should chant; naraù-a person. "A student should always chant the name Puruñottama. A prisoner should always chant the name Dämodara. Text 291 keçavaà puëòarékäkñaà aniçaà hi tathä japet netra-bädhäsu sarväsu håñékeçaà bhayeñu ca keçavam-Keçava; puëòarékäkñam-Puëòarékäkña; aniçam-day and night; hi-indeed; tathä-so; japet-should chant; netra- bädhäsu-in eye troubles; sarväsu-all; håñékeçam-Håñékeça; bhayeñu-in fears; ca-and. "When there are eye troubles one should chant the names Keçava and Puëòarékäkña. When there are fears one should chant the name Håñékeça.

Text 292 acyutaà cämåtaà caiva japed auñadha-karmaëi saìgrämäbhimukho gacchan saàsmared aparäjitam acyutam-Acyuta; ca-and; amåtam-Amåta; ca-and; eva-indeed; japet-should chant; auñadha-karmaëi-in the actyion of medicine; saìgräma-battle; abhimukhaù-facing; gacchan-going; saàsmaret-should remember; aparäjitam-Aparäjita. "When taking medicine one should chant the names Acyuta and Amåta. When going into battle one should remember the Lord by chanting the name Aparäjita. Text 293 cakriëaà gadinaà caiva çärìginaà khaòginaà tathä kñemärthé pravasan nityaà dikñu pracyädiñu smaret cakriëam-Cakré; gadinam-Gadé; ca-and; eva-indeed; çärìginam-Çärìgé; khaòginam-Khaògé; tathä-so; kñema- auspiciousness; arthé-desiring; pravasan-going on a journey; nityam-always; dékñu-in trhe directions; pracyädiñu- beginning with the east; smaret-should remember. "A person desiring auspiciousness as he goes on a journey to the east or to another direction should always remember the Lord by chanting the names Cakré, Gadé, Çärìgé, and Khaògé. Text 294 ajitaà cädhipaà caiva sarvaà sarveçvaraà tathä saàsmaret puruño bhaktyä vyavahäreñu sarvadä ajitaà cädhipaà caiva sarvaà sarveçvaram-these names; tathä-so; saàsmaret-should remember; puruñaù-a person; bhaktyä-with devotion; vyavahäreñu-in ordinary activities; sarvadä-always. "In his ordinary activities a person should always remember the Lord with devotion by chanting the names Ajita, Adhipa, Sarva, and Sarveçvara. Text 295 näräyaëaà sarva-kälaà kñuta-praskhalanädiñu graha-nakñatra-péòañu deva-bädhäsu sarvataù näräyaëam-Näräyaëa; sarva-kälam-Sarva-käla; kñuta-praskhalanädiñu-in coughs, sneezes, stumbling, and similar troubles; graha-nakñatra-of planets and stars; péòañu-in trouble; deva-bädhäsu-in trouble from the demigods; sarvataù-in all respects. "When one sneezes, coughs, stumbles, is troubled in another way like that, or is troubled by the demigods or the stars and planets, one should always chant the name Näräyaëa. Text 296

dasyu-vairi-nirodheñu vyäghra-siàhädi-saìkaöe andhakäre tamas-tévre narasiàham anusmaret dasyu-theives; vairi-enemies; nirodheñu-in attacks; vyäghra-siàhädi-saìkaöe-in attacks by tigers, lions, or other ferocious beasts; andhakäre-in blinding darkness; tamas-tévre-in darkness; narasiàham-Narasiàha; anusmaret-should remember. "When attacked by thieves, enemies, tigers, lions, or other ferocious assailants, or when trapped in blinding darkness, one should remember the Lord by chanting the name Narasiàha. Text 297 agni-dähe samutpanne saàsarej jala-çäyinam garuòadhvajänusmaraëäd viña-véryaà vyapohati agni-dähe-fire; samutpanne-manifested; saàsmaret-one should remember; jala-çäyinam-Jalaçäyé; garuòadhvaja- Garuòadhvaja; anusmaraëädby remembering; viña-véryam-the power of poison; vyapohati-counteracts. "When trapped in blazing fire one should remember the Lord by chanting the name Jalaçäyé. By chanting the name Garuòadhvaja one counteracts the power of poison. Text 298 snäne devärcane home praëipäte pradakñiëe kértayed bhagavan-näma väsudeveti tat-paraù snäne-in bathing; devärcane-in worshiping the Lord; home-in a homa; praëipäte-in bowing down; pradakñiëe-in circumambulating; kértayet-should glorify; bhagavan-näma-the name of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; väsudeva- väsudeva; iti-thus; tat-paraù-devoted to the Lord. "While bathing, worshiping the Lord, offering homa, bowing down, or circumambulating, a devotee should chant the name Väsudeva and other names of the Lord. Text 299 sthäpane vitta-dhanyäder apadhyäne ca duñöaje kurvéta tan-manä bhütvä anantäcyuta-kértanam sthäpane-in establishment; vitta-of wealth; dhanya-and treasure; ädeù-beginning with; apadhyäne-in worry; ca-and; duñöaje-caused by the wicked; kurvéta-one should do; tan-manä-his mind; bhütvä-being; ananta-Ananta; acyuta-and Acyuta; kértanam-chanting. "When storing money or other valuables, or when made to worry by wicked men, one should chant the names Ananta and Acyuta. Text 300

näräyaëaà çärìga-dharaà çrédharaà puruñottamam vämanaà khaòginaà caiva duñöa-svapne sadä smaret näräyaëaà çärìga-dharaà çrédharaà puruñottamam vämanaà khaòginam-these names; ca-and; eva-indeed; duñöa- svapne-in a nightmare; sadä-alwasy; smaret-should remember. "When having a nightmare one should always remember the Lord by chanting the names Näräyaëa, Çärìga-dhara, Çrédhara, Puruñottama, Vämana, and Khaògé. Text 301 mahärëavädau paryaìka- çäyinaà ca naraù smaret balabhadraà samåddhy-arthaà sarva-karmaëi saàsmaret mahärëavädau-in places beginning with the great ocean; paryaìka-on a bed; çäyinam-reclining; ca-and; naraù-a pewrson; smaret-should remember; balabhadram-Balabhadra. samåddhy-artham-to attain success; sarva-karmaëi-in all actions; saàsmaret-should remember. "Whether in the midst of the great ocean, reclining on a couch, or in some other place, in order to succeed in all one's efforts, a person should remember the Lord by chanting the name Balabhadra. Text 302 jagat-patim apatyärthaà stuvan bhaktyä na sédati çrésaà sarväbhyudayike karmaëy äçu prakértyet jagat-patim-jagat-pati; apatyärtham-for the sake of good children; stuvan-praying; bhaktyä-with devotion; not-and; sédati-perishes; çrésam-Çréça; sarväbhyudayike karmaëi-in all auspicious activities; äçu-at once; prakértyet-should chant. "One who with devotion chants the name Jagat-pati and prays to beget good children will not disappointed. In all auspicious rituals and festivals one should chant the name Çréça. Text 303 ariñöeñu hy açeñeñu viçokaà ca sadä japet maru-prapätägni-jala- bandhanädiñu måtyuñu svatantra-paratantreñu väsudevaà japed budhaù ariñöeñu-in calamities; hi-indeed; açeñeñu-all; viçokam-Viçoka; ca-and; sadä-always; japet-should chant; maru-in a desert; prapäta-in a attack; agni-in fire; jala-in water; bandhana-in bondage; ädiñu-beginning with; måtyuñu-in death; svatantra-independent; paratantreñu-dependent on others; väsudevam-Vasudeva; japet-should chant; budhaù-intelligent. "In all calamities one should chant the name Viçoka. In a desert, attacked by others, in fire, in water, in bondage, on the verge of death, or in other troubles, whether free or in the grip of others, a wise person should chant the name Väsudeva.

Text 304 sarvärtha-çakti-yuktasya deva-devasya cakriëaù yathäbhirocate näma tat sarvärtheñu kértayet sarvärtha-çakti-yuktasya-with the power to fulfill all eishes; deva-devasya-of the master of the demigods; cakriëaù-who holds the cakra; yathä-as; abhirocate-onme desires; näma-the name; tat-that; sarvärtheñu-for all desires; kértayet-should chant. "To fulfill all desires, however one likes one should chant the names of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who is the master of the demigods, who carries the Sudarçana-cakra, and who has the power to grant all requests. Text 305 sarvärtha-siddhim äpnoti nämnäm ekärthatä yathä sarväëy etäni nämäni parasya brahmaëo hareù sarvärtha-siddhim-the fulfillment of all desires; äpnoti-obtains; nämnäm-of the names; ekärthatä-one desire; yathä-as; sarväëi-all; etäni-them; nämäni-names; parasya-of the Supreme; brahmaëaù-Brahman; hareù-Lord Kåñëa. "These are all names of Lord Kåñëa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead. A person who understands this one truth about these names attains all his desires." In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, in a conversation of Märkaëòeya Muni and King Vajra, it is said: "When crossing the waters one should remember the Lord by chanting the names of Kürma, Varäha, and Matysa. In the midst of a blazing fire one should chant these names without stop.
