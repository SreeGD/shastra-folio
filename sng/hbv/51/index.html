Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 301-306
Translation

Texts 222 and 223 tathä brahma-vaivarte yat-pädodakam ädhäya çivaù çirasi nåtyati yan-näbhi-nalinäd äséd brahmä loka-pitämahaù yad-icchä-çakti-vikñobhäd brahmäëòodbhava-saìkñayau tam ärädhaya govindaà sthänam ägryaà yadécchasi tathä-so; brahma-vaivarte-in Brahma-vaivarta Puräëa; yat-pädodakam-the water that has washed His feet; ädhäya- placing; çivaù-Lord çiva; çirasi-on his head; nåtyati-dances; yan-näbhi-nalinät-from the lotus of His navel; äsét-was; brahmä-Brahmä; loka-pitämahaù-the grandfather of the worlds; yad-icchä-çakti-vikñobhät-by the pwoer of whose desire; brahmäëòodbhava-saìkñayau-the creation and destruction of thr material universes; tam-Him; ärädhaya-worship; govindam-Lord Kåñëa; sthänam-the place; ägryam-transcendental; yadi-if; icchasi-you desire. In the Brahma-vaivarta Puräëa it is said: "If you wish to enter the spiritual world, then please worship Lord Govinda, by whose desire the material universes are created and destroyed, from the lotus of whose navel Brahmä, the grandfather of the worlds was born, the water of whose feet Lord Çiva sprinkles on his head as he dances." Text 224 närasiàhe märkaëòeya-sahasränéka-samväde yas tu sampüjayen nityaà narasiàha nareçvara sa svarga-mokña-bhägé syän nätra käryä vicäraëä närasiàhe-in the Nriàha Puräëa; märkaëòeya-sahasränéka-samväde-in the conversation of Markandeya and sahasranika; yaù-one who; tu-indeed; sampüjayet-may worship; nityam-always; narasiàha-O Lord Nåsiàha; nareçvara-O master of the living entites; sa-He; svarga-mokña-bhägé-the enjoyer of Svarga and liberation; syät-is; na-not; atra-here; käryä-to be done; vicäraëä-consideration. In the Nåsiàha Puräëa, in a conversation of Märkaëòeya and Sahasränéka it is said: "One who, calling out, `O Lord Nåsiàha! O master of the living entities!', regularly worships Lord Nåsiàha, will attain both Svargaloka and liberation. Of this there is no doubt. Text 225 tasmäd eka-manä bhütvä yaväj jévaà pratijïayä arcanän narasiàhasya sampräpnoty abhiväïchitam tasmät-therefore; eka-manä-with a single mind; bhütvä-becoming; yaväj-as long as; jévam-life; pratijïayä-with a vow; arcanän-from the worship; narasiàhasya-of Lord Nåsiàha; sampräpnoti-attains; abhiväïchitam-desire.

"A person who devotedly spends his entire life worshiping Lord Nåsiàha in this way will attain whatever he desires." Text 226 tatraiva çré-vyäsa-çuka-samväde çré-märkaëòeya-måtyuïjaya-samvädänantaraà narake pacyamänas tu yamena paribhäñitaù kià tvayä närcito devaù keçavaù kleça-näçanaù tatra-there; eva-inded; çré-vyäsa-çuka-samväde-in the conversation of Sri Vytas and Sri Suka; çré-märkaëòeya- måtyuïjaya-samvädänantaram-after a conversation of Sri Markandeya nd Lord Siva; narake-in hell; pacyamänaù-being cooked; tu-inded; yamena-by Yamaraja; paribhäñitaù-said; kim-why?; tvayä-by you; na-not; ärcitaù-worshiped; devaù-th Supreme Personality of Godhead; keçavaù-Lord Kåñëa; kleça-od sufferings; näçanaù-the destroyer. In the Nåsiàhe Puräëa, after a conversation of Lord Çiva and Märkaëòeya Muni, in a conversation of Çré Vyäsa and Çré Çukadeva Gosvämé, is the following statement: "They who do not worship Lord Kåñëa suffer in hell, where they are tortured by Yamaräja. Why, then, do you not worship Lord Kåñëa, the destroyer of sufferings? Text 227 udakenäpy aläbhe tu dravyänäà püjitaù prabhuù yo dadäti svakaà lokaà sa tvayä kià na püjitaù udakena-by water; api-also; aläbhe-in non-attainment; tu-indeed; dravyänäm-of things; püjitaù-worshiped; prabhuù- the Supreme Personality of Godhead; yaù-who; dadäti-gives; svakam-own; lokam-realm; sa-He; tvayä-by you; kim-why?; na-not; püjitaù-worshiped. "If other things are not available, one may worship Lord Kåñëa by offering Him a cup of water. Why do you not worship Him? Text 228 narasiàho håñékeçaù puëòaréka-nibhekñaëaù smaraëän mukti-do nåëäà sa tvayä kià na püjitaù narasiàhaù-Nåsima; håñékeçaù-the master of the senses; puëòaréka-nibhekñaëaù-His eyes like lotus petals; smaraëän- by remembering; mukti-daù-giving liberation; nåëäm-of the people; sa-He; tvayä-by you; kim-why?; na-not; püjitaù- worshiped. "To they who remember Him, lotus-eyed Lord Nåsiàha, the supreme master of the senses, easily gives liberation. Why do you not worship Him? Text 229 båhan-näradéye 'diti-mahätmye çré-sütoktau yatra püjä-paro viñëos tatra vighno na bädhate

räjä ca taskaraç cäpi vyädhayaç ca na santi hi båhan-näradéye-in Båhan-näradéya Puräëa; aditi-mahätmye-in the glories of Aditi; çré-sütoktau-in the statement of Çré Süta Gosvämé; yatra-where; püjä-to worship; paraù-devoted; viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; tatra-there; vighnaù-obstacle; na-not; bädhate-stops; räjä-king; ca-and; taskaraù-thief; ca-and; api-also; vyädhayaù-diseases; ca-and; na-not; santi-are; hi-indeed. In the Närada Puräëa, in the chapter on the glories of Aditi, Çré Süta Gosvämé explains: "When one is devoted to the worship of Lord Viñëu, no obstacles stand in his way. Kings, thieves, and diseases do not attack him. Text 230 pretäù piçäcäù kuñmäëòäù grahä bälagrahäs tathä däkiëyo räkñasäç caiva na bädhante 'cyutärcakam pretäù-pretas; piçäcäù-pisacas; kuñmäëòäù-kusmandas; grahä-grahas; bälagrahäù-balagrahas; tathä-so; däkiëyaù- dakinis; räkñasäù-raksasas; ca-and; eva-indeed; na-not; bädhante-stop; acyutärcakam-one who worships the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead. "Pretas, piçäcas, kuñmäëòas, grahas, bälagrahas, däkiëés, and räkñasas cannot attack a devotee of the infallible Personality of Godhead." Text 231 tatraiva yama-bhagératha-samväde patraiù puñpaiù phalair värcyä püjä-rahitam acyutam sa yäti viñëu-sälokyaà kula-saptati-samyutaù tatra-there; eva-indeed; yama-bhagératha-samväde-in a conversation of Yama and Bhagiratha; patraiù-with leaves; puñpaiù-flowers; phalaiù-fruits; vä-or; arcyä-to be worshiped; püjä-worship; rahitam-in a secluded place; acyutam-the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead; sa-he; yäti-goes; viñëu-sälokyam-to the same planet as Lord Viñëu; kula- saptati-samyutaù-with seventy generations of his relatives. In the Närada Puräëa, in a conversation of Yamaräja and Bhagératha it is said: "A person who in a secluded place worships Lord Viñëu with fruits, leaves, and flowers, travels, with seventy generations of his relatives, to the abode of Lord Viñëu." Text 232 tatraiva dhvajäropana-mahätmye çré-viñëu-dütänäm uktau utkränti-käle yan-näma çrutavanto 'pi vai sakåt labhante paramaà sthänaà kim u çuçrüñaëe rataù tatra-there; eva-indeed; dhvajäropana-mahätmye-in the Dhvajaropana-mahatmya; çré-viñëu-dütänäm-of the Viñëudutas; uktau-in the statement; utkränti-käle-at the time of dath; yan-näma-whose name; çrutavantaù-hearingt; api- even; vai-indeed; sakåt-once; labhante-attain; paramam-supreme; sthänam-destination; kim u-what?; çuçrüñaëe-with

faith; rataù-engaged. In the Närada Puräëa, in the Dhvajäropana-mähätmya chapter, the Viñëudütas explains: "They who at the time of hear a single utterance of the Lord's holy name go to the supreme spiritual abode. What need be said, then, of they who serve the Lord with faith?" Text 233 muhürtaà vä muhürtärdhaà yas tiñöhed dhari-mandire sa yäti paramaà sthänaà kim u çuçrüñaëe rataù muhürtam-a moment; vä-or; muhürtärdham-half a moment; yaù-one who; tiñöhet-stays; hari-mandire-in the temple of Lord Kåñëa; sa-he; yäti-goes; paramam-to the supreme; sthänam-abode; kim u-what?; çuçrüñaëe-with faith; rataù- engaged. "A person who for a moment, or even half a moment, visits a temple of Lord Kåñëa, goes to the supreme spiritual abode. What need be said, then, of they who serve the Lord with faith?" Text 234 tatraiva vibhaëòaka-muneù sumati-nåpaà prati avaçenäpi yat karma kåtaà tu su-mahat phalam dadäti nåëäà räjendra kià punaù samyag-arcanä tatra-there; eva-indeed; vibhaëòaka-muneù-of Vibhandaka Muni; sumati-nåpam-King Sumati; prati-to; avaçena- whimsically; api-also; yat-what; karma-deed; kåtam-done; tu-indeed; su-mahat-very great; phalam-result; dadäti-gives; nåëäm-of the human beings; räjendra-O great king; kim-what?; punaù-more; samyag-arcanä-properly engaged in worship. In the Närada Puräëa, Vibhaëòaka Muni tells King Sumati: "Even a person who on a whim does some service to the Lord attains a very great result. What need be said, then, of they who serve the Lord with with care?" Text 235 präyaçcitta-prakaraëänte samparkäd yadi vä mohäd yas tu püjayate harim sarva-päpa-vinirmuktaù sa yäti paramaà padam präyaçcitta-prakaraëänte-after the description of prayascitta; samparkät-because of contact; yadi-if; vä-or; mohät-by mistake; yaù-one who; tu-indeed; püjayate-worships; harim-Lord Kåñëa; sarva-päpa-vinirmuktaù-free of all sins; sa-he; yäti-goes; paramam-to the supreme; padam-abode. After the description of präyaçcitta it is said: "A person who, somehow or other, by meeting the devotees or by mistake, worships Lord Kåñëa, becomes free of all sins and goes to the supreme abode.

In answer to a question about Çré Viñëu-toña-vidhi it is said: "I will tell you the truth. I will tell you what is auspicious. Again and again I will tell what is the best thing to know: In this worthless world of birth and death the best thing is the worship of Lord Viñëu.
