hbv.59

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 349-354
Translation

Text 385 sarva-sat-karmädhikatvaà go-koöi-dänaà grahaëe khagasya prayäga-gaìgodaka-kalpa-väsaù yajïäyutaà meru-suvarëa-dänaà govinda-kérter na samaà çatäàçaiù sarva-sat-karmädhikatvam-better than all pious deeds; go-koöi-dänam-giving ten million cows; grahaëe-in acceptance; khagasyn thousand; meru-suvarëa-a heap of gold like Mount Meru; dänam-gift; govinda-kérteù-of the glories of Lord Govinda; na-not; samam-equal; çatäàçaiù-with a hundred part. Chanting the Holy Name Is More Important Than Performing Pious Deeds Giving ten-million cows in charity during a solar eclipse, staying for a kalpa in the Gaìgä's waters at Prayäga, performing ten-thousand yajïas, and giving in charity a Mount Meru of gold are not equal to a hundredth part of chanting Lord Govinda's holy names. Text 386 baudhäyana-saàhitäyäm iñöäpürtäni karmäëi su-bahüni kåtäny api bhava-hetüni täny eva harer näma tu muktidam baudhayana-saàhitäyäm-in the Baudhäyana-saàhitä; iñöäpürtäni-iñöäpürta; karmäëi-deeds; su-bahüni-many; kåtäni- done; api-even; bhava-hetüni-the causes of repeated birth; täni-they; eva-indeed; hareù-of Lord Hari; näma-the name; tu- indeed; muktidam-the giver of liberation. In the Baudhäyana-saàhitä it is said: "Many pious deeds bring only repeated birth. Lord Hari's holy name brings liberation." Text 387 gäruòe çré-çaunakämbaréña-samväde väjapeya-sahasräëäà nityaà phalam abhépsasi prätar utthäya bhupäla kuru govinda-kértanam gäruòe-in the Garuòa Puräëa; çré-çaunakämbaréña-samväde-in a conversation of Çré Çaunaka Muni and King Ambaréña; väjapeya-sahasräëäm-of a thousand vajapeya-yajnas; nityam-always; phalam-result; abhépsasi-you desire; prätaù-in the moring; utthäya-rising; bhupäla-O king; kuru-do; govinda-kértanam-the chanting of Lord Govinda's holy names. In the Garuòa Puräëa, Çré Çaunaka Muni tells King Ambaréña: "O king, if you wish to attain the benefit of performing a thousand väjapeya-yajïas, then rise early in the morning and chant Lord Govinda's holy names. Text 388 kià kariñyati saìkhyena

kià yogair nara-näyaka muktim icchasi räjendra kuru govinda-kértanam kim-what is the need?; kariñyati-will so; saìkhyena-of sankhya philosophy; kim-what is the need?; yogaiù-of yoga; nara-näyaka-O king; muktim-liberation; icchasi-you desire; räjendra-O great king; kuru-do; govinda-kértanam-the chanting of Lord Govinda's holy names. "What is the use of studying the saìkhya philosophy? What is the use of practicing yoga? O king, if you desire liberation, please chant Lord Govinda's holy names." Text 389 tåtéya-skandhe çré-kapiladevaà prati devahüty-uktau aho bata çvapaco 'to garéyän yaj-jihvägre vartate näma tubhyam tepus tapas te juhuvur sasnur äryä brahmänücur näma gåhnanti ye te tåtéya-skandhe-in the Third Canto; çré-kapiladevaà prati-to Lord kapiladeva; devahüty-uktau-in the words of Devahüti; aho bata-Oh, how glorious; çvapacaù-a dogeater; ataù-hence; garéyän-worshipable; yat-of whom; jihvägre-on the tip of the tongue; vartate-is; näma-the holy name; tubhyam-unto you; tepus tapaù-practiced austerities; te-they; juhuvuù-executed fire-sacrifices; sasnuù-took bath in the sacred rivers; aryä-Aryans; brahmänücuù-studied the Vedas; näma-the holy name; gåhnanti-accept; ye-they who; te-Your. In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (3.33.7), Devahüti tells Lord Kapiladeva: "Oh, how glorious are they whose tongues are chanting Your holy name! Even if born in the families of dogeaters, such persons are worshipable. Persons who chant the holy name of Your Lordship must have executed all kinds of austerities and fire-sacrifices and achieved all the good manners of the Aryans. To be chanting the holy name of Your Lordship, they must have bathed at holy places of pilgrimage, studied the Vedas and fulfilled everything required."* Text 390 sarva-tértha-pradatvaà skände brahma-närada-samväde cäturmasya-mähätmye etat ñaò-varga-haraëaà ripu-nigrahaëaà param adhyätma-mülam etad dhi viñëor nämänukértanam sarva-tértha-pradatvam-giving the result of all pilgrimages; skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; brahma-närada-samväde-in a conversation of Narada and Brahma; cäturmasya-mähätmye-in the glories of Caturmasya; etat-this; ñaò-varga-haraëam- removing the six distresses; ripu-nigrahaëam-destriying enemies; param-supreme; adhyätma-mülam-the root of spiritual knowledge; etat-this; dhi-indeed; viñëoù-of Lord v; nämänukértanam-the chanting of the holy names. Chanting the Holy Name Gives the Benefit of All Pilgrimages In the Skanda Puräëa, Cäturmäsya-mähätmya, in a conversation of Brahmä and Närada, it is said: "Chanting the holy names of Lord Viñëu removes the six distresses, thwarts enemies, and sows the seed of transcendental knowledge." Text 391

viñëu-dharmottare hådi kåtvä tathä kämaà abhiñöaà dvija-puìgaväù ekaà näma japed yas tu çataà kämän aväpnuyät viñëu-dharmottare-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa; hådi-in the heart; kåtvä-doing; tathä-so; kämam- desire; abhiñöam-desired; dvija-puìgaväù-O best of brähmaëas; ekam-one; näma-name; japet-may chant; yaù-who; tu- indeed; çatam-a hundred; kämän-desires; aväpnuyät-attains. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, it is said: "O best of brähmaëas, a person who once chants the Lord's holy name attains a hundred of the desires in his heart." Text 392 tatraiva çré-kåñëämåta-stotre sarva-maìgala-maìgalyam äyuñyaà vyädhi-näçanam bhukti-mukti-pradaà divyaà väsudevasya kértanam tatra-there; eva-indeed; çré-kåñëämåta-stotre-in the Çré Kåñëämåta-stotra; sarva-maìgala-maìgalya-the auspiciousness of allvyam-transcendental; väsudevasya-of Lord Väsudeva; kértanam-the glory. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, in the Çré Kåñëämåta-stotra, it is said: "The chanting of Lord Kåñëa's holy name brings long life, the cure of disease, happiness, liberation, and the best of all auspiciousness." Text 393 çré-näräyaëa-vyüha-stave parihäsopähasädyair viñëor gåhëanti näma te kåtärthas te 'pi manujäs tebhyo 'péha namo namaù çré-näräyaëa-vyüha-stave-in the Çré Näräyaëa-vyüha-stava; parihäsa-joking; upähasa-mocking; ädyaiù-beginning with; viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; gåhëanti-chant; näma-the holy name; te-they; kåtärthaù-successful; te-they; api-also; manujäù- persons; tebhyaù-to them; api-laos; iha-here; namo namaù-obeisnaces. In the Çré Näräyaëa-vyüha-stava it is said: "They who, joking, mocking, or in any other way, chant the holy names of Lord Viñëu are all-successful and all- perfect in this world. I bow down to offer my respectful obeisances to them." Text 394 värähe ca te dhanyäs te kåtärthäç ca tair eva sukåtaà kåtam tair äptaà janmanaù präpyaà ye kale kértayanti mäm värähe-in the Varäha Puräëa; ca-and; te-they; dhanyäù-fortunate; te-they; kåtärthäù-successful; ca-and; taiù-by them;

eva-indeed; sukåtam-pious deeds; kåtam-done; taiù-by them; äptam-attained; janmanaù-of birth; präpyam-to be attained; ye-who; kale-in time; kértayanti-glorify; mäm-Me. In the Varäha Puräëa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead declares: "they who chant My holy names are all-successful and all-perfect. They have performed all pious deeds. They have attained the goal of life." Text 395 viçeñataù kalau sakåd uccärayanty etad durlabhaà cäkåtätmanäm kalau yuge harer näma te kåtärthä na saàçayaù viçeñataù-specifically; kalau-inj the kali-yuga; sakåt-once; uccärayanti-chant; etat-thsi; durlabham-rare; ca-and; äkåtätmanäm-of they who have not performed pious deeds; kalau-in Kali; yuge-yuga; hareù-of Lord Hari; näma-the name; te-they; kåtärthä-successful; na-no; saàçayaù-doubt. Chanting the Holy Name Is Especially Beneficial in the Kali-yuga All-successful and all-perfect are they who in the Kali-yuga once chant the Lord's holy name, which cannot be chanted without having performed many pious deeds. Of this there is no doubt. Text 396 ekädaça-skandhe ca kalià sabhäjayanty aryä guëa-jïäù sära-bhäginaù yatra saìkértanenaiva sarvaù svärtho 'bhilabhyate ekädaça-skandhe-in the Eleventh Canto; ca-and; kalim-the age of Kali; sabhäjayanti-thye praise; aryä-progressive souls; guëa-jïäù-who know the true value (of the age); sära-bhäginaù-who are able to pick out the essence; yatra-in which; saìkértanena-by the congregational chanting of the holy name of the Supreme Lord; eva-merely; sarvaù-all; svärthaù- desired goals; abhilabhyate-are attained. In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (11.5.36) it is said: "They who are advanced and highly qualified and are interested in the essence of life, know the good qualities of Kali- yuga. Such people worship the age of Kali because in this age, simply by chanting the Hare Kåñëa mahä-mantra, one can advance in spiritual knowledge and attain life's goal."* Text 397 skände tatraiva tathä caivottamaà loke tapaù çré-hari-kértanam kalau yuge viçeñena viñëu-prétyai samäcaret skände-In the Skanda Puräëa; tatra-there; eva-indeed; tathä-so; ca-and; eva-indeed; uttamam-supreme; loke-in this world; tapaù-austerities; çré-hari-of Lord Kåñëa; kértanam-chanting thr holy names; kalau-in kali; yuge-yuga; viçeñena- specifically; viñëu-prétyai-to please Lord Viñëu; samäcaret-one should perform.

In the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "In this world the supreme austerity is chanting the holy names of Lord Hari. Especially in kali-yuga, to please Lord Hari one should chant His holy names. Texts 398 and 399 sarva-çaktimattvam skände däna-vrata-tapas-tértha- kñeträdénäà ca yaù sthitaù çaktayo deva-mahatäà sarva-päpa-haräù çubhäù räjasüyäçvamedhänäà jïänasyädhyätma-vastunaù äkåñya hariëä sarväù sthäpitäù sveñu nämasu sarva-çaktimattvam-having all powers; skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; däna-charity; vrata-vows; tapaù-austerity; tértha- pilgrimage; kñetra-places; ädénäm-beginning wqith; ca-and; yaù-one who; sthitaù-situated; çaktayaù-powers; deva-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; mahatäm-of ther devotees; sarva-päpa-all sins; haraù-removing; çubhaù-auspicious; räjasüya-açvamedhänäm-of räjasüya and açvamedha-yajnas; jïänasya adhyätma-vastunaù-of transcendental knowledge; äkåñya-extracting; hariëä-by Lord Kåñëa; sarvaù-all; sthäpitaù-placed; sveñu-in His own; nämasu-names. The Holy Name Is All-powerful In the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "Lord Kåñëa has extracted from charity, vows, austerities, pilgrimages, the worship of the Lord, the worship of His devotees, räjasüya-yajïas, açvamedha yajïas, and transcendental knowledge all their auspicious powers to remove sins, and placed those powers in His holy names. Text 400 väto 'py ato harer nämnä ugräëäm api duùsahaù sarveñäà päpa-räçénäà yathaiva tamasäà raviù vä-or; ataù-from that; api-also; ataù-from that; hareù-of Lord Kåñëa; nämnä-by the name; ugräëäm-terrible; api-even; duùsahaù-unbearable; sarveñäm-of all; päpa-räçénäm-sins; yathä-as; eva-indeed; tamasäm-of darkness; raviù-the sun. "As the sun destroys darkness, so Lord Kåñëa's holy name destroys a host of terrible sins." ata eva-therefore; brahmäëde-in the Brahmanada Puräëa; sarvärtha-çakti-yuktasya-all-powerful; deva-devasya-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; cakriëaù-who holds the cakra; yat-what; ca-and; abhirucitam-desired; näma-name; tat- that; sarvärtheñu-in all desires; yojayet-may be employed. In the Brahmäëòa Puräëa it is said: "The Supreme Personality of Godhead, who is the master of the demigods, who hold the Sudarçana-cakra, and who has the power to fulfill all desires, has placed in His holy name the power to fulfill all desires." Chanting the Holy Name Makes One Honored by the Whole World In the Närada Puräëa it is said: "Honored everywhere are they who always chant, `Näräyaëa! Jagannätha, Väsudeva! Janärdana!'
