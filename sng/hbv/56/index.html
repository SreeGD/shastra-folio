In the Padma Puräëa, Vaiçäkha-mähätmya, at the end of the story of Devaçarmä, Närada Muni explains: "The dear holy name of Lord Govinda at once destroys the sinful reactions of ten thousand murders, a thousand drunken binges, ten million adulteries with the spiritual master's wife, and very many thefts. In the Padma Puräëa, in a conversation of Yamaräja and a brähmaëa, it is said: "By chanting the holy name of unlimitedly powerful Lord Kåñëa, all sins perish, as darkness perishes at sunrise." "O best of brähmaëas, I do not any way for the living entities to atone for all their sins, any way other than chanting the holy names of Lord Kåñëa."

In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (6.2.7, 6.2.9-11, 6.2.14-15, and 6.2.18), in the story of Ajämila, it is said: "Ajämila has already atoned for all his sinful actions. Indeed, he has atoned not only for the sins performed in one life but for those performed in millions of lives, for in a helpless condition he chanted the holy name of Näräyaëa. Even though he did not chant purely, he chanted without offense, and therefore he is now pure and eligible for liberation."* Texts 332 and 333 stenaù suräpo mitra-dhrug brahma-hä guru-talpa-gaù stré-räja-pitå-go-hantä ye ca pätakino 'pare sarveñäm apy aghavataà idam eva su-niskåtam näma-vyäharaëaà viñëor yatas tad-viñayä matiù stenaù-one who steals; suräpaù-a drunkard; mitra-dhrug-one who turns against a friend or relative; brahma-hä-one who kills a brähmaëa; guru-talpa-gaù-one who indulges in sex with the wife of his teacher or guru; stré-women; räja- king; pitå-father; gaù-cows; hantä-the killer; ye-those who; ca-also; pätakinaù-committed sinful actiivities; apare-many others; sarveñäm-of all of them; api-although; aghavatam-persons who have committed many sins; idam-this; eva- certainly; su-niskåtam-perfect atonement; näma-vyäharaëam-chanting of the holy name; viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; yataù- because of which; tad-viñayä-on the person who chants the holy name; matiù-His attention. "The chanting of the holy name of Lord Viñëu is the best process of atonement for a thief of gold or other valuables, for a drunkard, for one who betrays a friend or relative, or for one who indulges in sex with the wife of his guru or other superior. It is also the best method of atonement for one who murders women, the king, or his father, for one who slaughters cows, and for all other sinful men. Simply by chanting the holy name of Lord Viñëu, such sinful persons may attract the attention of the Supreme Lord, who therefore considers, `Because this man has chanted My holy name, My duty is to give him protection.'* Text 334 na niñkåtair uditair brahma-vädibhis tathä viçuddhaty aghavän vratädibhiù yathä harer näma-padair udähåtais tad-uttamaçloka-guëopalambhakam na-not; niñkåtaiù-by the process of atonement; uditaiù-prescribed; brahma-vädibhiù-by learned scholars such as Manu; tathä-to that extent; viçuddhati-becomes purified; aghavän-a sinful man; vratädibhiù-by observing vows and regulative principles; yathä-as; hareù-of Lord hari; näma-padaiù-by the syllables of ther holy name; udähåtaiù-chanted; tat- that;uttamaçloka-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; guëa-of the transcendental qualities; upalambhakam-reminding

one. "By following the Vedic ritualistic ceremonies or undergoing atonement, sinful men do not become as purified as by chanting once the holy name of Lord Hari. Although ritualistic atonement may free one from sinful reactions, it does not awaken devotional service, unlike the chanting of the Lord's names, which reminds one of the Lord's fame, qualities, attributes, pastimes, and paraphernalia."* Text 335 saìkeöyaà parihäsyaà vä stobhaà helanam eva vä vaikuëöha-näma-grahaëaà açeñägha-haraà viduù saìkeöyam-as an assignation; parihäsyam-jokingly; vä-or; stobham-as musical entertainment; helanam-neglectfully; eva-certainly; vä-or; vaikuëöha-of the Lord; näma-grahaëam-chanting the holy name; açeña-unlimited; agha-haram- neutralizing the effect of sinful life; viduù-advanced transcendentalists know. "One who chants the holy name of the Lord is immediately freed form the reactions of unlimited sins, even if he chants indirectly (to indicate something else), jokingly, for musical entertainment, or even neglectfully. This is accepted by all the learned scholars of the scriptures.* Text 336 patitaù skhalito bhagnaù sandañöas tapta ähataù harir ity avaçenäha pumän närhati yätanaù patitaù-fallen down; skhalitaù-slipped; bhagnaù-having broken his bones; sandañöaù-bitten; tapta-severely attacked by fever or similar painful conditions; ähataù-injured; hariù-Lord Kåñëa; iti-thus; avaçena-accidentally; äha-chants; pumän-a person; na-not; arhati-deserves; yätanaù-hellish conditions. "If one chants the holy name of Lord Hari and then dies because of an accidental misfortune, such as falling from the top of a house, slipping and suffering broken bones while traveling on the road, being bitten by a serpent, being afflicted with pain and high fever, or being injured by a weapon, one is immediately absolved from having to enter hellish life, even though he is sinful."* Text 337 ajïänäd athavä jïanäd uttamaù-sloka-näma yat saìkértitam aghaà puàso dahed edho yathänalaù ajïänät-out of ignorance; athavä-or; jïanät-with knowledge; uttamaçloka-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; näma-the name; yat-that which; saìkértitam-chanted; agham-sin; puàsaù-of a person; dahet-burns to ashes; edhaù-grass; yathä-just as; analaù-fire. In the Skanda Puräëa the result of chanting the holy name in the Kali-yuga is described in these words: "In the Kali-yuga there is no sin, either of action, words, or mind, that cannot be destroyed by chanting the holy name of Lord Govinda." In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa , it is said: "As water has the power to stop fire, and as the sunrise has the power to stop darkness, so the chanting of Lord Kåñëa's holy name has the power to stop a flood of sins.
