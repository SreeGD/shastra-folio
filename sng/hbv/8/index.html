Translation

na tatra sañcariṣyanti vyādhi-durbhikṣa-taskarāḥ

yatra—where; aṣṭākṣara-saāsiddhaḥ—perfect in the eight-syllable mantra; mahā-bhāgaḥ—very fortunate; mahīyate— is glorified; na—not; tatra—there; sañcariṣyanti—will go; vyādhi-durbhikṣa-taskarāḥ—dosease, poevrty, and thieves.

"A country that honors a person perfect in chanting the eight-syllable mantra will not be touched by disease, poverty, or theft.

Text 133

deva-dānava-gandharvāḥ—the demigods, demons, and gandharvas; siddha-vidyadharādayaḥ—the beings headed by siddhas and vidyadharas; praṇamanti—bow down; mahā-mānam—to the exalted person; aṣṭākṣara-vidam—who knows the eight-syllable mantra; naram—person.

"The Supreme Personality of Godhead, Lord Nārāyaṇa, personally appears in the mouth as the eight-syllable mantra."

"O son, the mantra Namo Nārāyaṇāya fulfills all desires and gives all kinds of liberation to they who chant it with devotion.

The Glory of the Regal Nṛsiāha-mantra in the Meter Anuṣṭubh

In the Nṛsiāha-tāpanī Upaniṣad it is said:

"The demigods approached Lord Brahmā and said: Please describe to us the result of chanting the regal Nṛsiāha- mantra in the meter anuṣṭubh. "Lord Brahmā replied: One who regularly chants the regal Nṛsiāha-mantra in the meter anuṣṭubh becomes pure like the demigods. He becomes pure like fire. He becomes pure like the wind. He becomes pure like the sun. He becomes pure like the moon. He becomes pure like the truth. He becomes pure like Brahmā. He becomes pure like Viṣṇu. He becomes pure like Śiva. He becomes purified of all sins."
