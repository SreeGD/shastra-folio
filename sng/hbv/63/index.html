hbv.63

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 373-378
Translation & commentary

"Neither Brahmā nor Śeṣa are able to describe the great result earned by a person who once says the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa."

Text 473

In the Viṣṇu-rahasya, the Supreme Personality of Godhead explains

"O people, I raise My arms and speak to you the truth. To whoever every day, or at the time of death chants japa, calling to Me, `O Mukunda, O Narasiāha! O Janārdana!' I become a debtor. I fulfill his desires. I give him something that makes all material things seem like a cluster of pebbles or twigs."

The Glory of Remembering the Holy Name

In the Itihāsottama it is said

"A person who in a dream somehow remembers Lord Kṛṣṇa's holy names destroys a multitude of sins. What, then, is the fate of one who carefully chants Lord Kṛṣṇa's holy names?"
