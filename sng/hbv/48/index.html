çré-kürma-puräëe na viñëv-ärädhanät puëyaà vidyate karma vaidikam tasmäd anädi-madhyäntaà nityam ärädhayed dharim atha-now; çré-bhagavad-arcana-mahätmyam-the glory of worshiping the Supreme Personality of Godhead; çré-kurma- puräëe-in the Kürma Puräëa; na-not; viñëv-arädhänät-than the worship of Lord Viñëu; puëyam-piety; vidyate-is; karma- work; vaidikam-Vedic; tasmät-from that; anädi-without beginning; madhya-middle; antam-or end; nityam-eternal; arädhäyet-should worship; dharim-Lord Hari. The Glory of Worshiping the Supreme Personality of Godhead In the Kürma Puräëa it is said: "No work described in the Vedas is more sacred that the worship of Lord Viñëu. Therefore one should always worship Lord Viñëu, who has no beginning, middle, or end." Text 177 tatraiva bhågv-ädén prati säkñät çré-bhagavad-uktau ye 'rcayiñyanti mäà bhaktyä nityaà kali-yuge dvijäù vidhinä veda-dåñöena te gamiñyanti tat-padam tatra-there; eva-indeed; bhågv-ädén-to the sages headed by Brhgu Muni; prati-to; säkñät-directly; çré-bhagavad-uktau-in the words of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; ye-who; arcayiñyanti-will worship; mäm-Me; bhaktyä-with devotion; nityam-always; kali-yuge-in Kali-yuga; dvijäù-O brähmaëas; vidhinä-properly; veda-dåñöena-following the Vedas; te-they; gamiñyanti-will go; tat-padam-to that abode. In the Kürma Puräëa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead explains to Bhågu and the sages: "O brähmaëas, in the Kali-yuga they who again and again properly worship Me, following the rules of the Vedas, will go to My transcendental abode." Text 178 viñëu-rahasye çré-viñëor arcanaà ye tu prakurvanti narä bhuvi te yänti çäçvataà viñëor änandaà paramaà padam. iti. viñëu-rahasye-in the Viñëu-rahasya; çré-viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; arcanam-worship; ye-who; tu-indeed; prakurvanti-do; narä-people; bhuvi-on the earth; te-they; yänti-go; çäçvatam-eternal; viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; änandam-bliss; paramam- supreme; padam.-abode; iti-thus. In the Viñëu-rahasya it is said: "They who worship Lord Viñëu will go to His eternal and blissful abode."
