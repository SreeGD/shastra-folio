hbv.53

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 313-318
Translation

mätåvat parirakñantaà såñöi-saàhära-kärakam yo närcayati deveçaà taà vidyäd brahma-ghätakam atha-now; püjä-of worship; nityatä-the eternality; mahäbhärate-in the Mahäbhärata; mätåvat-like a mother; parirakñantam-protectingt; såñöi-creation; saàhära-destruction; kärakam-the cause; yaù-one who; na-not; arcayati- worships; deveçam-the master of the demigods; tam-Him; vidyät-should know; brahma-ghätakam-a killer of a brähmaëa. The Supreme Personality of Godhead Should Always Be Worshiped In the Mahäbhärata it is said: "Please know that a person who will not worship the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who is the master of the demigods and the creator and destroyer of the worlds, and who protects everyone as of He were their mother, is sinful like a brähmaëa-killer." Text 263 ata evoktaà brhan-näradéye padodaka-mähätmyäkhyänärambhe hari-püjä-vidhänäà tu yasya veñmäni no dvijäù çmaçäna-sadåçaà vidyän na kadäpi viçec ca tat ata eva-therefore; uktam-said; brhan-näradéye-in the Närada Puräëa; padodaka-mähätmyäkhyänärambhe-in the beginning of the Padoadaka-mahatmya; hari-püjä-vidhänam-the worship of Lord Viñëu; tu-indeed; yasya-of whom; veñmäni-in the home; naù-not; dvijaù-a brähmaëa; çmaçäna-a funeral ground; sadåçam-like; vidyän-should know; na-not; kadäpi-ever; viçet-should enter; ca-and; tat-that. In the Närada Puräëa, at the beginning of the Pädodaka-mähätmya chapter, it is said: "O brähmaëas, please know that the home of someone who does not worship Lord Viñëu is like a cremation ground. One should never enter such a home." Text 264 ata evoktaà viñëu-dharmottare puñpair vä yadi vä patraiù phalair vä yadi vämbubhiù yañöavyaù puëòarékäkñas tyaktvä kärya-çatäni ca ata eva-thus; uktam-said; viñëu-dharmottare-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khanda; puñpaiù-with flowers; vä-or; yadi-if; vä-or; patraiù-with leaves; phalaiù-with fruits; vä-or; yadi-if; vä-or; ambubhiù-with water; yañöavyaù-should be worshiped; puëòarékäkñaù-the lotus-eyed Supreme Personality of Godhead; tyaktvä-abandoning kärya-çatäni-hundreds of ordinary duties; ca-and. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, it is said: "Forget a hundred material duties and instead worship the lotus-eyed Supreme Personality of Godhead with flowers, or with leaves, or with fruit, or with water." Text 265 kià ca näradéye

nimitteñu ca sarveñu tat-tat-käla-viçeñataù püjayed deva-deveçaà dravyaà sampadya yatnataù kià ca-furthermore; näradéye-in the Närada Puräëa; nimitteñu-in reasons; ca-and; sarveñu-all; tat-tat-käla-viçeñataù- according to various times; püjayet-should worship; deva-deveçam-the master of the demigods; dravyam-thing; sampadya-attaining; yatnataù-with care. In the Närada Puräëa it is also said: "On special holy days one should carefully collect many gifts and offer them in the worship of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, the master of the demigods." Text 266 ata evoktaà bhagavatä hayagréveëa hayaçérña-païcarätre pratiñöitärcä na tyajyä yävaj jévaà samarcayet varaà präëasya vä tyägaù çiraso väpi karttanam ata eva-therefore; uktam-said; bhagavatä-by the Supreme Personality of Godhead; hayagréveëa-Lord hayagréva; hayaçérña-païcarätre-in the hayasirsa-pancaratra; pratiñöita-established; arcä-worship; na-not; tyajyä-be be abandoned; yävaj-as long as; jévam-life; samarcayet-should worship; varam-best; präëasya-of life; vä-or; tyägaù-abandonment; çirasaù- of the head; vä-or; api-even; karttanam-cutting. In the Hayaçérña-païcarätra Lord Hayagréva explains: "When the Deity of the Lord is installed the worship of the Deity should never be stopped. One should worship as long as he lives. Better than stopping the Lord's worship is to give up one's life or cut off one's head." Text 267 püjäyä nityatälekhi prak ca naivedya-bhakñaëe mähätmyaà ca paraà çäla- gräma-cakra-prasaìgataù püjäyä-of worship; nityatä-eternality; alekhi-I wrote; prak-before; ca-and; naivedya-bhakñaëe-in eating food offered to the Lord; mähätmyam-the glory; ca-and; param-then; çälagräma-cakra-prasaìgataù-in connection with the Çälagräma and the cakra. Previously, first in discussing the eating of food offered to the Lord, and then in discussing the Çälagräma and cakra stones, I also explained that one should always worship the Lord. Text 268 püjäìgänäà ca mähätmyaà yad yad vilikhitaà purä tat sarvam iha püjäyäà paryavasyati hi svataù püjäìgänäm-of the different parts of worship; ca-and; mähätmyam-the glory; yad yat-whatever; vilikhitam-written; purä-before; tat-that; sarvam-all; iha-here; püjäyäm-in worship; paryavasyati-is completed; hi-here; svataù-of itself.

I have already written of the glories of the different aspects of Deity-worship. Thus the discussion of Deity-worship has come to a close. Text 269 püjä-mahima-mattebhaù çästräraëya-vihäriëaù kéöena kati saìgrähyäù prabhävaà çré-harer vinä püjä-mahima-mattebhaùintoxicated by the glories of the worship, of Lord Kåñëa; çästra-of the scriptures; äraëya-in the forest; vihäriëaù--enjoying pastimes; kéöena-by an insect; kati-how much?; saìgrähyäù-can be accumulated; prabhävam- the power; çré-hareù-of Lord Kåñëa; vinä-without. Without the mercy of Lord Kåñëa, how much can this tiny insect, intoxicated by the nectar glories of the worship of Lord Kåñëa, collect as it flies here and there in the forest of the Vedic literatures? Text 270 atha çré-bhagavan-näma sadä seveta sarvataù tan-mähätmyaà ca vikhyätaà saìkñepeëätra likhyate atha-now; çré-bhagavat-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; näma-the holy name; sadä-always; seveta-should serve; sarvataù-in all respects; tan-mähätmyam-its glories; ca-and; vikhyätam-fame; saìkñepeëa-briefly; atra-here; likhyate- is written. One should always and in every way serve the holy name of the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Now in this book we will briefly write of the holy name's glories. Text 271 atha çré-bhagavan-näma-mähätmyam tatra çré-bhagavan-näma- viçeñasya ca sevanam åñibhiù kåpayädiñöaà tat-tat-käma-hatätmanäm atha-now; çré-bhagavan-näma-mähätmyam-the glories of the holy name; tatra-there; çré-bhagavan-näma-of the Lord's holy name; viçeñasya-sepcifically; ca-and; sevanam-service; åñibhiù-bythe sages; kåpayä-by the mercy; ädiñöam-indicated; tat-tat-käma-various material desires; hata-killed; ätmanäm-of people. The Glories of the Lord's Holy Name Out of kindness to they who have been destroyed by a host of material desires, the great sages have taught about service to the Lord's holy name. Text 272 atha käma-viçeñena çré-bhagavan-näma-viçeña-seva-mähätmyam tatra papa-kñayärthaà

çré-kaurme çré-çabda-pürvaà jaya-çabda-madhyaà jaya-dvayäd uttaratas tathä hi triù-sapta-kåtvo narasiàha-näma japtaà nihanyäd api vipra-hatyäm atha-now; käma-viçeñena-with specific desire; çré-bhagavan-näma-viçeña-seva-mähätmyam-the glori of service to the Lord's Holy Name; tatra-there; papa-kñayärtham-the destruction of sin; çré-kaurme-in Çré Kürma Puräëa; çré-çabda- pürvam-preceded by the word Çré ; jaya-çabda-teh word jaya; madhyam-in the middle; jaya-dvayät-from two jayas; uttarataù-after; tathä-then; hi-indeed; triù-sapta-kåtvaù-21 times; narasiàha-näma-the name opf Lord Nåsiàha; japtam- cahnted; nihanyät-kills; api-also; vipra-hatyäm-the sin of killing a brähmaëa. The Glories of Serving the Lord's Holy Name In Order to Attain Specific Goals Chanting the Lord's Holy Name to Become Free of Sins In the Kürma Puräëa it is said: "Saying the word Çré first, with the word Jaya in the middle, and then the word Jaya twice, by chanting the holy mantra of Lord Nåsiàha twenty-one times, one becomes free of the sin of killing a brähmaëa." Commentary by Çréla Sanätana Gosvämé The mantra chanted here is: Çré Nåsiàha, Jaya Nåsiàha, Jaya Jaya Nåsiàha. Text 273 mahä-bhaya-niväraëärthaà tatraiva çré-pürvo narasiàho dvir jayäd uttaratas tu saù triù-säpta-kåtvo japato mahä-bhaya-niväraëaù mahä-bhaya-niväraëärtham-to become free from great fears; tatra-there; eva-indeed; çré-pürvaù-with Çré first; narasiàhaù-Nåsiàha; dviù-twice; jayät-from jaya; uttarataù-then; tu-indeed; saù-he; triù-säpta-kåtvaù-twenty-one times; japataù-chanting; mahä-bhaya-great fear; niväraëaù-stopping. Chanting the Lord's Holy Name to Become Free of Great Fears In the Kürma Puräëa it is also said: es, one becomes free of great fears." Commentary by Çréla Sanätana Gosvämé The mantra here is: Çré Nåsiàha Jaya Jaya Nåsiàha. Text 274 käla-viçeñe tu maìgalärthaà

viñëu-dharmottare märkaëòeya-vajra-samväde puruñaà väsudevaà ca tathä saìkarñaëaà vibhum pradyumnam aniruddhaà ca kramäd abdeñu kértayet käla-viçeñe-at a specific time; tu-indeed; maìgalärtham-for auspiciousness; viñëu-dharmottare-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa; märkaëòeya-of Markandeya; vajra-and Vajra; samväde-in the conversation; puruñam-the Supreme Personality of Godhead; väsudevam-Väsudeva; ca-and; tathä-so; saìkarñaëam-Saìkarñaëa; vibhum-all-powerful; pradyumnam-Pradyumna; aniruddham-Aniruddha; ca-and; kramät-in sequence; abdeñu-in the years; kértayet-should chant. Chanting the Lord's Holy Name to Attain Auspiciousness at Certain Times In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, in a conversation of Märkaëòeya Muni and King Vajra it is said: "At the times of the different years one should chant the names of the puruña-avatäras: Väsudeva, Saìkarñaëa, Pradyumna, and Aniruddha." Commentary by Çréla Sanätana Gosvämé These names should be chanted for the first five years. Texts 275 and 276 balabhadraà tathä kåñëaà kértayed anaya-dvaye mädhavaà puëòarékäkñaà tathä vai bhoga-çäyinam padmanäbhaà håñékeçaà tathä devaà trivikramam krameëa räja-çärdüla vasantädiñu kértayet balabhadram-Balabhadra; tathä-then; kåñëam-Kåñëa; kértayet-should chant; anaya-dvaye-at the time of daksinayana; mädhavam-Mädhava; puëòarékäkñam-Puëòarékäkña; tathä-so; vai-indeed; bhoga-çäyinam-Bhoga-çäyé; padmanäbham- Padmanäbha; håñékeçam-Håñékeça; tathä-so; devam-Deva; trivikramam-Trivikrama; krameëa-one after another; räja- çärdüla-O tiger of kings; vasantädiñu-in the seasons beginning with springtime; kértayet-one should chant. "At the time of daksiëäyana one should chant the holy names of Balabhadra and Kåñëa. O tiger of kings, at the time of spring and the other seasons one should chant the holy names of Mädhava, Puëòarékäkña, Bhoga-çäyé, Padmanäbha, Håñékeça, Deva, and Trivikrama.
