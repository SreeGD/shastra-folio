Of The Various Activities of Devotional Service, Chanting the Holy Name is the Best In the Viñëu-cintämaëi, Lord Çiva tells Goddess Umä: "With a great struggle it is possible to meditate on Lord Kåñëa. However, simply by moving the lips one can easily chant Lord Kåñëa's holy name. For this reason chanting the Lord's name is more effective than trying to meditate on Him."
