hbv.60

Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 355-360
Translation

uttiñöhaàç ca vadaàs tathä ye vadanti harer näma tebhyo nityaà namo namaù çré-süta-by Çré Suta Gosvami; uktaàsaid; tatra-there; eva-indeed; yajïadhvaja-of Yajnadhvaja; upakhyäna-of the story; ante-at the end; svapan-sleeping; bhaïjan-eating; vrajaàù-walking; tiñöhann-standing; uttiñöhan-rising; ca-and; vadaàù- speaking; tathä-so; ye-they who; vadanti-speak; hareù-of Lord k; näma-the holy name; tebhyaù-to them; nityam-always; namaù-obeisances; namaù-obeisances. In the Närada Puräëa, at the end of the story of Yajïdhvaja, Çré Süta Gosvämi explains: "Again and I again I offer my respectful obeisances to they who while sleeping, eating, walking, sitting, standing, and speaking, chant the holy names of Lord Kåñëa." Text 405 çré-näräyaëa-vyüha-stave stré çüdraù pukkaço väpi ye cänye päpa-yoëayaù kértayanti harià bhaktyä tebhyo 'péha namo nämaù çré-näräyaëa-vyüha-stave-in the Çré Näräyaëa-vyüha-stava; stré-woman; çüdraù-sudra; pukkaçaù-outcaste; vä-or; api- also; ye-who; ca-and; anye-others; päpa-yoëayaù-born from sinful wombs; kértayanti-glorify; harim-Lord Kåñëa; bhaktyä- with devotion; tebhyaù-to them; pi-also; iha-here; namo nämaù-obeisances. In the Çré Näräyaëa-vyüha-stava it is said: "Again and again I bow down and offer my respectful obeisances to the women, çüdras, barbarians, those born from sinful wombs, or anyone else who with devotion chants the holy names of Lord Kåñëa." Texts 406 and 407 agaty-eka-gatitvaà pädme båhat-sahasra-näma-kathanärambhe ananya-gatayo martyä bhogino 'pi parantapaù jïäna-vairägya-rahitä brahmacaryädi-varjitäù sarva-dharmojjhitä viñëor näma-mätraika-jalpakäù sukhena yäà gatià yänti na täà sarve 'pi dharmikäù agati-of they who have no shelter; eka-the only; gatitvam-shelter; pädme-in the Padma Puräëa; båhat-sahasra-näma-of the Båhat-sahasra-näma; kathanärambhe-in the beginning; ananya-gatayaù-they who have no other shelter; martyä- human beings; bhoginaù-sense enjoyers; api-even; parantapäù-troublesome to others; jïäna-knowledge; vairägya- renunciation; rahitä-without; brahmacarya-celibacy; ädi-beginning with; varjitäù-without; sarva-dharmojjhitä-without any piety; viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; näma-mätraika-jalpakäù-chanting thre holy name once; sukhena-easily; yäm-which; gatim-abode; yänti-enter; na-not; täm-that; sarve-all; api-even; dharmikäù-the pious. The Holy Name Is the Shelter of They Who Have No Other Shelter

"They who are hedonists, troublesome to others, without knowledge, renunciation, celibacy, piety, or other virtues, but who, having no shelter, once chant the holy name of the Lord, can easily go to the supreme spiritual abode where the ordinary pious souls cannot enter." Text 408 sadä sarvatra sevyatvaà viñëu-dharme kñatrabandhüpakhyäne na deça-niyamas tasmin na käla-niyamas tathä nocchiñöädau niñedho 'sti çré-harer nämni lubdhaka sadä-always; sarvatra-everywhere; sevyatvam-to be served; viñëu-dharme-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa; kñatrabandhüpakhyäne-in the story of Kñatrabandhu; na-not; deça-of place; niyamaù-restruction; tasmin-in this; na-not; käla-niyamaù-restrictiojn of time; tathä-so; na-not; ucchiñöa-barbarians; ädau-beinning with; niñedhaù-prohibition; asti-is; çré-hareù-of Lord Kåñëa; nämni-in the name; lubdhaka-O hunter. The Holy Name Should Be Chanted Always and Everywhere In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, in the story of Kñatrabandhu, it is said: "O hunter, there is no time or place when one is forbidden to chant Lord Kåñëa's holy name, nor are barbarians or sinners forbidden to chant." Text 409 skände pädme vaiçäkha-mähätmye viñëu-dharmottare ca cakräyudhasya nämäni sadä sarvatra kértayet näçaucaà kértane tasya sa pavitra-karo yataù skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; pädme-the Padma Puräëa; vaiçäkha-mähätmye-the Vaisakha-mahatmya; viñëu- dharmottare-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khanda; ca-and; cakra-cakra; äyudhasya-which has the weapon; nämäni- the names; sadä-always; sarvatra-everywhere; kértayet-should chant; na-not; açaucam-impurity; kértane-in chanting; tasya-of him; sa-He; pavitra-karaù-making pure; yataù-because. In the Skanda Puräëa, the Padma Puräëa Vaiçäkha-mähätmya, and the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa Uttara-khaëòa, it is said: "Always and everywhere one should chant the holy names of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, whose weapon is the Sudarçana-cakra. No one is so impure that he cannot chant. The Supreme Personality of Godhead will purify anyone who chants." Text 410 punaù skände na deça-kälävasthäsu çuddhy-ädikam apkeñate kintu svatantram evaitan- näma kämita-kämadam punaù-again; skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; na-not; deça-of place; käla-time; avasthäsu-and situation; çuddhi-purity; ädikam-beginning with; apkeñate-waits; kintu-indeed; svatantram-independence; eva-indeed; etat-of Him; näma-the holy name; kämita-kämadam-fulfilling desires.

Again, in the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "The holy name does not wait for any particular time, place, or situation. Nor does the holy name require that they who chant it be pure in heart. The holy name does as its wishes. The holy name fulfills all the desires of everyone." Text 411 vaiçvänara-saàhitäyäà na deça-käla-niyamo na çaucäçauca-nirëayaù paraà saìkértanäd eva räma rämeti mucyate vaiçvänara-saàhitäyäm-i the Vaiçvänara-saàhitä; na-not; deça-käla-niyamaù-restriction of time and place; na-not; çaucäçauca-nirëayaù-consideratyion of pure or impure; param-great; saìkértanät-from chanting; eva-indeed; räma-Räma; räma-Räma; iti-this; mucyate-is freed. In the Vaiçvänara-saàhitä it is said; "There is no time or place when one is forbidden to chant the Lord's holy name, nor does it matter that the chanter is pure or impure. Anyone who chants: Räma! Räma! will become liberated." Text 412 vaiñëava-cintämaëau çré-yudhiñöhiraà prati çré-närada-väkyaà na deça-niyamo räjan na käla-niyamas tathä vidyate nätra sandeho viñëor nämänukértane vaiñëava-cintämaëau-in the Vaiñëava-cintämaëi; çré-yudhiñöhiraàto King Yudhisthira; prati-to; çré-närada-väkyam-the words of Närada Muni; na-not; deça-place; niyamaù-restriction; räjan-O king; na-not; käla-niyamaù-restriction of time; tathä-so; vidyate-is; na-not; atra-here; sandehaù-doubt; viñëoù-of Lord Viñëu; nämänukértane-in chanting the holy names. In the Vaiñëava-cintämaëi, Närada Muni tells King Yudhiñöhira: "O king, there is no time or place when one is forbidden to chant Lord Viñëu's holy name. Of this there is no doubt. Text 413 kälo 'sti däne yajïe ca snäne kälo 'sti sajjape viñëu-saìkértane kälo nästy atra påthivé-tale kälaù-time; asti-is; däne-in giving charity; yajïe-in performing yajnas; ca-and; snäne-in bathing; kälaù-time; asti-is; sat- jape-in chanting mantras; viñëu-saìkértane-in chanting Lord Viñëu's holy names; kälaù-time; na-not; asti-is; atra-here; påthivé-tale-on the earth. "There is an appropriate time to give charity and to perform yajïas. There is an appropriate time to bathe and to chant certain mantras. However, in this world there is no specific time when one should chant the holy names of Lord Viñëu. One may chant them at any time."

Text 414 dvitéya-skande etan nirvidyamänänäà icchatäm akuto-bhayam yoginäà nåpa nirëétaà harer nämänukértanam dvitéya-skande-in the Second Canto; etan-it is; nirvidyamänänäm-of those who are completely free from all material desires; icchatäm-of those who are deswireous of all sorts of material enjoyment; akuto-bhayam-free form all doubts and fear; yoginäm-of all who are self-satisfied; nåpa-O king; nirëétam-decided truth; hareù-of the Lord, Çré Kåñëa; näma-holy name; anu-after someone, always; kértanam-chanting. In Çrémad-Bhägavatam (2.1.11) it is said: "O king, constant chanting of the holy name of the Lord after the ways of the great authorities is the doubtless and fearless way of success for all, including those who are free from all material desires, those who are desirous of all material enjoyment, and those who are self-satisfied by dint of transcendental knowledge."* Text 415 mukti-pradatvaà värähe näräyaëäcyutänanta väsudeveti yo naraù satataà kértayed bhümi yänti mallayatäà sa hi mukti-pradatvam-the giver of liberation; värähe-in the Varäha Puräëa; näräyaëa-Näräyaëa; acyuta-Acyuta; ananta- Ananta; väsudeva-Väsudeva; iti-thus; yaù-one who; naraù-a person; satatam-always; kértayet-chants; bhümi-O earth; yänti-goes; mat-layatäm-liberation; sa-he; hi-indeed. The Holy Name Grants Liberation In the Varäha Puräëa it is said: "O earth-goddess, anyone who always chants, `Näräyaëa! Acyuta! Ananta! Väsudeva!', attains liberation." Text 416 gäruòe kià kariñyati saìkhyena kià yogair nara-näyaka muktim icchasi räjendra kuru govinda-kértanam gäruòe-in the Garuòa Puräëa; kim-what is the use?; kariñyati-will do; saìkhyena-with sankhya; kim-what is the use?; yogaiù-of yoga; nara-näyaka-O king; muktim-liberation; icchasi-you desire; räjendra-O king; kuru-do; govinda-kértanam- the chanting of Lord Kåñëa's names. In the Garuòa Puräëa it is said: "What is the use of saìkhya! What is the use of yoga! O king, if you desire liberation, then please chant the holy names of Lord Govinda."

Text 417 skände sakåd uccaritaà yena harir ity akñara-dvayam baddhaù parikaras tena mokñäya gamanaà prati skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; sakåt-once; uccaritam-spoken; yena-by whom; hariù-Hari; iti-thus; akñara-dvayam-two syllables; baddhaù-bound; parikaraù-belt; tena-by him; mokñäya-for liberation; gamanam-going; prati-to. In the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "By once chanting the holy name of the Lord, which consist of the two syllables `Ha-ri', one guarantees his path to liberation."* Text 418 brahma-puräëe apy anya-citto 'çuddho vä yaù sadä kértayed dharim so 'pi doña-kñayän muktià labhec cedi-patir yathä brahma-puräëe-in the Brahma Puräëa; api-also; anya-cittaù-with thoughts on something else; açuddhaù-impure; vä- or; yaù-one who; sadä-always; kértayet-chants; harim-Lord Hari; saù-he; api-even; doña-kñayän-from the disappearance of fualts; muktim-liberation; labhec-attains; cedi-patiù-Sisupala; yathä-as. In the Brahmä Puräëa it is said: "Even if impure at heart or rapt in material thoughts, a person who always chants the holy names of Lord Kåñëa will become free of all sins and attain liberation, as the demon Çiçupäla did." Text 419 pädme devahüti-stutau sakåd uccärayed yas tu näräyaëam atandritaù çuddhäntaùkaraëo bhütvä nirväëam adhigacchati pädme-in the Padma Puräëa; devahüti-stutau-in the prayersv of Devahuti; sakåt-once; uccärayet-says; yaù-who; tu- indeed; näräyaëam-Näräyaëa; atandritaù-without fatiugue; çuddhäntaùkaraëaù-pure at heart; bhütvä-becoming; nirväëam-liberation; adhigacchati-attains. In the Padma Puräëa, in the prayers of Devahüti, she says: "A person who once says the word Näräyaëa become pure in heart and attains liberation." Text 420 mätsye para-dära-rato väpi paräpa-kåti-kärakaù

sa çuddho muktim äpnoti harer nämänukértanät mätsye-in the Matsya Puräëa; para-dära-rataù-a adulterer; vä-or; api-even; paräpakåti-kärakaù-a murderer; sa-he; çuddhaù-pure; muktim-liberation; äpnoti-attains; hareù-of Lord Kåñëa; nämänukértanät-by chanting the holy names. In the Matsya Puräëa it is said: "Even an adulterer or a murderer becomes pure-hearted and attains liberation by chanting Lord Kåñëa's holy names." In the Vaiçampäyana-saàhitä it is said: "Even a person who stays far from all pious deeds and delights in sins becomes liberated by chanting the holy names of Lord Viñëu." Text 422 båhan-näradéye yathä kathaïcid yan-nämni kértite vä çrute 'pi vä päpino 'pi viçuddhäù syuù çuddhä mokñam aväpnuyuù båhan-näradéye-in the Närada Puräëa; yathä--as; kathaïcit-somehow; yan-nämni-when whose name; kértite-is chanted; vä-or; çrute-heard; api-and; vä-or; päpinaù-sinners; api-even; viçuddhäù-pure; syuù-become; çuddhä-pure; mokñam-liberation; aväpnuyuù-attain. In the Närada Puräëa it is said: "Even sinners who somehow chant or hear Lord Viñëu's holy names become purified and attain liberation."
