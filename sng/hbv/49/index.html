hbv.49

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 289-294
Translation

Text 179 tatraiva çré-bhagavad-uktau na me dhyäna-rataù samyag- yoginaù parituñöaye tathä bhavanti devarñe kriyä-yoga-ratä yathä tatra-there; eva-indeed; çré-bhagavad-uktau-in the words of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; na-not; me-on Me; dhyäna-rataù-devoted to meditation; samyag- yoginaù-practiced in yoga; parituñöaye-for pleasing; tatha-so; bhavanti-are; devarñe-O Narada; kriyä-yoga-ratä-engaged in devotional service; yathä-as. In the Viñëu-rahasya, the Supreme Personality of Godhead explains: "They who follow the yoga of meditation do please Me as much as they who follow the yoga of work. Text 180 kriyä-yogo hi me 'bhiñöaù para-yogät svanuñöhität tuñöir me sambhavet pumbhir bhaktimadbhir amatsaraiù kriyä-yogaù-following the yoga of work; hi-indeed; me-of me; abhiñöaù-pleased; para-yogät-than the best yoga; svanuñöhität-properly situated; tuñöiù-pleasure; me-of me; sambhavet-is; pumbhiù-by people; bhaktimadbhiù-with devotion; amatsaraiù-without envy. "One who follows the yoga of work pleases Me more than other yogés. I am pleased by they who love Me and are free from envy and hatred, Text 181 ye 'rcayanti narä nityaà kriyä-yoga-ratäù svayam dhyäyanti ye ca mäà nityaà teñäà çreñöhäù kriyä matäù ye-who; arcayanti-worship; narä-people; nityam-always; kriyä-yoga-ratäù-engaged in the yoga of work; svayam- personally; dhyäyanti-meditate; ye-eho; ca-and; mäm-on me; nityam-always; teñäm-of them; çreñöhäù-the best; kriyä-in work; matäù-considered. "They who always worship me and think of Me are the best of they who follow the yoga of work. Text 182 kriyä-hénasya devarñe tathä dhyänaà na muktidam na tathä mäà vidur vipra dhyäninas tattvato vinä kriyä-yoga-ratäù samyag labhante mäà samädhinä kriyä-hénasya-without work; devarñe-O Narada; tathä-so; dhyänam-meditation; na-not; muktidam-gives liberation; na- not; tathä-so; mäm-Me; viduù-know; vipra-O brähmaëa; dhyäninaù-meditating; tattvataù-in truth; vinä-without; kriyä- yoga-ratäù-following the yoga of work; samyag-properly; labhante-attain; mäm-Me; samädhinä-in the trance of

meditation. "O Närada, without working for My sake, meditation does not give liberation. O brähmaëa, they who only meditate cannot understand Me in truth. They who work for My sake become rapt in a trance of meditation on Me and thus they attain Me. Text 183 yathä hi kämadaà nåëäà mama tuñöi-karaà param bhakti-yogaà mahä-puëyaà bhukti-mukti-pradaà çubham yathä-as; hi-indeed; kämadam-fulfilling desires; nåëäm-of the people; mama-of Me; tuñöi-karam-pleasing; param-great; bhakti-yogam-the yoga of devotional service; mahä-puëyam-very sacred; bhukti-mukti-pradam-giving happiness and liberation; çubham-auspicious. "The sacred and auspicious yoga of devotional service pleases me and fulfills the devotee's desires. It brings happiness and liberation. Text 184 samvatsareëa yat puëyaà labhante dhyänino mama präpyate tad ihaikähät kriyä-yoga-parair naraiù samvatsareëa-in a year; yat-what; puëyam-piety; labhante-attains; dhyäninaù-meditrating; mama-of Me; präpyate-is attained; tat-that; iha-here; eka-one; ähät-in a day; kriyä-yoga-paraiù-by they who follow the yoga of work; naraiù-by people. "In a single day they who follow the yoga of work attain the same pious results the meditating yogés struggle to attain in an entire year." Text 185 ädi-puraëe na karma-sädåçaà dhyänaà na karma-sädåçaà phalam na karma-sädåças tyägo na karma-sädåçaà tapaù na karma-sädåçaà puëyaà na karma-sädåçé gatiù ädi-puraëe-in the Adi Puräëa; na-not; karma-sädåçam-like work; dhyänam-meditation; na-not; karma-sädåçam-like work; phalam-the result; na-not; karma-sädåçaù-like work; tyägaù-renunciation; na-not; karma-sädåçam-like work; tapaù- austerity; na-not; karma-sädåçam-like work; puëyam-piety; na-not; karma-sädåçé-like work; gatiù-destination. In the Adi Puräëa it is said: "No meditation is equal to working for the Supreme Personality of Godhead. No result is equal to that obtained by working for the Supreme Personality of Godhead. No renunciation is equal to working for the Supreme Personality of Godhead. No austerity is equal to working for the Supreme Personality of Godhead. No piety is equal to working for the Supreme Personality of Godhead. No destination is equal to that obtained by working for the Supreme Personality of Godhead."

Text 186 näradéye bhakti-grähyo håñékeço na dhanair dharaëé-suräù bhaktyä sampüjito viñëuù pradädati saméhitam näradéye-in the Narada Puräëa; bhakti-grähyaù--attained by devotional service; håñékeçaù-Lord Kåñëa, the master of the senses; na-not; dhanaiù-by wealth; dharaëé-suräù-O brähmaëas; bhaktyä-with devotion; sampüjitaù-worshiped; viñëuù- Lord Viñëu; pradädati-gives; saméhitam-desire. In the Närada Puräëa it is said: "O brähmaëas, the Supreme Personality of Godhead is attained by devotional service, not by giving money. Lord Viñëu fulfills the desires of they who worship Him with devotion. Text 187 jalenäpi jagannäthaù püjitaù kleça-hä hariù paritoñaà vrajaty äçu tåñärtaù su-jalair yathä jalena-with water; api-also; jagannätha-the master ofntheuniverses; püjitaù-worshiped; kleça-ha-destroying sufferings; hariù-Lord Kåñëa; paritoñam-satisfaction; vrajati-attains; äçu-at once; tåñärtaù-thirsty; su-jalaiù-with water; yathä-as. "As a thirsty man is at once pleased when he drinks some clear water, so Lord Kåñëa, the master of the universes and the killer of all sufferings, is pleased when He is worshiped with an offering of water." Text 188 hari-bhakti-sudhodaye kåtäpi dambha-häsyärthe sevä tärayate janän viphalä nänya-karmeva kåpäluù ko nv ataù paraù hari-bhakti-sudhodaye-in the Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya; kåtä-performed; api-even; dambha-häsyärthe-for the sake of pride or mockery; sevä-service; tärayate-delivers; janän-the people; viphalä-worthless; na-not; anya-karma-other work; iva-like; kåpäluù-merciful; kaù-who?; nu-indeed; ataù-than He; paraù-more. In the Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya it is said: "Even if performed with pride or the intent to mock, devotional service delivers the people. Such devotional service is not useless, as it would be if it were some other work. Who is more merciful than the Supreme Personality of Godhead?" Text 189 brahma-vaivarte sa samarädhito devo mukti-kåt syäd yathä tathä anicchayäpi hüta-bhuk

saàspåñöo dahati dvija brahma-vaivarte-in the Brahma-vaivarta Puräëa; sa-He; samarädhitaù-worshiped; devaù-the Supreme Personality of Godhead; mukti-kåt-giving liberation; syät-is; yathä-as; tathä-so; anicchayä-without desire; api-also; hüta-bhuk-fire; saàspåñöaù-touched; dahati-burns; dvija-O brähmaëa. In the Brahma-vaivarta Puräëa it is said: "As when one accidentally touches fire, it burns, so when one somehow or other worships the Supreme Personality of Godhead, He responds by giving liberation." Text 190 dhanavän putravän bhogé yaçasvé bhaya-varjitaù medhävé matimän präjïo bhavaty arädhänäd dhareù dhanavän-wealthy; putravän-with good children; bhogé-happy; yaçasvé-famous; bhaya-varjitaù-fearless; medhävé- intelligent; matimän-philosopher; präjïaù-wise; bhavati-becomes; arädhänät-by worshiping; hareù-Lord Kåñëa. "By worshiping Lord Kåñëa one becomes wealthy, happy, famous, fearless, intelligent, wise, thoughtful, and the parent of good children." Texts 191 and 192 skände sanatkumära-märkaëòeya-samväde visiñöaù sarva-dharmäc ca dharmo viñëv-arcanaà nåëäm sarva-yajïa-tapo-homäs tértha-snänais ca yat phalam tat phalaà koti-guëitaà viñëuà sampüjya cäpnuyät tasmät sarva-prayatnena näräyaëam ihärcayet skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; sanatkumära-märkaëòeya-samväde-in the conversation of Sanatkumara and markandeya; visiñöaù-specific; sarva-all; dharmät-from dharma; ca-and; dharmaù-the religion; viñëv-arcanam-the worship of Lord Viñëu; nåëäm-of men; sarva-yajïa-tapo-homäù-all yajnas, austerities, and homas; tértha-snänaiù-bathing in holy rivers; ca-and; yat-what; phalam-result; tat-that; phalam-result; koti-guëitam-millions of times; viñëum-Lord Viñëu; sampüjya-worshiping; ca-and; äpnuyät-attains. In the Skanda Puräëa, in a conversation of Sanatkumära and Märkaëòeya, it is said: "The worship of Lord Viñëu is the best of all religions. By worshiping Lord Viñëu one attains a result many millions of times greater than the results of all yajïas, homas, austerities, and bathing in holy places. Therefore one should diligently worship Lord Viñëu." Text 193 tatraiva çré-çivomä-samväde yaù pradadyäd dvijendräya sarväà bhümià sa-sägaräm

arcayed yaù sakåd viñëuà tat phalaà labhate naraù tatra-there; eva-indeed; çré-çivomä-samväde-in a conversation of Lord Çiva and Goddess Umä; yaù-who; pradadyät- gives; dvijendräya-to a kin gof brähmaëas; sarväm-all; bhümim-the earth; sa-sägaräm-with the oceans; arcayet-worships; yaù-one whop; sakåt-once; viñëum-Lord Viñëu; tat-that; phalam-result; labhate-attains; naraù-a person. In the Skanda Puräëa, in a conversation of Lord Çiva and Goddess Umä, it is said: "A person who once worships Lord Viñëu attains the same result that is attained by giving the entire earth with all its oceans in charity to the king of brähmaëas. Text 194 mäsärdham api yo viñëuà nairäntaryeëa püjayet puruñottamaù sa vijïo (?????) viñëu-bhakto na saàçayaù mäsärdham-for half a month; api-even; yaù-one who; viñëum-Lord Viñëu; nairäntaryeëa-continually; püjayet- worships; puruñottamaù-the best of persons; sa-he; vijïaù-wise; viñëu-bhaktaù-a devotee of Lord Viñëu; na-no; saàçayaù-doubt. "One who for half a month diligently worships Lord Viñëu is the best of men. He is wise. He is a great devotee of Lord Viñëu. Of this there is no doubt. Text 195 madhyandina-gate sürye yo viñëuà paripüjayet vasu-pürëa-mahé-dätur yat puëyaà tad aväpnuyät madhyandina-gate-at midday; sürye-the sun; yaù-one who; viñëum-Lord Viñëu; paripüjayet-worships; vasu-pürëa- with all its wealth; mahé-the earth; dätuù-giving in charity; yat-what; puëyam-piety; tat-that; aväpnuyät-attains. "A person who at midday worships Lord Viñëu attains the same pious result as that obtained by giving the entire earth, with all its wealth, in charity." Text 196 prätar utthäya yo viñëuà satataà paripüjayet agniñöoma-sahasrasya labhate phalam uttamam prätaù-early in the morning; utthäya-rising; yaù-who; viñëum-Lord Viñëu; satatam-always; paripüjayet-worships; agniñöoma-sahasrasya-of a thousand agnistoma-yajnas; labhate-attains; phalam-result; uttamam-supreme. "A person who regularly rises early and worships Lord Viñëu attains a pious result better than that obtained by performing a thousand agniñöoma-yajïas. Text 197 yo viñëuà präyato bhütvä säyam-käle samarcayet

gaväà medhasya yajïasya phalam äpnoti durlabham yaù-one who; viñëum-Lord Viñëu; präyataù-mostly; bhütvä-becoming; säyam-käle-at evening; samarcayet-worships; gaväm-of cows; medhasya-medha; yajïasya-of a yaujna; phalam-result; äpnoti-attains; durlabham-rare. "A person who in the evening worships Lord Viñëu attains the same result as that obtained by performing a gomedha- yajïa. Text 198 evaà sarväsu veläsu aveläsu ca keçavam sampüjayän naro bhaktyä sarvän kämän aväpnuyät evam-thus; sarväsu-in all; veläsu-the three sandhya times; aveläsu-at the other times; ca-and; keçavam-Lord k; sampüjayät-worships; naraù-a person; bhaktyä-with devotion; sarvän-all; kämän-desires; aväpnuyät-attains. "A person at any time, at the sandhyä times, or at any other time as well, devotedly worships Lord Kåñëa, attains all his desires. Text 199 kià punar yo 'rcayen nityaà sarva-deva-namaskåtam dhanyaù saù kåta-kåtyas ca viñëulokam aväpnuyät kim-what; punaù-more; yaù-one who; arcayet-worships; nityam-always; sarva-deva-namaskåtam-to whom all the demigods bow down; dhanyaù-fortunate; saù-he; kåta-kåtyaù-successful; ca-and; viñëulokam-Viñëuloka; aväpnuyät- attains. "What, then, can be said of one who always worships Lord Viñëu? Such a person is very fortunate. His life is a perfect success. He goes to the realm of Lord Viñëu. "They who are simply initiated into the worship of Lord Kåñëa attain liberation. What can said, then, of they who always worship the Lord with devotion?" Text 201 tatraiva sri-brahma-närada-samväde
