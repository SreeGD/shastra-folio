hbv.55

1 verse
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa (full edition) pp. 325-330
Translation

"By remembering the Lord by chanting the name Garuòadhvaja, one becomes free from calamities and cures fevers, headaches, and poisoning. Text 308 balabhadraà tu yuddhärthé kåñyärambhe haläyudham uttäraëaà vanijyärthé rämam abhyudaye nåpa balabhadram-Balabhadra; tu-indeed; yuddhärthé-wishing to fight; kåñyärambhe-in the beginning of plowing; haläyudham-Haläyudha; uttäraëam-Uttäraëa; vanijyärthé-to sell goods; rämam-Räma; abhyudaye-in festivals; nåpa-O king. "O king, a warrior eager to fight should chant the name Balabhadra. While plowing one should chant the name Haläyudha. A person eager to sell goods should chant the name Uttäraëa. In auspicious festivals one should chant the name Räma. Text 309 maìgalyaà maìgalaà viñëuà maìgalyeñu ca kértayet uttiñöhan kértayed viñëuà prasvapan mädhavaà naraù bhojane caiva govindaà sarvatra madhusüdanam maìgalyam-giving auspiciousness; maìgalam-auspicious; viñëum-Viñëu; maìgalyeñu-in asupcious ceremonies; ca- and; kértayet-should chant; uttiñöhan-rising; kértayet-should chant; viñëum-Viñëu; prasvapan-sleeping; mädhavam- mädhava; naraù-a person; bhojane-in eating; ca-and; eva-indeed; govindam-Govinda; sarvatra-everywhere; madhusüdanam-Madhusüdana. "In auspicious ceremonies one should chant the auspicious name Viñëu. When rising one should chant the name Viñëu. While going to sleep one should chant the name Mädhava. While eating one should chant the name Govinda. In every situation one should chant the name Madhusüdana. Text 310 tatraivänyatra auçadhe cintayed viñëuà bhojane ca janärdanam çayane padmanäbhaà ca maithune ca prajäpatim tatra-there; eva-indeed; anyatra-in another place; auçadhe-in medicine; cintayet-should meditate; viñëum-Viñëu; bhojane-in eating; ca-and; janärdanam-Janärdana; çayane-in sleeping; padmanäbham-Padmanäbha; ca-and; maithune-in sex; ca-and; prajäpatim-Prajäati. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa it is also said: "When taking medicine one should remember the Lord by chanting the name Viñëu. When eating one should chant the name Janärdana. When going to sleep one should chant the name Padmanäbha. In the garbhädhäna-saàskära one should chant the name Prajäpati. Text 311

saìgräme cakriëaà kruddhaà sthäna-bhraàçe trivikramam näräyaëaà våñotsarge çrédharaà priya-saìgame jala-madhye ca värähaà pävake jalaçäyinam saìgräme-in battle; cakriëam-Cakré; kruddham-angry; sthäna-bhraàçe-in falling from one's post; trivikramam- Trivikrama; näräyaëam-Näräyaëa; våñotsarge-in giving a bull; çrédharam-Çrédhara; priya-saìgame-in meeting the beloved; jala-madhye-in the water; ca-and; väräham-varäha; pävake-in fire; jalaçäyinam-Jalaçäyé. "In battle one should chant the name Kruddha-cakré. When there is danger of losing one's post one should chant the name Trivikrama. In giving a bull in charity one should chant the name Näräyaëa. In meeting what is pleasing one should chant the name Çrédhara. In the midst of the waters one should chant the name Varäha. In the midst of fire one should chant the name Jalaçäyé. Text 312 känane narasiàhaà ca parvate raghunandanam duùsvapne smara govindaà viçuddhau madhusüdanam mäyäsu vämanaà devaà sarva-käryeñu mädhavam känane-in a forest; narasiàham-Narasiàha; ca-and; parvate-on a mountain; raghunandanam-Raghunandana; duùsvapne-in a nightmare; smara-remember; govindam-Govinda; viçuddhau-to becoem purified; madhusüdanam- Madhusdana; mäyäsu-in bewilderments; vämanam-Vämana; devam-Deva; sarva-käryeñu-in all activities; mädhavam- Mädhava. "In a forest one should chant the name Narasiàha. On a mountain one should chant the name Raghunandana. In a nightmare one should chant the name Govinda. To become purified one should chant the name Madhusüdana. In a bewildering situation one should chant the name Vämanadeva. In all activities one should chant the name Mädhava." Text 313 kià ca kértayed väsudevaà ca anukteñv api yädava käryärambhe tathä räjan yatheñöhaà näma kértayet kià ca-furthermore; kértayet-one should chant; väsudevam-Väsudeva; ca-and; anukteñv-in not speaking; api-even; yädava-O descendent of King Yadu; käryärambhe-in activities; tathä-so; räjan-O king; yatheñöham-as desired; näma-the name; kértayet-should chant. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa it is also said: "O descendent of King Yadu, even without actually pronouncing the syllables one should chant the name Väsudeva. O king, in all activities, whenever one wishes, one should chant the holy names of the Lord. Text 314 sarväni nämäni hi tasya räjan sarvärtha-siddhayai tu bhavanti puàsaù

tasmäd yatheñöhaà khalu kåñëa-näma sarveñu käryeñu japeta bhaktyä sarväni-all; nämäni-names; hi-indeed; tasya-of Him; räjan-O king; sarvärtha-siddhayai-to attain all desires; tu-indeed; bhavanti-are; puàsaù-of the people; tasmät-therfeore; yatheñöham-as desired; khalu-indeed; kåñëa-näma-the nameof Lord Kåñëa; sarveñu-in all; käryeñu-activities; japeta-should chant; bhaktyä-with devotion. "O king, all the names of the Supreme Personality of Godhead fulfill all the desires of the living entities. Therefore, whenever one wishes, in every activity one should chant the holy name of Lord Kåñëa with devotion. Text 315 taträkhila-päponmulanatvam atha sämänyataù çré-bhagavan-näma-kértana-mahätmyaà viñëu-dharme hari-bhakti-sudhodaye coktaà näradena aho su-nirmalä yüyaà rägo hi hari-kértane avidhüya tamaù kåtsnaà nåëäm udeti süryavat tatra-there; akhila-päpa-all sins; unmulanatvam-uprooting; atha-then; sämänyataù-in a general way; çré-bhagavan-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; näma-fo the name; kértana-chanting; mähätmyam-the glory; viñëu-dharme-in thre Viñëu-dharma Puräëa; hari-bhakti-sudhodaye-in the hari-bhakti-sudhodaya; ca-and; uktam-said; näradena-by Närada Muni; ahaù-Oh!; su-nirmalä-very pure; yüyam-you; rägaù-love; hi-indeed; hari-kértane-for chanting the names of Lord Kåñëa; avidhüya-shaking off; tamaù-darkness; kåtsnam-all; nåëäm-of men; udeti-rises; süryavat-like the sun. First, The Holy Name of the Lord Uproots All Sins, and Then a General Description of the Glories of Chanting the Holy Name In both the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa and the Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, Närada Muni explains: "Because you are attracted to the Lord's holy name, you are all very pure in heart. When the holy name rises in one's heart it shines like the sun and shakes away all darkness." Text 316 gäruòe päpänalasya déptasya mä kurvantu bhayaà naraù govinda-näma-meghaughair naçyate néra-bindubhiù gäruòe-in the Garuòa Puräëa; päpa-of sin; analasya-of the fire; déptasya-blazing; mä-don't; kurvantu-do; bhayam-fear; naraù-people; govinda-näma-of the name of Govinda; megha-of clouds; aughaiù-with the floods; naçyate-destroyed; néra- bindubhiù-with drops of water. In the Garuòa Puräëa it is said: "O people, please do not fear this blazing fire of sin. The monsoon clouds of Lord Govinda's holy name will extinguish it with a flood of rain. Text 317 avaçenäpi yan-nämni

kértane sarva-pätakaiù pumän vimucyate sadyaù siàha-trastair mågair iva avaçena-spontaneously; api-even; yan-nämni-in whose name; kértane-in the chanting; sarva-pätakaiù-by all sins; pumän-a person; vimucyate-is freed; sadyaù-at once; siàha-trastaiù-frightened by a lion; mågaiù-by deer; iva-like. "When one chants the holy name of Lord Govinda, all his sins flee far away, like deer frightened by a lion. Text 318 yan-näma-kértanaà bhaktyä viläpanam anuttamam maitreyäçeña-päpänäà dhätünäm iva pävakaù yan-näma-kértanam-chanting whose name; bhaktyä-with dewvotion; viläpanam-saying; anuttamam-unequalled; maitreya-O Maitreya; açeña-päpänäm-of all sins; dhätünäm-of metals; iva-like; pävakaù-fire. "O Maitreya, as fire purifies metal cast into it, so chanting the holy name of Lord Govinda with devotion purifies the heart of all sins. Text 319 yasmin nyasta-matir na yäti narakaà svargo 'pi yac-cintane vighno yatra niveñitätma-manaso brähmo 'pi loko 'lpakaù muktià cetasi yaù sthito 'mala-dhiyaà puàsäà dadäty avyayaù kià citraà yad-aghaà prayäti vilayaà taträcyute kértite yasmin-in which; nyasta-placed; matiù-thought; na-not; yäti-goes; narakam-to hell; svargaù-Svargaloka; api-also; yac- cintane-in the thought of whom; vighnaù-obstacle; yatra-where; niveñita-entered; ätma-manasaù-the heart; brähmaù- Brahma; api-even; lokaù-planet; alpakaù-slight; muktim-liberation; cetasi-in the heart; yaù-who; sthitaù-situated; amala- dhiyam-pure heart; puàsäm-of the people; dadati-burns; avyayaù-enetrnal; kim-what?; citram-wonder; yad-agham-to sin; prayäti-goes; vilayam-destruction; tatra-there; acyute-when the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead; kértite-is glorified. "One whose thoughts are placed in the holy name of Lord Govinda does not go to hell. He thinks Svargaloka is an obstacle and Brahmaloka is not important. Eternal Lord Kåñëa stays in his pure heart and offers him liberation. How can it be surprising, then, that all sins perish when the holy name of the infallible Supreme Personality of Godhead is chanted?" Text 320 viñëu-dharmottare säyaà prätas tathä kåtvä deva-devasya kértanam sarva-päpa-vinirmuktaù svarga-loke mahéyate viñëu-dharmottare-in the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa; sayam-in the evening; prätaù-in the morning; tathä- so; kåtvä-doing; deva-devasya-of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; kértanam-glory; sarva-päpa-vinirmuktaù-free from all sin; svarga-loke-in Svargaloka; mahéyate-is glorified. In the Viñëu-dharma Puräëa, Uttara-khaëòa, it is said: "A person who in the morning and evening chants the holy names of the Supreme Personality of Godhead becomes

free of all sins. He is glorified in Svargaloka. Text 321 vämane näräyaëo näma naro naräëäà prasiddha-cauraù kathitaù påthivyäm aneka-janmarjita-päpa-saïcayaà haraty açeñaà çrutam atra eva hi vämane-in the vämana Puräëa; näräyaëaù-näräyaëa; näma-name; naraù-a person; naräëäm-of persons; prasiddha- cauraù-famous as a thief; kathitaù-said; påthivyäm-on the earth; aneka-janmarjita-päpa-saïcayam-sins gathered in many births; harati-removes; açeñam-all; çrutam-heard; atra-here; eva-indeed; hi-certainly. In the Vämana Puräëa it is said: "The holy name of Lord Näräayaëa is famous as the greatest thief on the earth. When it is heard, that name robs one of the sinful reactions accumulated in many, many births." Text 322 skände govindeti tathä proktaà bhaktyä vä bhakti-varjitaiù dahate sarva-päpäni yugäntägnir ivotthitaù skände-in the Skanda Puräëa; govinda-Govinda; iti-thus; tathä-so; proktam-spoken; bhaktyä-with devotion; vä-or; bhakti-varjitaiù-without devotion; dahate-burns; sarva-päpäni-all sins; yugäntägniù-the fires at the end of the yuga; iva- like; utthitaù-risen. In the Skanda Puräëa it is said: "Spoken with or without devotion, the holy name of Lords Govinda rises like the fire at the yuga's end, and burns away all sins. Text 323 govinda-nämnä yaù kaçcin naro bhavati bhü-tale kértanäd eva tasyäpi päpaà yäti sahasradhä govinda-nämnä-with the name of Lord Govinda; yaù-one who; kaçcin-someone; naraù-a person; bhavati-is; bhü-tale- onm the earth; kértanät-by chanting; eva-indeed; tasya-of him; api-also; päpam-sin; yäti-goes; sahasradhä-a thousand times. "Thousands of sins flee from anyone who chants the holy name of Lord Govinda in this world." Text 324 käçé-khaëòe pramädäd api saàspåñöo

yathänala-kaëo dahet tathauñöha-püta-saàspåñöaà hari-näma dahed agham käçé-khaëòe-in the Käçé-khaëòa; pramädät-foolishly; api-even; saàspåñöaù-touched; yathä-as; anala-kaëaù-a spark; dahet-burns; tathä-so; auñöha-püta-the lips; saàspåñöam-touching; hari-näma-the name of Lord Kåñëa; dahet-burns; agham-sin. Text 325 båhan-näradéye lubdhakopakhyänänte naräëäà viñayändhänäà mamatäkula-cetäsäm ekam eva harer näma sarva-päpa-vinäçnam båhan-näradéye-in the Närada Puräëa; lubdhakopakhyänänte-at the end of the story of the hunter; naräëäm-of the people; viñayändhänäm-blinded with material desires; mamatäkula-cetäsäm-their hearts filled with possessiveness; ekam- one; eva-indeed; hareù-of Lord Kåñëa; näma-the name; sarva-päpa-vinäçnam-destroying all sins. In the Närada Puräëa, at the end of the story of the hunter, it is said: "One utterance of the holy name of Lord Kåñëa destroys all the sins of the people of this world, people blinded by material desires, people whose hearts are filled with the desire to claim the various things of this world."
