Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.6.25-26

పృశ్న‍ిగర్భస్తు తే బుద్ధిమాత్మానం భగవాన్ పర: । క్రీడన్తం పాతు గోవిన్ద: శయానం పాతు మాధవ: ॥ ౨౫ ॥ వ్రజన్తమవ్యాద్వైకుణ్ఠ ఆసీనం త్వాం శ్రియ: పతి: । భుఞ్జానం యజ్ఞభుక్ పాతు సర్వగ్రహభయఙ్కర: ॥ ౨౬ ॥

Important Words
  • govindaḥ — Lord Govinda
    Compound — go (cow, senses, earth) + vinda (one who gives pleasure or recovers). The name first appears in Bhāgavata before the famous Govardhana līlā that will explain it (SB 10.27). Invoked here precisely for the krīḍā-state — playing — because Govinda is the Lord-of-play par excellence.
  • mādhavaḥ — Lord Mādhava
    From madhu (honey/sweetness, also the name of a slain demon) + va. Mādhava is the husband of Lakṣmī (Mā) and the source of sweetness; invoked over sleep because the mādhurya-rasa intensifies in restful, intimate hours.
  • vaikuṇṭhaḥ — Lord Vaikuṇṭha
    From vi (without) + kuṇṭha (obstruction) — 'the unobstructed one,' and by extension His abode. Invoked over walking, because the Lord whose movements never meet obstruction is the right guardian for our walking, where we so easily stumble.
  • yajñabhuk — Lord Yajñabhuk, the enjoyer of sacrifice
    Compound — yajña + bhuj (enjoyer). The Gītā's repeated declaration: bhoktāraṁ yajña-tapasāṁ (Gītā 5.29). He is invoked over eating, since for the devotee no food is private — every meal is yajña and He is its bhuktā.
  • sarva - graha - bhayam - karaḥ — (He who is) fear-producing to all malefic planets
    Compound — sarva (all) + graha (planet, seizer) + bhayam-kara (fear-causing). A startling phrase: the demigods of inauspiciousness themselves fear this Lord. Therefore He is the right guardian over the most exposed motion of the body — eating — when grahas are traditionally most active.
May Lord Pṛśnigarbha protect Your intelligence, and the Supreme Personality of Godhead Your soul. While You are playing, may Govinda protect You, and while You are sleeping may Mādhava protect You. May Lord Vaikuṇṭha protect You while You are walking, and may Lord Nārāyaṇa, the husband of the goddess of fortune, protect You while You are sitting. Similarly, may Lord Yajñabhuk, the fearful enemy of all evil planets, always protect You while You enjoy life.
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
May Lord Pṛśnigarbha protect Your intelligence, and the Supreme Personality of Godhead Your soul. While You are playing, may Govinda protect You, and while You are sleeping may Mādhava protect You. May Lord Vaikuṇṭha protect You while You are walking, and may Lord Nārāyaṇa, the husband of the goddess of fortune, protect You while You are sitting. Similarly, may Lord Yajṣabhuk, the fearful enemy of all evil planets, always protect You while You enjoy life.
Jīva Gosvāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

Bhagavān paraḥ means Svayam Bhagavān. The subtle meaning here is that the expansions should protect Kṛṣṇa’s limbs since the expansions are the limbs in his possession and Svayam Bhagavān should protect himself (ātmānam) since he is the possessor of the limbs. The avatāras protecting his limbs are his āvaraṇa deities or protective forms surrounding him. Names are then mentioned in relation to Kṛṣṇa’s activities. Govinda means “he who plays among the cows.” The gopīs perceive different suitable forms in relation to the activities of Kṛṣṇa. Mādhava refers to the Lord sleeping on Śeṣa, while Lakṣmī massages his feet. He should protect Kṛṣṇa’s lying down and sleeping. Vaikuṇṭha is the avatāra who was the son of Vikuṇṭha, who came out of the palace using his feet to solve the quarrel between the Kumāras and Jaya and Vijaya. He should protect Kṛṣṇa’s walking. The husband of Lakṣmī refers to Mahā-nārāyaṇa who sits on a huge throne in Vaikuṇṭha. He should protect Kṛṣṇa’s sitting. The enjoyer of sacrifices should protect his enjoyment. The phrase “giving fear to all planets” should modify all the different forms of the Lord mentioned. All the deities mentioned protect Kṛṣṇa from the bad influence of planets while he performs all his activities.

Śrīdhara Svāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

The phrase “giving fear to all planets” should modify all the different forms of the Lord mentioned. All the deities mentioned protect Kṛṣṇa from the bad influence of planets while he performs all his activities.

Personal Guidance
Kṛṣṇa's plenary expansions guard every act — playing, sleeping, walking, sitting, eating — so entrust each ordinary motion of the day to Him.
Practical Applications
  • Before each transition today — rising, walking out, sitting at work, eating, lying down — silently call on Kṛṣṇa by one of His names as Govinda, Mādhava, or Nārāyaṇa.
  • Refuse the illusion of self-protection: when fear of accident, illness, or unseen forces arises, remember that Yajñabhuk is the real shield and that anxious self-management is wasted ahaṅkāra.
  • Treat one ordinary meal today as offered food eaten under Yajñabhuk's protection — pause, offer it to Kṛṣṇa, and eat without distraction or grievance.
Analogy

Pṛśnigarbha for the intellect, Bhagavān for the soul, Govinda for playing, Mādhava for sleeping, Vaikuṇṭha for walking, Lakṣmī-pati for sitting, Yajñabhuk for eating is the wife who packs her child's lunchbox with a different little prayer for each break of the school day — one for the bell, one for the recess, one for the way home. Every motion the soul makes has a named Bhagavān assigned to it.

Stories
Mīrā singing through the day

Mīrā Bai (Gauḍīya hagiography records her in the line of Vaiṣṇava bhaktās) is said to have sung to Giridhārī Gopāla through every movement of the day — grinding, drawing water, walking, eating, sleeping. She did not separate 'sādhana time' from 'kitchen time'; each motion was a name.

The gopīs' rakṣā-mantra in 10.6.25–26 sanctifies exactly this attitude. Govinda when playing, Mādhava when sleeping — Mīrā lived this verse as adult bhakti.
