Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.6.11

సా ముఞ్చ ముఞ్చాలమితి ప్రభాషిణీ నిష్పీడ్యమానాఖిలజీవమర్మణి । వివృత్య నేత్రే చరణౌ భుజౌ ముహు: ప్రస్విన్నగాత్రా క్షిపతీ రురోద హ ॥ ౧౧ ॥

Unbearably pressed in every vital point, the demon Pūtanā began to cry, “Please leave me, leave me! Suck my breast no longer!” Perspiring, her eyes wide open and her arms and legs flailing, she cried very loudly again and again.
Purport

The Rākṣasī was severely punished by Kṛṣṇa. She threw her arms and legs about, and Kṛṣṇa also began to kick her with His legs to punish her properly for her mischievous activities.

— A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda
Gauḍīya Ācāryas
Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura Gauḍīya
Translation
Unbearably pressed in every vital point, the demon Pūtanā began to cry, "Please leave me, leave me! Suck my breast no longer!" Perspiring, her eyes wide open and her arms and legs flailing, she cried very loudly again and again.
Commentary

Being squeezed by the child in all her sensitive points, she began flailing her hands and legs.

Jīva Gosvāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

After saying “get away from me” she could not repeat it since she had no strength to utter any more. He pressed all the points (marma) containing life in her body. She cried out in pain, while crying. The word ha expresses joy.

Śrīdhara Svāmī Gauḍīya
Commentary

She shouted loudly, but uselessly (alam). He pressed all the points where life resided in her. She cried loudly. Ha expresses bliss.

Personal Guidance
Even the demoness Pūtanā, struck at every vital point by Kṛṣṇa, must cry out and release her grip — what we cling to in māyā cannot withstand His touch.
Practical Applications
  • Name one viṣaya you have been 'feeding' as Pūtanā fed Kṛṣṇa, and today refuse it one concrete time — skip the scroll, the snack, the second look.
  • When an attachment screams 'leave me, leave me!' as it loosens, do not rescue it; let Kṛṣṇa finish the work and chant His names through the discomfort.
  • Before sleep, offer your day's vital points — ambitions, fears, resentments — to Kṛṣṇa's feet, asking Him to press out whatever is not bhakti.
