Room Conversation

Conversation · November 7th 1977 · Vṛndāvana

Kavirāja: [Hindi with Prabhupāda]

[background conversation about bathing Prabhupāda]

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: We do it about two times a day with a towel.

Kavirāja: [Hindi with Bhakti-caru]

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: Rose water? Water? [indistinct comments by Upendra]

Kavirāja: [Hindi with Prabhupāda; asks about parikramā]

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: You have to give Prabhupāda some strength to go on the parikramā.

Svarūpa Dāmodara: He said in three days.

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: Three days. That's good.

Kavirāja: [Hindi with Bhakti-caru and Prabhupāda about different foods]

Bhakti-caru: [Hindi about cāpāṭi and ḍāl]

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: But whether Prabhupāda will eat some? Prabhupāda, you will try to eat the Gujarati roṭī and ḍāl?

Prabhupāda: Hmm.

Kavirāja: [Hindi]

Prabhupāda: [Hindi]

Bhakti-caru: [long Hindi conversation about different foods and taste with Prabhupāda and kavirāja] Śrīla Prabhupāda? [Hindi] [break]

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: ...Gujarati roṭīs?

Bhakti-caru: [Hindi about Gujarati roṭīs and ḍāl and rice, khicuṛi, purīs, chutney]

Prabhupāda: [Hindi with Bhakti-caru] Mālpura I'll take.

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: Mālpura.

Bhakti-caru: [Hindi]

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: You want to try some mālpura later on tonight?

Bhakti-caru: [Hindi with Prabhupāda] Prabhupāda will let us know. [Hindi] He says one too hot. Just like the way it was today. [Hindi with Prabhupāda; suggests śrīkand]

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: Śrīkand. That's very good for you, Śrīla Prabhupāda, śrīkand.

Prabhupāda: Oh, śrīkand.

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: That's a good preparation for you, Śrīla Prabhupāda, very nutritious.

Bhakti-caru: [Hindi with Prabhupāda about different foods]

Prabhupāda: So, where is Baradrāj?

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: Baradrāj is here, Śrīla Prabhupāda.

Prabhupāda: So you can sing.

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: Jaya Śrīla Prabhupāda.

Prabhupāda: Softly.

Baradrāj: Softly. [break] [end]
