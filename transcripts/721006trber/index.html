Tour

Other · October 6th 1972 · Berkeley

Prabhupāda: Well.

Devotee: [indistinct] [sounds like a big empty room]

Prabhupāda: I have heard the chanting.

Karāndhara: They have, instead of chain they have made this a rail.

Prabhupāda: Very nice. So this is our own home.

Karāndhara: Yes it is owned.

Prabhupāda: How you can change it? Covered with so much dust.

[Jingling noises]

Devotee: [Offers obeisances]

nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya kṛṣṇa-preṣṭhāya bhū-tale śrīmate bhaktivedānta-svāmin iti nāmine.

Prabhupāda: Very good you are making [indistinct].

Devotee: My wife.

Brahmānanda: We are making it a large canopy for the Deities.

Prabhupāda: [indistinct].

Brahmānanda: Yes. And then we will buy one from India.

Devotees: [Offers obeisances]

nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya kṛṣṇa-preṣṭhāya bhū-tale śrīmate bhaktivedānta-svāmin iti nāmine...

Prabhupāda: Who has painted this Sanskrit?

Brahmānanda: Oh.

Karāndhara: Jayānanda’s wife, Trayī.

Prabhupāda: Oh.

Karāndhara: It is the Īśopaniṣads.

Prabhupāda: Yes.

[indistinct dialogue between devotees]

Karāndhara: Would you like to sit Prabhupāda or see the rest of the temple?

Prabhupāda: I can [indistinct] it. So prasādam is there or here?

Brahmānanda: There. For you? Yes in there. Do you wish to take here?

Prabhupāda: Anywhere.

[Indistinct dialogue between devotees]

Brahmānanda: Śrīla Prabhupāda I was wondering if you’d like to [indistinct].

Prabhupāda: That’s alright.

[Phone ringing. Chanting. Moving noises. Indistinct dialogue] [break]

Devotee: ...I’ve so many things to do in this house I missed it.

Prabhupāda: And this is not...

Devotee: Spiritual Sky. [indistinct dialogue] [loud traffic noise] [break] [end]
