Morning Walk, Partial Recording

Walk · June 1st 1974 · Geneva

Prabhupāda: [indistinct] [break].

Bhagavān: So I hope he will still come.

Prabhupāda: He is Prime Minister of France?

Bhagavān: He just became the Prime Minister of France.

Prabhupāda: Hmm. Young man?

Bhagavān: Forty? [break]

Prabhupāda: ...is still very dangerous. You see? Padaṁ padam in every step [SB 10.14.58]. There are some fruits on this tree? No. Oh, here is eucalyptus? No.

[break] ...even by children, they are living.

Prabhupāda: There is only one offspring? [end]
