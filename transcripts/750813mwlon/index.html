Morning Walk [partially recorded]

Walk · August 13th 1975 · London

Prabhupāda: ...ratha car?

Haṁsadūta: Well, we're thinking of trying again for the permission to get the use of it.

Prabhupāda: Yes.

Haṁsadūta: Then we'll keep it. No, their objection was that it went..., it jumped the curb; it went out of control at one point, and therefore...

Prabhupāda: That we can rectify. We can make such arrangement it will not jump; it will sit down. [Haṁsadūta laughs] [break] ...don't work.

Haṁsadūta: It can work to take the ratha car out.

Prabhupāda: Huh?

Haṁsadūta: We...

Prabhupāda: Oh.

Haṁsadūta: We always take it off to take the ratha car out. [japa] [end]
