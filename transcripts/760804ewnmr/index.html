Evening Walk [partially recorded]

Walk · August 4th 1976 · New Māyāpur

Prabhupāda: Oh, can I see? [break]

Bhagavān: [aside:] Is it clean? Carnations, and these are gladiolus, for the vases, those long flowers. This is the chili peppers over here, green peppers. What's the best way to go, Haribol? Over here?

Haribolānanda: ...wants to see over there? The flowers and everything? [break] [tape of Prabhupāda singing in background]

Prabhupāda: ...that this voice does not belong to this universe.

Devotees: Haribol!

Prabhupāda: That is the secret. It is imported from the spiritual world. Golokera prema-dhana, hari-nāma-saṅkīrtana. Golokera. It is coming from Goloka Vṛndāvana.

[sings śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya prabhu-nityānanda]

[pause]

Prabhupāda: That girl has returned? No.

Hari-śauri: That girl from Thailand? She's still in Paris?

Bhagavān: She's in Paris.

Hari-śauri: She's still in Paris. [end]
