Prabhupāda: Translation. Translation, read.

Amogha: "But he who discards scriptural injunctions, and acts according to his own whims, attains neither perfection nor happiness nor the supreme destination."

Prabhupāda: That's it. [Hindi] ...bogus, bogus. This is statement of Kṛṣṇa. Can you give us the statement of Kṛṣṇa? And accept some foolish statement. So anyone who's speaking against the injunction of the śāstra, he's a bogus. There is another verse, find out = tasmād śāstra pramāṇaṁ te. Tasmād, t-a-s-m-a-d.
