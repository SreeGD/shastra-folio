Room Conversation

Conversation · October 17th 1977 · Vṛndāvana

Bhavānanda: Śrīla Prabhupāda, Acyutānanda Mahārāja has come.

Prabhupāda: Here?

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: Acyutānanda Mahārāja.

Prabhupāda: Here. Where you are preaching now?

Acyutānanda: I was in Calcutta during the Māyāpur affairs. Then I went to South India. Now I'm going to America. My stay in India may be terminated. And I received American passport. I'm able to travel now. Rāmeśvara Prabhu is sending me a ticket.

Prabhupāda: Huh? Rāmeśvara?

Bhavānanda: Rāmeśvara Mahārāja is sending a ticket for him to go.

Prabhupāda: Oh. So you can go now, America, eh?

Bhavānanda: Yes.

Acyutānanda = Yes.

Prabhupāda: That's good. You attended the conference?

Acyutānanda: Yes.

Prabhupāda: You liked?

Hari-śauri: You liked it?

Acyutānanda: Oh, yes. It was very successful. One scientist has actually been touched. His heart is changed, Dr. Ramaya. He, in fact, defended our one point with one other scientist.

Prabhupāda: Yes.

Tamāla Kṛṣṇa: [indistinct aside with Hari-śauri] You are ready for bathing, Śrīla Prabhupāda?

Prabhupāda: Yes. [end]
