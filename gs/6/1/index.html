GS 6.1.1
Translation

śrī-nārada uvāca

kṛṣṇāya vāsudevāya devakī-nandanāya ca nanda-gopa-kumarāya govindāya namo namaḥ

śrī-nārada uvāca—Śrī Nārada said; kṛṣṇāya—to Lord Kṛṣṇa; vāsudevāya—the son of Vasudeva; devakī-nandanāya—the son of Devcakī; ca—and; nanda-gopa-kumarāya—the son of Nanda-gopa; govindāya—to the happiness of the cows, land and senses; namaḥ—obeisances; namaḥ—obeisances.

Śrī Nārada said: I offer my respectful obeisances to Lord Kṛṣṇa, the son of Vasudeva, Devakī, and the gopa Nanda, and the happiness of the cows, land, and senses.
