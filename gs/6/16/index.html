Word-for-word
siddhāśramasyaof Siddhāśrama māhātmyam—glories; śṛṇuplease hear; rājanO king; mahā-matenoble-hearted; yasyaof which; smaraṇahearing; mātreṇasimply by; sarva-pāpaiḥall sins; pramucyatefreed.
