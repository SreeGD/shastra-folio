tvaraṁ mṛdhe vai jayatām arīn svān
Word-for-word
ahaḥOh; sthitesituated; rājanithe king; duṣṭa-cittewicked at heart; nanot; ko 'pisomeone; vaktumto say; kṣamaable; evaindeed; sakhyaḥfriends; tasmātfrom this; dhiindeed; naḥof us; puṇya-balenaby the power of piety; cetif; tauThey; tvaramquickly; mṛdhein the fight; vaiindeed; jayatāmmay be victory; arīnenemies; svānown.
Translation

Friends, while this wicked king is present no one dare say anything. We simply pray that by the power of Their pious deeds these two boys will defeat Their enemies. .pa
