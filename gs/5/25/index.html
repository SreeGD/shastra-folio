śrī-nārada uvāca paripūrṇatamaḥ sākṣāc chrī-kṛṣṇo bhagavān hariḥ svayaṁ hi mathurā-nāthaḥ keśavaḥ kleśa-nāsanaḥ śrī-nārada uvāca—Śrī Nārada said; paripūrṇatamaḥ—the Supreme Personality of Godhead; sākṣāc—directly; chrī-kṛṣṇaḥ—Śrī Kṛṣṇa; bhagavān—the Lord; hariḥ—Hari; svayam—personally; hi—indeed; mathurā-nāthaḥ—the Lord of Mathurā; keśavaḥ—Kṛṣṇa; kleśa-nāsanaḥ—the destroyer of sufferings.

Śrī Nārada said: Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the perfect Supreme Personality of Godhead, who destroys all sufferings, is the Deity of Mathurā.
