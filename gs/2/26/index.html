Word-for-word
śrī-bahulāśva uvācaŚrī Bahulāśva said; aghāsurādi-daityānāmof the demons headed by Aghasura; jyotiḥthe light; kṛṣṇein Kṛṣṇa; samāviśatentered; śrīdāmniin Śrīdama; sakhacūḍāsyaof sankhacuda; kasmālwhy?; līnammerging; babhūvawas; haindeed.
