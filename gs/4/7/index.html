Word-for-word
rāsein thew rasa dance; vimala-putrīṇāmthe daughters of King Vimala; ānandaof bliss; jala-bindubhiḥwith drops of perspiration; cyutaiḥfallen; vimala-kuṇḍaḥVimala-kuṇḍa; abhūtbecame; tīrthānāmof holy places; tīrthamthe holy place; uttamamthe best.
Translation

As they enjoyed the happiness of the rāsa dance, King Viumala's daughters perspired. Their perspiration became Vimala-kuṇḍa, the best of holy places.
