GS 4.9.1
IAST
śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
vṛṣabhānu-sute su-bhru
sarva-śāstrārtha-pāra-ge
viḍambayantī tvaṁ vācā
vācaṁ vācaspater muneḥ
Word-for-word
śrī-gopya ūcuḥthe gopīs said; vṛṣabhānu-suteO daughter of King Vṛṣabhānu; su-bhruO girl with the beautiful eyebrows; sarva-śāstrārthathe meanings of all the scriptures; pāra-geO You who travel to the farther shore; viḍambayantiimitating; tvamYou; vācāwith words; vācamthe words; vācaspateḥof Brhaspati; muneḥthe sage.
Translation

The gopīs said: O beautiful-eyebrowed daughter of King Vṛṣabhānu, O girl who has traveled to the farther shore of the deep meanings of all the scriptures, Your words are like Bṛhaspati Muni's words.

tvaṁ sākṣāj jṣāna-śevadhiḥ
Word-for-word
rādheO Rādhā; kenaby whom?; kenaby whom?; purābefore; kṛtamdone; tatthat; brūhiplease tell; naḥto us; viśeṣeṇaspecifically; tvamYou; sākṣātdirectly; jṣāna-śevadhiḥfilled with knowledge.
Translation

O Rādhā, what great souls followed ekādaśī in ancient times? Please tell us. You know all this.

Word-for-word
śrī-rādhā uvācaSrī Rādhā said; ādauin the beginning; devaiḥwith the demigods; kṛtamdone; gopyaḥO gopīs; varamblessing; ekādaśī-vratamthe vow of ekādaśī; bhraṣṭa-rājyasyalost kingdom; lābhārthamto attain; daityānāmof the demigods; nāśanāyafor the destruction; caand.

Srī Rādhā said: O gopīs, in the beginning of creation the demigods followed ekādaśī to destroy the demons and regain their lost kingdom.

In the city of Bhadrāvatī, King Ketumān was sonless. Following ekādaśī on a great saint's advice, he obtained a son.

Word-for-word

Translation

Once the demigoddesses gave ekādaśī to a brāhmaṇī. By following ekādaśī that brāhmaṇī attained great wealth and happiness like that on Svargaloka.

Word-for-word

Cursed by Indra, Puṣpadantī and Mālyavān became demons, but by following ekādaśī they again became Gandharvas.

GS 4.9.10

Word-for-word
layānteat the end of the cosmic devastation; caand; samutpannāmanifested; dhatṛ-vṛkṣa-taleon the tree of the creator; surāḥthe demigods; ekādaśī-vratamekādaśī; cakruḥdid; sarva-kalyāṇa-hetaveto attain all auspiciousness.

When, after the period of cosmic devastation had ended, the demigods were again manifested from the tree of creation, they followed ekādaśī to attain all auspiciousness.

Word-for-word
