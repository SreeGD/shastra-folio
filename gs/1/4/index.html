śrī-rāmaḥ uvācaŚrī Rāma said; samīcīnamright; vacaḥstatement; satyamtruth; yuṣmābhiḥby you; gaditamsaid; caalso; meof Me; ekaone; patnīwife; vrataḥvow; ahamI; hiIndeed; rājaking; ṛṣiḥsage; sītayāby Sītā; ekayāalone.
Translation & commentary

Śrī Rāma said: Your words are right. What you say to Me is true. (Still,) I am religious king, and I have vowed to accept only Sītā as My wife.
