Bṛhad-bhāgavatāmṛta

by Śrīla Sanātana Gosvāmī

Sanātana Gosvāmī's 16th-century epic in two khaṇḍas: Nārada Muni's quest for the topmost recipient of Krishna's mercy (Prathama), and Gopa-kumāra's journey through every loka in search of pure love (Uttara). With the author's own Dig-darśinī-ṭīkā commentary.
