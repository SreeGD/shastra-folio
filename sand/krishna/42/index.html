Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 43.15

न च सुर-स्त्रीणां सम्भव-वाक्यं श्री-महिषी-वृन्द-परं तासाम् अपि तन् निज-
शक्ति-रूपत्वेन दर्शयिष्यमाणत्वात्. श्री-शुकः.

na ca sura-strīṇāṃ sambhava-vākyaṃ śrī-mahiṣī-vṛnda-paraṃ tāsām api tan nija-

śakti-rūpatvena darśayiṣyamāṇatvāt. śrī-śukaḥ.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

na—not; ca—also; sura-strīṇām—the women of the heavenly planets; sambhava-vākyam—description; śrī-mahiṣī-vṛnda—than the goddesses of fortune; param—superior; tāsām—of them; api—also; tat—His; nija—own; śakti—potency; rūpatvena—in the form; darśayiṣyamānatvāt—because of revealing; śrī-śukaḥ—spoken by Śrī Śukadeva Gosvāmī.

English translation and comments

We may therefore understand that the phrase "sura-striyaḥ" refers neither to the wives of the demigods, nor the goddesses of fortune, nor the queens of Dvārakā, but to the gopīs, who are the internal potencies of Lord Kṛṣṇa.

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 44.1

तद् एवम् अवतार-प्रसङ्गे ऽपि श्री-कृष्णस्य स्वयं भगवत्त्वम् एवायातम्.
यस्माद् एवं तस्माद् एव श्री-भागवते महा-स्रोतृ-वक्तृणाम् अपि श्री-कृष्ण एव
तात्पर्यं लक्ष्यते. अत्र श्री-विदुरस्य

tad evam avatāra-prasaṅge 'pi śrī-kṛṣṇasya svayaṃ bhagavattvam evāyātam.

yasmād evaṃ tasmād eva śrī-bhāgavate mahā-srotṛ-vaktṛṇām api śrī-kṛṣṇa eva

tātparyaṃ lakṣyate. atra śrī-vidurasya

[Word-by-word analysis:]

tat—that; evam—in this way; avatāra-prasaṅge—in the context of the incarnations of Godhead; api—also; śrī-kṛṣṇasya—of Śrī Kṛṣṇa; svayam bhagavattvam—the position as the Original Personality of Godhead; eva—certainly; āyātam—is attained; yasmāt—because; evam—in this way; tasmāt—therefore; eva—certainly; śrī-bhāgavate—in the Śrīmad Bhāgavatam; maha—great; śrotṛ—of hearers; vaktṛnām—and or speakers; api—also; śrī—kṛṣṇa; tātparyam—explanation; lakṣyate—is observed; atra—in this connection; śrī-vidūrasya—the statement of Śrī Vidura (Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 4.17.6-7).

English translation and comments

Because Śrī Kṛṣṇa is the Original Personality of Godhead, the source of all incarnations, His glories are heard and described throughout the Śrīmad- Bhāgavatam. This is described in the following statement of Śrī Vidura (Śrīmad- Bhāgavatam 4.17.6-7):

Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 45.2

तत्-प्रशंसया प्रीत-मनस्त्वेन चास्यापि तथैव तात्पर्यं लभ्यते. अत एवात्र
श्री-वसुदेव-नन्दनत्वेनैव वासुदेव-शब्दः प्रयुक्तः श्री-सूतः.

tat-praśaṃsayā prīta-manastvena cāsyāpi tathaiva tātparyaṃ labhyate. ata evātra

śrī-vasudeva-nandanatvenaiva vāsudeva-śabdaḥ prayuktaḥ śrī-sūtaḥ.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

tat—Him; praśaṃsaya—by praise; prīta—pleased; manastvena—with the mind; ca—also; asya—of Him; api—even; tathā—in the same way; eva—certainly; tātparyam—meaning; labhyate—is attained; ataḥ eva—therefore; atra—in this connection; śrī-vasudeva—of Mahārāja Vasudeva; nandanatvena—because of being the son; vāsudeva-śabdaḥ—the name "Vāsudeva"; prayuktaḥ—proper; śrī-sūtaḥ—spoken by Sūta Gosvāmī.

English translation and comments

In connection with this verse we may note that by hearing the glorification of the Supreme Lord, Maitreya Muni became pleased at heart. We may also observe that Lord Kṛṣṇa is known by the name Vāsudeva because He is the son of Mahārāja Vasudeva.
