Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.151

किम् एतच् छाश्वतं ब्रह्म
वेदान्त-शत-रूपितम्
ताप त्रयर्ति-दग्धानां
जीवनं छत्रतां गतम्

kim etac chāśvataṃ brahma

vedānta-śata-rūpitam

tāpa trayarti-dagdhānāṃ

jīvanaṃ chatratāṃ gatam

[Word-by-word analysis:]

kim—what?; etat—this; sāśvatam—eternal; brahma— spiritual place; vedānta—in the Vedānta Scriptures; śaata— hundreds; rūpitam—described; tāpa-traya-arti—by the threefold; misiries; dagdhānām—burned; jīvanam—in relation to the living entities; chatratām—the status of an umbrella; gatam—attained.

English translation and comments

"Please tell us what is this eternal spiritual place, which is described hundreds of times in the Vedānta literatures, and which gives shelter to they who suffer the threefold miseries of material existance?

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 106.152
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.152

दर्शनाद् एव चास्याथ
कृतार्थाः स्म जगद्-गुरो
वारं वारं तवाप्य् अत्र
दृष्टिर् लग्ना जनार्दन
परमाश्चर्य-रूपो ऽपि
साश्चर्य इव पश्यसि

darśanād eva cāsyātha

kṛtārthāḥ sma jagad-guro

vāraṃ vāraṃ tavāpy atra

dṛṣṭir lagnā janārdana

paramāścarya-rūpo 'pi

sāścarya iva paśyasi

[Word-by-word analysis:]

darsanat—from the sight; eva—certainly; ca—also; asya—of this place; atha—now; krta-arthah sma—became perfect and happy; jagat-guro—O master of the universe; varam varam—again and again; tava—Your; api—even; atra—here; drstiḥ—glance; lagna— is placed; janardana—O Lord Janardana; parama—supremely; ascarya—wonderful; rupaḥ—form; api—although; sa—with; ascaryaḥ—wonder; iva—as if; pasyasi—you see.

English translation and comments

"O Lord of the universe, simply by seeing this place, the living entities become perfect and happy. O Lord Janārdana, although Your transcendental form is the most wonderful thing, as You continuously glance upon this place, You Yourself become filled with wonder."

[Word-by-word analysis:]

Lord Viṣṇu answered: "This effulgent place like a parasol shining in the sky is known on this earth as the city of Vārāṇasī.

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 106.154
IAST
prthivyām
bhūmimayī
majjam/eneṣu
ānandamayī
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.154

रत्नं सुवर्णे खचितम् यथा भवेत्
तथा पृथिव्यां खचिता हि काशिका
न काशिका भूमिमयी कदाचित्
ततो न मज्जेन् मम सद्-गतिर् यतः
जडेषु सर्वेष्व् अपि मज्जमानेष्व्
इयं चिद्-आनन्दमयी न मज्जेत् इत्य् आदि.

ratnaṃ suvarṇe khacitam yathā bhavet

tathā pṛthivyāṃ khacitā hi kāśikā

na kāśikā bhūmimayī kadācit

tato na majjen mama sad-gatir yataḥ

jaḍeṣu sarveṣv api majjamāneṣv

iyaṃ cid-ānandamayī na majjet ity ādi.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

ratnam—a precious jewel; suvarṇe—in gold; khacitam—set; yathā—just as; bhavet—may be; tathā—in the same way; prthivyām—on this earth; khacitā—is set; hi—certainly; kāśikā—Varanasi; na—not; kāśikā—Varanasi; bhūmimayī— made of material elements; kadācit—at any time; tataḥ—and therefore; na—does not; majjet—become submerged; mm—My; sat— eternal; gatiḥ—abode; yataḥ—because; jaḍeṣu—when the inert material elements; sarveṣu—all; api—even; majjam/eneṣu—are becoming submerged; iyam—this place; cit—full of transcendental knowledge; ānandamayī—and bliss; na—does not; majjet—become submerged.

English translation and comments

"This city of Vārāṇasī is placed on the earth as a precious jewel is set in a golden ornament. Vārāṇasī is my eternal transcendental abode, and it is not composed of material elements. When the inert material elements become submerged in the waters of annihilation, this abode of transcendental knowledge and bliss, the city of Vārāṇasī, is not touched by that destructive flood."

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 106.155
IAST
ekā-sthayoḥ
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.155

तथाग्रे च
चेतना-जडयोर् ऐक्यं
याद्वन् नैकस्थयोर् अपि
तथा काशी ब्रह्म-रूपा
जडा पृथ्वी न सङ्गता

tathāgre ca

cetanā-jaḍayor aikyaṃ

yādvan naikasthayor api

tathā kāśī brahma-rūpā

jaḍā pṛthvī na saṅgatā

[Word-by-word analysis:]

tatha—in the same way; agre—in the beginning; ca—also; cetanā—of the consciousness; jaḍayoḥ—and of the inert material elements; aikyam—oneness; yādvat—to which extent; na—not; ekā-sthayoḥ—in one place; api—although; tathā—in the same way; kāśī—varanasi; brahma—spiritual; rūpā—form; jaḍā—inert material element; pṛthvī—earth; na—not; saṅgatā—in contact.

English translation and comments

The Supreme Lord also describes Vārāṇasī in the following verses from the beginning of the Brahma-vaivarta Purāṇa: "Consciousness and inert material elements remain eternally distinct, even though they may both be situated in the same place (the body). In the same way, the spiritual abode known as Vārāṇasī remains always distinct from the material earth on which it rests.

निर्मानं तु जडस्यात्र

[Word-by-word analysis:]

चेतना-शब्देनात्रान्तर्याम्य् उपलक्ष्यते. जड-शब्देन तु देहः, परमात्मन

[Word-by-word analysis:]

English translation and comments

In Text 155 the word "cetanā" means "the Supersoul". Because the Supersoul is discussed, the word "jada" here means "the material body".
