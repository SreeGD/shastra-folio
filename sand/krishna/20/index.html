Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 28.15:

एवम् पुंश इति भगवान् इति च प्रथमम् उपक्रमोद्दिष्टस्य तस्य शब्द-

द्वयस्य तत्-सहोदरेण तेनैव शब्देन च प्रतिनिर्देशत्ताव् एव खल्व् एताव् इति

स्मरयति. उद्देश-प्रतिनिर्देशयोः प्रतीतिस्थगितता-निराशनाय विद्वद्भिर् एक एव

शब्दः प्रयुज्यते तत्-सम-वर्णो वा; यथा ज्योतिष्टोमाधिकरणे वसन्ते वसन्ते

च ज्योतिषा यजेत इत्य् अत्र ज्योतिः-शब्दो ज्योतिष्टोम-विषये भवति.

evam puṃśa iti bhagavān iti ca prathamam upakramoddiṣṭasya tasya śabda-

dvayasya tat-sahodareṇa tenaiva śabdena ca pratinirdeśattāv eva khalv etāv iti

smarayati. uddeśa-pratinirdeśayoḥ pratītisthagitatā-nirāśanāya vidvadbhir eka eva

śabdaḥ prayujyate tat-sama-varṇo vā;  yathā jyotiṣṭomādhikaraṇe vasante vasante

ca jyotiṣā yajeta ity atra jyotiḥ-śabdo jyotiṣṭoma-viṣaye bhavati.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

evam—in this way; puṃsaḥ—of the puruṣa incarnation; iti—thus; bhagavān—the Original Personality of Godhead; iti—thus; ca—also; prathamam—in the beginning; upakrama-uddiṣṭasya—of the statement; tasya—that; śabda—of the words; dvayasya—of the pair; tat—His; sahodareṇa—with the brother (Lord Balarāma); tena—with Him; eva—certainly; śabdena—by the word; ca—also; pratinirdeśattau—the two repetitions; eva—certainly; khalu—indeed; etau—the two; iti—thus; smarayeti—causes to remember; uddeśa—of the first statement; pratinirdeśayoḥ—and of the repetition; pratīti—of the conclusion; sthagitatā—hidden meaning; nirāśanāya—for refuting; vidvadbhiḥ—by the learned; ekaḥ—one; eva—certainly; śabdaḥ—word; prayujyate—is uded; tat-sama-varṇaḥ—spelled in the same way; vā—also; yathā—just as; jyotiṣṭoma-adhikaraṇe—in the connection with the Jyotiṣṭoma sacrifice; vasante vasante-every spring; ca—also; jyotiṣa—with the Jyotiṣṭoma ceremony; yajeta—one should offer sacrifice; iti—thus; atra—here; jyotiḥ-śabdaḥ—the word "jyotiḥ"; jyotiṣṭoma-viṣaye—in connection with the Jyotiṣṭoma sacrifice; bhavati—is.

English translation and comments:

The words "puṃśaḥ" (the puruṣa-incarnations) and "bhagavān" (the Original Personality of Godhead) are also used in this chapter of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam (Canto One, Chapter Three), in the first verse (jagṛhe pauruṣaṃ rūpaṃ bhagavān mahad-ādibhiḥ), and in verse 23, where the Lord is described with His brother (rāma-kṛṣṇāv it bhuvo bhagavān aharad bharam). To refute any agrument by the learned that it should be interpreted to have a different, hidden meaning, the word "bhagavān" is used twice in this chapter. This is so just as the word jyotiḥ is repeated in the chapter describing the jyotiṣṭoma-yajña (vasante vasante ca jyotiṣā yajeta: every spring one should offer a jyotiṣṭoma-yajña).

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 28.16
IAST
tattvavāda
śabda-sthāne
mula-rūpī
vibhinnaṃśaḥ
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 28.16:

अत्र तत्त्ववाद-गुरवस् तु "च-शब्द-स्थाने स्व-शब्दम् पठित्वैवम् आचाक्षते-

एते प्रोक्ता अवतारा मूल-रूपी स्वयम् एव. किं स्वरूपः? स्वांश-कलाः, न तु

जीववद् विभिन्नांशः.

atra tattvavāda-guravas tu "ca-śabda-sthāne sva-śabdam paṭhitvaivam ācākṣate-

ete proktā avatārā mūla-rūpī svayam eva. kiṃ svarūpaḥ? svāṃśa-kalāḥ, na tu

jīvavad vibhinnāṃśaḥ.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

atra—in this connection; tattvavāda—the followers of Śrīpada Madhvācārya; guravaḥ—the spiritual masters; tu—but; ca—also; śabda-sthāne—in place of the word; sva—"sva" (own); śabdam—the word; paṭhitvā—reading; evam—in the following way; ācakṣate—declare; ete—these; proktāḥ—mentioned; avatārāḥ—incarnations; mula-rūpī—the origin; svayam—in person; eva—certainly; kim—what are; svarūpaḥ—His own forms; sva—own; aṃśa—plenary portions; kalāḥ—and portions of the plenary portions; na—not; tu—but; jīvavat—as the individual living entities; vibhinnaṃśaḥ—eternally distinct parts.

English translation and comments:

The tattvavāda-gurus accept a different reading of this verse, replacing the word "ca" (and) with the word "sva" (own). They say: "The word 'ete' in this verse refers to the incarnations of the Lord and the word 'bhagavān svayam' refers to the Lord Himself, whose form is the origin of the incarnations. What is the Lord's form? His forms are mentioned in the words svāṃśa-kalāḥ (The incarnations, which are His own plenary portions and portions of the portions, are His forms). His forms are not vibhinnāṃśa (separated parts), as are the jīvas."

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 28.17
IAST
vibhinnaṃśaḥ
sāmarthyam
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 28.17:

यथा वराहे

स्वांसश् चाथ विभिन्नंश

इति द्वेधांश इष्यते

अंशिनो यत् तु सामार्थ्यं

यत् स्वरूपं यथा स्थितिः

yathā varāhe

svāṃsaś cātha vibhinnaṃśa

iti dvedhāṃśa iṣyate

aṃśino yat tu sāmārthyaṃ

yat svarūpaṃ yathā sthitiḥ

[Word-by-word analysis:]

svāṃśaḥ—Personal expansions; ca—also; atha—now; vibhinnaṃśaḥ—separate expansions; iti—thus; dvedha—two; aṃśaḥ—expansions; iṣyate—are distinguished; aṃśinaḥ—of the source of all expansions; yat—which; tu—also; sāmarthyam—ability; yat—which; svarūpam—own from; yathā—just as; sthitiḥ—status.

English translation and comments:

"The two kinds of expansions from the Supreme Personality of Godhead are: 1. svāṃśa (personal expansions) and 2. vibhinnāṃśa (separate persons). The svāṃśa expansions are unlimitedly powerful. Their form and personality are the same as the Supreme Personality of Godhead Himself.

तद् एव नानुमात्रो ऽपि

Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 28.19:

अत्रोच्यते अंशानाम् अंशि-सामार्थ्यादिकं तद्-ऐक्येनैव मन्तव्यम्. तच् च

यथाविदासिन इत्य् आदौ तस्याक्षयत्वेन तासाम् अक्षयत्वं यथा ताद्वत्,

अंशांशित्वानुपपत्तेर् एव. तथा च श्री-वासुदेवानिरुद्धयोः सर्वथा संये

प्रसक्ते कदाचिद् अनिरुद्धेणापि श्री-वासुदेवस्याविर्भावना प्रसज्ज्येत. तच् च

श्रुत-विपरीतम् इत्य् असद् एव. तस्माद् अस्त्य् एवावटर्य्-अवतारयोस् तारतम्यम्.

atrocyate aṃśānām aṃśi-sāmārthyādikaṃ tad-aikyenaiva mantavyam. tac ca

yathāvidāsina ity ādau tasyākṣayatvena tāsām akṣayatvaṃ yathā tādvat,

aṃśāṃśitvānupapatter eva. tathā ca śrī-vāsudevāniruddhayoḥ sarvathā saṃye

prasakte kadācid aniruddheṇāpi śrī-vāsudevasyāvirbhāvanā prasajjyeta. tac ca

śruta-viparītam ity asad eva. tasmād asty evāvaṭary-avatārayos tāratamyam.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

atra—in this connection; ucyate—it is said; aṃśānām—of the expansions; aṃśi—of the source of the incarnations; sāmarthya—power; ādikam—etc.; tat—with Him; aikyena—with equality; eva—certainly; mantavyam—should be considered; tat—therefore; ca—also; yathāvidāsina iti ādau—in the verse beginning "yathāvidāsina"; tasya—of Him; akṣayatvena—with eternity; tāsām—of them; akṣayatvam—eternity; yathā—just as; tadvat—in the same way; aṃśa-aṃśitva—of the stte of the incarnations or the source of the incarnations; anupapatteḥ—of the state of being inapplicable; eva—certainly; tathā—in the same way; ca—also; śrī-vāsudeva—of Lord Vāsudeva; aniruddhayoḥ—and Lord Aniruddha; sarvathā—always; sāmye—in equality; prasakte—devoted; kadācit—sometimes; aniruddhena—by Lord Aniruddha; api—also; śrī-vāsudevasya—of Lord Vāsudeva; āvirbhāvana—the appearance; prasajjyeta—is aspired for; tat—that; ca—also; śruta-viparīta—contradicting the Vedic literatures; iti—thus; asat—false; eva—certainly; tasmāt—therefore; asti—there is; eva—certainly; avatāri—of the source of the incarnation; avatārayoḥ—and of the incarnation; tāratamyam—gradations of higher and lower.

English translation and comments:

Here it is said that both the Original Personality of Godhead and His expansions (aṃśas) are equally powerful. In the phrase "yathāvidāsinaḥ kulyāḥ sarasaḥ syuḥ sahasraśaḥ" (The incarnations of the Lord are innumerable like rivulets flowing from inexaustible sources of water) (1.3.26) it may be understood that, because the Lord and His expansions share the same nature, as the original Supreme Personality of Godhead is eternal, so His expansions are also eternal. Although Lord Vāsudeva and Lord Aniruddha are equal in all respects, Lord Aniruddha sometimes devotedly meditates on Lord Vāsudeva. To argue that this is cotradicted by the Śruti-śāstra is wrong. For the Supreme Lord, who is the source of all incarnations, and for all His expansions, there are gradations of higher and lower.

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 28.21
IAST
vāsudevasya
saṅkarṣaṇāt
vyākhyānam
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 28.21:

इत्य् आदौ वासुदेवस्य सङ्कर्षणाद् अपि परत्वं श्रूयते. यत् तु तेषां तथा

व्याख्यानम् तत्र कृष्णस् तु इत्य् अनर्थकं स्यात्, भगवान् स्वयम् इत्य्

अनेनैवाभिप्रेत-सिद्धेः.

ity ādau vāsudevasya saṅkarṣaṇād api paratvaṃ śrūyate. yat tu teṣāṃ tathā

vyākhyānam tatra kṛṣṇas tu ity anarthakaṃ syāt, bhagavān svayam ity

anenaivābhipreta-siddheḥ.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

iti—thus; ādau—beginning; vāsudevasya—of Lord Vāsudeva; saṅkarṣaṇāt—from Lord Saṅkarṣaṇa; api—even; paratvam—superiority; śrūyate—is heard; yat—which; tu—also; teṣām—of them; tathā—in that way; vyākhyānam—explanation; tatra—in that connection; kṛṣṇaḥ—Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa; tu—but; iti—thus; anarthakam—without meaning; syāt—is; bhagavān—the Original Personality of Godhead; svayam—in person; iti—thus; anena—by this; eva—certainly; abhipreta—intented meaning; siddheḥ—because of the perfection.

English translation and comments:

This verse clearly states that Lord Vāsudeva is superior to Lord Saṅkarṣaṇa. These gradations of higher and lower do not refer to Kṛṣṇa. The phrase kṛṣṇas tu (but Kṛṣṇa) separates Him from them. Kṛṣṇa is then declared to be "bhagavān svayam" (the original Supreme Personality of Godhead).

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 28.22
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 28.22:

किं च, तैः स्वयम् एव प्रकासादिवान् नैवम् परः इति सूत्रे स्फुटम् अंशांशि-

भेदो दार्शितः.

kiṃ ca, taiḥ svayam eva prakāsādivān naivam paraḥ iti sūtre sphuṭam aṃśāṃśi-

bhedo dārśitaḥ.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

kim ca-furthermore; taiḥ—by them; svayam—in person; eva—certainly; prakāśa—of manifestations; ādi—original source; vat—like; na—not; evam—in this way; paraḥ—superior; iti—thus; sūtre—in the Vedānta sūtra; sphuṭam—clearly; aṃśa—of the expansions; aṃśi—of the Original Personality of Godhead, the source of all expansions; bhedaḥ—the difference; darśitaḥ—is observed.

English translation and comments:

"No one is superior to the Original Personality of Godhead, from whom everything has expanded." In this sūtra the difference between the Original Personality of Godhead and His expansions is clearly shown.
