Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.127

किम् तस्य स्थानम् इति श्री-गान्धर्व्याः प्रश्नस्योत्तरम् इदम्.

kim tasya sthānam iti śrī-gāndharvyāḥ praśnasyottaram idam.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

kim—what?; tasya—of Him; sthānam—the abode; iti—thus; śrī-gāndharvyāḥ—of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; praśnasya—of the question; uttaram—the reply; idam—this.

English translation and comments

{ }These verses from the Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad were spoken by Durvāsā Muni in reply to Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī's questions about the nature of Lord Kṛṣṇa's transcendental abode.

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 106.128

śrī-raghunāthasyā
ayodhyāyām
āyodhyā-mahātmye

bhratrbhiḥ
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.128

एवम् एव श्री-रघुनाथस्याप्य् अयोध्यायां श्रूयते. यथा स्कान्दायोध्या-
महात्म्ये स्वर्गं द्वारम् उद्दिश्य
चतुर्धा च तनुं कृत्वा
देव-देवो हरिः स्वयम्
अत्रैव रमते नित्यं
भ्रतृभिः सह राघवः इति.

evam eva śrī-raghunāthasyāpy ayodhyāyāṃ śrūyate. yathā skāndāyodhyā-

mahātmye svargaṃ dvāram uddiśya

caturdhā ca tanuṃ kṛtvā

deva-devo hariḥ svayam

atraiva ramate nityaṃ

bhratṛbhiḥ saha rāghavaḥ iti.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

evam—in the same way; eva—certainly; śrī-raghunāthasyā—of Lord Ramacandra; api—also; ayodhyāyām—in Ayodhya; śrūyate—is heard; yathā—just as; skāndā—in the Skāndā Puraṇa; āyodhyā-mahātmye—in the glorification of Ayodhya; svarga-dvaram—the entrance to the celestial world; usidya—in relation to; caturdha—fourfold; ca—also; tanum—form; krtva— manifesting; deva-devaḥ—the Personality of Godhead, who is worshipped by all the demigods; hari—Lord Hari; svayam—personally; atra—there; ramate—performs pastimes; nityam— eternally; bhratrbhiḥ—brothers; saha—with; raghavaḥ—Lord Ramacandra; iti—thus.

English translation and comments

As Vṛndāvana is the eternal abode of Lord Kṛṣṇa, so Ayodhyā is the eternal abode of Lord Rāmacandra. This is confirmed in the following verse from the Ayodhyā-māhātmya of the Skanda Purāṇa: "The Personality of Godhead, Lord Rāmacandra, expanding Himself in the forms of His brothers (Lakṣmaṇa, Śatrughna, and Bharata), eternally enjoys pastimes in the transcendental abode of Ayodhyā."

Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.129

अत एव यत्र यत्र हरेः स्थानं
वैकुण्ठं तद् विदुर् बुधाः
इत्य् अनुसारेण महा-भगवतः स्थानत्वा
महा-वैकुण्ठ एवसौ, यतो वैकुण्ठात् तस्य गरीयस्त्वं
श्रूयते.

ata eva yatra yatra hareḥ sthānaṃ

vaikuṇṭhaṃ tad vidur budhāḥ

ity anusāreṇa mahā-bhagavataḥ sthānatvā

mahā-vaikuṇṭha evasau, yato vaikuṇṭhāt tasya garīyastvaṃ

śrūyate.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

atah eva-therefore; yatra yatra—wherever; hareḥ—of Lord Hari; sthānam—the abode; vaikuṇṭham—Vaikuṇṭha; tat—that; viduḥ—understand; budāḥ—the learned; iti—thus; anusāreṇa— in accordance with this statement; mahā-bhagavataḥ— of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; sthānatvāt—because of being the abode; mahā-vaikuṇṭhaḥ—the topmost spiritual planet; eva—certainly; asau—this is; yataḥ—therefore; vaikuṇṭhāt—than Vaikuṇṭha; tasya— of Vṛndāvana; garīyastvam—superiority; sruyate—is heard.

English translation and comments

Wherever the Personality of Godhead stays is understood to be the spiritual world. This is confirmed in the following statement of Vedic literature: "The learned know that wherever Lord Hari stays is not within the realm of matter, but is in the spiritual world." From this we may conclude that the place where the Supreme Lord stays in His original form as Lord Kṛṣṇa is the highest of all spiritual planets, above Lord Nārāyaṇa's Vaikuṇṭhaloka.

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 106.130
IAST
pātāla-khaṇḍe
sapta-purīnam
vaikuṇṭhaḥ
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.130

यथ पाताल-खण्डे
एवं सप्त-पुरीनां तु
सर्वोत्कृष्टं च माथुरम्
श्रूयतां महिमा देवी
वैकुण्ठो भुवनोत्तमः इति.

yatha pātāla-khaṇḍe

evaṃ sapta-purīnāṃ tu

sarvotkṛṣṭaṃ ca māthuram

śrūyatāṃ mahimā devī

vaikuṇṭho bhuvanottamaḥ iti.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

yatha—just as; pātāla-khaṇḍe—in the Patala khanda f the Padma Purana; evam—in this way; sapta-purīnam—of the seven holy cities o India; tu—but; sarva—of all; utkṛṣṭam—the best; ca—and; māthuram—the region of Mathurā ; śrūyatām— let it be heard; mahimā—the glory; devī—O goddess; vaikuṇṭhaḥ—the spiritual world; bhuvana—the material sphere; uttamaḥ—above; iti—thus.

English translation and comments

That the place of the Lord's pastimes is in the spiritual world, and not part of the material world, is confirmed in the Pātāla-khaṇḍa of the Padma Purāṇa: "O goddess please hear the glories of Mathurā, the best of the seven holy cities. Mathurā is not part of the material world. It is in the spiritual sky."

[Word-by-word analysis:]

atah eva-therefore; atra—in the Patala khanda of the Padma Purana; eva—certainly; aho—Oh; madhu—purī; ; the district of Mathura; dhanyā—fortunate; vaikuṇṭhāt-to Vaikuṇṭhā, ca— and; garīyasī—superior; iti—thus.

English translation and comments

That the district of Mathurā is situated in the highest portion of the spiritual sky is confirmed in the Pātāla-khaṇḍa of the Padma Purāṇa: "How fortunate is Mathurā! It is better than Vaikuṇṭhā!"

IAST
mathurā-maṇḍalasya
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.132

अथ श्री-वृण्डावनस्य तत्त्वादिकं मथुरा-मण्डलस्यैव तत्त्वेन सिद्धम्.

atha Śrī-vṛṇḍāvanasya tattvādikaṃ mathurā-maṇḍalasyaiva tattvena siddham.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

atha—now; śrī—vṛndāvanasya oof Vṛndāvana; tattva— actual nature; adikam—beginning; mathurā-maṇḍalasya—of the district of Mathurā ; tattvena—by the actual nature; siddham—is proven.

English translation and comments

By understanding that the district of Mathurā is situated in the highest part of the spiritual world, we can also understand the exalted nature of Śrī Vṛndāvana- dhāma, the most sacred part of the district of Mathurā.

तत्र प्रश्नः

English translation and comments
