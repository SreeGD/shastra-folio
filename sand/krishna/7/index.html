Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 4.5
IAST
tṛtīyasya
dvitīya-puruṣasya
upalakṣya
śrī-maitreyena
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 4.5:

तृतीयस्याष्टमे तु द्वितीय-पुरुषस्य व्यूहम् उपलक्ष्य श्री-मैत्रेयेण

tṛtīyasyāṣṭame tu dvitīya-puruṣasya vyūham upalakṣya śrī-maitreyeṇa

[Word-by-word analysis:]

tṛtīyasya—of the Third Canto of Śrīmad Bhāgavatam; aṣṭame—in the Eighth Chapter; tu—also; dvitīya-puruṣasya— of the second puruṣa incarnation; vyūham—the form; upalakṣya—with reference to; śrī-maitreyena—by Maitreya Muni.

English translation and comments:

In the following verses from the Third Canto, Eighth Chapter of Śrīmad- Bhāgavatam, Maiteya Muni describes the form of the second puruṣa-incarnation (Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu).

Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 4.10:

तत्र श्री-भगवन्तं सुष्ठु स्पष्टी-कर्तुं गर्भोदकस्थस्य द्वितीयस्य

पुरुषस्य व्यूहस्य नानावतारित्वं विवृणोति

tatra śrī-bhagavantaṃ suṣṭhu spaṣṭī-kartuṃ garbhodakasthasya dvitīyasya

[Word-by-word analysis:]

tatra—in this matter; śrī-bhagavantam—ŚRī Bhagavān ; suśthu—nicely; spaṣṭi-kartum—to clarify; garbhodakasthasya— resting on the Garbhodaka ocean; dvitīyasaya—of the second; puruṣasya—puruṣa incarnation; vyūhasya—of the form; nānā— various; avatāritvam—the source of the other incarnation; vivṛṇoti—describes.
