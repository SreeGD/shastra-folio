Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 106.35
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.35:

लोकाधिपान् स्मरेत् इत्य् आद्य्-अनन्तरम् च

lokādhipān smaret ity ādy-anantaram ca

[Word-by-word analysis:]

lokā—of the planets; ādhipān—the dieties; smaret—one should meditate; iti—thus; adi—the passage beginning; anantaram—the passage following; ca—also.

English translation and comments:

{ }After the passage beginning "lokādhipān smaret", a description of the highest part of the spiritual world is spoken by Lord Śiva in the following words:

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 106.36
IAST
nānā-sattva
sukhā-āvaham
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.36:

पीयूष-लतिका-कीर्णं

नाना-सत्त्व-निषेवितम्

सर्वर्तु-सुखदं स्वच्छं

सर्व-जन्तु-सुखावहम्

pīyūṣa-latikā-kīrṇaṃ

nānā-sattva-niṣevitam

sarvartu-sukhadaṃ svacchaṃ

sarva-jantu-sukhāvaham

[Word-by-word analysis:]

pīyūṣa—of nectar; latikā—with creepers; kīrṇam—filled; nānā-sattva—with various transcendental qualities; sevitam—decorated; sarva—all; rtu—seasons; sukhadam—bringing happiness; svaccham—pure; sarva—all; jantu—living entities; sukhā-āvaham—full of happiness.

English translation and comments:

{ }"A wise man should meditate on the Kālindī river, which is filled with nectar vines, served by many virtues, blissful in every season, splendid, delightful to everyone, . . .

Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.37:

नीलोत्पल-दल-श्यामां

वायुना चलितां मृदु

वृन्दावन-परागैस् तु

वसितां कृष्ण-वल्लभाम्

nīlotpala-dala-śyāmāṃ

vṛndāvana-parāgais tu

nīla-utpala—of blue lotus; dala—petals; śyāmām—dark; vāyunā—by the breeze; calitām—moved; mṛdu—gently; vṛndāvana—of Vṛndāvana; parāgaiḥ—by the pollen of flowers; tu—also; vasitām—fragant; kṛṣṇa—to Lord Kṛṣṇa; vallabhām—dear.

English translation and comments:

{ } . . . dark with blue lotus petals, filled with gentle breezes, fragrant with the pollen of Vṛndāvana's flowers, dear to Lord Kṛṣṇa, . . .

IAST
madhyagām
Translation

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.38:

सीम्नि कुञ्ज-लतां योषित्-

क्रीडा-मण्ङप-मध्यगाम्

कालिन्दीं सम्स्मरेद् धीमाह्

सुवर्ण-लता-पङ्कजाम्

sīmni—on the shore; kuñja—groves; latām—and creepers; yoṣit—for the young girls of Vṛndāvana; krīḍā—for pastimes; maṇḍapa—pavillions; madhyagām—in the midst; kālindīm—the daughter of Mount Kalinda; samsmaret—one should meditate; dhīmān—an intelligent person; suvarṇa—with golden; tatā—shore; paṅkajam—and lotus flowers.

English translation and comments:

{ }. . . surrounded by groves of vines and pavilions where gopīs enjoy pastimes, and filled with golden lotus vines.

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 106.42:

English translation and comments:

{ }" . . . which again and again trembles in the breezes blowing over the Kālindī's waters, and is filled with flowers, trees, and birds.
