Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 82.45

अवतारा ह्य् असङ्ख्येया
असन् सत्त्व-स्वभाविनः
विंशतिस् तेषु मुख्यान् यान्
श्रूत्वा मुच्येन् महांहसः

avatārā hy asaṅkhyeyā

asan sattva-svabhāvinaḥ

viṃśatis teṣu mukhyān yān

śrūtvā mucyen mahāṃhasaḥ

[Word-by-word analysis:]

avatārāḥ—the incarnations of Godhead; hi—certainly; asaṅkhyeyāḥ—innumerable; asan—are; sattva-svabhāvinaḥ—with spiritual forms; viṃśatiḥ—twenty; teṣu—among them; mukhyān—most important; yān—which; śrūtvā—hearing about; mucyet—one may become free; maha-aṃhasaḥ—from the greatest sins.

English translation and comments

"The innumerable incarnations of the Supreme Lord all manifest eternal spiritual forms, and among them twenty incarnations are most prominent. If one hears the glories of these twenty incarnations, he will become freed from the most abominable sins." S

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 82.1
IAST
śṛṇu nārada vakṣyāmi iti adi
pratipādaka
ādi-śabḍat-from the word "ādi" etāni
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 82.1

अथ शृणु नारद वक्ष्यामि इत्य् आदि चरण-चिह्न-प्रतिपादक-पद्म-वचनान्ते
आदि-शब्दाद् एतान्य् अपि पद्यानि ज्ञेयानि

atha śṛṇu nārada vakṣyāmi ity ādi caraṇa-cihna-pratipādaka-padma-vacanānte

ādi-śabdād etāny api padyāni jñeyāni

[Word-by-word analysis:]

atha—now; śṛṇu nārada vakṣyāmi iti adi—the quotation which begins on page 476; caraṇa—of the Lord's lotus feet; cihna—the markings; pratipādaka—describing; padma—of the Padma Purāṇa; vacaṇa—the statement; ante—at the conclusion; ādi-śabḍat-from the word "ādi" etāni—these; api—also; padyāni—verses; jñeyani—may be understood.

English translation and comments

The following verses from the Vedic literatures may be appended to this description of Lord Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet.

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 82.2
IAST
tri-aṅgula-mānataḥ
tat-agrataḥ
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 82.2

मध्ये ध्वजा तु विज्ञेया
पद्मं त्र्याङ्गुल-मानतः
वज्रं वै दक्षिणे पर्श्वे
अङ्कुशो वै तद्-अग्रतः

madhye dhvajā tu vijñeyā

padmaṃ tryāṅgula-mānataḥ

vajraṃ vai dakṣiṇe parśve

aṅkuśo vai tad-agrataḥ

[Word-by-word analysis:]

madhye—in the middle; dhvajā—a flag; tu—also; vijñayā—should be understood; padmam—a lotus flower; tri-aṅgula-mānataḥ—the measurement of 3 fingers; vajram—a thunderbolt; vai—certainly; dakṣiṇe—on the right; parśve—side; aṅkuśaḥ—a rod for controlling elephants; vai—certainly; tat-agrataḥ—before that.

English translation and comments

"In the middle of Lord Kṛṣṇa's lotus foot there is the mark of a flag, and also a lotus flower, three fingers distant form the front of the foot. On the left side of His foot there is the mark of a thunderbolt and before that there is the mark of a rod for controlling elephants. Test 3 yavo 'py aṅgustha-mūle syāt svastikaṃ yatra kutracit ādiṃ caraṇam ārabhya yāvad vai madhyamā sthitā yavaḥ-a barleycorn; api-also; aṅgustha-of the big toe; mūle at the base; syāt- is; svastikam-a svastika; yatra-where kutracit-somewhere; ādim-beginning; caraṇam-foot; ārabhya- having begun; yāvat-to which extent; vai-certainly; madhyamā-in the middle; sthitā-situated. "At the base of the Lord's big toe there is the mark of a barleycorn and also the mark of a svastika.

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 82.4
IAST
ūrdhva-rekhā
padma-sāmjñake
aṣṭa-konam-an eight
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 82.4

तावद् वै ऊर्ध्व-रेखा च
कथिता पद्म-संज्ञके
अष्ट-कोणं तु भो वत्स
मानं चाष्टाङ्गुलैश् च तत्

tāvad vai ūrdhva-rekhā ca

kathitā padma-saṃjñake

aṣṭa-koṇaṃ tu bho vatsa

mānaṃ cāṣṭāṅgulaiś ca tat

[Word-by-word analysis:]

tāvat—in that way; vai—certainly; ūrdhva-rekhā—an auspicious line going from top to bottom; ca—also; kathitā—is described; padma-sāmjñake—in the Padma Purāna; aṣṭa-konam-an eight—pointed str; tu—also; bho—oh; vatsa—o son; mānam—measurement; ca—also; aṣṭa—eight; āngulaih—with fingers; ca—also; tat—that.

English translation and comments

"There is also an ūrdhva-rekhā line beginning at the junction of the big-toe and fore-toe and extending to the middle of the Lord's lotus feet. This ūrdhva-rekhā line is also described in the Padma Purāṇa. Eight fingers away from the middle toe there is the mark of an eight-pointed star.

[Word-by-word analysis:]

nirdiṣṭam—desribed; dakṣiṇe—on the right; pāde—foot; iti—thus; āhuh—they have explained; munayaḥ—the sages; kila—certainly; evam—thus; pādasya—of the foot; cihnani—the marks; tāni—they; eva—certainly; vaiṣṇava—of the devotees; uttama—o best.

English translation and comments

"O best of the Vaiṣṇavas, the great sages have described the markings on Lord Kṛṣṇa's right foot in this way.

Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 82.6

दक्सिनेतर-स्थानानि
संवदामीह साम्प्रतम्
चतुर्-अङ्गुल-मानेन
त्व् अङ्गुलीनां समीपतः
इन्द्र-चापं ततो विद्याद्
अन्यत्र न भवेत् क्वचित्
त्रि-कोनं मध्यणिर्दिष्टं
कलशो यत्र कुत्रचित्

daksinetara-sthānāni

saṃvadāmīha sāmpratam

catur-aṅgula-mānena

tv aṅgulīnāṃ samīpataḥ

indra-cāpaṃ tato vidyād

anyatra na bhavet kvacit

tri-konaṃ madhyaṇirdiṣṭaṃ

kalaśo yatra kutracit

[Word-by-word analysis:]

dakṣiṇa—of the right foot; itara—other; sthānāni— places; samvadāmi—Iam explaining; ina—here; sāmpratam—now; catuḥ-aṅgula—of four finger; mānena—with the measurement; tu—also; angulīnām samīpatah—near the toes; indra-cāpam—a rainbow; tataḥ—then; vidyāt—may be; anyatra—in another place; na—not; bhavet—may be; anyatra—in another place; na— not; bhavet—may be; kvacit—anywhere; tri-konam—a triangle; madhya—in the middle; nirdiṣṭam—indicated; kalaśaḥ-a water—pot; yatra—where; kutracit—somewhere.

English translation and comments

"I have thus described the markings on the Lord's right foot, and now I shall describe the left foot of the Lord. On the Lord's left foot, near the toes, there is a rainbow four fingers in length. In the middle of the foot there are also a triangle and water-pot.

Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha 82.8
IAST
nirūpitam
pramānataḥ
Translation & commentary

Sanskrit text in Devanagari and Unicode transliteration of verse 82.8

बिन्दुर् वै मत्स्य -चिह्नं च
ह्य् आद्य्-अन्ते वै निरूपितम्
गोश्पदं तेषु विज्ञेयम्
आद्य्-अङ्गुल-प्रमानतः इत्य् आदि

bindur vai matsya -cihnaṃ ca

gośpadaṃ teṣu vijñeyam

ādy-aṅgula-pramānataḥ ity ādi

[Word-by-word analysis:]

binduḥ—a drop; vai—certainly; matsya—of a fish; cihnam—the mark; ca—also; hi—certainly; ādi—from beginning; ante—to end; vai—certainly; nirūpitam—described; gośpadam—a cow's hoofprint; teṣu—among them; vijñeyam—may be known; ādi—one; angula—finger; pramānataḥ—in measurement; iti—thus; ādi—in the passage beginning.

English translation and comments

"There are also a kuṅkuma spot, the mark of a fish, and the mark of a cow's hoofprint, one finger in diameter. In this way Ihave completely described the markings on the lotus feet of Lord Kṛṣṇa."
