Thus it is said: “You are the original Personality of Godhead, controller of all creations and transcendental to the material energy. You have cast away the effects of the material en¬ ergy by Your spiritual potency. You are always situated in eternal bliss in Your own Self” ( Bhag. 1.7.23). Therefore we understand the phrase mayam ca tadapasrayam [in Bhagavatam 1.7.4] to mean that Maya takes shelter of Him in an inferior position, hiding from His sight; thus she does not constitute His svarupa, or essential na¬ ture. As said later on, “Maya, feeling ashamed, runs away from the Lord's direct presence” (Bhag. 2.7.47).
