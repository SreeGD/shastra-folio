“vrttir bhutani bhutanam caranam acarani ca
krta svena nmarh tatra kamac codanayapi va”
caranam bhutanam samanyato ‘carani ca~karac carani ca

codanayapi va ya niyata vrttirftvika krta sa vrttir ucyata ityarthah.
“Vrtti means the process of sustenance, by which the mov¬
ing beings live upon the nonmoving. For a human being,
vrtti specifically means acting for one’s livelihood in a man¬
ner suited to one’s personal nature. Such action may be
carried out either in pursuit of selfish desire or in accor¬
dance with the Vedic injunctions” ( Bhag . 12.7.13). Mobile
living beings generally thrive on immobile ones, but the word
ca (and) in this verse hints that, when the desire impels
them, moving creatures will also subsist on other moving
creatures. For human beings, however, the means of liveli¬
hood is prescribed according to their individual natures, on
the basis of either selfish desire or scriptural injunction. All
this is called vrtti.
