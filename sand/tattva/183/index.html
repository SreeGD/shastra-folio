Commentary _ffib _ Srila Jiva GosvamI here exposes further complications that arise from accepting Brahman’s upadhis as empirically real. The monists believe that a jiva can become free from the bondage of his upadhis by becoming educated through the srutis. This is their idea from the following sruti statements: Tat tvam asi: “Thou art that” ( Chandogya Up. 6.8.7).
