Tattva-sandarbha (English by Kapoor) 252
Translation & commentary

Anuccheda 56 the fact that the soul does not create the elements needed for the maintenance of his body and that material nature, being inert, cannot create anything without the help of a sentient being. What's more, the soul takes no part in main¬ taining the material body during deep sleep, when he is com¬ pletely aloof from it. In this way, knowing the characterstics of the jiva and his relationship to the body can help us understand something of the Supersoul and Brahman. And taking into account the common quality of consciousness shared by the jiva and Brahman, the latter can be identified as the nondual reality, the subject of Srimad-Bhagavatam. All these conclusions are based on an a’nalysis of the individual jiva's situation. Now Sriia Jiva GosvamI begins a new analysis, based on the total situation of the universe.
