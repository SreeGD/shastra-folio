1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 60
Translation

TEXT 1 ragera bhajana-patha kahi ebe abhimata loka-beda-sara ei bani sakhira anuga hand braje siddha-deha pana ei bhabe jiiddbe parani ragera —of the attachment; bhajana-patha —the path of worship; kahi — let me explain; ebe —now; abhimata —opinion; loka —worldly; beda — vedic; sara —essence; ei —this; bani —statements; sakhira —of the sakhis; anuga —following; hand —being; braje —in Braja; siddha-deha —spiritual body; pana —having received; ei bhabe —in this way; judabe —cool down; param —the soul. Now, I will explain the process of worshipping the Supreme Lord through the path of attachment (raganuga bhakti). These statements are the essence and in accordance with the Vedas and the pure devotees. * One should serve the Lord in Braja following in the footsteps of the sakhis with a suitable spiritual body. In this way, one’s soul will be cooled and satisfied. TEXTS 2-3 The Truth About Sri Radhika and the Sakhis srl radhikara sakhljata taha ba kahiba kata mukhya sakhl kariye ganana lalita bisakha tatha sucitra campakalata rangadevl sudevi kathana tungabidya indurekha ei asta-sakhl lekha ebe kahi narma-sakhi gana ihon seba saha carl priya-prestha nama dhari prema seba kare anuksana s srl radhikara —of Sri Radhika; sakhl —the sakhis; jata —all; taha ba — that; kahiba —will explain; kata —how much; mukhya —principal; / sakhl —the sakhis; kariye —doing; ganana —counting; lalita —Sri Lalita; / bisakha —Sri Visakha; tatha —and; sucitra —Sri Sucitra; campakalata — Sri Campakalata; rangadevl —Sri Rangadevl; sudevi —Sri Sudevi; / / kathana —description; tungabidya —Sri Tungabidya; indurekha —Sri Indurekha; ei —this; asta-sakhl —eight sakhis; lekha —explaining; ebe — now; kahi —are known as; narma-sakhi gana —most intimate sakhis; ihon-seba —all these; saha carl —companions; priya-prestha —most beloved; nama —names; dhari —known as; prema seba —loving devotional service; kare —do; anuksana —constantly / The different types of sakhis of Sri Radhika are countless. Therefore, I will only describe the principal ones. Lalita, Visaka, Sucitra, Champakalata, Rangadevl, Sudevi, Tungavidya, Indurekha are the eight / principal sakhis. They are the most beloved companions of Sri Radhika and are constantly serving Their Lordships with profound love. They are most intimately associated with Radha and Krsna. *See Appendix 1
