1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 56
Translation & commentary

TEXT 5 dasanete trna dhari ha ha kisora-kisori caranabje nibedana kari braja-raja-kumara-syama brsabhanii kumarl nama srl-radhika-nama manoharl dasanete —in the mouth; trna —straw; dhari —holdings; ha ha kisorakisori —O Young Couple; caranabje —unto the lotus feet; nibedana kari —offer prayers; braja-raja-kumara-syama —Syama, the son of King of Braja; brsabhanii kumarl nama —the name of daughter of King Brsabhanu; srl-radhika-nama manoharl —the most enchanting Radhika. / O Divine Youthful Couple, O Syamasundara, the son of the King of Braja, O Radhika the beautiful enchanting daughter of King Brsabhanu, taking a straw in my mouth, I pray to Your lotus feet. / / Sri Krsna is the manoharl. He captivates the mind of Sri Radhika and other goddesses of fortune.
