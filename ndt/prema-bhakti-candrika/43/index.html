Prema-bhakti-candrikā 43
Translation

TEXT 20 vrndavane kimathavanija mandira vd karagrhe kimathava kanakasane vd indram jaje kimathava narakam bhajami sri krsna-sevanamrta na sukham kadapi vrndavane —in Vrndavan; kimathava —or; nija mandira —own house; va —or; karagrhe —in prison; kimathava —or; kanakasane —on a golden throne; va —or; Indram —the post of Indra; jaje —awarded; kimathava — or; narakam —in the hell; bhajami —reach; sri krsna —Lord Krsna; sevanamrta —the nectar of this service; na —not; sukham —happiness; kadapi —ever "Whether I live in Vrndavana or at home, whether I live in the prison or I sit on a golden throne, whether I am awarded the post of Indra or go to hell, there is no happiness anywhere without the worship of Krsna." If one constantly resides in Braja and hears and chants about the names / and pastimes of Sri Krsna, with the residents of Braja, then he certainly receives transcendental bliss. In other words, it is the source of the highest pleasure.
