Prema-bhakti-candrikā 106
Translation & commentary

TEXT 9 rddha-krsne nahi rati anya jane bale pad prema-bhakd rid nahi jane nahi bhakdra sandhana bharame karaye dhyana brtha tdra se char a jibane radha-krsne —in Radha and Krsna; nahi —do not; rad —attachment; anya jane —to another person; bale —says; pad —the Lord; premabhakd —loving devotional service; rid —characteristics; nahi jane —do not know; nahi —do not; bhakdra —of devotional service; sandhana — trace; bharame —in illusion; karaye dhyana —meditate; brtha —useless; tdra —his; se —that; char a —insignificant; jibane —life. / / Those who have not developed attachment for Sri Sri Radha-Krsna, not knowing the process of loving devotional service, consider others as their lord and protector. Such people do not inquire about devotional service, rather they engage in pseudo meditation. Their lives are useless and insignificant.
