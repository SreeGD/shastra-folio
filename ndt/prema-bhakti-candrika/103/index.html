Prema-bhakti-candrikā 103
Translation & commentary

TEXT 6 bisaya bisama-gati nahi bhaja braja-pati srl-nanda nandana sukha-sara swarga ara apabarga samsara naraka-bhoga sarba-nasa janama-bikara bisaya —material sense objects; bisama —dangerous; gati —results; nahi —do not; bhaja —worship; braja-pati —the Lord of Braja; sri-nanda nandana —the son of Nanda Maharaja; sukha-sara —the abode of bliss; swarga —heaven; ara —and; apabarga —liberation; samsara —material existence; naraka —hell; bhoga —enjoy; sarba-nasa —ruins everything; janama —human form of life; bikara —wasted. The result of material enjoyments are dangerous. Why do you not A worship Sri Nandanandana, the Lord of Braja and the abode of ecstatic bliss? Desires for heavenly planets, liberation and enjoying the material world are hellish. They ruin one’s human form of life.
