1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 85
Translation & commentary

TEXT 16 karml jnanl misra-bhakta nd habe taya anurakta suddha-bhajanete kara mana braja-janera jei mata take habe anugata ei se parama tattwa-dhana karml —the performers of fruitive activities; jndnl —impersonalists; misra-bhakta —mixed devotees; nd —do not; habe —will be; taya —in that; anurakta —attached; s'uddha —pure; bhajanete —in worshipping the Lord; kara mana —engage your mind; braja-janera —of the residents of Braja; jei mata —the opinion; tahe —in that; habe —will be; anugata — follow; ei se —this is the; parama tattwa —supreme truth; dhana — wealth. Do not mingle with fruitive workers, mental speculators and mixed devotees. Instead engage yourself in pure devotional service under the guidance of the residents of Braja. This is the highest treasure.
