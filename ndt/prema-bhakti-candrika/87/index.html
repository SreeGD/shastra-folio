1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 87
Translation

TEXT 18 radha-krsna sn-carana tate saba samarpana srl-carane balihari jana dufihu nama suni suni bhakta mukhe puni puni parama ananda sukha-pana / radha-krsna —Sri Radha and Krsna; srl-carana —lotus feet; tate —in that; saba —everything; samarpana —surrender; srl-carane —unto the lotus feet; balihari jana —great; dunhu —both; nama —the holy names; suni suni —repeatedly hearing; bhakta mukhe —through the mouths of the devotees; puni puni —again and again; parama ananda —great happiness; sukha-pana —will be satisfied 1 surrender myself unto the lotus feet of Radha and Krsna and take Their merciful shelter. 1 will repeatedly hear the names of Radha and Krsna from the mouths of pure devotees and attain great happiness and satisfaction.
