prema-bhakti-candrika.57

1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 57
Translation

TEXT 6 kanaka-ketakl rai syama marakata kai darapa-darapa karu cura nata-bara-sikharinl natinlra siromanl dunhu-gune dunhun-mana jhiira / kanaka —golden; ketakl —the color of ketakl flower; rai —Sri Radhika; / / syama —Sr! Syamasundara; marakata-kai —the color of blue sapphire; darapa-darapa —pride; karu ciira —defeat; natabara —the king of dancers; sikharinl —crest jewels; natinlra —of the dancers; siromanl — topmost; dunhu-gune —by the qualities of each other; dunhun-mana — the minds of both; jhiira —overwhelmed. / / / Sri Radhika resembles a golden pine flower, and Sri Syamasundara / resembles a blue sapphire. Their beauty defeats the pride of all. Sri / / Radhika and Sri Syama are the topmost dancers and entrance one another by Their jewel-like qualities. Kai means desire. Peacock feather is the special decoration on the head / of Krsna who is the best of dancers. The head of Sri Radha, who is also dancing, is decorated with manl, jewels.
