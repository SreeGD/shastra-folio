Prema-bhakti-candrikā 81
Translation & commentary

TEXT 12 rajara je rajya-pata jena natujara nata dekhite dekhite kichu naya hena maya kare jei parama iswara sei tanre mana sada kara bhaya rajara —of the king; je —that; rajya-pata —the kingdom; jena —as if; natujara nata —act of play; dekhite dekhite —gradually; kichu naya —as if nothing; hena —such; maya —illusory; kare —does; jei —whoever; parama iswara —the Supreme Lord; sei —his; tanre —to him; mana —O my mind; sada —always; kara bhaya —be afraid of. The state and administration of a king is just like an act of a play. It gradually vanquishes in due course of time. O my mind, it is He, the Supreme Lord who causes this show by His illusory energy. Be always afraid of falling under its control.
