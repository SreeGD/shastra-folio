prema-bhakti-candrika.98

1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 98
Translation

TEXT 1 bacanera agocara brndabana llla-stala swaprakasa premananda-ghana jahate prakata silkha nahi jara-mrtyil-dilhkha krsna-llla-rasa anuksana bacanera —words; agocara —beyond; brndabana —the abode of Vrndavana; llla-stala —various places of pastimes; swaprakasa —self manifest; premananda —ecstatic love; ghana —intense; jahate —in which; prakata —manifested; silkha —happiness; nahi —does not exist; jara —old age; mrtyu —death; diihkha —distress; krsna —Lord Krsna; lllarasa —the nectarean pastimes; anuksana —always. / Sri Vrndavana, the place of the pastimes of Radha and Krsna, is indescribable. It is self-manifest and full of ecstatic love. Happiness is eternally present there. It is devoid of the miseries of old age and death. The pastimes of Krsna are constantly performed there. / Srlla Narottama dasa Thakura describes Vrndavana saying vacanera agocara. When it is not possible to fully describe a place, then the phrase vacanera agocara is used. “idarh vrndavanam ramyarin mama dhamaiva kevalam atra ye pasavo paksl-vrksah klta naramarah ye vasanti mamadhisnye mrta yanti mamalayam” “This Vrndavana is My own most beloved abode. Everyone who lives here in My abode—the animals, birds, trees, insects, humans and demigods—when they die they go to My eternal kingdom.”
