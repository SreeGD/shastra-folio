Prema-bhakti-candrikā 22
Translation

TEXT 9 s Suddha-bhakti-tattva Truth About Pure Devotional Service bhagabata-sastra-marma naba-bidha bhakti-dharma sadayei kariba su-sebana anya-devasraya ndi tomare kahila bhai ei bhakti parama-karana / bhagabata —Srlmad-Bhagavatam; sastra —scriptures; marma —purport; naba —nine; bidha —types; bhakti-dharma —devotional service; sadayei —always; kariba —will do; su-sebana —serve; anya —other; devasraya —taking shelter of the demigods; nai —not; tomare —to you; kahila —telling; bhai —O dear brother; ei —this; bhakti —devotional service; parama —supreme; karana —method. / The nine processes of devotional service are the essence of Srlmad- Bhagavatam. O brother! The best method of attaining loving devotional service is to always engage in performing these nine limbs of devotional service without taking shelter of the demigods. O my dear brother (mind), do not take shelter of any demigod such as / Lord Brahma or Lord Siva, but take shelter of Lord Krsna without deviation and engage in the nine types of devotional service.
