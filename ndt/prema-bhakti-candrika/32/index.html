prema-bhakti-candrika.32

1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 32
Translation

TEXT 9 kama krodha moha lobha mada mdtsarjya dambha-saha sthane sthane nijukta kariba dnanda kari hrdaya ripu kari parajaya anayase gobinda bhajiba kama —lust; krodha —anger; moha —illusion; lobha —greed; mada — pride; mdtsarjya —envy; dambha-saha —with vanity; sthane sthane —in their respective places; nijukta —engage; kariba —will do; dnanda — happiness; kari —doing; hrdaya —heart; ripu —the enemies; kari —do; parajaya —defeat; anayase —easily; gobinda —Lord Govinda; bhajiba — will worship. I will engage lust, anger, greed, illusion, envy and pride in their proper places. In this way, I will defeat the enemies and with ecstacy in my heart, I will worship Govinda without difficulty.
