prema-bhakti-candrika.48

1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 48
Translation

TEXT 4 tumi ta parama deba ndhi more upekhiba suna suna pranera isvara jadi karon aparadha tathapiha tumi ndtha seba diya kara anucara tumi ta —You are; parama deba —Supreme Lord; ndhi —not; more —me; upekhiba —will neglect; suna suna —please hear; pranera isvara —O Lord of my life; jadi —if; karon —do; aparadha —offense; tathapiha —still; tumi —You; natha —Lord; seba —service; diya —by giving; kara —make; anucara —servant. O Lord of my heart, please hear my prayer. You are the Supreme Lord. Please do not reject me. Even if I commit offenses, still You are my only protector. Please give me some insignificant service and consider me Your own servant.
