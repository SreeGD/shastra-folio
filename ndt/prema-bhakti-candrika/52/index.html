prema-bhakti-candrika.52

1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 52
Translation & commentary

TEXT 1 ana katha ana byatha nahi jena jana tatha tomara carana smrti-sajhe abirata abikala tuya guna kala-kala gai jena satera samajhe ana katha —mundane topics; ana byatha —mundane pains; nahi —do not; jena—may; jana —go; tatha —there; tomara —Your; carana smrti — remembering the lotus feet; sajhe —decorate; abirata —without interruption; abikala —properly; tuya —Your; guna —qualities; kalakala —glorify; gai —chant; jena —may I; satera —of the devotees; samajhe —in the society Wherever there are discussions on mundane topics, pains and pleasures, may I restrain myself from going there. Let the rememberance of Your lotus feet decorate my heart. May I properly chant Your transcendental qualities unceasingly in the society of devotees. Where there are other talks, there is anxiety and anguish, and I shall not go to such a place.
