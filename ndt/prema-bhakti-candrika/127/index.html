Prema-bhakti-candrikā 127
Translation

TEXT 16 Manah Siksa Instructions To the Mind samsara batojare kama-phanse bandhi mare phukara karaha haridasa karaha bhakata-sanga prema-katha-rasa-ranga tabe haya bipada binasa samsara —the material world; batojare —plundering; kama —lusty desires; phanse —with the hopes; bandhi —binding; mare —killing; / phukara karaha —lament; haridasa —O servant of Sri Hari; karaha bhakata-sanga —associate with devotees; prema-katha —topics of loving dealings; rasa-ranga —nectarean pastimes; tabe —than; haya —is; bipada —danger; binasa —vanquishes. The material world is cheating you. Lusty desires are killing you by hanging you with ropes. Lament, O servant of Hari. Associate with devotees and engage in discussing the topics of love of God. Then, all your dangers will be vanquished.
