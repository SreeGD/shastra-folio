prema-bhakti-candrika.4

1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 4
Translation

TEXT 4 gandharva garboksapana svalasyavismapitasesa krti vraja svasrtapana prathithaya tasmai namo namah srlla narottamaya gandharva —of Gandharvas; garva —pride; ksapana —destroyed; svalasya —whose dancing; vismapita —brought to amazement; asesa krti vraja —the assembly of learned musicians and dancers; sva srsta pana prathitaya —who has initiated manohara sahi style of singing and playing mrdanga; tasmai— unto him; namo namah —I offer my obeisances again ✓ and again; srlla narottamaya —unto Srlla Narottama ✓ Again and again I offer my obeisances unto Srila Narottama dasa Thakura. His dancing is so beautiful that it conquers the pride of the Gandharvas and amazes the assembly of all expert dancers and singers. ✓ Srila Narottama dasa Thakura has thus initiated his own style of singing and playing the mrdanga called manohara sahi.
