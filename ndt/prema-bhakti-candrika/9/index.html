prema-bhakti-candrika.9

1 verse
Prema-bhakti-candrikā 9
Translation

TEXT 9 sri radhika krsna vilasa sindhau nimajjatah srila narottamasya pathed ya evastakam etad uccaih asau tadiyam padavirin prayati / / sri radhika krsna —of Sri Sr! Radha and Krsna; vilasa sindhau —in the ocean of pastimes; nimajjatah —drawn (absorbed); srila narottamasya — / of Srila Narottama dasa Thakura; pathet —should read; yah —he who; eva —certainly; astakam etad —this astaka; uccaih —aloud; asau —he; / tadiyam padavim —to that same abode obtained by Srila Narottama; prayati —goes / / A person who is absorbed in the ocean of the pastimes of Sri Sri Radha and Krsna should read these eight prayers aloud. And by so doing, he will ✓ certainly reach the same abode obtained by Srila Narottama himself.
