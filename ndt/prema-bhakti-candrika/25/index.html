Prema-bhakti-candrikā 25
Translation

TEXT 2 maha janera jei patha tate habe aniirata purbapara kariya bicara sadhana-smarana-lila ihate na kara held kaya mane kariya susara maha janera —of the mahaj anas; jei —that which; patha —path; tate —in that; habe —will be; aniirata —attached; purbapara —previous and later; kariya —doing; bicara —consideration; sadhana —practise; smarana — remembrance; lila —pastimes; ihate —in this; na —not; kara —do; held — neglect; kaya-mane —with body and mind; kariya —doing; susara — essence. The essence of sadhana bhakti is to carefully consider and follow the path displayed by the previous and the present mahajanas (great personalities). One should not neglect the practice of remembering the pastimes of the Lord, for devotional service should be performed with the mind as well as the body. Previous mahajanas refers to the sages of Dandakaranya, and the srutis described in Brhat Vamana Parana, and Candrakant, etc. They were extremely eager to have Krsna as their lover. Hence, they accepted the mood and form of a beloved gopl, and engaged in the loving service of / / Sri Krsna. Sri Bilvamangala Thakura also attained the mood of a sakhi, / being greedy for the mood of a sakhi of Srlmatl Radhika. Thus he displayed the mood of a sakhi and the process of worshipping Krsna under the guidance of the sakhis. Since these personalties appeared / before Srlman Mahaprabhu, they are known as the previous mahajanas. / However, the present mahajanas are the six Gosvamls headed by Srlla Rupa and Sanatana Gosvamls. The word susara means highest perfection.
